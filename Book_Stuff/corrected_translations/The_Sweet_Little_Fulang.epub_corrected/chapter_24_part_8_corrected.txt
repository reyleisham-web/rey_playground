Chapter 24 Part 8


Shen Xuanqing had gone to school when he was young and knew a few words, but the books he studied were serious, so naturally he didn’t know the characters for warm, fragrant, and nephrite in the obscene songs. He only knew how to hold his fulang, so he would feel more at ease, and his head was filled with something. Yet the more he clung, the more Lu Gu’s scent seemed to surround him, as if he were pulled by an uncontrollable thought, leaving him unsettled and impatient.

Lu Gu remained ignorant of worldly matters, and no one had taught him when he got married. Shuang’er and the women of the village attended to muxiang (medicinal herb) soup after marriage, and they had to wear fragrance on their bodies, or Du Hehua wouldn’t boil water for them to bathe. As for other things, usually a woman would secretly teach them from a rough picture book, but Du Hehua hadn’t found such a picture book for him, so he didn’t understand what he was doing at all.

The head was newly covered, and the night was precious. The sun above pierced the dark clouds, and the mountains and forests washed by the rain looked fresh. Before the cold could fade, Lu Gu sat in the yard basking in the sun. He saw a few leaves on the ground beaten by wind and rain, with water marks; the leaves had turned yellow, which suddenly brought an autumnal feeling.

Shen Xuanqing went out with a fish basket and a net, saying he would go catch fish, shrimp, and loaches by the river. Both of them had risen late this morning, so he didn’t go out to hunt, and he didn’t see the sun until noon.

Lu Gu was a little drowsy from the sun, and his mind became dazed. It was at that moment that he realized he truly seemed to have become Shen Xuanqing’s ger last night. He had been bought to be Shen Xuanqing’s fulang. He hadn’t expected this before, but now it had indeed happened. It seemed this was what a real husband should be like. While he was afraid of shame, he also accepted his fate.

So what if he didn’t admit it? He didn’t dare to move last night; he bit his lower lip in pain and didn’t say a word. He couldn’t change his mind today and say no, and besides, he didn’t have the courage to tell Shen Xuanqing.

He sat alone for a long time, and when he finally couldn’t bear it anymore and wanted to go back to his room to sleep, the dog jumped over the threshold and rushed in from the outside, followed by Shen Xuanqing. His clothes were wet, but he didn’t care; his rolled-up sleeves were not lowered, revealing his slender and strong forearms, his legs long, his waist slender and straight. When he saw Lu Gu at this moment, his face wore spring smiles more than on that day. In the sun it was striking.

Because of his drowsiness, Lu Gu raised his head and stared blankly, not avoiding his eyes for the first time. This look made him realize that Shen Xuanqing looked so handsome and heroic.

The vague and cold outline became clear in his heart, and he seemed to suddenly realize that Shen Xuanqing looked like this.

“I used a dense net to carry two big fish. If they were trapped in the net, they would not die because there was river water. The edge of the net was under the blue stone. If you want to eat tomorrow or the day after tomorrow, you can go there and fetch it.” Shen Xuanqing walked over a few steps, tall and handsome, his posture still upright; even a bunt could not hide his bearing. These steps could be called high-spirited.

He smiled more than usual, crouching halfway in front of Lu Gu, showing him the fish basket, and said with a smile: “These loaches and other fish are for you to make soup, and there are small shrimps. It rained yesterday, the river has risen, and the white-shelled shrimps in the mountain streams have been washed down. I caught a lot and boiled it in the water for you to eat.”

The small fish basket was heavy, and it was clear that a lot had been caught. Seeing himself reflected in Lu Gu’s smiling eyes, he realized he had been staring at him for a long time, and panic seized him; he quickly lowered his head and pretended to look at the fish basket. After what had happened last night, being looked at by his husband, Shen Xuanqing was no longer at a loss when facing Lu Gu as before.

He half-squatted and half-kneeled in front of Lu Gu, resting his left knee lightly; the sun shone on Lu Gu’s face, making it look more radiant. Lu Gu’s cheeks were not fleshy, but they were soft—he had known that last night.

The breath gradually approached, and the cheek was lightly rubbed by the other’s lips. The fear from last night returned, and Lu Gu tightly grasped the fabric on Shen Xuanqing’s leg.

After a while, Shen Xuanqing realized his gaffe, reluctantly backed away, and said in a hoarse voice, “I’m going to kill the loach.”

“Yeah.” Lu Gu nodded with lowered brows; the place where his cheek had been rubbed was hot, and he couldn’t lift his head.

Shen Xuanqing got up and walked a few steps to the kitchen, then turned his head and said, “If you’re tired, why don’t you sleep for a while, and I will call you when it’s time to eat.”

Lu Gu answered okay and hid in the room.

As for Shen Xuanqing, who cooked by himself, as long as it was not frying, he could handle it. At home, he had seen Wei Lanxiang stewing loach. After killing it, she would slice some ginger and scallions, put them in a jar, add water, and boil it in a small medicine stove. The water just boiled; Lu Gu steamed some steamed cakes yesterday, and he warmed a few to serve.

Lu Gu couldn’t hear movement outside after lying down for a long time, and he fell into a deep sleep. It wasn’t that he was in poor health; he was used to work and weeding on weekdays, and he could endure the tremors of life. If he progressed step by step like an ordinary young couple, he wouldn’t be so tired.

It was just that Shen Xuanqing was too young; his tanned muscles seemed to have nowhere to exert their strength, and this was the first time he was careless. He was a little reckless; he didn’t want to relinquish the taste of meat, and the world had been turned upside down.

Life had changed, and this change stayed; Lu Gu gradually got used to it from time to time. With more personal care, there were changes inside and outside the house. A few wildflowers were moved by Lu Gu to the front of the courtyard gate. After watering, these plants survived and bloomed large red or yellow flowers, adding color to the front of the house.

He saw that there were sophisticated people in the village who could grow flowers; when he saw beautiful ones in the mountains, he even dug up their roots and brought them back. The morning breeze blew, and the flower branches swayed gently.

Lu Gu locked the door, the bamboo basket strapped to his back. After turning around, he walked down the mountain with Shen Xuanqing, who pulled the cart. The cart was full of prey, and it was time to sell.
