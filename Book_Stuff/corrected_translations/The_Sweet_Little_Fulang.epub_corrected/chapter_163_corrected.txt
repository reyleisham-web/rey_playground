Chapter 163


Lu Wen had never been favored by the Li family’s old madam, but after becoming pregnant with the Li family’s bloodline, his standing improved a great deal compared to before.

Li Mingshan was the Li family’s only son. He had no brothers or sisters, and the family line, though not thriving, wasn’t extinguished. Who would have thought that both his wife and concubine would end up pregnant one after another? Outsiders praised the fertility of the woman and the shuang’er he married, and Li Mingshan himself was regarded as capable, which pleased the old madam immensely. Whether it was visiting temples to pray for blessings or giving alms to accumulate merit, he now brought not only Luo Hongchou but even Lu Wen along.

With the old madam’s rising favor, Lu Wen finally gained some face within the Li family and received many benefits. His meals, clothing, and daily necessities grew far more refined than before.

After learning of his pregnancy,he sent a servant to report the good news to Du Lianhua. As a mere concubine, even though the old madam was somewhat pleased by the news, she did not send anyone to announce it to the Lu family. In contrast, when Luo Hongchou was diagnosed pregnant, the old madam joyfully dispatched her personal old maidservant to the Luo family to deliver the news.

Lu Wen understood the difference between himself and Luo Hongchou all too well—and it all boiled down to family background. He believed his own character, appearance, and talents were no worse than anyone else’s. If not for his inferior family background, he would never have entered the Li family as a concubine.

Du Lianhua had raised him all these years and had treated him well. Even after remarrying into the Lu family, she hadn’t allowed Lu Wen to suffer much hardship. Yet Lu Wen also knew that this wasn’t solely his mother’s doing. Having lost his father at a young age, he learned to read people. After joining the Lu family, unwilling to endure the bitterness of living under someone else’s roof, he did everything he could to win Lu Daxiang’s regard and make him treat Lu Wen as his own child. With a sweet tongue and diligent hands, Lu Daxiang treated him reasonably well and even later sent him to study in town. If not for that, he wouldn’t be able to send money, grain, and clothes to Lu Daxiang now.

Country folk didn’t need much money—only a few hundred copper coins at most, or two or three taels of silver at the extreme. He had coaxed Li Mingshan into letting him keep a fair amount of private savings. Moreover, supporting his mother and stepfather earned him a reputation for filial piety, proving he wasn’t the sort to scoff at the poor and chase the rich. And after becoming pregnant, both the Li family’s master and the old madam rewarded him—far more generously than Li Mingshan, who hadn’t yet taken over the family business could provide.

Only after entering the Li family did he realize how narrow his previous worldview had been. A bowl of plain noodles worth ten copper coins had once seemed like treasure, something he would crave if he couldn’t have it. Fortunately, he had always been prudent and never shared his rural experiences with others, choosing instead to listen and observe quietly to avoid becoming a laughingstock.

When he heard of the pregnancy, Du Lianhua brought an old hen from home, saying she would stew it into soup to nourish him. Lu Wen knew his mother meant well, but that hen was truly… embarrassingly shabby.

After his pregnancy, the Li family even served him precious bird’s nest soup every few days. Not to mention old hens, if he wanted beef, he could have it anytime. Though it couldn’t compare to Luo Hongchou’s provisions in the main family, it was still worlds apart from what he had known in his past life.

Two more maids were assigned to serve him, along with an old maidservant transferred from the old madam’s courtyard. Since this was his first pregnancy and he lacked experience, having an elderly maidservant by his side to guide him was deemed safer.

Lu Wen didn’t want to blame his rural mother, but Du Lianhua truly had a crude and tacky air about her. Every time she came to Lu Wen’s courtyard, she would touch and gawp at everything, even marveling at a spittoon, causing Lu Wen to lose face repeatedly—both openly and subtly. The woman from the main family even mocked him to his face.

The Luo family sent expensive bird’s nest, tonics, and medicinal supplements. Since they owned a silk shop, bolts of satin and silk were carried into the main family in chests, not to mention jewels and jade. In comparison, the old hen Du Lianhua had brought seemed so insignificant yet so conspicuously out of place.

Lu Wen knew full well that his mother’s visit wasn’t merely to deliver the hen and congratulate him; she had come to ask for money.

Du Lianhua never openly demanded it, not with maids watching inside and outside the courtyard. How could she lose face in front of such a wealthy family? She claimed everyone at home was well and that Lu Wen needn’t worry, only mentioning that Lu Wu was growing lately, his clothes too short, and that he missed his brother. But Fenggu Town was too far, and he couldn’t bring Lu Wu along.

Lu Wen fixed his gaze on the old hen with its legs tied to the ground, then listened to her words. Though his expression remained gentle, inwardly he burned with shame, afraid the maids and servants would laugh at him. He immediately had someone fetch silver and two bolts of cloth, excusing himself by saying he felt unwell and tired, and urged Du Lianhua to go home and make new clothes for the family—thus fulfilling his filial duty as a son.

With the silver and cloth in hand, Du Lianhua returned to Angu Village with her back straight and chin held high, smugly flaunting her wealth to everyone she met, completely unaware that Lu Wen had sent her away, out of embarrassment.

Since the old hen was a gift from his mother, Lu Wen couldn’t simply throw it away, nor could he vent his frustration on it. He held his nose, forced a pleased expression, and ordered the maids to take the hen to the kitchen to stew, saying he’d drink the soup after his nap.

After lying down, he dismissed the maids and the old maidservant. The contrast stirred in him turmoil and unsettled thoughts.

Even though he had married into the Li family, if he didn’t take care of his mother, his stepfather, and that brother of his, Li Mingshan might not say anything, but who knew what the old madam would think? Fortunately, country folk didn’t spend much. Sometimes, after giving Du Lianhua money, when Li Mingshan came to his courtyard, Lu Wen could coax him with sweet words and recoup his losses.

Lu Wu was still young, but at least this stepbrother shared the same mother and was somewhat close to him, not siding entirely with Lu Daxiang or that Lu Gu.

Thinking of Lu Gu, although he resented how the Shen family had disrespected him on the street back then, he also felt a sense of escape. Fortunately, he hadn’t married that hunter, Shen Xuanqing.
