Chapter 9 Part 2


The scent of medicine drifted from the courtyard. Lu Gu sat in front of the small medicinal stove, fanning the flame with a palm fan. Not long after, Shen Xuanqing came over.

Wei Lanxiang was busy cutting meat and vegetables in the kitchen, preparing for lunch.

Lu Gu kept his head down, not daring to look around, but Shen Xuanqing walked straight up to him and stood beside him. Neither of them spoke. Shen Xuanqing paused for a moment before drawing a thin stick of wood from under the eaves and stuffing it into the stove.

The fire caught just right. Lu Gu didn’t understand why he added another piece, but he didn’t bother to ask. Still, Shen Xuanqing opened his mouth and explained, “I light the fire and use it in the backyard.”

Hearing this, Lu Gu didn’t dare refuse. He answered, but could only nod, unable to say more.

After the firewood had burned awhile, the two fell silent again. Shen Xuanqing lowered his eyes, and his gaze naturally fell on the little fulang sitting in front of the stove. The sun was just right, and Lu Gu’s complexion looked better than before; perhaps the heat of the fire had something to do with his cheeks flushing a little.

There was a narrow red mark between the eyebrows of shuang’er. It was often said that the brighter the mark, the healthier the body and the easier it is to grow. The linen cloth wrapping Lu Gu’s head covered the red mark, but Shen Xuanqing had seen it before he was injured. He was too thin, and the mark wasn’t as bright as Lu Wen’s, but it wasn’t too dim either.

Shen Xuanqing could see nothing on Lu Gu’s face, but his eyes fixed on Lu Gu’s. He had just calmed down and was looking at his fulang carefully for the first time in a while. His eyebrows, eyes, and facial features weren’t different; he might even be handsome. It was just that this person was too cowed and shy, thin and timid, always bowing his head in silence, walking softly, easy to overlook.

Lu Gu grew more uncomfortable, and the knuckles of his fingers gripping the palm fan whitened, betraying his nerves. Shen Xuanqing noticed his rudeness and lowered his head in apology. After a moment, Lu Gu relaxed somewhat. When Shen Xuanqing looked at him again, Lu Gu no longer flinched away. Now that he had been “bought,” Shen Xuanqing wouldn’t glare at him for long periods. Soon, a scent of burning herbs came from the backyard.

After covering the chicken and duck manure piles with soil, Shen Xuanqing tossed on a bundle of green medicinal leaves, which could repel flies and insects and also mask the livestock odor in the backyard. He kept it for a few days.

——

Only the four of them had lunch in the afternoon. Shen Yaoqing and Ji Qiuyue were still at their parents’ home and wouldn’t return until evening.

Wei Lanxiang mixed brown sugar with boiled water and divided it into two bowls for Lu Gu to serve on the table. She and Shen Yan each drank half a bowl, and the remaining bowl was for Lu Gu and Shen Xuanqing. Once they were seated, she said to Shen Xuanqing, “This is your third aunt who brought it. You two should try it too.”

Sugar was more expensive than eggs, even though it was only a small package, it was Zhou Xiangjun’s heart.

After the wedding feast, the Shen family didn’t have to work at the stove or stay to wash the dishes, so he and Shen Shunwang went back early and went to the fields to repair the ridges, having missed the chance to visit the Lu family.

Seeing that Wei Lanxiang hadn’t left the house all day yesterday, he went to the fields and saw Shen Yaoqing, who told him that the second sister-in-law wasn’t well and had lay in the house for a day.

Lu Gu had grown up; he had drunk sugar water several times when his mother was still there. After tasting the sweetness, he remembered the taste, but he didn’t dare drink it casually and pushed the cup toward Shen Xuanqing. Seeing his hesitation, Shen Xuanqing took a sip from the bowl, placed it in front of Lu Gu, and said, “I won’t drink any more; you can finish the rest.”

Lu Gu was not very familiar with such a good thing as sugar water. Wei Lanxiang naturally preferred her son, but since Shen Xuanqing had tasted it himself and it didn’t concern Lu Gu, she couldn’t say more. Besides, Lu Gu was injured, so it would be better for him to drink a few more sips of sugar water. She didn’t mind giving up a portion.

After the meal, Shen Xuanqing shouldered the hoe and prepared to go down to the fields. Before leaving, he said to Wei Lanxiang, “Mother, I’ll go to the mountains tomorrow and come back the day after tomorrow.”

Wei Lanxiang replied, “Very well. I’ll bake some cakes for you to bring along.” After finishing, she glanced at Lu Gu, who was washing the dishes in the kitchen. Lu Gu had originally married a fulang and had hoped to accompany Shen Xuanqing into the mountains, but his head wound hadn’t yet healed, so there was no way he could go.

The sugar water had stirred sweetness all the way through Lu Gu, from his body to his heart; even his eyebrows and eyes seemed livelier, and his gaze grew brighter. He stood in the kitchen himself, and no one witnessed such a fresh and happy expression on his face, which had long been withdrawn and depressed for many years, even if only for a moment.

——

Before the chicken crowed, the sky was gray outside. Lu Gu woke to the sound of Shen Xuanqing moving about. He knew Shen Xuanqing would be going into the mountains this morning, but he hadn’t expected it to be so early.

Shen Xuanqing pulled on his coat in the dim light from the half-opened window and said, “You don’t need to get up; I’ll dress and go.” Lu Gu had already sat up; he didn’t know what he would do, but he whispered timidly, “I’ll come out with you.”

Now that Shen Xuanqing was going into the mountains, if he lay back down and went back to sleep, the Shen family might take offense. So even though he was very afraid of Shen Xuanqing, in order to live under the Shen roof and not be rejected, he summoned his courage and spoke the words.
