Chapter 109


Swallows darted through the air and sparrows chirped in the branches. The window of the west room was open, and Lu Gu could be seen packing clothes in the courtyard. Tomorrow they would go up the mountain again.

The weather was sunny and warm; it wasn’t too cold even if the wind blew inside the house. But once you stepped outside into the open fields, the wind would be strong. These days, children liked to fly kites.

Two kites had been bought a year ago—one swallow kite and one eagle kite. The whole family had taken advantage of a two-day window to fly them recently. Shen Yan was the happiest person that day.

In the woodshed, Shen Xuanqing used a shovel to scoop rabbit dung into the dung basket, then poured it into the backyard to accumulate. They would use it later to fertilize the fields, so the wheat seedlings and the rice would grow better.

He kept six small rabbits and five big rabbits. The big rabbits had been caught alive by him and his dog. Two of them had injured their legs. After shoveling the manure, he leaned the shovel against the woodpile, then went forward to retrieve the two injured rabbits for a look. Now they had completely healed, and there was no recurrence of ulceration.

He tucked the two big rabbits back and looked at the half-grown little rabbits in the cage. The rabbits grew very fast. They were more than four months old now; apart from being slightly smaller than the big rabbits, there wasn’t much difference.

Because they were domesticated, the rabbits were fed well with grass and vegetables every day, and none of them were thin. When he came to feed them two days ago, he noticed the youngsters had indeed grown up, and both the male and female showed signs of being in estrus. Fortunately, he had foreseen this and kept the male and female rabbits separately.

In the past two years, he had caught living rabbits and raised them at home. If the rabbits gave birth, he would raise the babies they produced and sell them when they were big enough, so he had gained plenty of experience.

Although a litter of rabbits could mate and produce babies, it depended on luck. If he was lucky, the baby rabbits would survive; if unlucky, several would die. The three living rabbits he caught later were to separate the baby rabbits in this litter.

After all, more than four months was a bit too young. It would be better to raise them for another month. When they were strong, they would be used as breeding rabbits. After two months, a litter of rabbits would be born. After feeding them for the first half of the year, they would become fat rabbits. The three living big rabbits he had caught later were kept separate from the little rabbits in this nest.

The big rabbits had been kept together for about half a month now. He was afraid the baby rabbits would be too small to survive the winter. Now that the weather was warmer, they could breed as many as they wanted.

“Brother,” Shen Xuanqing called out from outside.

“Did you call me? Wait a moment.” Shen Yaoqing was watering the vegetable garden outside. He lifted the bucket and poured out the last bit of water before coming over.

“By the end of next month, these six little rabbits will be ready to mate. Then you can sort them out and don’t let the litters be randomly mated,” Shen Xuanqing said. The three big rabbits he had caught later were all males, and the remaining four were females. It didn’t matter which was used, as long as they were not from the same litter.

“I’ll catch a few more living female rabbits later and pick out the strong ones as breeding rabbits.” He paused, then said, “If we raise too many rabbits in the future and there isn’t enough space in the woodshed, we might as well build a rabbit hutch in the backyard, so it won’t take up too much space in the woodshed.”

Shen Yaoqing lifted the dung basket and set it outside so it wouldn’t block the way. Hearing this, he asked, “You mean you want to raise more?”

Shen Xuanqing nodded. “Yes. You’ve raised them before, so there’s no need to grope in the dark. Selling ten or twenty of them would bring a bit of money, but if we raise forty or fifty, we’ll earn more. They grow quickly and can be sold in half a year, so it’s worth it.”

Rabbits were more expensive than chickens and ducks. A fat one could fetch at least thirty copper coins. If there were fifty of them, that would be fifteen hundred copper coins. In about half a year, they could earn one tael and five hundred copper coins, or at least one tael of silver.

They had thought this way before when they raised rabbits, but they were really short of money at the time and couldn’t wait more than half a year. Later, they even sold the breeding rabbits to pay off their debts.

Shen Yaoqing’s heart stirred at the thought. It wasn’t merely about gathering more rabbit grass on weekdays. He, their mother, and Shen Yan were all at home. If they could earn an extra one or two taels of silver a year—five hundred copper coins was a lot in the village. Country people like them rarely spent money on food and drink, and every copper coin saved could become a family fortune.

“It’s not impossible to build a hutch, but rabbits like to dig. You can’t build it like a chicken coop or a duck pen,” Shen Yaoqing reflected.

The two of them were thinking about rabbits when Lu Gu called from their room, “Erqing, do you want to bring this black cotton coat with patches on the sleeves?”

“Okay, you go pack up first. You have to leave early tomorrow morning. Don’t worry about the rabbit hutch. I’ll take care of it.” Shen Yaoqing patted his second brother’s arm.

Shen Xuanqing lifted the dung basket and nodded. “Okay.”
