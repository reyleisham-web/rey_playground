Chapter 4 Part 2


The Shen family had been scolded again and again. Shen Xuanqing set the iron pot down in his hand; he could not bear it any longer. Even Wei Lanxiang could not take it, and she ended up breaking Lu Daxiang’s leg in a howl of misery.

Du Hehua threw herself onto Lu Daxiang, crying bitterly. Someone outside shouted for a doctor, and Lu Dahe rushed in. He saw Lu Daxiang’s leg broken and cried out that the Shen family would not be taking responsibility, but when he saw Shen Xuanqing’s gloomy, towering figure with eyes like a wolf-leopard full of cold ferocity, he abruptly fell silent.

Lu Daxiang was carried away. Du Hehua wanted to follow, but she was afraid the house would be evacuated. Thinking of Lu Daxiang’s broken leg, she knew it would cost a lot to treat him, and with Lu Gu involved, hatred welled up in her heart.

Lu Daxiang would have to pay money, but at this moment Du Hehua wished Lu Gu would die so she wouldn’t pay a penny. Since Shen Xuanqing was involved, he would have to lose money!

When it came to money, Du Hehua’s wits turned faster than anyone else, and she immediately schemed against the Shen family.

Just as she cried and rushed to appeal to Bao Zhiru again, Shen Xuanqing kicked the iron pot on the ground and spoke in a cold voice that they did not want the things. The Lu family would compensate him with 10 taels of silver. As for the remaining 10 taels, they were the money he had spent to buy Lu Gu, and henceforth the two would no longer communicate. If not, then no one would benefit.

Du Hehua was initially reluctant, but after seeing Shen Xuanqing’s cold, terrifying expression, she grit her teeth and agreed.

Her agreement was born of calculation. Not only would she keep these belongings, but she could also discard Lu Gu, the “death star,” otherwise she would have to spend even more money to treat the death star.

Before dark, Lu Gu followed Shen Xuanqing to Qingxi Village.

The red banner hanging from the beam above the Shen house door hadn’t been taken down, and the yard had been tidied up.

Wei Lanxiang was exhausted today; when she returned, she had a stuffed chest and a pounding head, so she went to lie down early.

Ji Qiuyue took Shen Yan to tidy the kitchen, and when she heard a movement, she looked out the window. The usual smile was gone from her face as she asked through the window, “Second brother is hungry, Daqing is going to return the table and chairs—wait. We’ll have dinner when he comes back.”

“Yeah,” Shen Xuanqing replied, sounding a little dull. He noticed there were still a few tables in the yard, and he was going to move them, but after noticing the red clothes on his body, he went into his room to change first.

As for Lu Gu, he didn’t even know where he should stand. Ji Qiuyue saw that his face was terrifyingly pale, and the fine linen cloth wrapped around his head was still oozing red blood. She couldn’t help feeling pity and said, “There’s no chair for you. You can sit first.”

Lu Gu had nowhere to go. He simply sat under the eaves, as she instructed, his hands and feet cramped, yet he did not dare to move much.

Shen Xuanqing, who had changed into home clothes, saw him when he came out, but said nothing and went on with his own tasks.

As for whether Lu Gu had come to the Shen family to live or to die, he never thought about it.

Even though he had moved, Lu Gu still felt that everything before him was unfamiliar; no one wanted to look at him, and no one paid him any attention. Yet he did not pity himself, nor did he understand why. He was simply at a loss. After thinking of Grandma Wang’s words, he decided to be more careful and not make mistakes, especially not to let Shen Xuanqing discover anything amiss, or his legs would be broken again.

The aroma of food wafted from the kitchen, especially the scent of meat, which made Lu Gu, who had only eaten a bowl of noodle soup all day, feel even more uncomfortable.

Ji Qiuyue carried a bowl of egg custard to Wei Lanxiang’s room. Seeing Lu Gu’s restrained, uneasy look, she sighed inwardly and said, “Daqing and the others will be back soon. You and Shen Yan should bring the dishes out first.”

Seeing Lu Gu hurry toward the kitchen, she shook her head secretly. It wasn’t that she deliberately kept Lu Gu from moving; the scene was simply unbearable. It would be better if he did something.

Soon the Shen brothers returned, and Lu Gu and Shen Yan arranged the meals.

In the main room, the Shen family members sat down one after another with far more silence than before. It was Shen Yaoqing who spoke first, “Eat. You can’t go hungry in your own home, and the big things can wait until you’ve eaten.”

Shen Xuanqing said nothing, but he picked up his chopsticks, and Ji Qiuyue did the same. She glanced at the outside yard and asked Shen Yan, “Where’s the person?”

Wei Lanxiang slept after eating a bowl of egg custard in her room. She didn’t eat with them, but Lu Gu was missing.

“He was still there just now,” Shen Yan replied, peering into the yard.

“You eat first; I’ll look for him.” Ji Qiuyue put down her chopsticks again. To be honest, she was so furious today that she had little appetite.

There were no chairs under the eaves, and no one in the kitchen. Finally, she found Lu Gu in the firewood room, pushed open the door, and asked, “Why don’t you come to eat?”

Lu Gu, curled up in a haystack, clutched his stomach to keep himself from vomiting. He wanted to eat, but he hadn’t anticipated the door to the firewood room opening. He sat up in a panic, and his eyes widened as he looked at Ji Qiuyue.

After hearing what Ji Qiuyue said, his panic deepened.

In the Shen family he had eaten hot egg noodle soup, which was far better than the watery soup left over from the Lu family, but there were only five bowls, and the five members of the Shen family naturally didn’t give him a share.

After serving the table, he left the main room, not knowing where to go. If he were still in Anjia Village, his father and stepmother would be eating dinner. He had been told to go out to work, so he slipped to the back hillside to pick wild vegetables. He ate the fruit, but he did not dare roam around the Shen household. In the end, he could only find a firewood room to sleep, enduring the hunger, and the dizziness began to ease.
