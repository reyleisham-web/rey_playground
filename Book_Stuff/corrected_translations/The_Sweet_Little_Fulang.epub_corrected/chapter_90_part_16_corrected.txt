Chapter 90 Part 16


Li Wanyun and Lao Zhang were both carried into the room. Lu Gu came over and picked up the small piece of meat lying on the ground. It would be a pity to waste it, so he washed it in the kitchen and placed it in a basket for Li Wanyun.

He entered the room and looked at Li Wanyun. Seeing that she was all right and the wound on her neck had been bandaged, he mentioned that the meat had been put in the basket before he went home with Wei Lanxiang.

After the neighbors left, Li Wanyun steadied herself for a while, wiped away her tears, and went to the kitchen.

Lu Gu sat dazed in front of the stove. Shen Xuanqing came in, sighed softly, and stroked his hair. He said that Li Wanyun had really been determined to injure herself today, but it wasn’t a bad thing. Ordinary people wouldn’t dare to take on the crime of killing someone, especially today with everyone in the village having witnessed everything. In the future, the Zhang family’s relatives wouldn’t dare come and grab things so easily. Lu Gu always believed in what he said. He breathed a sigh of relief and thought it might be true—the skin jelly would freeze after overnight.

After the skin jelly was boiled, let it cool and it would freeze overnight.

The next day, Lu Gu lifted the cloth covering the wooden basin and took a look. The crystal-clear skin jelly looked good and delicious, but it was quite cold, so it needed to be eaten with hot dishes.

Seeing that Shen Xuanqing was planning to catch and slaughter the chickens in the backyard, Wei Lanxiang stopped him and said, “The eldest child, kill two more ducks and prepare them in advance today.” Normally, the chickens were slaughtered on the 27th and the ducks on the 28th to leaven the dough, but these days they were very busy, so there was no need to kill them on separate days; there was no need to boil water again tomorrow.

The skin jelly had frozen. Shen Xuanqing came in, cut a piece with a knife, pinched it between his fingers, and fed it to Lu Gu, smiling. “Try it; you made it yourself.”

He didn’t know what had happened in these days, but teasing his fulang was really amusing. When Lu Gu opened his mouth to bite, Shen Xuanqing quickly stuffed the jelly in and laughed at the silly Lu Gu.

Lu Gu closed his mouth, feeling a little embarrassed at first, but Shen Xuanqing kept laughing. It was obvious he had done it on purpose, and an unexplainable feeling stirred in Lu Gu’s heart. He didn’t feel angry at all, only wondering why Shen Xuanqing laughed like that...

Lu Gu couldn’t figure it out; he pursed his lips and prepared to fetch firewood to boil the water. They planned to kill the chickens and ducks, and hot water would be needed later to scald the feathers.

“Angry?” Seeing that he was about to leave, Shen Xuanqing quickly stopped him, smiling. “I didn’t mean it. I’ll give you something to eat.” Then he cut another slice of skin jelly. Lu Gu wanted to protest that he wasn’t angry, but words failed as the food went straight into his mouth.

The crystal skin jelly was cold and chewy. Apart from its chill, it was tasty, and Lu Gu smiled at the thought that he had boiled it himself.

“Er Qing, you and your elder brother will kill the ducks later,” Wei Lanxiang called from outside.

“Understood, mother,” Shen Xuanqing answered. Seeing there wasn’t much firewood left by the stove, he said, “I’ll go fetch some firewood; you light the fire first.”

“Um.” Lu Gu was chewing something in his mouth, so he could only nod.

After Shen Xuanqing went out, Lu Gu sat down in front of the stove and suddenly remembered Ji Qiuyue. Sometimes Brother Daqing deliberately made sister-in-law angry, and she would beat him every time, then scold him for being too deficient. Just now Shen Xuanqing had laughed at him, as if he were really deficient, and it made people want to strike him.

The word “beat” sprang into Lu Gu’s mind, and he jumped a little. He had never beaten anyone, let alone Shen Xuanqing.

Sparks flew from the flint, and the fire began to crackle. Shen Xuanqing and Wei Lanxiang, who were carrying firewood, came in one after another.

“Let’s make noodles today; tomorrow is the 28th, so the noodles will be ready for tomorrow. We’ll steam buns and steamed stuffed buns. On the 29th, the day after tomorrow, we’ll have fried chicken, fried duck, and meatballs.” Wei Lanxiang said, holding a bowl to scoop flour.

The days leading up to New Year’s Eve were very busy, as they needed to prepare all kinds of food for the New Year, and Lu Gu couldn’t handle it alone. Slaughtering the chickens and ducks and cutting the meat into pieces were tasks for the men. At the Chinese New Year, there must be chicken, duck, and fish.

The surface of the Qingxi River was frozen; some parts were solid, but others weren’t. In winter, parents feared most that their children would wander onto the frozen river and fall through.

Shen Yaoqing brought the dead chickens and ducks to the front yard. While waiting for Lu Gu to boil the water, he asked Shen Yan if she wanted to eat fresh fish. After getting an answer, he took the fishing rod and led Shen Yan to the back of the house. He moved a stone and cracked it into the frozen river to make a hole. He set the fishing rod and asked Shen Yan to wait for a bite.

“Don’t worry, Dahui is here. When I go back, I’ll have your Guzi brother come over too,” Shen Yaoqing patted her on the head with a smile.

“Then you must remember.” Shen Yan tucked her hands and a small piece of the fishing rod into her sleeves to keep warm.

This place was close to home, just behind the house, and Da Hui and Gouzai followed, so they could rest assured. Before leaving, Shen Yaoqing said, “Remember, don’t go into the river, just stand here.”

Not long after, Lu Gu returned with Tang pozi in his arms and a small bench. The two sat by the river to fish. It was nice to have Tang pozi there; it kept their hands warm as they fished in the snow, and both of them were dressed in thick clothes so they wouldn’t feel the cold.
