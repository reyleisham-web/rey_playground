Chapter 113 Part 22


The copper coins scattered when the bride arrived at the wedding were called carp coins, used to pray for fertility. The more sons, the greater the happiness. The carp had long been an auspicious symbol. There was a painting of a fat baby holding a carp in Ji Qiuyue's room. Although they weren't married yet, he had embroidered many other handkerchiefs with different patterns, and he wanted to stitch one that was both auspicious and unique for Ji Qiuyue.

Ji Qiuyue took the handkerchief, unfolded it, and saw a pair of red carp waving their tails, with copper coins scattered beneath. The embroidery was not large, but it was beautiful and novel. She was amazed and praised repeatedly: "It's really beautiful—you have such a deft hand."

Even Shen Xuanqing, a few steps behind, heard her and raised his head to glance at the handkerchief. He had been busy on the mountain and hadn’t paid much attention when Lu Gu was doing the embroidery. He hadn’t expected that Lu Gu would embroider something like this, so he also praised it: "It's beautiful."

Everyone liked to hear praise, and Lu Gu was no exception. He blushed when praised, but his crescent-shaped eyes showed that he was actually very happy. When they got home, Ji Qiuyue showed Wei Lanxiang and the others the handkerchief, and they praised him as well, which made him feel bashful.

"Sit down and rest. Mom will cook for you." Wei Lanxiang brought out some pastries from the room to fill their stomachs. Then she walked toward the kitchen and asked Shen Yan to pour them some water. She remembered there was still honey in the honey jar at home, so she asked Shen Yan to make some honey water for them to drink.

"Why did you come back so late this time?" Shen Yaoqing asked while unloading the cart in the yard. He took Dabai down and was relieved to see that the dog could still walk.

"It's not easy to go up the mountain. It's better to think of ways to hunt live game, which is better than rushing on the road," Shen Xuanqing said after taking a sip of water.

In the past, they had always gone down the mountain once every half a month. Not to mention himself, it was too hard for Lu Gu to follow him on the mountain road. After running back and forth, he was afraid that the little meat Lu Gu had gained with great difficulty in the winter would be lost. He hadn't thought much about it before going up the mountain, but decided to stay for a few more days after he got there.

"That's right." Shen Yaoqing moved the rabbit cage aside. Running back and forth and working hard were actually quite tiring. Seeing that the rabbits in the cage were still alive, he raised his head and said, "I have built the rabbit cage in the backyard. There’s no rush now. You can rest for a while and go have a look later."

Seeing that there were still empty oil cans on the cart, he said, "I just came back from buying oil the day before yesterday. I bought a lot of it, and some sesame oil. I’ll give them to you two."

"Yes." Shen Xuanqing nodded and reached for a pastry.

"Brother Guzi, I have saved up all the chicken eggs and duck eggs for you. There are quite a few." Shen Yan’s almond-shaped eyes were filled with smiles. She had always been very close to Lu Gu. She felt that she had contributed a lot by saving so many chicken and duck eggs, and her tone was cheerful.

She and Ji Qiuyue had been together for a long time, so she spoke a bit like her.

Shen Xuanqing next to her laughed when he heard her words. This girl had learned how to ask for favors and take credit, so he said, "You should also get a share of the money from feeding the chickens, so ask Brother Guzi to give you some of the money from the eggs."

Lu Gu, who had a pastry in his mouth, had the same idea, but he couldn't speak. He could only nod along with Shen Xuanqing's words. When he wasn’t at home, Shen Yan helped him take care of the chickens and ducks. He couldn’t just take all the benefits for himself. Besides, Shen Yan had always treated him well, so how could he not share some of the benefits?
