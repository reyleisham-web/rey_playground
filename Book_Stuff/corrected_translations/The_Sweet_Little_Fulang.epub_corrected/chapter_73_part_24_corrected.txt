Chapter 73 Part 24


Seeing him like this, Shen Yaoqing patted him on the shoulder and spoke earnestly. “If you earn money, you still need to love your fulang. Our family can’t afford gold and jade. Even if you can’t afford them, if you have the heart, your fulang will be happy if you buy a silver one, right? When father was around, didn’t he give mother a silver hairpin and a silver bracelet?”

Lu Gu was dressed plainly, like most fulangs in the village. Fulangs didn’t have to work in the fields, and their hair wasn’t combed the way farmers’ were. They could wear hairpins and bracelets too, but they didn’t have as many styles as women did.

“Not to mention anything else—when your sister-in-law first married me, I bought her a hairpin with money I earned. She was so happy that she didn’t pull my ear for half a year.”

Shen Yaoqing realized that he had spoken out about his incompetence, and quickly made amends: “Of course, she doesn’t pull my ears very often, and besides, I let her.”

Shen Xuanqing raised his eyebrows and smiled, but didn’t press him; he said: “I know, big brother. I’ll buy one when I see it.”

“Hey, that’s right—don’t just grow tall; grow a kind heart too.” Shen Yaoqing patted him on the shoulder again, relieved.

There weren’t many goods to sell today. Besides the wild soapberries, there were ten badger skins, and there was no need to pull the cart. Shen Xuanqing told the family, and Lu Gu sent him out of the courtyard.

When Shen Xuanqing was far away, Lu Gu called back the puppy and went to the hall to continue embroidering a coat for Shen Yan. The women at home were busy with needlework. Shen Yaoqing chopped firewood for a while, and seeing the sun high in the sky, he took a hemp rope and a hatchet and went up the mountain to collect firewood. The firewood he had gathered lately hadn’t sold, so he stuffed it into the woodshed. With nothing else to do these past two days, he went up the mountain with Shen Xuanqing to cut more and bring it back.

By almost noon, Lu Gu was cooking in the kitchen, and Shen Yan was tending the fire. At that moment, the puppy and Dahui barked and ran out of the yard. Hearing it, Lu Gu knew Shen Xuanqing had returned, but the vegetables hadn’t been cut yet and the meal would be served later, so he couldn’t stop; he looked out from the window.

Not long after, Shen Xuanqing walked into the courtyard with a bamboo basket on his back, approached the kitchen, and smiled when he saw Lu Gu. “I’m back.”

Lu Gu’s eyes brightened a little, and he smiled lightly.

“Mother.” Seeing Wei Lanxiang come out, Shen Xuanqing shouted. He unloaded the bamboo basket and drew out two oil-paper umbrellas from it. Standing in the kitchen, Lu Gu saw round green fruits beneath the bamboo basket. The green was so bright that it looked very fresh in the withering stillness of early winter.

“Wandering along the pier, I bought some oranges when I saw a seller,” Shen Xuanqing said, and he glanced at Lu Gu with a smile.

His fulang was timid and had never seen the world. Every time he encountered something he had never seen before, his eyes would widen slightly, curious and surprised, obviously wanting to know what it was, but because he was shy, he didn’t go forward. Every time Shen Xuanqing saw Lu Gu like this, he couldn’t help laughing.

“This color feels fresh no matter how many times I see it.” Wei Lanxiang smacked her lips and said, “Are they cheaper this year?”

Back when Shen Shunfu was alive, he bought oranges for his family a few times. The seller came by boat from the far south, and the few times he bought them weren’t cheap, so he bought only a small amount.

“It’s okay.” Shen Xuanqing showed the two oil-paper umbrellas to Wei Lanxiang, and he didn’t mention the price of the oranges. “You can use these two when there is snow.”

Wei Lanxiang opened the umbrella to take a look; the surface of the umbrella was painted with flowers, and it was beautiful. Shen Yan added some firewood to the bottom of the stove and ran out to look at it. Such a beautiful oil-paper umbrella made her laugh so hard that her eyes nearly disappeared, and she held it in her hand, saying to Lu Gu, “Brother Guzi, look.”

Lu Gu put the chopped vegetables into the bowl, looked up and said, “It looks good.”

Every winter, when heavy snow fell, he didn’t go out much. The light snow might look small as it drifted, but it quickly turned to water when it touched the body and head, soaking clothes and hair; wearing a bamboo hat became impossible. It was better to carry an oil-paper umbrella.

Painted umbrellas were more expensive, but seeing the family so happy, Shen Xuanqing felt the money was well spent.

“Go and eat it with sister-in-law.” He took a few oranges from the bamboo basket and handed them to Shen Yan, and gave two to Wei Lanxiang, he said, “Mother, you can eat too.”

After that, he took two more oranges and entered the kitchen, handing one to Lu Gu and saying, “Smell it—the orange peel is different from other fruits.”

Lu Gu wiped his hands on the apron, and took it carefully. The green orange was quite big, plump and heavy. Gently sniffing it, the unique fragrance was indeed different.

Shen Xuanqing peeled the other one, squeezed the peeled orange, and a little juice splashed out toward Lu Gu. Lu Gu quickly stepped back half a step, causing Shen Xuanqing to chuckle.
