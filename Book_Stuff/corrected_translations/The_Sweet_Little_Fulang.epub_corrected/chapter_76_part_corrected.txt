Chapter 76


Lu Gu thought again. Shen Xuanqing was usually cautious and prudent, and with so many people at home in broad daylight, he wouldn’t buy things indiscriminately. As he reread the written lines, there were three characters he didn’t recognize, though trying to recall, those three seemed different from the others.

At this moment, Shen Xuanqing pulled out a book and a youzhong ladle (有种马勺), and smiled: “From now on, when the pot is occupied, just use this one for cooking.”

“Second brother, why do you still have a book?” Shen Yan came over to look at the youzhong ladle, asking curiously.

“This is the Three-Character Classic. Your brother Guzi says he wants to learn how to recognize characters.” After hearing Shen Xuanqing’s words, Lu Gu was completely relieved. After shaking off the lingering fear, he picked up the book and opened it carefully.

The thin book felt like a weight Lu Gu had never touched before. In the past, Shen Xuanqing had some books at home when he studied, but after a couple of misfortunes, they had all been sold.

Standing by the side, Shen Yan saw Lu Gu open the book and come over to take a closer look. Her eyes paused slightly on a page, recalling that her father had inquired about a school in the town before he left and had wanted to send her to study and learn a few characters. She was nine years old, at the age to study the basic texts.

Shen Xuanqing and Shen Yaoqing had studied at a private school in a neighboring village. Shen Yan couldn’t go there because she was a girl. There was a private school for Shuang’er, a girl in Fenggu Town, run by a very knowledgeable and courageous female teacher.

“I also bought brushes, inks, papers and an inkstone. Anyway, it’s to teach, so you can come to learn too. You won’t suffer if you recognize a few words.” Shen Xuanqing spoke to Shen Yan, handed the youzhong ladle to Wei Lanxiang, who came to see it, and drew out the Four Treasures of the Study from the bamboo basket.

Ink and inkstone were a bit more expensive, but one inkstone would suffice. He bought a lot of paper; he would need more if he wanted to write and practice calligraphy. He bought four brushes; except for Lu Gu and Shen Yan, he could write a few words in winter when he had nothing to do, and one brush was kept as a spare.

Lu Gu had a thin brush that looked more like one for drawing threads, but he had never used paper and brushes for writing. The boys sent to private schools in the countryside used paper and brushes at school, but they often wrote on the ground with tree branches when they returned home. As a beginner, he wanted to use the Four Treasures of the Study, but he felt a little uneasy, fearing that his writing would ruin the paper and ink, so he whispered, “I’d better take a branch and write on the ground first.”

“No, if you want to learn to write, just write on the paper. Don’t worry, I can afford it.” Shen Xuanqing said, knowing that writing on the ground with a branch was different from writing with brush and paper, and now the money in hand was enough, so there was no need to be frugal about it; the paper he bought wasn’t too expensive.

Studying wasn’t something Lu Gu understood, and he didn’t have much opinion, so naturally he listened to Shen Xuanqing in everything.

While they were talking, Ji Qiuyue and Shen Yaoqing came out from the backyard and saw the youzhong ladle that Wei Lanxiang held in her hand at a glance. Ji Qiuyue cried happily, “Mother, is this the youzhong ladle?”

“Erqing bought it back, so let’s use it today.” Wei Lanxiang looked at the youzhong ladle again and again, and joy filled the corners of her eyes. “It’s time to cook; I’m going to cut the vegetables. Daqing, go and fetch the medicine stove and place it at the kitchen door for me.”

She was overjoyed and refused to let others cook, wanting to try it herself.

“Is this youzhong ladle expensive?” Ji Qiuyue asked as she approached the books and the Four Treasures of the Study.

Salt and iron were expensive things, Shen Xuanqing smiled. “It’s cheaper than a big pot, and it’s good to have one for cooking at home.” He didn’t say the exact price, and Ji Qiuyue didn’t ask; she was curious and asked why he had also bought paper and brushes, as well as ink and inkstone—so complete.

“Second brother wants to teach brother Guzi how to read, and I’ll follow suit.” Shen Yan replied bluntly.

“I’ll just say it.” Ji Qiuyue smiled.

At that moment, Shen Yaoqing moved the medicine stove from under the eaves to the kitchen door and said, “Why don’t you follow suit? I don’t have enough brushes and papers; I’ll buy some.”

Hearing this, Ji Qiuyue quickly waved her hands and said, “I still can’t do it. I’m stupid; it’s enough as long as I can write my own name. Let them study hard, and I’ll have time to sew clothes in the winter.”

Shen Xuanqing had intended to tell her that brushes and paper were enough, but upon hearing her say that, he couldn’t press the matter. It wasn’t easy to force someone, and besides, she was pregnant, so he didn’t dare to let her tire herself.

After inspecting what had been bought, he saw Wei Lanxiang washing vegetables, and Lu Gu wasn’t idle either. He went into the kitchen to wash the rice and light the fire.

When Wei Lanxiang cooked with the youzhong ladle, the whole family gathered around to watch. They were all iron pots, but the youzhong ladle was more delicate and compact.

After the meal, the youzhong ladle was laid out to dry in the yard, and there was nothing else to do. Shen Xuanqing moved the table out of the room; the sun was bright and warm outside, so it felt better to study in the courtyard than in the room. Shen Xuanqing, Shen Yan, and Lu Gu sat down in the courtyard, the book spread out in front of Lu Gu and Shen Yan, and they read together.

Shen Xuanqing knew how to recite the Three-Character Classic, so he didn’t need to read the book. He guided the two of them to recite it line by line, rubbing ink as he read.

The Three-Character Classic was recited with such fluency that even Ji Qiuyue, who was sewing in the room, came out to listen closely, moving a chair to sit under the eaves.

After Shen Xuanqing read “Ming Ju Yang,” Lu Gu and Shen Yan started from the beginning again, and only after two passes did they recognize the words word by word.

Lu Gu looked at the first character “人” in the book and thought to himself, This is a person.
