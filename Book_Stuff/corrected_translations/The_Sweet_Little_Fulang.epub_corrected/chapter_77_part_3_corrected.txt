Chapter 77 Part 3


After a good part of the morning spent reading and writing, Shen Yaoqing, who had wandered around the village, returned. He called Shen Xuanqing to go out and survey the fields, because Boss Lu wanted to sell the land.

The sky grew increasingly gray. After his brothers went out, Lu Gu tidied up his brushes and ink, washed his hands, and went into the kitchen to prepare lunch.

Surveying the land didn't take much effort. Shen Xuanqing was willing to buy it once he saw that it was fertile, but he wanted to own it outright; the other party couldn’t redeem it later. They were all acquaintances in Qingxi Village. Boss Lu needed money urgently, so after thinking it over for a while he agreed, and the five acres of fertile land was sold to Shen Xuanqing at five taels per mu.

Boss Lu moved quickly; he even asked the village’s own writer to draft the land deed. The Lu family’s relatives, neighbors, and villagers all came to bear witness. Shen Xuanqing paid the money, and the five acres of fertile land became Shen Xuanqing’s.

Snowflakes were falling outside. Shen Xuanqing folded the land deed and tucked it into his bosom. Before he could speak, Boss Lu said: “I know what you want to say. It’s not easy to go to Fucheng in the snow today. After this weather clears, I’ll go with you to obtain the official seal.”

“In that case, I’ll call you when the time comes,” Shen Xuanqing promised.

“All right.” Shen Xuanqing handed over twenty-five taels happily; unlike others who had to bargain for a long time and still end up empty-handed, he got what he wanted quickly. With the money in his arms, Boss Lu breathed a sigh of relief and handed over the land with a smile.

The land deed would be a red deed after the land tax was paid and the official seal stamped; otherwise, it would be a white deed. Deeds to land were highly regarded by the imperial authorities, and the government only recognized red deeds, so everything had to be done properly.

When they came out, Shen Yaoqing looked at the falling snow and said, “Meng Dayue slaughtered pigs today, and last night your sister-in-law said she wanted fresh pork, so I’ll go buy some.”

Hearing this, Shen Xuanqing remembered that he wanted to give Lu Gu some bone sticks, but hadn’t bought them on the way back. He opened his mouth and said, “It’s better to buy some bone sticks and stew them with the medicine stove. The more you eat, the warmer you’ll be. The bones are also full of lean meat.”

“That’s good; you don’t have to go out in the snow today. It’s nice to eat meat and bones at home,” Shen Yaoqing clapped his hands in approval, then added, “Go back and tell mother that you bought the land, and show her the land deed. I’m going to buy the meat.”

“Yes.” Shen Xuanqing didn’t argue; he called Dahui to go home first.

There were fewer bones on the bone sticks, and they were cheaper than the meat. The family was large, and there were dogs; the bones could still be eaten by the dogs, so Shen Yaoqing bought more.

It was already noon by the time they bought it, and the steamed buns and rice porridge in the pot were hot. Lu Gu was busy in the kitchen. When he heard that Ji Qiuyue wanted fried pork slices, he couldn’t move his hands fast enough and called Shen Yan to cut the meat. Ji Qiuyue loved spicy food, so he planned to fry the pork slices with dried chilies; the dried chilies were very spicy—just a little would give a tingling heat.

When frying the meat slices, Lu Gu stuffed a lot of firewood into the stove. He intended to stir-fry them on high heat so the family could eat sooner. But today was the first time he used a youzhong ladle, so he added too much firewood. He couldn’t control the heat, and he accidentally fried the sliced meat a bit dry; the lean meat couldn’t be seen, and the white fat meat browned clearly. Fortunately, the meat they bought was leaner and fatter at the same time.

Meat was more expensive than vegetables, so he returned to the kitchen with a youzhong ladle, poured the sliced meat into a bowl, and hesitated before taking it out.

When Shen Xuanqing came to help serve the dishes, he saw his embarrassment and could tell what had happened just by looking at the bowl of nearly charred meat.

“You can eat it if it isn’t black, it isn’t fried,” Shen Xuanqing said, to prove his point, he even pinched a piece of meat and tasted it. He raised his brows slightly and added, “The fatty meat looks like the dried pot meat I ate at Brother Yang Xian’s tavern before. The dried pot meat is fatty but not greasy and delicious.”

Lu Gu’s stir-fried vegetables and fried meat were all taught by Ji Qiuyue and Wei Lanxiang. Both of them rarely cooked tavern-style dishes from the city. Unexpectedly, this time Lu Gu’s meat turned out not only unspoiled but delicious.

After it was served, others looked at it and thought it might be scorched, but Wei Lanxiang didn’t say anything. Shen Yaoqing still remembered it at his age and sometimes teased her about it.

After a while, she recalled the times she’d cooked something black-in-color; she wasn’t a harsh person, so she had no words and would eat it as long as it wasn’t black.

Who would have thought the taste would be good after she tasted it herself? Even Ji Qiuyue, who hadn’t wanted to eat fatty meat at first, ate several slices. She had been pregnant for more than a month and sometimes felt nauseous, so she didn’t like fatty meat; the meat Shen Yaoqing bought today was leaner.

Seeing that everyone said it was delicious, Lu Gu finally relaxed. Even if everyone had burnt meat in their hands, no one complained, and they simply ate what they could. It would be a pity to waste it.
