Chapter 114


The Shen family raised their daughter differently from others. In the village, the daughters or shuang’ers from other households often had to toil to earn money, many of them supporting their families, with only a little money left in their hands, or sometimes none at all. When Lu Gu was still in the Lu family, all the handkerchiefs and sachets he made were sold by Du Hehua, and he earned not a copper.

In the past two years, Shen Yan had gradually saved more than twenty copper coins. Wei Lanxiang never asked her for money, and neither did anyone else in the Shen family. She was simple-minded and sometimes laughed foolishly, and even Ji Qiuyue liked this sister-in-law.

Shen Yan’s eyes lit up at the thought that Lu Gu might give her money. She envied watching her two brothers selling things and making money all day long.

“Really?” she asked, astonished.

Lu Gu swallowed the pastry and said, “Of course it’s true.”

Back when he had worked in the Lu family, he hadn’t earned a single copper, so he understood the ache of money slipping into someone else’s hands.

Wei Lanxiang, Shen Yan’s biological mother, wouldn’t go to extremes. Yet the moment money was in her own hands, things changed. Just now, Shen Xuanqing never kept a copper coin of the money he earned and left it all to him. Then he understood the advantage of having money: he could decide what to buy, and it was completely different.

Perhaps for that reason, though he wasn’t as clear as Li Wanyun, who knew she could straighten her back only when money was in her hands, he also vaguely understood it.

Shen Yan’s eyes widened with happiness. She didn’t know what to say, and she just smiled with almond-shaped eyes and hurried to the kitchen to bring honey water for Lu Gu.

“Brother, how is the paddy field?” Shen Xuanqing asked after finishing the pastry in his hand.

Shen Yaoqing picked up a pheasant and weighed it with a quick feel of his hand. Hearing Shen Xuanqing’s question, he replied, “We finished planting the rice seedlings a few days ago. But the three of us weren’t enough, so we asked Uncle Jinhu and Uncle Jinlong for help. We paid them twenty copper coins for meal money. The five of us worked in the fields for another four days before we finished.”

During the busy spring ploughing season, if a family hired helpers, they had to provide meals. The meals had to be rice, two vegetable dishes, and some oily or meaty dishes. Farm work required hard work; how could the hired helpers have energy to work if they didn’t eat well? If the food was too poor and rumors spread, the family would be mocked.

“By the way, I have something to tell you two,” Ji Qiuyue spoke from the side, looking a little uneasy. She glances at Shen Yaoqing, then said, “Didn’t Guzi buy Wanyun’s chickens before? She also gave a free rooster, but the rooster was old. We also have a rooster at home, so we killed the old rooster and used it for the four meals.”

Seeing Lu Gu blink, she felt a little guilty and lowered her voice to explain, “The rooster at home is young. Mom said that if she has time this year, she wants to hatch some chicks. There’s no use keeping the old rooster, so…”

Lu Gu and Shen Xuanqing were on the mountain at that time, and there was no way to discuss it with them.

Shen Yaoqing watched their expressions as well. If it had been just Shen Xuanqing, he could have killed the rooster and told them later. But Lu Gu was his brother’s fulang (younger male); he was timid and younger than him, so he had to think before saying a few polite words, and he remained speechless for a moment.

“That rooster is indeed too old,” Shen Xuanqing spoke up. Seeing that Lu Gu was stunned but showed no other emotion, he smiled and said, “Just kill it. It’s enough to have a rooster at home that crows; otherwise it will be too noisy.”

Lu Gu was momentarily stunned, remembering that he had paid for the rooster. Li Wanyun had asked for sixty copper coins and he had given her seventy, but no one but Shen Xuanqing knew about it, so his reaction came a beat late.

“Yeah.” He nodded along with Shen Xuanqing’s words, without anger or annoyance.

Unlike the chickens and ducks he kept, Lu Gu had bought Li Wanyun’s chickens merely to help her. It was just an old rooster; no harm in the family slaughtering and eating it. Moreover, when Shen Yaoqing bought black chickens for Ji Qiuyue in winter, he had benefited from it and eaten a lot of meat. Sometimes Shen Xuanqing openly favored him and would give him an extra bowl of black chicken soup to replenish his strength.

“Brother Guzi, try it quickly. I’ve put in a lot of honey for you,” Shen Yan said, coming over with two bowls. She handed one to Shen Xuanqing, not even looking at her second brother, and gave the other to Lu Gu, then she tucked herself beside Lu Gu with a smile.

Shen Xuanqing took the bowl. Seeing her acting so “self-serving,” he felt helpless and amused, and asked, “Didn’t you put honey in mine?”

“Who said I didn’t put honey in yours? You’ll know once you drink it,” Shen Yan teased, trying to please Lu Gu. After growing up together, she was not shy with either of her brothers.

Now that the rooster matter was settled, there would be no more grudges. Shen Yaoqing also thought of Li Wanyun and said, “She didn’t come back for Aunt Zhang’s seventh day and thirty-fifth day. The second branch of the Zhang family and those relatives even went to the Li family. I don’t know what they quarrelled about, but Li Wanyun didn’t come back, and it seems she no longer recognizes the Zhang family.”

[头七 tóu qī: the seventh day after a person’s death is considered a significant day when the soul of the deceased is believed to return to the family home]

[五七 wǔ qī: the thirty-fifth day after a person’s death is considered a significant day of mourning]

“It’s fine to let her be,” Shen Xuanqing took a sip of honey water and said, “Why bother spending your whole life paying for the dead.”
