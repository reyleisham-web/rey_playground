Chapter 8 Part 1

Part 1

Farmers didn’t have many free days a year, and not long after the roosters crowed, Qingxi Village at the foot of the mountain gradually grew lively.

Lu Gu slept well last night, and when Shen Xuanqing moved, he woke as well. After all, he hadn’t slept in, and he felt a small stroke of luck.

Ji Qiuyue’s and Wei Lanxiang’s words last night made him realize, before he fell asleep, that although he had been sold to the Shen family, he wasn’t just a servant. He was Shen Xuanqing’s ger. This was no small thing. He never imagined he would become a ger one day, so the thought caught him off guard.

Shen Xuanqing rose, and Lu Gu sat up anxiously, fearing he would be told to lie back down. No one had taught him how to serve his husband after marriage, and the thin quilt clenched in his hands.

Shen Xuanqing put on his shoes and went to the courtyard. To him, Lu Gu’s existence was obvious—after all, there was only one quilt at night. Even though he had completely severed ties with the Lu family, Lu Gu was now Shen Xuanqing’s fulang, so it would be inappropriate to leave him out in the cold.

He stood beside the bed and saw Lu Gu nervous, his face pale. The linen headscarf around his head had loosened from sleeping all night, so Shen Xuanqing finally found the words and said, “Wang Langzhong said that today we will change the dressing. Get up, pack up, and go while it’s cool in the morning.”

Lu Gu nodded again and again, fearing Shen Xuanqing would be unhappy if he didn’t speak. He hurriedly murmured in a low voice, “Okay.”

The ger was even more timid than a rabbit, and Shen Xuanqing frowned, but Lu Gu lowered his head, not meeting his eyes, or else he would be terrified for days.

The Shen family woke one after another to wash in the courtyard. Even Lu Gu used a new basin and even green salt to brush his teeth and rinse his mouth. This was prepared for the newly married ger. After all, Lu Wen had been studying in town for a few days and had some practical knowledge of town etiquette.

When he was with the Lu family, the green salt that needed money to buy was not Lu Gu’s share. Afraid that people would say his teeth were dirty, he would break a willow branch and bite and chew one in his mouth to clean his teeth.

A faint wisp of smoke rose from the chimney; Ji Qiuyue boiled water and, in passing, heated some assorted flour steamed cakes.

Normally, people in the village ate two meals a day, unless they were busy with farming, in which case they ate three. The Shen family had two young men who did a lot of work and had big appetites. Otherwise, you wouldn’t have the energy to work on an empty stomach.

But this was also due to the recent banquet, so they could eat like this, which wouldn’t have happened in the past two years. Except for Shen Xuanqing and Shen Yaoqing, who ate two steamed cakes, the other four each had half. They had eaten too much oil and meat in the past few days, so they grabbed a bowl of small pickles and ate them.

Breakfast in the morning was simple—there was no need to lay chopsticks on the table—so Ji Qiuyue handed the cakes by hand, and even the small pickles were pinched. Some people crouched or stood in the yard to eat.

Lu Gu held half a steamed cake. The cake was soft and warm. After everyone else took theirs, he picked a small pickle from the bowl and, like the rest of the Shen family, tucked it into the cake. It was salty and crispy in one bite, and it went well with the steamed cakes.

He managed to drink a few sips of hot water and eat so early, and even he had a share, which made Lu Gu feel grateful while also a bit panicked.

Farmers rarely chewed and swallowed slowly, and the steamed cakes weren’t very big. Shen Xuanqing could finish them in two or three bites; two cakes were enough for him early in the morning.

Lu Gu had been secretly watching him; he ate quickly, fearing he would delay the departure.

“Mother, I’ll go to change the medicine with him,” Shen Xuanqing said to Wei Lanxiang, and the two went out together.

It was early; the sun had just risen. This summer was hotter than before, but Qingxi Village, being near the mountains, was still a little chilly in the morning, though not enough to freeze anyone.

Lu Gu followed Shen Xuanqing out of the village and met several people along the way.

The Shen family’s reputation spread through Qingxi Village, and everyone was curious. Seeing a strange shuang’er following Shen Xuanqing, they guessed he was another Lu family shuang’er, and they kept staring at him.

Anyone who heard this kind of thing felt aggrieved, and no one stepped forward to ask outright; they simply watched him in silence.

Staring at him, Lu Gu lowered his head. If he hadn’t been able to see Shen Xuanqing’s legs and feet, he would have trembled with fright.
