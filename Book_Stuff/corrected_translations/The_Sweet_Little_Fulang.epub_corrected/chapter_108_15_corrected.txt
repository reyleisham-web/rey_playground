Chapter 108 Part 15


Lu Gu had never touched rouge or face powder before, so buying them for the first time made his heart beat faster. He was so anxious about it that he didn’t even notice he had wandered out of town.

As soon as Shen Xuanqing stopped, Lu Gu stopped as well. When he recovered enough to ask what was going on, Shen Xuanqing’s thumb briefly brushed his lips, wiping them.

Shen Xuanqing froze. His hands were a little dirty, stark against Lu Gu’s fair skin. When he had sold bamboo shoots and wild onions, his hands had inevitably gotten stained, but his handkerchief had been used to wipe sweat. Since the lip balm had already rubbed all over, it would be best to wipe it clean and then let Lu Gu wash it again.

Lu Gu’s lips were rubbed a bit hard, and he frowned unconsciously. He didn’t avoid it out of trust, but he looked up at Shen Xuanqing in confusion.

A few villagers coming out of the town drew near. After the rubbing, Shen Xuanqing felt a prickle of annoyance seeing that Lu Gu’s lips had turned red from the rubbing, not much different from the lip balm’s color.

“What’s wrong?” Lu Gu asked anxiously.

Shen Xuanqing stepped forward with the bamboo basket. After a few steps he muttered, “Don’t wipe it again.”

These words left Lu Gu more perplexed. He worried there was something wrong with him, and with his lips still tinted red by the balm, his ears reddened with embarrassment as he murmured, “Yeah.”

They continued in silence. After a while, Shen Xuanqing turned to look at Lu Gu’s embarrassed expression. He realized he hadn’t explained himself clearly, but he didn’t know what to say.

The reason he had ruthlessly wiped off Lu Gu’s lip balm was jealousy.

A box of good lip balm in town cost 160 copper coins—far more than what the villagers gave Lu Gu. Once applied, it made him look so pretty and delicate. On the way out of town, many men paid attention to Lu Gu, and he wanted to glare back at them.

Fortunately, those men weren’t tall enough to threaten him, and none dared to stare too long.

As soon as they were out of town, the crowd thinned. The red tint on Lu Gu’s lips made him feel worse the more he looked at it, so he wiped it off and felt better.

“Don’t overthink it. I didn’t handle it well just now. This thing is expensive, and not many people in the village use it. Facial cream and hand cream have no color and are unobtrusive, but the lip balm is too red—if the villagers see it, they’ll grow envious. It isn’t worth the talk behind your back. You’ll have another chance to use lip balm later. It’s not a festival now, and it’s too conspicuous to wear it.”

Shen Xuanqing’s explanation grew more serious the more he spoke. Lu Gu could only accept it as true.

Lu Gu agreed, nodding. “Okay. If we don’t need it, we won’t buy it again; otherwise it would be a waste.”

With this resolution, both of them returned to a better mood.

On the way home, they met two acquaintances from a neighboring village, and the four of them walked back together.

Lu Gu and Lin He’s wife took the lead. Shen Xuanqing, who was tall, chatted with Lin He as they walked. He glanced at the small boxes in Lu Gu’s bamboo basket and suddenly remembered he had bought another ointment at the cosmetics shop earlier—the clerk there had sold it to him. A bottle of ointment worth eight copper coins, it would last for some time.
