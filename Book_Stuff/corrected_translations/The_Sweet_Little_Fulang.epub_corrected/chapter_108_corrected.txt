Chapter 108


After Shen Xuanqing caught the scent of the facial cream on Lu Gu’s hand, the shopkeeper applied three more kinds of facial cream to Lu Gu’s hands in turn. He dabbed the cream onto the back of Lu Gu’s hands, and it was clear that the skin where the cream had been applied looked smoother and more moisturized, while the areas left bare appeared dry.

All four kinds of facial cream were good. Lu Gu hesitated, unable to decide which one to buy. In the end, Shen Xuanqing said he wanted the peach blossom and plum blossom varieties.

The peach-blossom-scented one was indeed pleasant, while the red-plum blossom carried a faint, fresh, and elegant fragrance. Without deliberately sniffing the cream boxes or Lu Gu’s hands, one could still catch a faint scent that drifted on the breeze.

“Sir, you have an excellent eye. These peach blossom and plum blossom creams are the bestsellers.” The shopkeeper closed the lids of the facial cream and handed over the two boxes Shen Xuanqing wanted. People in commerce always speak nicely, so the shopkeeper took the opportunity to flatter them.

Before mentioning the price of the two boxes, he added, “Sir, I also have hand cream here. Apply some after washing your hands each day. It can moisturize your hands and make your skin more delicate. Would you like to take a look?”

Shen Xuanqing had just noticed that Lu Gu’s hands were a little dry, so he nodded and said, “Bring it here and let me see.”

“Okay, please wait a moment.” The shopkeeper’s smile deepened.

“Do you want to buy hand cream too?” Lu Gu asked in a low voice. He had to do a lot of work every day—weeding, digging wild vegetables, chopping wood, cleaning rabbit manure. It was easy to get his hands dirty, and he washed them several times a day. If he had to apply hand cream and then wash his hands again, that would be a waste. Not to mention cooking and washing dishes.

Shen Xuanqing understood what he meant, and after a moment’s hesitation, he said, “Boss, please give me the cheaper hand cream—two boxes.”

“It’s not a waste to wash your hands with the cheap ones.” He turned to Lu Gu and said.

The shopkeeper then produced two boxes of cheap hand cream, came over with a smile, and said, “Here you go. Only fifty copper coins a box, but good for hands.”

The price of a small box of facial cream was three hundred copper coins, which was much more expensive than the hand cream. Lu Gu blinked, thinking it was steep, but before he could speak, Shen Xuanqing opened his purse from his bosom.

“A total of seven coins.” Shen Xuanqing counted the money and handed it over.

The shopkeeper accepted the money with a smile. “Okay, thank you very much.”

He added casually, “Please come here often. I have rouge and face powder, as well as lip balm (口脂).”

Then he paused, looked at Lu Gu, and smiled, “It’s hard to find a suitable color for lip balm. If you don’t mind, why don’t you two stay for a while and let my wife help this fulang try it out? Or he can also try rouge if he wants.”

Seeing that Shen Xuanqing was clearly moved, the shopkeeper didn’t wait for them to answer and called to the back, “Dingxiang, come out and help the customer put on lip balm.”

“Coming, coming.” A beautiful woman stepped out. The rouge and powder on her face were just right, giving people an immediate sense of beauty and ease.

After she helped Lu Gu apply the lip balm, she looked at him and smiled, “This color suits you really well. It would be even better with a little rouge.”

Lu Gu’s lips were originally pale, but after the slightly red lip balm, they looked fuller. It wasn’t flashy, but it brightened his whole face. The color of his lips matched the red mark between his eyebrows, making him look incredibly beautiful.

Lu Gu stared at himself in the bronze mirror and felt a bit unsettled. When he raised his eyes, he found Shen Xuanqing’s blank gaze on him. He grew more embarrassed, his cheeks glowing with a blush even without rouge.

“This lip balm can also moisturize dry lips. It’s best to use some when your lips are dry and chapped,” the shopkeeper reminded, eager to show off his expertise.

“Uh-huh.” Shen Xuanqing finally came to his senses and, for once, looked a little embarrassed in front of outsiders. He asked, “Forget the rouge—how much does this lip balm cost?”

As they left the cosmetics shop, the shopkeeper saw them off. This business was quite bustling.

There were the facial creams and hand creams in the bamboo basket, and they had also bought a box of lip balm. Lu Gu could still smell a faint lip-balm scent on his own lips. His face was still hot, and he felt both embarrassed and shy. How could a country folk like him pay such attention to appearance? Besides, he had never applied lip balm before, so he didn’t know how to use it properly. If he used it and it didn’t look right, he would become the butt of jokes.
