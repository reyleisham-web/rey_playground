Chapter 55


The feeling that spread through Lu Gu was different from ordinary pain, and it frightened him. The reaction in his body was strange, even the tremor in his throat seemed to change meaning. He had always believed such things were painful, and occasionally there would be a hint of relief once the pain passed, but now he had grown a little used to it, believing it would pass if he endured it.

When he heard that his groan sounded different, fear seized him at once. He bit his lower lip tightly; the pain at his lip kept him conscious, on guard, afraid to make a sound.

Shen Xuanqing heard the change as well, paused in the darkness, his back arched like a leopard lurking in the night, ready to spring. When he heard Lu Gu’s voice, his strong, smooth muscles tensed for a moment, and the fire within him seemed to waver and die down. The moment of heat passed, and the sound vanished, so he discarded everything else and reached out with his left hand to touch Lu Gu’s cheek.

Seeing that Lu Gu was biting his lip, he worried that his heavy breathing would frighten the timid Fulang, so he almost held his breath, only the rising and falling of Lu Gu’s chest betraying his impatience. Gently cupping Lu Gu’s chin, he spoke in a low voice: “Don’t bite.” He touched him with his right hand, and a hoarse breath sounded in Lu Gu’s ear.

For a while Lu Gu calmed; the tension eased, and he stopped biting his lip. In this kind of moment, the only person he could rely on was Shen Xuanqing, so he gradually surrendered to the influence, and before anyone could do anything about it, it was already too late.

The night stretched on, and the darkness covered everything, making it impossible to tell what happened.

In the early morning, dew dripped off the trees in the woods. Small birds hopped among the branches, their feathers damp with dew, and they fluttered and chirped.

The forest outside woke, and there was some stir in the courtyard behind the high mud walls. Shen Xuanqing opened his eyes, rolled over, and held Lu Gu close, tucking the quilt around them. After lying there for a while, he could not hold back and planted a few light kisses on Lu Gu’s soft cheek before getting up to dress. When he stood, he tucked Lu Gu’s underwear, casually discarded last night, back onto the bed. By the time Lu Gu woke, his underwear no longer felt cold.

The door opened and closed, and Lu Gu slowly opened his eyes. He had just woken, but he did not dare look at Shen Xuanqing, keeping his eyes closed.

There seemed to be a lingering warmth on his cheek from the kiss, and he could not help but wipe it with the back of his hand. It wasn’t moisture; rather, he felt such a kiss was too embarrassing to acknowledge, and he instinctively tried to erase it.

Last night, he had not dared to move, not even to part his lips, but when his breath became shallow, he could only manage a small struggle. They were both very young, and even though Shen Xuanqing tried his best to copy what he had seen, their first time had jolted their teeth together a few times.

Until footsteps sounded outside the door and the dogs began to bark, Lu Gu, who had been lying for a long while, felt nervous and realized he had forgotten to dress. He closed his eyes again, unsure why.

Shen Xuanqing opened the door, entered, and banished the lingering shadow from his brows, smiling brightly. He saw that Lu Gu hadn’t woken yet; he scratched his head, feeling a little helpless. The water had boiled, and yesterday’s pancakes steamed hot.

“Breakfast is ready. The pancakes are hot; I’ve put them in the pot. After you rest a bit, you can eat, and I’ll leave first after you’ve eaten, and come back in the evening,” Shen Xuanqing said with a smile, then paused briefly before turning away.

Autumn was turning colder and rainier, but today the sun was bright. The main thing was to take advantage of the weather to hunt more game and sell it for money; other things could wait. Besides, it was not certain he could endure not to do something with Lu Gu, but this was also a way to rest.

Lu Gu seldom missed seeing Shen Xuanqing off. He only opened his eyes after the barking of the dog faded away, then reached under the bed to pull out his underwear and put it on, blushing.

The puppy nudged the door with its head, pushed it slightly, and barked twice from under the bed, wagging its tail happily.

Lu Gu rose, lifted the pot lid, pulled out a drawer, and scooped hot water from the pot to wash his hands. It was cold early in the morning, and soaking his hands in cold water hurt his skin, so hot water was better.

On the chopping block, Shen Xuanqing set a drink down in a bowl, and after washing it, he came in to taste it; it was honey water.

This time, when they went to the mountain, Wei Lanxiang had brought pickled cowpeas, sour and spicy. He sat on the stool in front of the stove, eating the cowpeas with one hand and pancakes with the other.

Although he felt uncomfortable upon waking, he was much better than yesterday; at least the pain had eased somewhat.

Two pancakes were enough for Lu Gu. He washed his hands after eating, otherwise the oil on his fingers would make working difficult. There was no Dahui today, and it would be hard for him to travel far to gather medicinal materials. After driving the chickens and ducks to graze outside, he shouldered a bamboo basket and brought the puppy to the river to gather wild vegetables.

Purslane grew vigorously along the riverbanks, a weed that proved tenacious. When it dried, it would still cling to life in the sun. As long as a root remained, it could survive. It grew well in the wild fields by the river; farmers would hate it if it grew in cultivated fields.

Lu Gu didn’t take the old purslane, only the tender shoots, to avoid bringing dirt back with him; once back, he would wash and dry them. This time, Shen Xuanqing had found him a broken mat not used at home. It was cleaned and left to dry for a day before he carried it up, just enough to spread the wild greens on the ground.
