Chapter 11 Part 22


After arriving at Qingxi Village, under the old tree at the gate, a group of elder women—wives and old ladies—absorbed the bright sun. Some stood in line in the square, some peeled beans, and all of them gathered to gossip about various people and things in the village.

Seeing Wei Lanxiang bring Lu Gu back, the old lady stepped up and asked face to face, “Lanxiang, what are you doing?”

Wei Lanxiang smiled. “It’s not a piece of fabric for our Gu ger. Go back and make him some new clothes.” She pulled a roll of cloth from the basket, draped it over Lu Gu’s body, and asked a few old women, “Doesn’t this fabric suit our Gu ger?”

“Oh, it’s really beautiful.”

“Our little fulang looks really handsome.”

“This fabric is so bright, it must have cost a lot of money.”

Wei Lanxiang listened to their gossip and replied with a smile, “No, it’s only 80 pennies.”

They sucked in their breath in unison. “Amazing!”

“That’s what you’re willing to do.”

Wei Lanxiang smiled without answering, then asked, “Grandma Ajin, I remember you had someone bring the old clothes you couldn’t sell before. Do you have any more?”

Grandma Ajin hurriedly set the chai bean aside, leaned forward, and said, “There’s more.”

Wei Lanxiang folded the cloth and tucked it back into the basket. “Not much, but if there’s more, I’ll give you three pennies, and you can take it and make two pairs of new shoes for Gu ger.”

“Yes, there are enough for you.” Grandma Ajin stopped peeling beans and rushed with Wei Lanxiang toward the village, carrying a dustpan, with three pennies in hand.

The clothes in her family were said to be old clothes merely for appearances, but in fact they were tattered garments the farmers couldn’t mend or sell. Besides their own, they also picked up clothes others wouldn’t take. There were plenty of them, Wei Lanxiang heard, and people bought them—the price was three pennies—to make shoe soles; otherwise who would buy a bundle of rags?

Ji Qiuyue was squatting in the yard, chopping grass, when she saw Lu Gu return with a bundle of rags. She wondered, “Where did this come from?”

Wei Lanxiang replied smoothly, “There are no old clothes at home, and I bought them from Grandma Ajin. We’ll make the soles for Gu ger and Shen Yan.”

The Shen family had prepared new clothes before Shen Xuanqing got married, so when Wei Lanxiang bought new fabric for Lu Gu and said it would be used to make clothes, Ji Qiuyue wasn’t offended. She knew Lu Gu didn’t even have another pair of shoes, so he was pitiful and wouldn’t say a word.

Still, she was a little surprised to see her mother-in-law suddenly show such concern for Lu ger.

Lu Gu laid the rag pile on the ground and, with Wei Lanxiang, picked it up. If there was dirt, he set it aside. Then they washed them with water, so as not to waste.

Shen Yan was guiding the ducks into the pond and carried a wooden basin to the back of the house to wash the tatters, and she saw him as expected.

“Brother Lu Gu.” Shen Yan had just toppled a bale of grass and sat on a white stone to rest. When she saw him, she cried out and asked what he was doing.

Hearing that Lu Gu said his mother planned to make shoes for them, she was naturally pleased.

The busy, steady day slipped by quickly. After dinner that evening, Lu Gu washed the dishes as usual, while Wei Lanxiang and Ji Qiuyue wandered around the village before it grew dark.

On the way, Ji Qiuyue could not hold back and asked, “Mother, why did you decide to make clothes for Lu Gu?”

Wei Lanxiang sighed softly before explaining the reason.

Du Hehua was not easy to get along with. After the quarrel in Anjia Village, Shen Xuanqing injured Lu Daxiang, and Du Hehua cried whenever she saw anyone, saying that their Shen family was not good people, and she planted even uglier words about Shen Xuanqing in people’s minds.

There had been gossip in the surrounding villages, even Zhou Xiangjun had heard of it. When he came that day, he told her that gossip was always bad; even if you aren’t in the wrong, people will talk behind your back.

Shen Xuanqing and Lu Gu were drenched with sewage by Du Hehua outside. Let’s not mention other things. Shen Yan would marry later, so she couldn’t be dragged down or tied to a bad family.

Now she not only wanted to make new clothes and shoes for Lu Gu, but also looked after the people. No matter who looked at it, no one could say the Shen family was at fault, and Shen Xuanqing’s reputation could be restored.

Ji Qiuyue suddenly realized that this was true.

The mother-in-law and daughter-in-law’s visit continued. When Wei Lanxiang spoke of marrying Lu Gu off, she sighed and admitted that Shen Xuanqing bought Lu Gu to save him; otherwise Lu Gu would have been ruined by his stepmother. How could he survive—even if he did survive, it would be as rough as Du Hehua’s hand, and Lu Gu would be tortured to death in the Lu family.

The Xia family fulang, who had been chatting with her, nodded and sighed, patting Wei Lanxiang’s hand for comfort.

This Xia family fulang was kind-hearted and warm; when he encountered injustice, he always spoke out. He spoke a lot, but he could not hide it. He told her this today so that half of Qingxi Village would know tomorrow.

Lu Gu, who was cleaning up in the kitchen, didn’t know anything, and even if he did, he wouldn’t mind. Shen Xuanqing had bought him from the Lu family, and he could eat and sleep well here. For him, this was truly a salvation.

Wei Lanxiang was very selfish toward him, but that was different from Du Hehua. Du Hehua only dressed him neatly, but didn’t even feed him. So, telling outsiders about that was one thing; compared to the bad things Du Hehua did, this was nothing.

Lu Gu lay on the bed that night, the only one sleeping alone. He felt a touch of comfort, but he didn’t dare move anything.

He remembered the indigo fabric and the shoes. It was almost unbelievable, like a dream. Du Hehua’s words—that he might not deserve new clothes—still echoed in his ears.

But now, Du Hehua could not beat him anymore.
