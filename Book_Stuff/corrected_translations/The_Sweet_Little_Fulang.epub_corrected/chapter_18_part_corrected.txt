Chapter 18


Since it had been agreed that Lu Gu would accompany him into the mountains, he had to pack today and set off early tomorrow morning. Not to mention anything else, he at least had to prepare enough rations.

Shen Yan dug up onions, ginger, and garlic in the courtyard and pulled some vegetables, while Wei Lanxiang summoned Ji Qiuyue and Lu Gu to help pack rice and flour in the kitchen.

They had the most miscellaneous flour at home, about a hundred catties. Half of the fine white flour they had traded for when they got married before hadn’t moved yet, so they packed twenty catties in cloth bags.

Now it was different from before: Shen Xuanqing didn’t need to do part‑time work and only carried large sacks, and Lu Gu didn’t need to change his medicine anymore. He would stay longer in the mountains. In a rush to sell things, he would come down once every ten or so months; not to mention for two people to eat, it took more to save a trip.

The Shen family ate old rice. Last year, when the rice in the paddy field ripened, they sold all the new rice at a good price and bought cheap old rice to eat. Now there wasn’t much left, only three bags.

There were a lot of people in the family, and they had to eat a lot of food each day, especially the two young men who had big appetites. No matter whether it was a rice barrel or a flour barrel, it would be emptied quickly if you didn’t dig out enough bowls when cooking. The new rice was cooked this year; there would be more in the past two months. Wei Lanxiang had originally planned to add more water to the porridge, at least until the new rice was harvested.

Now it was all right; if you had money in hand, you didn’t have to worry about running out of food. She directly asked Shen Xuanqing to go to the grain hut to carry a bag. A hundred catties would be enough for them to eat for a month.

“When the rice is ripe, let’s eat new rice this year. If it’s not all sold out, I’ll bring two hundred catties for you two. If you want to eat steamed dry rice, you can eat it. Don’t feel bad.” Wei Lanxiang smiled at Lu Gu.

Lu Gu hurriedly nodded in agreement, tied the cloth bag containing the white flour tightly in his hands, and looked up from the window to see Shen Xuanqing in the courtyard.

Shen Xuanqing carried the rice out and set it on the ground, then took the two‑wheeled cart from the corner of the courtyard.

“Mother, no need; big brother is here. It’s cold outside and you should just rest. We won’t delay, we’re leaving now.”

The cart had two strong wooden wheels at the front. On the side near the handle there were two vertical sticks on the left and right, which could support the ground after being lowered.

After Shen Xuanqing had put the cart away, he loaded the rice and flour onto it, fearing the mountain road would be rough, so he tied them with hemp rope a few times.

“Brother Erqing, is this enough for the two of you?” Shen Yan was still squatting in the yard, pointing to the pile of vegetables she had dug up and asking.

Lu Gu came over with the cloth bag containing the white flour and tried to put it on the cart. Hearing this, he looked at it too. There were ginger, scallions, and green vegetables, as well as chili, beans, and two big pumpkins. There was a lot, enough to eat for several days.

“That’s enough.” Shen Xuanqing said. He took the cloth bag from Lu Gu; it weighed almost thirty catties.

“Bring another braid of garlic.” Wei Lanxiang pulled a braid of garlic hanging under the eaves, and then she remembered something and added, “You don’t even have a jar of pickles. Why don’t you bring a jar of pickles up there? Get up early and you can eat them with pancakes.”

The more they talked, the more they added. Shen Xuanqing thought it was troublesome and wanted to say no, but when he looked up and saw Lu Gu, he remembered that he wouldn’t eat alone in the future, so it would be better to bring more food. There was no need to take the jar back; they could use it to marinate after eating the pickles.

“Where’s the meat?” Wei Lanxiang yelled again.

Shen Xuanqing said: “No, you keep it for yourselves; I’ll make some when I get up there.”

The meat referred to the few hares and pheasants he had brought back this time; they hadn’t all been sold out, and each kept one to let the family eat meat.

Wei Lanxiang saw that there were a lot of things in the cart, but she still couldn’t sit still, so she asked again: “What about eggs? You like them, so take all the eggs we’ve saved these days with you.”

Wei Lanxiang felt that she was showing favoritism when she said this, and quickly added to Ji Qiuyue: “We eat duck eggs at home, and there are quite a few. I won’t sell them for now; I’ll make up for you and Daqing.”

Although Shen Xuanqing had a lot of money, it was given to her. Ji Qiuyue hadn’t seen any money and couldn’t get it. Shen Xuanqing would give the money to her whether he sold crops or worked as a part‑time laborer during the slack season. If the eggs were all divided to Lu Gu, it would always make people feel unhappy, right?

Ji Qiuyue had been married for three years and had never had trouble with her family. She and Shen Yan would dig wild vegetables and pick fruits to supplement the family’s rations. How could she make the oldest daughter‑in‑law feel left out?

Sure enough, when Ji Qiuyue heard this, her tightly pursed lips relaxed, and she smiled again. In fact, meat was enough for her; it was shared by Shen Xuanqing and left for them. It didn’t hurt to take some eggs.

But if the mother‑in‑law could speak up for her, she would be fair and considerate; otherwise, how could she feel at ease.

The cart was full, and the eggs were fragile, so they had to be packed in bamboo baskets and thickly padded with straw, so Lu Gu could carry them on the road tomorrow morning.

After finishing all this, Wei Lanxiang remembered that Lu Gu was going to bring clothes, but the two pairs of shoes were not ready. She hurried to fetch a sewing basket from the house, and before she came out, she called again: “Qiuyue, pack some beans and go with Shen Yan to exchange for some pieces of tofu, and we’ll mix it with tofu.”

“Got it, Mother.” Ji Qiuyue also shouted.
