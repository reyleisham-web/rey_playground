Chapter 174


Shen Xuanqing came over and sat beside the bed, their legs brushing. When Lu Gu’s hand rested in his, Lu Gu didn’t tremble or pull away. The faint smile he wore grew warmer. Noting that Shen Xuanqing’s hair was still damp from washing, he said cheerfully, “Let’s sit outside while the sun’s still up and dry your hair.”

“Alright.” As long as he had his fulang with him, Shen Xuanqing would not refuse.

Lu Gu glanced at the cradle and added, “I’ll carry Zhao'er. You bring the cradle to the yard so he can sleep or play in it. Otherwise we’d have to hold him all the time.” He spoke casually, but it was the truth. Zhao'er was actually quite well-behaved—when he slept, he required no attention. But once awake, Lu Gu had to carry or hold him while he worked, which left him exhausted. Fortunately, when there was no work, he could rest and set Zhao'er in the cradle to sleep or play.

Shen Xuanqing’s expression flickered. “When did the family start transplanting rice seedlings?”

Why the sudden question?

Lu Gu was puzzled, but answered honestly, “Early yesterday morning. Mother and Eldest Brother went with the others. Even Yan Yan came back covered in mud these past two days.” So it had indeed been two days.

Seeing the occasional weariness in Lu Gu’s expression, Shen Xuanqing felt a twinge of displeasure. Rural fulangs weren’t treated as delicately—in nearby villages it wasn’t unusual for pregnant women or fulangs to still labor in the fields—but he couldn’t help feeling irked.

Lu Gu, having grown up in such an environment, saw nothing unusual about it. Aside from these two days of spring plowing, he hadn’t needed to cook, wash dishes, do laundry, or cut grass. Once his belly grew, Wei Lanxiang wouldn’t even let him feed the chickens and ducks. Perhaps because he hadn’t done much work since becoming pregnant, the sudden labor left him unaccustomed.

Shen Xuanqing was no saint. His displeasure came from two things: first, his own delay in the mountains causing him to miss spring plowing, and second, his family’s oversight. Before leaving, he had explicitly told them to take care of Lu Gu and not overwork him. Yet here they were, letting him exhaust himself.

“What’s wrong?” Lu Gu noticed his expression and grew puzzled.

Shen Xuanqing sighed softly, his earlier irritability fading as he understood. With so many livestock at home now, fieldwork took priority. His mother and the others would naturally focus on the land first. If they hadn’t raised so many animals, Lu Gu would simply need to cook and care for the child.

He knew the logic and couldn’t truly blame anyone. Busy times meant some things slipped through the cracks. When he went to the fields earlier, the whole family was sweating, covered in mud—how could he hold resentment? Seeing Lu Gu’s concern, he forced a smile and said, “It’s nothing. I just regret coming back so late and leaving you to do so much work at home.”

So that was it. Lu Gu’s spirits lifted immediately, his eyes shining. “It’s really nothing. I’m just feeding the livestock twice a day, and I can rest when I’m free. Sister-in-law comes back often to nurse the baby, and she carries the water jug herself, so I don’t have to haul water to the fields.”

Feeding the animals and carrying grass weren’t too bad, but hauling water buckets was strenuous. Still, seeing Lu Gu’s cheerful demeanor, Shen Xuanqing’s lingering displeasure gradually faded. Now that he was back, Lu Gu wouldn’t have to overwork. No need to dwell on it or add unnecessary worries.

“Let’s go sit outside.” He stood, lifting Zhao'er for Lu Gu to carry, then followed behind with the wooden cradle.

The sun hadn’t set yet, and the wind was mild today, but they chose a sheltered spot—the child was too small to be out in the open.

Zhao'er lay in the cradle, playing with the handkerchief, his chubby little hands tugging at it before stuffing it into his mouth to chew, leaving it soaked with drool.

With no one else around, Shen Xuanqing held his fulang’s hand and lifted his spirits again. “Once we’re done with these busy days, I’ll go to town and buy some nice fabric. I’ll have Mother make you two new outfits and a pair of shoes.”

To Shen Xuanqing’s eyes, Lu Gu had endured enough hardship. Since he couldn’t offer much else, new clothes were the least he could do to ease the burden.

“I already have clothes,” Lu Gu said, his eyes curving into crescents.

“Have what? Those are from last year.” Shen Xuanqing frowned slightly, pinching his cheek. He found an excuse and added, “You’re not plump yet, but once your belly grows bigger, last year’s clothes won’t fit. They’ll probably be too tight.”

“If it were just you feeling uncomfortable, fine. But if my son gets squeezed and something happens, how will you make it up to me?” His tone carried a hint of frustration at Lu Gu’s reluctance to ask for anything.

Lu Gu grinned, wide enough that his teeth showed, understanding his concern.

Shen Xuanqing wanted to keep a stern face to show his displeasure, but seeing Lu Gu’s radiant smile, all reproach vanished. Gazing at that warm, joyful expression, he could only let out a nearly inaudible sigh before smiling himself. Unable to resist, he leaned in and kissed his fulang.
