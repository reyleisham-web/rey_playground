Chapter 62


After collecting a total of 800 copper coins, Lu Gu was so happy that he didn’t know what to say. His eyes crinkled with a shy smile, and he couldn’t keep from looking at Shen Xuanqing.

Shen Xuanqing was laughing as well, and the two of them sat facing each other for a while.

“ I’m going to get the twine,” Lu Gu said, lowering his head in embarrassment. Seeing so many copper coins on the table, he thought they should be strung together, or they would stay scattered, so he got up to fetch the twine.

Shen Xuanqing had only been watching Lu Gu a moment ago, and only now began to count the money. In his purse there were five taels of broken silver, more than three hundred copper coins, as well as the untouched twenty-eight taels of silver from the dwarf deer, and the ten taels of silver he had saved before. If you paid someone to watch people, you wouldn’t need to worry about Lu Wen and Li Mingshan’s affairs. He had spent more than two taels today, and only about a dozen copper coins were left on him. These scattered copper coins were enough for daily expenses. Compared with thirty-eight taels, the eleven taels might not seem like much, but to these country folks, it was a lot of money.

Lu Gu came over with a ball of twine. His 800 copper coins weren’t enough to exchange for silver, so he decided to string a hundred of them, leaving a bit of twine at the end to tie a knot. He had earned a little money, not as much as Shen Xuanqing, but in the village, a few hundred coins would be a big deal for any fulang, not to mention that he still had nineteen taels of silver, and people might hardly believe it when he told them.

The reason Lu Gu could save money like this was because Shen Xuanqing treated him well and didn’t take his money away, and he didn’t let him pay for things outside. Shen Xuanqing also helped him string the money; the eight hundred copper coins were threaded together, each small coin heavy. The mouth of the money bag was open, there was five taels of silver in it, and his nineteen taels of silver were also there. Lu Gu looked at it—the more he looked, the happier he became, and the more reluctant he was to put the copper coins into the money bag.

Shen Xuanqing hadn’t counted his copper coins yet, so he poured them out from the big money bag and also took a string of twine. He would go to Fucheng to sell fox fur in the next two days.

“I’ll help you.” Lu Gu tightened the mouth of the big money bag, and when he saw Shen Xuanqing stringing the money, he pulled a length of twine to help.

A total of 346 copper coins remained to be sorted; they strung 300 of the small copper coins with twine and put the remaining 46 copper coins into the purse. Since Fucheng was far away, he would certainly have to eat there. There was no need to untie the string of money if he had these 46 copper coins.

He pushed the remaining 20 copper coins to Lu Gu and said with a smile, “Your money is all threaded now. Keep it. If you want to exchange it for silver, you can go to the town to do it, and these 20 copper coins are for you to keep in case you want to buy something.”

Lu Gu felt embarrassed and smiled shyly, carefully tucking the twenty copper coins into a small purse so he could carry it close to his body when he went out without fear of losing it. In fact, he mostly bought needlework and silk with his money. Usually, whether at home or on the mountain, he seldom carried money on him, but these twenty copper coins felt different. If you had money, you wouldn’t be ashamed if you couldn’t afford something.

After hiding the two big money bags at the bottom of the box, the thin quilt and clothes were pressed on top to conceal them, and then the box was locked. Lu Gu relaxed completely.

The early morning of the next day, Shen Xuanqing picked up the bamboo basket and went out after breakfast. Before going to Fucheng, he had to visit the old hunter to ask about the price of fox fur in Fucheng and whether there was anyone who could buy it, so he could return tonight.

Lu Gu sent him off, and Dahui, the dog without a leash, instinctively wanted to follow him.

“Don’t follow,” Shen Xuanqing said, and Lu Gu called him back from behind. Dahui stopped.

After Shen Xuanqing walked away, Lu Gu returned to the yard. Shen Yan picked a few duck eggs that had been laid overnight in the backyard and asked, “Brother Guzi, is Erqing gone?”

“Go,” Lu Gu replied. Seeing the duck eggs, he couldn’t help thinking of the chickens and ducks on the mountain, so he felt a little fond of the fowl in the house and asked with concern, “Have you chopped the chicken grass?”

“No—Brother Daqing is cleaning the backyard. I’ll put the eggs away and chop them,” Shen Yan said as she headed toward the kitchen.

When going down the mountain, there was plenty of grass and chaff for the chickens and ducks, enough to feed them for a few days. Lu Gu, who was used to staying in the mountains, had always kept busy when Shen Xuanqing went hunting, and it wasn’t easy for him now. Seeing that there wasn’t much firewood, he sat on a bench in the courtyard to chop wood. Chopping wood while sitting spread his legs wide; the dog curled up and lay beside his left leg.

Wei Lanxiang came out with a sewing basket. Seeing how hardworking he was, she couldn’t hide the smile on her face. She sat under the eaves and chatted with Lu Gu while she mended the soles of the shoes. After a while, there would be plenty of food stored for the winter—qiuchai and some pickled white cabbage. They hadn’t grown much white cabbage this year, just two rows in the front yard vegetable patch; after a while they could buy some from other houses to stock up, which would be enough for the winter.
