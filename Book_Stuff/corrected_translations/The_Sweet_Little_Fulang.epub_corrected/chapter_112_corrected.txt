Chapter 112


At the first glimmer of light in the sky, there was movement in the yard. The crackling firewood filled the air with a light smoke as Lu Gu sat in front of the stove, a dazed look on his face; it was clear he wasn’t fully awake yet. He added two more pieces to the bottom of the stove and then yawned.

Shen Xuanqing, who had come in from outside, saw him in a daze and walked over to rub his head. “There’s nothing else to do here. If you still want to sleep after I leave, go ahead.”

“No, I’ll be fine after this.” After saying that, Lu Gu rubbed his face, looked up, smiled, and immediately felt more energetic.

Shen Xuanqing also smiled and couldn’t help but touch his face. After they had had sex that day, though Shen Xuanqing wanted to take it step by step and stop there, by nightfall Lu Gu had his hand on his chest, pushing him away, saying he hadn’t rested well and his body couldn’t bear it. He had no choice but to kiss him a little longer. It wasn’t bad to share the same bed with him for four or five days.

The morning was cold in the mountains, and the water seemed to seep into their bones. They used it to wash their faces and brush their teeth after warming it up, so they didn’t suffer so much. The two of them scooped up warm water to wash in the courtyard. Before carrying the wooden basin out, Lu Gu added two more ladles of water to the pot and firewood. The water would be boiled and ready for drinking after they finished washing.

It had been ten days since they first planted them. The four rows of mustard greens in the front yard had sprouted and were about half a finger long. Mustard greens grew very fast; in seven or eight days they would be two fingers long and could be plucked and eaten. They had deliberately planted more, so they could pluck as many as they wanted and reseed as needed. Mustard greens could also grow in summer and be eaten all the time.

Two rows of leeks had sprouted in the vegetable garden on the other side. They had been watered and tended, and could be harvested next month. At that time they could be scrambled with eggs for Shen Xuanqing.

They also planted a row of garlic and chili. The front yard was large, and one row would be enough for the two of them to eat. Spring onions and chives were planted in the same row. There were more spring onions and fewer chives. People in town often ate chives, and its price was higher than spring onions, but they required a lot of care to grow. Shen Yaoqing heard the news when he was looking for people to drink and chat in a neighboring village, so he asked someone to get some seeds. He didn’t want to sell them, but wanted to taste them at home. When Lu Gu and Shen Xuanqing went up the mountain, they brought some seeds with them.

Lu Gu had never planted chives before. Shen Xuanqing said they weren’t planted to earn money, but to let them have a taste at home. Hearing this, Lu Gu relaxed and didn’t pay much attention.

After washing his face, he saw that not only had the seeds grown, but they had grown a little taller. The feeling of success was unexpectedly satisfying.

“Brother Daqing said these chives are neither spicy nor choking. I’ll sprinkle some when making soup. They look good,” Lu Gu said happily.

Shen Xuanqing followed his eyes and looked over. “The chives are bright green, and they’ll look good when sprinkled on top of a dish. I’ll take two with me when I go out and eat them with steamed buns and pancakes. I’m a little tired of pickled vegetables.”

Eating and drinking well was a big deal. Only when people ate and drank properly could they feel comfortable physically and mentally. When he said he was tired of eating pickled vegetables, Lu Gu frowned slightly and began to think. After a moment, he said, “Next time we go up the mountain, I’ll bring you a few salted duck eggs. When we go back, the ducks at home should have laid a lot of eggs. Shen Yan said she would help collect them and save them for me. Mother can also pickle them into salted eggs so we can eat them at home.”

“Okay.” Shen Xuanqing nodded. Every time he saw Lu Gu’s serious expression when making plans, he couldn’t help but smile.

“Then you should bring two eggs today.” Lu Gu poured out the water, put away the basin and cloth, and hurried into the kitchen to get busy.

There was white steam rising from the pot lid, and the water had boiled. He scooped out two bowls and set them on the chopping board to cool, then scooped more than half of the pot into a pottery jug, added cold water, and placed it on the bamboo basket, along with the pancakes and eggs. He continued to cook. Normally the pancakes could be heated quickly, but today there were two extra eggs, so it took a little longer.

The water in the two bowls was for them to drink. When the large pot wasn’t used for cooking, it was used to boil water for drinking.

The sky was brightening. After breakfast, Lu Gu sent Shen Xuanqing out. Dahui and Dahei ran in front. He waited until Shen Xuanqing had gone far before returning to the yard.
