Chapter 88


Day by day passed quickly, and the twelfth lunar month drew near.

On the fifth day of the twelfth lunar month, Lu Gu had soaked the beans the night before. Today was the Five Beans Festival, and the family would eat five-bean porridge—red beans, soybeans, chai beans, black beans, and mung beans—plus yellow rice and peanuts to enrich the porridge.

It was said that the twelfth lunar month was a good time to appreciate the snow, but such leisure was mostly reserved for wealthy families. For farmers, the most important thing was to fill their stomachs first.

Early in the morning, the sky bright, Lu Gu boiled water in the kitchen. After the water boiled, Shen Xuanqing and Shen Yaoqing scooped water to wash up, and he set the pancakes on the bamboo steamer. With nothing else to do and the weather so cold that even his breath showed as white, Shen Xuanqing practiced boxing in the yard. This was something Lao Yangtou had taught him before; he had also learned a set of sword moves from him. People always said that poor folk are rich in martial arts—whether boxing or swordsmanship. When they began to practice, hunger set in and they ate more quickly. Even rice and flour couldn’t fill their stomachs fully, and they only felt truly full after eating meat.

Three years had passed since he started boxing, and his head was still a little rusty and unfocused, but it would be fine with more practice. The tempo—severity, speed, and slowness—each had its own rule. The speed could be slow or fast, the waist and legs were strong, and the moves were powerful and graceful.

Lu Gu heard a sound outside and looked through the crack of the window. He saw Shen Xuanqing punching and practicing martial arts. He was surprised, yet felt that this should be the case. He had seen Shen Xuanqing’s archery and hunting before; at that time he felt that Shen was different from ordinary men, a man who truly knew martial arts. He drew back a little and resumed his seat.

Shen Xuanqing looked up and saw Lu Gu standing at the kitchen door. A smile, expressionless as ever, spread across his face. After thinking for a moment, he said, “Practice boxing to keep fit. How about I teach you a few moves? It’s warm enough even if you wake up early.”

Lu Gu hadn’t expected Shen Xuanqing to teach him boxing just because he watched him practice for a bit. He was dazed, and Shen Xuanqing—who had nothing to do—dragged him into the yard to teach him.

“Wrong, this is how you form a fist.” Shen Xuanqing grinned as he guided Lu Gu’s smaller hand.

Lu Gu’s punches were limp, and his feet were unstable; clearly he wasn’t cut out for martial arts, and he was past the best age to start, but if he really stuck with it, his body might improve.

“Why are you teaching Guzi this?” Wei Lanxiang appeared as soon as she stepped into the yard, watching their boxing practice. She did not approve in her heart—how could her good son practice boxing like a rough fellow?

Hearing this, Shen Xuanqing replied, “Mother, boxing can strengthen the body. It helps you move your body and warm up, so why not practice? It isn’t a bad thing.”

Wei Lanxiang still felt it inappropriate, so she glanced at her son and said, “When you talk a lot, you think everything you say is justified.”

Lu Gu had no idea what she suspected; he knew it must be Shen Xuanqing’s instigation. Reading and writing were straightforward matters, quiet and serious, but no one would teach their fulangs how to punch and wield a knife. That was something only wild boys did. If the villagers saw, they would laugh behind their backs.

Shen Xuanqing didn’t reply to the rebuke. Living together like this, when one’s thoughts differed on some things, scolding would inevitably follow.

Seeing Lu Gu anxious, he looked at Wei Lanxiang helplessly and said, “Mother, I didn’t ask him to go out and spar with others; I’m only teaching him a few punches and kicks so he can move his hands and feet in the yard. This boxing method is warmer than stomping his feet around.”

Usually, Shen Xuanqing would bow his head and keep silent when scolded, but he would still do what needed to be done, and she couldn’t control it.

Today, it was rare for him to give her an explanation so softly. Wei Lanxiang took another look at him and said, “Well, don’t go out to practice. Stay at home; it’s not good for people to see.”

Lu Gu was pressed down by Shen Xuanqing into a horse stance and couldn’t escape. He whispered, “Mother, the pancakes in the pot should be hot, and the water is hot too.”

“Okay, you do your work first.” Wei Lanxiang replied, and went into the kitchen to scoop up water to wash.

After she entered the kitchen, Shen Xuanqing whispered in Lu Gu’s ear, “It’s all right—Mother didn’t scold you, it was me. Don’t be afraid, just practice your skills.”

The warm breath brushed his ear, and Shen Xuanqing’s voice—lower and deeper than usual—made Lu Gu lower his eyes in a hurry, and he let out a soft “huh” with his ears turning red.

His ears were plainly red; Shen Xuanqing didn’t mind at first when he saw this, but when he realized there was no one else in the yard, he pinched Lu Gu’s earlobes with his hands. They were soft, and the ears became even redder under his touch. He couldn’t help but smile.

In broad daylight, still in the house, Lu Gu felt extremely embarrassed as Shen Xuanqing pinched his ears. When he heard Shen Xuanqing’s voice again, he panicked and tried to push the mischievous hands away, his face flushed with a bright blush, and he stammered, “You—you… I’m going to wash the beans.”

Shen Xuanqing looked at him with a smile lingering in his eyes, his hands still behind his back, his face serious, but his fingertips rubbing Lu Gu’s earlobe as if he still longed for that touch.
