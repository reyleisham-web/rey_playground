Chapter 122 Part 12


Hearing her, Granny Wu craned her neck to look, her aging face etched with regret. She had thought the area had already been picked clean and hadn’t searched carefully enough.

“Granny Wu, here you go,” Shen Yan said, stepping over and pouring the wheat grains she’d gathered into Granny Wu’s handkerchief.

“This… this is too kind of you,” Granny Wu said, her dry lips quivering slightly.

Shen Yan brushed off the dirt from her hands. “It’s nothing. Just take it.”

Their family had also known poverty, and Shen Yan understood what it meant to go hungry. Every grain of wheat or rice was precious. Now that their own family was better off and had plenty to eat, giving away a few grains they had found wasn’t a big deal.

“Qiuyue!” Shen Yaoqing called from a distance, having just returned from the fields. It was time to go home for dinner. He didn’t want his wife to go hungry.

Lu Gu turned around and saw Shen Xuanqing, thinking to himself that he would have to wash his pants later, as they were covered in mud. The weather had been hot lately, so they would dry after hanging them overnight.

The next morning, after waking, Lu Gu roughly packed the clothes he would take up the mountain the following day. The morning was cool, and he overheard Shen Yaoqing and Ji Qiuyue outside discussing a prenatal checkup.

Nearby villages relied on Doctor Wang for medical treatment. Since recovering from his head injury last year, and because Doctor Wang was from Anjia Village, there had been no illnesses or emergencies. Lu Gu hadn’t stepped foot back in the Lu family home even once.

It wasn’t that he was cold-hearted or uncaring. Sometimes, when he was alone on the mountain, he would think of home, his childhood home. But then he would remember how Lu Daxiang had ignored him that day, letting Du Hehua slander and frame him, and his heart grew cold. He didn’t dare let his thoughts wander anymore.

“Are you done packing?” Shen Xuanqing came in from outside.

“I’ve just folded a few clothes. I can finish the rest tonight,” Lu Gu said, placing the bundle on the chest. He added, “We’ve been staying at home for a while now. I wonder how the vegetables at the mountain house are doing.”

Shen Xuanqing replied, “It’s cooler up there, so they may not have dried up yet. We can water them and see if they survive. Tomorrow we’ll bring some vegetables from home. They’ll last us a few days.”

He glanced at Lu Gu, hesitating for a moment before speaking up. “Let’s go with Sister-in-law and Big Brother for a walk.”

Lu Gu looked puzzled. Why would they tag along when Sister-in-law was going for a checkup?

“Today, Meng Dayue is slaughtering a pig. We can buy some pork on the way and bring it when we go up the mountain. We can render some lard for cooking,” Shen Xuanqing explained with a smile.

Realizing that he had already planned to go out for a walk and buy some meat, Lu Gu nodded happily. Besides, he could listen in on Ji Qiuyue’s checkup. After all, someday it might be his turn.

The thought made him feel a hint of embarrassment. There was no guarantee of that yet, so why was he thinking about checkups? Just then, Shen Yan called from outside, saying she was going to the new house to feed the dogs and asking if he wanted to join. This interrupted his thoughts.

Shen Yaoqing slept at the new house at night. When he came over for dinner, he locked the gate, leaving the four dogs at home. Since Lu Gu would be going to Anjia Village with them, Shen Yan went alone with a basket.

On the way, Ji Qiuyue walked slowly, Lu Gu staying by her side. Shen Xuanqing and Shen Yaoqing followed a few steps behind, chatting and laughing as they made their way to Doctor Wang’s house.

Entering the village and not seeing any of the Lu family members, Lu Gu breathed a sigh of relief. Some people were better left unseen, as they only brought discomfort.

“The pregnancy is stable. No need for medicine, just make sure her diet is nutritious. But remember, don’t overeat,” Doctor Wang said after examining Ji Qiuyue, who looked healthy and well-nourished.

Shen Yaoqing was overjoyed to hear that everything was stable. Stability, after all, was good.

After checking Ji Qiuyue’s pulse, Doctor Wang glanced at Lu Gu. Being from the same village, he was pleased to see Lu Gu looking well. Life was truly unpredictable.

Shen Xuanqing pushed Lu Gu to the table and made him sit down without hesitation. “Doctor Wang, could you check Lu Gu too? See if his weak constitution has improved.”

“Of course,” Doctor Wang nodded, guiding Lu Gu to place his hand on the pulse pillow.

“At eleven or twelve years old, the body is still growing. Missing those crucial years makes it hard to recover,” Doctor Wang sighed after finishing the examination, stroking his beard.

Noting Shen Xuanqing’s furrowed brows, as if displeased, Doctor Wang remembered that last year, to ensure the Shen family treated Lu Gu well, he had said that even with a weak constitution, Lu Gu could still have children after proper rest and care.

Now, glancing at Ji Qiuyue, then at Lu Gu, who showed no signs of pregnancy, Doctor Wang felt a bit uneasy.

Having practiced medicine for decades, Doctor Wang could easily discern Shen Xuanqing’s true intention in having Lu Gu examined. He gave an awkward smile and watched Lu Gu’s complexion.

The red mark on Lu Gu’s forehead was neither too light nor too dark, no different from that of ordinary shuang’er. He should be capable of bearing children, but his internal weakness made it hard to conceive.

After a moment’s thought, Doctor Wang asked Lu Gu, “Are you eighteen this year?”

“Yes,” Lu Gu replied. He was younger than Shen Xuanqing and could be considered eighteen this year.

Doctor Wang stroked his beard thoughtfully and said to Shen Xuanqing, “His weak foundation has improved somewhat. His complexion is noticeably better. The red mark between Shuang’er’s eyebrows is the most accurate indicator. Judging by its color, he is definitely capable of bearing children. It’s just that his internal weakness requires further recuperation. You two are still young. Even in a couple of years, you’ll still be in your prime.”

Lu Gu, who hadn’t given much thought to the matter before, was taken aback but quickly understood that it wasn’t easy for him to conceive.

“Thank you, Doctor Wang. I understand,” Shen Xuanqing said. After a moment, he asked, “Should we get some medicine?”

“No need for medicine. He has no other issues. Just keep nourishing him. If you have eggs at home, have him eat one every day, and include some meat in his diet,” Doctor Wang waved him off.

Since no medicine was prescribed, the consultation was cheap. Shen Yaoqing paid four copper coins.

After leaving Anjia Village, Shen Xuanqing and Lu Gu walked behind the others. Shen Xuanqing spoke softly, “It’s all right. It’s good to recuperate a bit more. We haven’t been earning much lately. Once we sell more game and have more money, we can take better care of you.”

Lu Gu felt a squeeze of his hand, and his unsettled heart steadied. He understood that Doctor Wang had implied he might have difficulty conceiving. No one wanted to marry a shuang’er who couldn’t bear children, especially since he wasn’t even officially married into the Shen family.

Originally, a wave of fear and a strange, sorrowful ache had risen in him. But when he looked up and saw Shen Xuanqing’s smiling face, and his hand gripping Lu Gu’s tightly without a trace of disdain, Lu Gu’s heart ached anew, and his eyes grew misty.
