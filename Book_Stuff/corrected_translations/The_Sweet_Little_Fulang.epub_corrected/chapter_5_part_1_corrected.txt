Chapter 5 Part 1

Part 1

At dinner, Lu Gu did not dare to come out. After joining Ji Qiuyue for dinner, he realized that Wei Lanxiang hadn’t come out to eat, and the bowl of noodles that no one touched belonged to him.

Shen Xuanqing ate quickly, finishing in seconds. He set the bowl down and went back to his room first.

With only Lu Gu and Shen Yan left at the table, Shen Xuanqing’s presence leaving made Lu Gu breathe a little easier. He relaxed somewhat, but still did not dare move the dishes on the table. He lowered his head and ate only the noodles and broth, which was already a big improvement over what he had eaten before. It would be better.

Suddenly, a piece of pork liver appeared in his bowl. Lu Gu was stunned for a moment; when he turned his head, he saw Shen Yan still holding the chopsticks, not retracting her hand.

“It’s a lot. It’s okay for you to eat a few mouthfuls,” Shen Yan said, her almond-shaped eyes bright and clear, though she seemed a touch unfamiliar and cautious because she didn’t know him well.

Lu Gu was the same—timid and dull, hardly able to speak.

Fortunately, Shen Yan didn’t mind. After she finished her meal, she got up to clear the table.

Lu Gu hurriedly ate the meat and noodles in his bowl. He felt at a loss, and remorse gnawed at him. Why had he eaten so slowly? If Shen Xuanqing ever found out, he would suffer.

He forgot entirely that he had been late and others had eaten earlier than him, thinking only that if he didn’t work hard, he’d only anger the Shen family by eating slowly.

“I’ll wash it,” his voice was soft as he gently took the empty bowl from Shen Yan’s hand.

Back in the Lu family, it was often not his turn to eat, and he had to wash the dishes. Lu Wen had made it his business not to do it, and his half-brother Lu Wu, who had moved to another place, never let him slack off.

There was still a lot of food left tonight, and the angry Shen family didn’t have much appetite; they were full, content after filling their stomachs.

Shen Yan didn’t stop him. Moreover, seeing how pale and thin he was, and how frightened he seemed to be if she spoke too loudly, she didn’t dare to say more.

Ji Qiuyue, leftovers from the wedding banquet, had already put them away. Lu Gu placed the leftovers in the bamboo steamer. His eyes fell on the basin that hadn’t been poured out after washing the vegetables, and he whispered to Shen Yan who came after him, “Is this the basin?”

Seeing Shen Yan nod, he set the empty bowls and chopsticks in, squatted down, and picked up the loofah to start washing.

Shen Yan seemed curious about him, and she squatted down opposite him.

Lu Gu spoke little and was timid, but Shen Yan was still just a girl, and she bore no ill intention. She had even given him some meat to eat, so he was not so vigilant and nervous around Shen Yan.

He was slow-witted and afraid of saying the wrong thing, so he buried himself in his own helplessness.

Shen Yan tilted her head and looked at the bride before her. She thought Lu Gu wasn’t as handsome as Lu Wen, but his eyebrows and eyes weren’t bad, and he was simply too thin. What a decent person.

But when she thought of Lu Wen’s breaking off the engagement and Du Hehua’s scolding, she felt Lu Wen wasn’t all that good. If she were to meet Lu Wen in the future, she would never give him a kind look. How could there be such a person?

At that moment, Ji Qiuyue came in. She had eaten a few bites and went back to her room to rest. She heard no movement from the main room outside.

Seeing a wounded-looking person washing dishes, she wanted to speak with Shen Yan, but she was really tired today, and Shen Yan seemed frightened as well, so she stopped.

Moreover, she could also sense Lu Gu’s embarrassment. If he didn’t do some work, he wouldn’t know where to put his hands and feet. Washing the dishes wasn’t a big job.

She picked up another wooden basin from the side, scooped clean water, and placed Lu Gu’s washed bowls into it to rinse.

With a little help, Lu Gu finished washing quickly. Just as he was about to drain the dirty water, Ji Qiuyue grabbed it first. He had nothing to do, so he stood in the kitchen, staring blankly for a moment.

Ji Qiuyue tucked the empty basin away and said to him, “You’re tired today. Just scoop some water to wash and go to sleep.”

Lu Gu and Shen Yan closed the courtyard door and went to wash in the courtyard together. Wiping her face and turning her head, Shen Yan saw Lu Gu’s pitiful, cramped look and pointed to her own basin, saying, “How about you use mine?”

After speaking, she remembered there was a new wooden basin for the newcomer in the second brother’s room. But she thought perhaps it should be for Lu Wen.

Although the second brother had said he bought Lu Gu, she really didn’t know what identity Lu Gu held at home.

“Forget it. You can use this,” Shen Yan replied. She couldn’t quite understand the matter, and a hint of sadness crossed her round face.

There were many trivial matters in getting married. The Shen family rose at dawn to work, and in the afternoon they went to Anjia Village to quarrel and fight with others. It would not be an exaggeration to say they were physically and mentally exhausted.
