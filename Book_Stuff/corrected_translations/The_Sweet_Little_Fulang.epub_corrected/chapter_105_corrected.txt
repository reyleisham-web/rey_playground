Chapter 105


Li Wanyun wiped away her tears and, picking up a bamboo basket from the ground, said: "I haven’t caught the thief yet, so I can’t say who the thief is. It’s just that I really can’t live this way any longer. I have no land, and no vegetable field, so I can only dig wild greens. But if a thief steals today, what if he steals again tomorrow when I’m out? Sooner or later this house will be emptied."

What she said was reasonable. A person could be a thief for a thousand days, but no one could guard against a thief for a thousand days. She was a widow with no one at home. No support, no money, yet others still trampled on her and bullied her.

"Alas." Wei Lanxiang let out a sigh. In this kind of situation Li Wanyun was all on her own. Fortunately Quanzi’s family next door was decent. If it had been others, perhaps they would bully her because they lived so close.

"Right now I don’t have a copper coin in my hands. I don’t have much rice or flour left at home. I only have a few chickens and ducks left in the backyard. I’m afraid the thief will steal my eggs and meat before I can get to them." Li Wanyun didn’t cry, but her face had looked pale for a while now.

She looked at Wei Lanxiang, Aunt Miao, and the other fulangs in the village, and said, "Grandma, Aunt, I’m not afraid of your jokes. I have no money to eat or drink. Borrowing things isn’t an option. I can’t pay you back. If anyone wants to buy the chickens and ducks in the backyard at a low price, so I can buy rice and flour to eat in the future. Otherwise, I won’t even be able to keep the chickens and ducks, and the thief will take advantage."

"Even if you sell the chickens and ducks for money, if the thief comes and steals it again later, then the money will be gone." Lin Jinhu, who lived in the middle of the village, also came over and joined the talk. He arrived late and couldn’t help but speak upon hearing this: "Even if you carry the money with you, what will you do if the thief comes to steal it at night?"

Before he finished speaking, his wife pinched the flesh on his arm to silence him. Li Wanyun was a widow. If a thief came at night, how could he just steal the money? What if he also stole the owner of the house—wouldn’t that sully her reputation?

After being pinched, Lin Jinhu realized he shouldn’t say such nonsense, and he shut up at once. But the words had already been spoken. Li Wanyun pursed her lips and said after a moment: "No, I’m going back to my parents’ house. I don’t have a cart at home, so it’s hard to bring the chickens and ducks. But if I can change them into money and go back, I can bring some rice and flour to my parents’ family. I can’t just come over and eat plain rice." [Eat plain rice: to eat for free; to sponge off others.]

"That’s not bad. The first seven days are over, and the rest isn’t urgent." Shen Xuanqing spoke up, giving Li Wanyun a way out.

Sometimes people are like this; they need someone to lead the way. Li Wanyun’s life had become difficult after her house had been robbed, and it wasn’t unusual for a woman to return to her parents’ house with her children for a few days.

Lu Gu had money in his hands, and Shen Xuanqing stood by to reassure. He was the first to ask Li Wanyun how she wanted to sell the chickens and ducks. If the chickens and ducks could lay eggs, they could sometimes be sold for twenty copper coins. The eggs could be sold as well, and in the future if the old hens and ducks could no longer lay eggs, they could be sold. No matter what, they wouldn’t lose money.

"Not many left—there are two ducks, a rooster, and four hens. If you want them all, the rooster will be given to you. A hen and a duck are ten copper coins each." Since it was Lu Gu who spoke, Li Wanyun gritted her teeth and lowered the price to the lowest possible, not mentioning even fifteen copper coins.

There were seven in total, which really wasn’t much. Lu Gu looked up at Shen Xuanqing and felt relieved when he saw him nod.

"Okay, then we’ll buy them all." Wei Lanxiang also said, and she glanced at Lu Gu.

"Mom, I’m going to get the money." Lu Gu understood what she meant. Since he was the one who wanted to buy them, there was no reason to let others in the family pay for it.

The transaction went smoothly. Lu Gu was the first to speak up, so even though others were greedy for such a cheap deal, there was nothing they could do. It wasn’t that no one wanted to spend an extra copper to break the deal; even if the price for a laying hen were twelve copper coins, it still would have been a good deal. But they remembered how Shen Xuanqing had punched the courtyard door, and with a man of such tall stature, others were unwilling to clash with his family, and Li Wanyun had already agreed.

By the time Lu Gu came over, the villagers had dispersed. It was getting dark, and they still had to wash dishes after dinner.

"This..." Li Wanyun roughly counted the copper coins, and there were seventy copper coins. According to what she just said, the rooster was given to Lu Gu for free, so sixty copper coins would be enough.

"You must keep it." Lu Gu didn’t say anything else; he simply asked, "When will you return to your parents’ home?"

Li Wanyun tucked the copper coins neatly into her arms, her eyes full of gratitude. "It’s late today; I’ll go tomorrow during the day."

In this case, Lu Gu nodded and said nothing more. He caught the two ducks on the ground and the remaining five chickens. Shen Xuanqing and Wei Lanxiang also helped him catch them. They called the puppy, and the three of them went home.

Li Wanyun looked at them and opened her mouth, wanting to give all the bamboo baskets at home to Lu Gu, but after thinking it over again, she finally gave up the idea and lowered her head to clean up the yard. Even if she would never come back in the future, she couldn’t give away all her belongings. She had to carry on with her life; otherwise people would grow suspicious.
