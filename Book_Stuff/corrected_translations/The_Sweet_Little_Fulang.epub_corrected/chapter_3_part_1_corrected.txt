Chapter 3 Part 1


Chapter 3

Anjia Village was not far from the river, and a red figure on the bank suddenly sprang into the turbulent water without hesitation. The river rose to his nose and mouth; Lu Gu did not even have the notion of struggling. He was tossed and torn by the whirling current, his head filled with murk and foam.

His eyes blurred. The wedding dress he wore—much heavier than the light summer clothes he usually wore—dragged him down as it soaked. The fabric grew heavy, and his light, helpless soul seemed to drift away, hanging in the air with no destination. His heart sank.

If he died, he wouldn’t have to endure the beating anymore.

He drifted calmly downstream; thoughts faded away. Yet a faint, lingering wish stirred—if he must die, at least he should leave behind a body with a coffin. His stepmother wouldn’t pay to give him one.

Inside the yard, the quarrel raged on. Bao Zhiru, furious, shouted, “Stop, stop for me!” As soon as he finished, someone cried out that Lu Gu had jumped into the river, and Bao Zhiru quickly ordered, “Hurry, save him!”

Life hung in the balance, and the fighters ceased. Du Hehua rose from the ground and spat blood at Wei Lanxiang’s feet. Half of her cheek was swollen, and her mouth was still full of blood. She cursed vaguely, dirt smearing her face, while no one else looked much better amid the chaos.

Wei Lanxiang, unable to vent her anger, was slapped again and kicked with her foot. Du Hehua dodged.

Bao Zhiru prepared to go out with the others, but as he turned back he shouted, “You will get beaten and scolded!”

Du Hehua had wanted to spit again, but she swallowed her contempt at the words.

From outside, voices rose—people rushing toward the river. It was Shuang’er who had fallen in; the men from Qingxi Village did not go to join in the fray.

Normally Du Hehua would have gone to see if others went, but she did not move. She stayed where she was, her hair a tangled mess tucked up.

Lu Daxiang did not go either. He clutched at his waist and ribs, hissing in pain, his body writhing with ache. He glared at Shen Xuanqing—not because he didn’t know him, but because this son of a bitch had the worst hand in all of it, and even picked him up to beat him. He swore under his breath, yet dared not speak aloud.

In the yard, there was always some firewood piled up. Ji Qiuyue helped Wei Lanxiang and two other women to sit on the pile to rest and compose themselves.

Shen Yan, guarded by them after the fight, looked understandably frightened. The wooden stick she held had fallen to the ground with a thud. During the melee she cried and called for her mother, even taking the opportunity to strike Du Hehua a few times.

But now Shen Yan’s apricot eyes were wide with fear. She was the youngest in the family and usually well-behaved. Even Ji Qiuyue would pinch her round face to tease her when there was nothing else to do. She’d never witnessed such a battle before.

Ji Qiuyue felt a little frightened when her sister-in-law regained her senses, so she patted Shen Yan’s arm to calm her, then, looking at Du Hehua, taunted, “People say Lu Wen is well-bred, but Lu Gu is not. The supposed virtue wasn’t there—he’s not a mother, and his stepmother didn’t panic when he jumped into the river. It turns out all the good things in the past were just a front, and behind it all lies a black heart.”

Before marrying into the Lu family, they hadn’t inquired much. They’d only seen Lu Gu a few times at the Lu family’s home. They’d been told he went to his uncle’s house to play, and so they didn’t take it to heart because they were in Anjia Village, not dealing with him directly. When others spoke of Du Hehua, they said she was good to Lu Gu. As for the occasional beatings and scoldings, who in the village didn’t whip their children from time to time?

Besides, whenever Du Hehua struck Lu Gu, if someone saw or heard, they would say Lu Gu had broken a bowl or caused some other misdeed, and she would be quick to swing her hand.

A farmer’s family was no better than any town or county family; there were few things that couldn’t be ruined.

Before today, the Shen family had thought the Lu family was good, but they never imagined it could be like this.

Not to mention at the river.

The turbulent stretch by the bank was empty, but downstream there were several places where people were washing clothes, and the red color of the water stood out starkly.

There were shouts from Anjia Village—someone had jumped into the river. Soon Lu Gu was rescued from the water by two Shuang’er who knew how to swim. He gulped down a mouthful of water; fortunately, he was pulled out in time and his life was no longer in danger.

A girl picked up the red-embroidered shoes downstream and carried them over.

A crowd gathered along the shore. Lu Gu stood there, wearing a red dress that was soaked and dripping water. The clothes were in decent condition, so the onlookers kept their distance rather than gawking openly.

“Why are you shaking like this?” Lu Gu’s body was freezing, and even Shuang’er who had rescued him looked puzzled. It was summer; the sun hadn’t set yet—falling into the river could not possibly have frozen him.

Feeling his forehead and hands, they were indeed icy. Someone shouted, “Come back, light a fire, and change his clothes.”

The gate to the Lu family’s yard stood open; the people from Qingxi Village remained inside. Du Hehua and Lu Daxiang wanted to chase after them but could not overpower them, so they endured.

When the eight Shuang’er and the other girls brought Lu Gu back, laying him down in the courtyard, they all rushed to change his clothing.

Du Hehua rolled her eyes and, with half her face swollen, cried, “There is nothing left at home. All his clothes were given to him as a dowry; where can I find clothes now?”

“Either they’ve said we’re bullying him, or if we don’t take advantage of our Lu Gu, how could he have jumped into the river?” someone argued.

Wei Lanxiang sat down without standing, then picked up a piece of wood from the ground and struck Du Hehua.

Ji Qiuyue added, “Yes, outsiders don’t dare say such things. Your family is different from others. You can smear someone’s innocence with empty words and force the decent Shuang’er to this point.”

Lu Gu’s face turned pale. He opened his mouth as if to explain, his white lips moving, but no sound escaped.

Du Hehua’s words made a girl unable to listen any longer. “Auntie, we don’t understand and we won’t listen to what you say. Look at Lu Gu’s pale face—if he doesn’t change into clean clothes, he’ll likely fall ill.”

“Yes, Auntie. No matter whose clothes are in the house, you can fetch them and dress him,” others agreed.

These Shuang’er and the girls were young, their speech clear and quick, and they surrounded their kin. Du Hehua could not sustain another outburst, so she pinched her nose and muttered, “The woodshed door is open.”

“How can someone lie in the firewood room?” someone casually suggested.

Du Hehua was dissatisfied and snapped, “Why can’t he lie down? He’s soaked through; who will wash the dirty bedding when he’s carried into the room?”

She’d likely worn clean clothes most times for reputation, but today the Shen family had caused such a scene that she felt she’d lost face long ago; enduring the beating only added to her anger.

So, except for Lu Gu, everyone looked to Lu Daxiang, who without waiting for a response, said impatiently, “Alright, let’s get him inside first.”

Thus, the trembling Lu Gu was led into the woodshed.

Seeing this, Bao Zhiru motioned to the men in the courtyard to come out. The men from Anjia Village and Qingxi Village followed, even Lu Daxiang, and no one objected.

Shen Xuanqing lagged behind; once all the men had come out, he remained silent, and he closed the courtyard gate from the outside.
