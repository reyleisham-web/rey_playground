Chapter 102 Part 24


Shen Xuanqing wasn’t short of a bite of meat, but after Lin Shouyi called him, he still shouldered the shovel and walked to the south slope with Shen Yaoqing. As he went, he thought Li Wanyun had a pretty sharp mind. Since all the people were dead, and Lao Zhang had treated her poorly in life, why should she spend money to set up three days of banquets for nothing?

On the other side, when Fourth Uncle Zhang heard that Lao Zhang was to be buried today, his plan to force Li Wanyun to host a flowing-water banquet would fail, so he colluded with Zhang Gui and Zhang Hongye and came to the Zhang family.

[liú shuǐ xí: flowing water banquet, a banquet where guests arrive at different times and are served as they come]
“You have to bury people right after they die, and you don’t even follow the rites for guiding the departed soul. What is this? Li Wanyun, you are unfilial!” Fourth Uncle Zhang found a new excuse to suppress Li Wanyun and shouted as soon as he stepped in.

But he had not counted on Lin Shouyi being there today. He spoke sternly: “Fourth child, I’m the one who let them dig the grave. Do you have a problem with that?”

Fourth Uncle Zhang had been hopping around for days, drawing public ire. The villagers whispered about him behind his back, calling him immoral, and he liked to sneak around to stay out of the spotlight. Lin Shouyi’s father was Lin Zhongcai, so he was almost certain to become the next village chief. He had always been gentle in the village, but now he looked angry and did not look kindly on Fourth Uncle Zhang.

Who didn’t know that Fourth Uncle Zhang had come for money and Zhang family stuff? Lin Shouyi looked from him to Li Wanyun. Comparing the two, a man couldn’t match a woman’s wit, and his disgust grew.

They were all poor folk, mud-streaked hands digging in the soil. It wasn’t hard to spare a widow a livelihood, so why force her to die?

The moment Lin Shouyi spoke, Fourth Uncle Zhang fell silent. After he returned yesterday, he’d spent most of the night thinking. Even if he couldn’t get the money, he planned to extend the banquet and have Li Wanyun hold the feast for ten days.

If any family from across the land could hold a death banquet like this, they’d be praised by everyone. If Li Wanyun refused to spend so much money, she’d be called unfilial, and Zhang family money wouldn’t fall into outsiders’ hands.

What he hadn’t anticipated was that Lao Zhang would be buried today. Once this man was buried, he wouldn’t get a single thing.

This crew of hard-hearted people pressed in again. Li Wanyun’s heart seethed with hatred as she resolved not to let them take a copper coin.

Fortunately Lin Shouyi was here today, and many women and fulangs from the village arrived, so she didn’t have to act as crazy as she did yesterday.

No matter how much the Zhang kinfolk fussed, what Lin Shouyi had approved couldn’t be changed. Besides, those young strong men had already gone to dig the grave on the south slope. They had even slaughtered chickens for the men digging the grave. He could never take back his words—that would be a slap in the face. If something like this happened, how could he be village chief in the future? So he gave Fourth Uncle Zhang and the others a lesson.

When the banquet started at noon, a dozen men who had returned from the south slope after digging the grave finally had something to eat.

It was funny when it was time for them to sit at the table. Two tables of meat were meant for the men who had dug the grave. Yet as soon as Fourth Uncle Zhang caught sight of the stewed chicken on the table, he pelted toward it, vying for a seat.

Most of the men who went to dig the grave were young. Some came to help out of pity for Li Wanyun, some fought for the meat, and everyone knew who had helped dig the grave with them. Suddenly Fourth Uncle Zhang, who hadn’t helped at all, slipped in to eat with them. The men who had dug the grave couldn’t bear it and started scolding him, then kicked Fourth Uncle Zhang out of the table.

More than a dozen men joined in. Fourth Uncle Zhang wanted to curse, but seeing the scene, he tucked his chin and sidestepped.

The Zhang family kinfolk were really annoying, squeezing villagers’ seats as though this money belonged to their clan. They insisted outsiders shouldn’t eat with them, and several women and fulangs argued with them.

Li Wanyun didn’t go out to eat; she could only watch those “good relatives”’ faces, but she couldn’t drive them away. As long as today ended, they would be quiet once the old woman was buried.

In some villages, people would hire a suona to play after elders’ deaths, but Li Wanyun didn’t. When the banquet ended, the coffin was carried to the south slope in a desolate manner.

Back at home, Lu Gu and Shen Yan were pasting red paper on the windows. They heard the sound of the coffin being carried by and craned their necks to watch. They spotted Shen Xuanqing’s figure in the crowd, but he passed by without so much as glancing at them.

They finished placing the red paper on the pasted areas. Ji Qiuyue was pregnant, and two Zhang family members had died in succession, so they couldn’t leave the house unbound.

The hens in the yard clucked loudly, several of them clucking in unison. Shen Yan heard them, but with glue on both hands, she said, “Brother Guzi, why don’t you check the backyard? I’ll finish here.”

Winter was too cold and hens laid few eggs. When spring came, the weather would warm, and the eggs would gradually pick up. Eggs were valuable, so whenever they heard any racket, they had to go collect them right away; it would be a pity if they missed the chance and the eggs were smashed.

“Okay.” Lu Gu took the egg basket and headed to the yard. It didn’t matter to him. He figured it was an old hen laying eggs, but he never expected it to be one of his own little hens.
