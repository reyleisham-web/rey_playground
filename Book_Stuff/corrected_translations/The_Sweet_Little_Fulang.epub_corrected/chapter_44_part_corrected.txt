Chapter 44


Lu Wen sat by the window, frowning in thought. The Shen family had humiliated him in the street, and he was furious, determined to find a way to suppress them. At worst, he would make Shen Xuanqing unable to move his prey in town, even if he couldn’t interfere for a long time.

But Li Mingshan hadn’t been seen for the past few days. The servant girl said she hadn’t seen the young master when she returned to Li’s house, and the maid said he had gone to find his classmates to write poems and compete.

Lu Wen wasn’t suspicious of trouble, after all, he didn’t know anything.

Just as he was thinking about asking the maid to go back, there was movement in the front yard. Lu Wen quietly used the secret door; only Li Mingshan came through the front door. This was the yard he owned. The front gate was usually locked, so no one could find Lu Wen here. Lu Wen lived here, and whenever he wanted to reach him, Li Mingshan would sometimes come in through the secret door. Li Mingshan’s classmate’s elder brother and Li Mingshan were classmates, so it was easy to come and go under the guise of a classmate.

Sometimes he wanted to stay with Lu Wen longer, but he felt that if others knew, it would become a weakness others could exploit. If Li Mingshan walked in through the main entrance of the courtyard, his classmates wouldn’t know.

Lu Wen greeted him from the room and saw the handsome Li Lang with a shy smile, and said, “You’re here.”

Li Mingshan, after drinking, still carried the scent of powder on his body. He was drunk now, and seeing Lu Wen—so beautiful and delicate—made him swallow his saliva and step forward, not even thinking about the aftertaste of the fragrance.

“Have you been drinking?” Lu Wen asked with concern, noticing the flushed cheeks. Drinking wasn’t a big deal, so he asked with care.

It was only after asking that, before hearing Li Mingshan’s words, that as soon as he drew near, Lu Wen smelled the powdery fragrance on his body and his face showed hesitation.

Yet Li Mingshan didn’t know this. He had read two poems praising Lu Wen’s beauty—the kind Lu Wen liked best—so he must be shy.

Lu Wen had planned to make him a bowl of hangover tea, but he stopped talking. The scent of cosmetics grew stronger, and finally he could not help but ask with reddened eyes, “Why is there a scent of powder on your body? Could it be there is someone else?”

He was about to cry, and the eye rims turned red; Li Mingshan immediately wanted to wipe his tears away, desperate to hide the scent from himself and stammer for words.

Lu Wen’s heart fluttered, and he asked again, “Or you went to drink flower wine?”

Li Mingshan’s heart surged, and he lied with practiced ease: “Why would you say that? I am devoted to you—don’t you know? I won’t hide anything from you. Today they dragged me to a brothel for a drink. I couldn’t help it, so I had to go.”

Hearing this, Lu Wen was stunned, tears like a broken thread. Li Mingshan hurriedly continued, “They poured me wine and brought prostitutes to serve me. I couldn’t refuse. I drank a few glasses, but I don’t want to do such dirty things, I pushed people away and ran, not knowing what to do on the way, so I came to find you.”

“Really?” Lu Wen was relieved; it was broad daylight now, and Li Mingshan had come to find him, so he believed him with most of his heart.

“Really, if it isn’t true, thunder would strike me…” Before Li Mingshan could finish, Lu Wen placed a hand over his mouth.

“Don’t say that. I believe you, or else you’ll break my heart.” Lu Wen’s tears ceased, and he looked at Li Mingshan with wholehearted affection.

With the beauty muffling his mouth and the fragrance clinging to his delicate hands, Li Mingshan’s desire rose. He had been with Lu Wen for so long that Lu Wen was a treasure to him. Bu Zicheng was blind to Lu Wen’s worth; he had once traded a prostitute for Lu Wen like some prize. Yet Li Mingshan had never touched him, and he wondered how to proceed without losing him.

The more he thought about it, the angrier he grew, but Bu Zicheng wasn’t around, and now that the beauty stood before him, he wouldn’t be distracted.

The impulse of the brothel rose again, and Li Mingshan, still pretending to be noble, spoke: “You don’t know—there’s something about the wine in that place that makes people feel ill afterward. I was uncomfortable too. They forced a prostitute on me to relieve myself, but I didn’t want to. I pushed them away and ran, not knowing what to do after, so I came to you.”

“Lu Wen, please be understanding and patient with me.” Li Mingshan couldn’t help begging; he even swore that he would never betray Lu Wen, that he would go to the Lu family to propose marriage and make Lu Wen the Li family’s fulang, and he would be Lu Wen’s ger.

Hearing Li Mingshan vow such loyalty—that he would become the Li family’s fulang for Lu Wen’s sake—Lu Wen felt both ashamed and elated, as if this life were complete with a loyal confidant in Li Lang.

After making the promise and taking an oath, Li Mingshan half-coaxed the blushing Lu Wen into the room.
