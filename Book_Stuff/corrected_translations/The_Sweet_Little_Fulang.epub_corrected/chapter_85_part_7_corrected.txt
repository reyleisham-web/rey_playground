Chapter 85 Part 7


After dinner, Lu Gu washed the dishes. Shen Yan brought in a jujube cake and shared half with him. The cake had been made for Ji Qiuyue; Shen Yan, after taking a bite, was greedy, but fearing Wei Lanxiang would say she was too gluttonous, she claimed that Brother Guzi wanted a bite as well and brought over a small piece.

Lu Gu couldn’t hold the cake with his hands, so Shen Yan stuffed the cake into his mouth directly. The jujube cake was soft and sweet, with a rich jujube fragrance. This cake was better than pears, and there was no need to fear keeping it in cold weather. Eating winter pears was too gritty, and they could only be eaten after being boiled in brown sugar water or plain water.

“I’ll boil the water.” Shen Yan was eating the cake, and when Lu Gu finished scooping the water and wiped the pot clean, he went outside to fetch firewood to boil more water for washing and soaking his feet at night.

Lu Gu went outside the door to wash the pot for boiling water. Before he could go back to the kitchen, he spotted a tall figure in the distance. Shen Xuanqing had returned, followed by Dahui and the village puppy.

When Shen Xuanqing drew nearer, he held the pot and smiled slightly, saying, “Have you eaten? I’ll warm up the meal for you.”

Shen Xuanqing smiled too, and the two of them walked inside after he said “um.”

As soon as he entered and saw Wei Lanxiang, Shen Xuanqing said, “Mother, the official seal is stamped.”

“It’s good.” Wei Lanxiang felt more at ease. Although it was Shen Xuanqing’s land, they did not separate the family—they lived together. In the future, when Shen Xuanqing went up the mountain, Shen Yaoqing would do the work in the fields, and she would help as well. The more land they owned and the more rice they would harvest, the better their lives would be.

Since he had bought the land by himself, Shen Xuanqing kept the deed, and he went into his room to put it away. After a while, Lu Gu came in as well.

“I’ve put the bamboo steamer on the pot; Yanyan is still boiling the water, and when it boils it will be hot.” After not seeing each other for a day, Lu Gu was still trying to find something to say, even he himself didn’t know why. Now he also called Yanyan, imitating Ji Qiuyue. Because Shen Yan was young, usually at home they would call her by her first name and surname or with a nickname; Wei Lanxiang would either call her by her first name directly, or call her little girl.

“The deed, you keep it.” Shen Xuanqing handed the deed to Lu Gu. Now that Lu Gu had read many characters, he opened the land deed and saw that there was a red official seal on it; the thin paper was exactly the deed to their family’s five acres of land.

“And this.” Shen Xuanqing drew a red envelope from his bosom, which contained something. Lu Gu looked up and saw a silver bracelet in the opened red envelope. He was a little panicked, and couldn’t tell who Shen Xuanqing had bought it for. How could an ordinary person buy two pieces of silver jewelry in a short period of time, and besides, the silver hairpin was still new and had never been worn, so Lu Gu didn’t think that the bracelet was for himself.

“You wear it; the hairpin is easy to fall off, but this one won’t fall off.” Seeing him in a daze, Shen Xuanqing took his left hand, put the silver bracelet on his wrist, and said, “Wear the hairpin for the New Year; if you don’t know how to comb your hair, you can ask your sister-in-law or mother—they both know how to do it.”

The family’s new clothes for the New Year were about to be made, and Shen Xuanqing knew this; he was happy to let his fulang wear new clothes and hairpins.

Lu Gu’s hands were clenched together, and Shen Xuanqing put the silver bracelet on him with a little effort. It was not too big or too small, and it fit just right. The shiny silver bracelet bore exquisite patterns. For the first time wearing a bracelet, Lu Gu felt the weight on his wrist, giving him a subtle sense of presence.

“The thin one is for Shen Yan, and the thick one is for my mother. Both of them will have something to wear during the New Year.”

Shen Xuanqing pulled out two more silver bracelets and said, “In the future, if there is something good, I will buy it for you, or if you like any jewelry, just tell me.”

Lu Gu’s mind was simple: Shen Xuanqing bought him a hairpin and a silver bracelet because he valued him; unable to say more, he replied with a soft “um.” For a moment, he couldn’t help but tug at the corner of Shen Xuanqing’s clothes, looked up into those starry eyes, then moved away in embarrassment.

Shen Xuanqing looked at him with a smile. Although the two were silent, they both felt happy.

After a while, Shen Yan shouted from the kitchen, “Second brother, the meal is hot.”

“Okay, I’m here,” Shen Xuanqing replied.

Lu Gu loosened the corner of his clothes, blushed, and said, “I’ll get it.” After speaking, he hurried to the kitchen.

“Mother, I bought this for you—another bracelet for the New Year.” When Shen Xuanqing came out and saw Wei Lanxiang, he took out the silver bracelet.

Wei Lanxiang was rubbing hide on a dustpan. It would be the twelfth lunar month in a few days. Whether it was five beans or eight beans, you had to cook beans and peanuts. She looked up and saw Shen Xuanqing had bought her a silver bracelet. She wiped her hands on her clothes and asked with a smile, “Is this expensive?”

“You don’t have to worry about it; just wear it.” Seeing that she was happy, Shen Xuanqing showed her the silver bracelet intended for Shen Yan. It was a little thinner, but just right for her young age.

Wei Lanxiang had once owned a lot of silver jewelry, but they were all sold later. It just so happened that Shen Yan came into the main room, and when she heard that a bracelet was for her, her almond-shaped eyes widened in delight, and Shen Xuanqing couldn’t help but laugh at her expression.

“Second brother, is this really for me?” she asked, taking the bracelet.

“Of course.” Shen Xuanqing smiled and asked, “Who is better, big brother or second brother?”

“Second brother,” Shen Yan replied without thinking, but Shen Yaoqing, who came out of a room, immediately grabbed her pigtails and teased with a smile, “Good Shen Yan—yesterday you said big brother is good, and I gave you the fish.”

Shen Yan protected her braids. Yesterday she had eaten the fresh fish caught by Brother Daqing, and Brother Erqing bought her a bracelet today, so she was very torn in her heart. After some hesitation, she carefully asked, “Then are Big Brother and Second Brother both good?”

Wei Lanxiang, feeling sorry for her daughter’s braids, raised her hand to stop Shen Yaoqing from pulling Shen Yan’s hair, scolding him with a smile, “Don’t pull her hair anymore.”

Only then did Shen Yaoqing stop, smiling. He knew he didn’t earn as much money as his second brother, but he wasn’t jealous either. Everyone has their own destiny. Besides, he himself could work in the fields and earn no less than others in the village. Now, even if he didn’t have much money and couldn’t buy bracelets for his sister and mother, he could still make them happy by buying headbands.

Shen Yan wore the bracelet beautifully, and she did not find the two elder brothers annoying at all.
