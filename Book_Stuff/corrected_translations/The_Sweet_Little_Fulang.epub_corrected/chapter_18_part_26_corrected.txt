Chapter 18 Part 26


Although today was not a festival, since the debts had been paid off, the whole family was happy, and it was Lu Gu’s first time going to the mountains with Shen Xuanqing, so he should eat more carefully.

Wei Lanxiang and Lu Gu were sewing at the eaves. Seeing that Shen Xuanqing had nothing else to do, she found him another task. She held the sole of his shoe and said, “Go fetch a bowl of mushrooms, then kill that pheasant. When that’s done, go to your third uncle’s house, pick a winter melon, and today we’ll have winter melon stewed chicken.”

They went to work separately, and when the orange sunset flooded the sky, they gathered around the table and ate happily.

Stir-fried black mushrooms with garlic slices, mixed with tofu and a pot of stewed chicken—these three dishes made a hearty, homemade meal, with steamed buns enough for six people.

Lu Gu no longer trembled with fear as he had before; he dared to pick up a bit of food with his chopsticks and eat.

The chicken soup was especially fresh. After the chicken was scooped out, Ji Qiuyue brought over a few bowls, rationed the rest of the broth, and finished it, otherwise it would be a pity to waste.

The whole family ate well, and as cleaning up completed, it grew late. The farmers followed the custom of working from sunrise to sunset, returning to their homes to rest without lighting lamps.

Wei Lanxiang still couldn’t sleep, so before night completely fell, she came to help Lu Gu pack his clothes.

“It’s cold in the mountains, especially at night, so remember to cover up with more quilts.” Seeing Lu Gu’s three pieces of clothing and two pairs of shoes, she paused, then said, “Your clothes are thin and won’t keep you warm. I’ve brought a jacket for you.”

“Mother.” Lu Gu’s eyes grew timid, and after hearing her words, he didn’t quite know what to say, finally calling her “mother” in a soft voice.

Wei Lanxiang saw his obedience and could not help but smile, then hurriedly added, “I’ll set up a lamp for you and bring it tomorrow morning.”

Before she went out, she spoke to Shen Xuanqing: “Go to town to buy some cotton and thick cloth, and then make him two thick jackets to wear warm.”

“Mother, use this to buy, and then make more quilts,” Shen Xuanqing understood what she meant, and from his sleeve he pulled out a few pieces of broken silver.

The door was open, and the two of them did not lower their voices to avoid anyone else. The rest of the family heard everything.

Wei Lanxiang accepted it with a smile; it was only for Lu Gu’s clothes, and Erqing paid for it, so she could tell the boss.

When Shen Yaoqing heard her talk about changing the jacket, she lit a lamp in the room, and the dim candlelight reflected on the half-open window, swaying gently with the breeze.

Wei Lanxiang pulled out a jacket that had already been washed from the bottom of a box, though it was a bit small for Lu Gu’s frame. It was cold in the mountains, so he shouldn’t wear something this thin to keep out the chill. This one wasn’t particularly thick; the advantage was that the chest and back were filled with cotton, and it even had two sleeves, so his arms wouldn’t be left exposed to the cold.

Shen Yan didn’t even ask about making a thicker coat. She had gone to the mountains once, and it could get cold at night, especially in autumn when there was more rain, so it would be even colder. Lu Gu went to cook and do laundry. Brother Erqing could eat delicious hot food, so hunting would be more exciting, right?

Even she knew it—Ji Qiuyue knew as well—that the clothes that needed to be made had to be made; besides, it took some hardship in the mountains, and travel took a long time, so she naturally didn’t object.

In the room, Lu Gu was steadying the sack, and Shen Xuanqing tucked the new quilt into the dowry box. He had two quilts up in the mountains for a long time. The two sheets could be washed as if they were new.

He was busy hunting, and he didn’t even know how to remove and wash the bedding. At most, he would take advantage of sunny weather to dry it, and if it got too dirty, he would carry it down the mountain and bring it back for Wei Lanxiang to wash.

Fortunately the scooter was large enough, and Shen Xuanqing was young and strong. He was only eighteen, and when he was very strong, hauling more than two hundred catties was no problem; but the road required more effort than usual.

In the dead of night, the lights were off, the noise had faded, and the moon moved steadily westward across the sky.

A few stars twinkled, and it was almost halfway through the night. Lu Gu, who hadn’t slept well, woke when he sensed Shen Xuanqing’s movements.

They had cleaned up everything that needed cleaning from the previous night. The two of them woke, washed their faces with cold water, rinsed their mouths with green salt, and by the time they were on the road, they could eat steamed buns without worrying about whether their mouths were clean.

“Erqing, Guzi, are you leaving now?” Wei Lanxiang called through the window. She thought for a moment and added, “I’ll send you off.”

Dahu had been released, and after Shen Xuanqing finished speaking, Lu Gu shouldered his bag, loaded the egg basket, and together they pushed the cart out.

Shen Yaoqing sent the two of them out. Lu Gu walked for a while and then turned back; there was no one at the door. In the night, it seemed that only he and Shen Xuanqing remained.

The cart squeaked softly; Dahu followed Lu Gu, and the two puppies that had been tied to the backyard for days were released. They ran forward, sniffing everywhere, only stopping when they were far ahead, turning their heads to wait for Shen Xuanqing to catch up.

Gradually reaching the slope up the mountain, Shen Xuanqing reversed the cart, the wheels now at the rear. He looped the cloth rope between the two handlebars over his shoulders so he could use his own strength to pull the cart up. It was time to exert effort, so they kept moving forward; otherwise, it would be difficult to push it up with both hands.

Lu Gu stayed behind the cart, because he was holding the egg basket and couldn’t help push for fear of breaking it, so he simply watched to keep everything intact.

Shen Xuanqing was used to heavy work and could pull it alone, so the two of them continued up the mountain together.
