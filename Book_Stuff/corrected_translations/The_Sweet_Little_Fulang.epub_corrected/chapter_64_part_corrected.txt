Chapter 64


The smoke was curling up, the overcast sky was not very warm, and a breeze blew from time to time. Shen Xuanqing couldn’t stay long enough to eat, and Zhou Xiangjun and Shen Yu stayed a while before leaving.

Shen Xuanqing returned late, missing lunch, and there was no dinner yet for the rest of the Shen family, so he was the only one eating. Lu Gu scrambled eggs for him. In the morning, Shen Yaoqing had bought a piece of meat from Meng Dayue in the neighboring village, cut some meat, and fried it with qiucai. The qiucai grew greener and tastier when fried in the meat oil. The scent of the meat drew the puppy to wander under the table, rubbing against Shen Xuanqing’s calf. While Wei Lanxiang wasn’t looking, Shen Xuanqing tossed him a piece, and the puppy, after eating, wanted more but was ignored.

“Guaizi, come here.” Lu Gu cleaned the pot, but the water in it hadn’t been emptied yet. After Shen Xuanqing finished eating, he would have to wash the dishes. The bottom of the stove was still warm, and the water in the pot was still hot. Seeing the puppy persistently begging as if he hadn’t heard, he seized one of the puppy’s front paws and dragged him away, away from Shen Xuanqing.

“Do you want to eat?” Shen Xuanqing asked when the dog came over.

Lu Gu shook his head and said, “No, you eat. We’ve already eaten.”

The puppy somehow slipped to Shen Xuanqing’s side again, tilted his head, barked, and squatted down, looking at Shen Xuanqing eagerly.

With everyone in the family present, Lu Gu felt embarrassed to sit at the table and eat with Shen Xuanqing, so he left after a short pause. He picked up some firewood and carried it into the kitchen.

Late autumn was cool, and it grew dark earlier. After washing up, the Shen family went back to their rooms. A candle burned in the room, and Lu Gu and Shen Xuanqing soaked their feet together. He sat on the edge of the bed, Shen Xuanqing pulled a chair to sit opposite him, and they placed mugwort leaves in the tub. The water darkened, and the white feet disappeared beneath the surface. Occasionally, their feet brushed against each other, and Lu Gu lowered his head and didn’t dare to move again.

Drawing the bamboo basket on the side closer, Shen Xuanqing drew a money bag from under a thick hemp rope, opened it, and found many silver ingots inside. Seeing so much money, a smile flitted across his face as he raised his eyes to the dumbfounded Lu Gu and said, “Two hundred and sixteen taels in total, and those two pieces of silver fox fur have good color, and even the master agreed—this is a good deal. Fox furs are expensive and hard to buy in Fucheng, so they were sold at a high price, thirty taels per piece. The remaining ten red fox furs are smooth and thick, one for ten taels each, and the remaining seven are slightly inferior. One piece sold for eight taels.”

Fox fur was rare and looked good in winter; it was light and warm, and the rich liked it the most, so it could be sold for so much money. Not to mention the village, even Fenggu Town would consider two hundred taels a substantial fortune. Shen Xuanqing took two silver ingots from the purse and handed them to Lu Gu, saying with a smile, “The silver ingots of fifty taels are too big; I asked the manager to turn them into ten-tael ingots, and the remaining sixteen taels were scattered silver.” After all, Fenggu Town was a town, and even the large twenty-tael ingots weren’t used much, let alone fifty-tael ingots, which would be awkward to carry.

A ten-tael ingot wasn’t small. Lu Gu only felt that the two ingots in his hand were heavy. He had seen five-tael and one-tael silver ingots before, and the one-tael ingot was the most compact. He had thought that if he had a thousand copper coins, he could go to the town to exchange for one tael of silver.

“You take it; you can buy whatever you want to eat.” Shen Xuanqing was dumbfounded when he saw Lu Gu holding the silver and silent for a long time, and he couldn’t help laughing.

Unexpectedly, Lu Gu handed it back after listening; Shen Xuanqing was puzzled. “You, you should save it, twenty taels are too much.”

There was so much money that Lu Gu stammered, looked up, and said seriously, “It’s two hundred taels.”

Seeing that Shen Xuanqing didn’t answer, Lu Gu stretched out his hand to grab Shen Xuanqing’s hand, opened it, and placed two silver ingots into his palm.

“Well, you take this broken silver.” Shen Xuanqing smiled even more, remembering that there were still thirty-eight taels in the box, eight taels could be taken out for daily use, and the remaining two hundred and thirty taels could be saved. It was good.

“En.” Lu Gu agreed with a light nod, but in his heart he was thinking that he already had enough money to buy needles, thread, and silk, so why spend so much?

The next day remained cloudy, and the chickens and ducks had been on the mountain for several days. Lu Gu was a little worried, but now that the two of them were getting more familiar, Shen Xuanqing could sometimes guess what he was thinking.

After breakfast, Shen Xuanqing said to Wei Lanxiang, “Mother, we’ll pack up and go to the mountain later.”

“So fast.” Wei Lanxiang bustled to get ready, packed dried persimmons for them, and said, “I’ll give you two packs. You can eat them when you’re free. You can take some with you when you go hunting, and chew them when you’re hungry.”

“Mother, you take these as well. Later the family will buy meat to make cured meat, make new clothes for you and Shen Yan, and buy her a silk flower or something.” Shen Xuanqing said, and handed ten taels of silver to her.

Peddlers would pass through the village from time to time. Broken silver was better in the countryside; rural women couldn’t spend all ten-tael silver ingots at once.

When he went to Fucheng this time, he saw a girl about Shen Yan’s age wearing a small silk flower on her head. Shen Yan only wore a hair tie, and she usually wore it only when she went up the mountain; the flowers wilt within a day or two. She loved these flowers. He wanted to buy some for his younger sister, but he didn’t understand this, and since Lu Gu didn’t like flowers, he didn’t buy them.

“Okay, mother knows.” Wei Lanxiang smiled from ear to ear and carefully put the money into her arms. When she fell asleep last night, she had wanted to ask the second child how much the fox furs were worth this time.

For a countryman, ten taels of silver would take a year or two to save. After taking so much money, she could rest easier. Shen Xuanqing had his own plans for the future, and besides, he had his fulang.
