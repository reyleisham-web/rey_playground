Chapter 9 Part 1

Part 1

The thin black dog ran forward a few steps and jumped into the chicken pen. Lu Gu could almost feel a gust of wind rushing toward him.

He had never seen such a vicious dog; even the chickens were frightened. One of the stupid hens bumped into his leg, and Lu Gu, who was already feeling weak, grew softer still. After shaking a few times, his whole body trembled unconsciously, and he didn’t even notice that the egg had fallen to the ground.

Da Hui kept barking, but it was in vain to stop the dog.

At the critical moment, an angry shout suddenly rang out—Shen Xuanqing appeared in the narrow passage. With that shout, the dog in front of Lu Gu halted its advance.

The thin dog turned its head and saw Shen Xuanqing gripping a shovel against the wall, ready to strike. Its demeanor and posture changed instantly; it let out a few pitiful, weak whines and crouched in the corner with its tail tucked.

Seeing it flinch and dare not attack, Shen Xuanqing did not strike. He stood in front of Lu Gu, frowning as he checked whether Lu Gu had been bitten.

Da Hui, who had barked for a while, stopped. The noise had been loud enough for Wei Lanxiang in the front yard to hear. She wondered what was happening. Just as she was about to call out to ask, a fellow about her age appeared at the gate. He carried a deflated little oil-paper bag and a bundle of wild vegetables.

Lu Gu regained his senses, remembered the egg, and realized he had nothing in his hands. He panicked, and when he lowered his head, he saw the egg had been smashed on the ground.

He hadn’t cried when he was almost bitten, but seeing the broken egg made Lu Gu burst into tears. He didn’t cry often, and this time the tears came simply from fear. He flinched and looked up at Shen Xuanqing. Tears welled up in his eyes, choking back the sobs.

How could Shen Xuanqing not know his fear? If it had been Shen Yan crying, he would have some way to deal with it—he would fetch something delicious or entertaining, and she would stop. But Lu Gu didn’t know how to coax him.

The yolk and the white were scattered all over the ground, and there was no saving the egg. Shen Xuanqing shoveled dirt from the side and covered the broken egg. He also ground up the eggshell. Once the loess covered it, it would be impossible to tell where the egg had been.

Lu Gu’s tears kept flowing, and he was stunned when he saw this. He looked at Shen Xuanqing, tears welling again in his eyes.

“It’s broken. If you don’t tell her, I won’t tell her. My mother doesn’t have to know—just pretend it isn’t there.” After Lu Gu’s tears subsided, Shen Xuanqing glanced toward the corner of the wall again.

The black dog was stupid and unruly, not as good as Da Hui or the two skinny dogs. Because it hunted and bit too much, it had a violent temper that could only be tempered by him, but in the past few years it had been trained to hunt with him, which had brought many benefits.

Unexpectedly, the first time Lu Gu came to work in the backyard, its tether had loosened.

Now that he had just married, he had spent a lot of money. In the past two days he had to work hard and go into the mountains. It wasn’t worth killing the dog to raise another one, so he would put it aside for the time being.

Besides, the black dog hadn’t bitten Lu Gu. If it did bite, it would be killed regardless of whether it was worth it or not. There was a rule here: dogs that had tasted human blood could not stay; they would become addicted.

Shen Xuanqing dragged the black dog out of the chicken pen, pulling it by the rope and giving it a few whips.

Lu Gu wiped his tears, steadied himself, and continued to look for eggs, not daring to be careless. He flipped over the chicken pens and found four in total. If one hadn’t been broken, there would have been five. Lu Gu carried the basket and was about to close the door of the chicken pen when Shen Xuanqing stopped him.

“I’ll close it after I clean it up. You go back to the decoction first.” Shen Xuanqing fetched the broom. He had originally come to the backyard to sweep the pens; in summer it was hot, so he had to clean up every two or three days. That was why Lu Gu hadn’t been bitten by the dog.

In the front yard, Wei Lanxiang was chatting with Zhou Xiangjun and sighing over the bad news. When Lu Gu came out, she took the basket for a look. There were only four eggs; the hens hadn’t laid well, though a few were still okay.

“This is your third uncle’s wife. In the future, let Erqing take you there to recognize the door. He also has a young couple, so you can be together even if there isn’t much.” Wei Lanxiang said as she walked toward the kitchen. After putting away the eggs, she took a piece of meat and two fish from the urn where the cured bacon was kept.

“Aunt.” Lu Gu spoke softly from the courtyard.

Zhou Xiangjun smiled, pursed his lips, and looked a little shy.

“Xiangjun, take this back and cook it for the third child and Yu Ge’er.” Wei Lanxiang handed over the meat and fish. Although the fish weren’t rare, these two had already been killed and marinated; they were ready to cook, so there wouldn’t be much trouble back home.

Zhou Xiangjun smiled awkwardly but did not refuse, saying, “Second sister-in-law’s kindness is much appreciated.”

There was work to be done at home, and Wei Lanxiang didn’t keep him any longer. “It’s getting late—go back and do it quickly. It’ll be ready in time for lunch.”

Zhou Xiangjun’s husband, Shen Shunwang, was the third uncle of the Shen brothers, and even if he didn’t come today, Wei Lanxiang would still ask Ji Qiuyue to send something to help keep the house comfortable against the heat.
