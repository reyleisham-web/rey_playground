Chapter 53 Part 5


Wild vegetables and fruits were not worth much, but Lu Gu still brought half a basket of mountain persimmons to sell. He picked only the hard persimmons, afraid they would be damaged on the road. He bought them back and left them to ripen for a few days before they softened.

The dwarf deer was led by Shen Xuanqing. On one side of the cart was the prey, and on the other side were Lu Gu’s mountain goods. Both of them stood behind the cart and began to sell.

Lu Gu, who had come to town many times with Shen Xuanqing, looked a bit timid, but he could say what he had to say, and now he was not afraid of talking to people; he could even respond to the bargainers, though he didn’t notice how much his demeanor had changed.

The market was crowded with people, and rabbits and pheasants sold best. They weren’t expensive for those in the town who had a little money. People bought one or two and went back for a “tooth-beating sacrifice,” but the dwarf deer was different. A whole deer stood here; ordinary people couldn’t afford it, and even if someone bought it to keep for a while, they wouldn’t be able to finish it.

Shen Xuanqing didn’t rush the customers who bought the dwarf deer, and he wasn’t in a hurry. When everything else was sold out, he went to the medical shop to sell Lu Gu’s basket of medlars, and then asked the price of the bamboo mushrooms.

The old doctor at the medical center overheard them asking about the bamboo mushrooms. This food was delicious; it could be used for stewing soup as well as medicinal dishes. It was more precious than ordinary mushrooms, and few people sold it. When a person was alive, he would have a little bit of appetite, so he asked Shen Xuanqing if he had brought any.

Seeing that there were both fresh and dried bamboo mushrooms in the basket, the old doctor twirled his long beard and offered a good price. Shen Xuanqing nodded, and Lu Gu would come here to sell goji berries after picking them; it was a good deal, and the price was really fair.

Such a small amount of bamboo mushrooms sold for three taels. Lu Gu’s eyes widened. Although he had sold a large basket of goji berries for a lot of silver, bamboo mushrooms were rare, and it was truly precious to sell them for three taels.

After the deal, Lu Gu pulled the cart; there was only a little wild soapberries left on the cart, which looked almost empty. Shen Xuanqing led the dwarf deer, and the two walked toward Fulai Restaurant.

The Qingxi River flowed through Fenggu Town, and there was a bustling wharf to the west of the town. Shen Xuanqing had often come here to work in the past two years and knew that there were several restaurants here. When shipowners and merchants came, they would naturally eat and stay in restaurants and inns, so this place was very lively.

Fulai Restaurant was the largest restaurant in Fenggu Town. It was a short distance from the wharf, but not far. Most of the rich and powerful were housed and fed here.

There were many people coming to the wharf—those who worked, bought and sold goods, and sold food and drink. It was almost noon, and Fulai Restaurant was crowded.

Shen Xuanqing stopped beside the restaurant, not blocking the door or disturbing business, letting Lu Gu hold the dwarf deer, and went inside to be questioned by himself. Lu Gu held the hemp rope tightly, fearing that the dwarf deer would bolt, waited for a while, and then saw Shen Xuanqing coming out, followed by a middle-aged man who was the accountant of Fulai Restaurant.

“Let’s talk in the back kitchen,” the accountant said, glancing at the dwarf deer before turning into the alley after speaking.

Shen Xuanqing led the dwarf deer and followed closely, with Lu Gu behind him, the cart in tow. Not far into the alley, a small door opened, and the bustle of cooking and serving could be heard inside.

The accountant asked directly, “How much do you sell this dwarf deer for?”

“Forty taels,” Shen Xuanqing replied.

“Forty taels?” The accountant frowned. “That’s too expensive. It’s not a sika deer, nor is there a lot of edible meat. Besides, after skinning and removing the internal organs, there isn’t much edible meat. What about twenty taels?”

The price was cut in half, which was a bit harsh. Shen Xuanqing also frowned and said, “The price is too low. Don’t look at the meat; it’s good for stewing, and now that autumn is cold, the deer is nourishing and can strengthen the body. Anyone who knows how to eat it will love it. The meat isn’t excellent, but its skin and bones are valuable, so I won’t say anything else. How about thirty taels?”

After some negotiating, Shen Xuanqing didn’t want to sell it too cheaply, so he finally sold the dwarf deer for twenty-eight taels, and the accountant had to pay twenty-eight taels. It felt auspicious to give up two taels, as if stepping back a little.

The dwarf deer was not more expensive than a sika deer; not to mention male and female sika deer with long horns, even if they were forty taels, there would still be buyers. But twenty-eight taels was not bad for this deal.

It was twenty-eight taels of silver, not twenty-eight copper coins. Even though Lu Gu had seen so much money when Shen Xuanqing counted the silver in the room before, he couldn’t hide his astonishment now.

Taking the money in one hand and handing the deer over with the other, Shen Xuanqing tucked the money into his clothes, bid the accountant farewell with a kind smile, then pulled the cart and walked out of the alley. Lu Gu followed him closely.

After leaving the alley, Shen Xuanqing’s smile widened, and he said, “Brother Daqing is unloading the wood at the pier. Let’s go take a look and grab a bite on the way. There’s a stewed-meat shop over there; pork offal is good. If you have the mixed stew soup, the broth can be made spicy, and you can sweat in winter, so you should try it too.”

After working through most of the morning and shouting for work, they needed to eat, and Lu Gu nodded in agreement.

Shen Yaoqing was working with others to carry the wood onto a big boat. When he heard Shen Xuanqing’s voice, he looked over and laughed, “Wait up; it’ll be over soon.”

The place was a bit crowded, so Shen Xuanqing took Lu Gu to eat the miscellaneous stew soup first. When Shen Yaoqing came over with sweat on his forehead, he also ordered a bowl for himself.

When the two brothers had worked here two years ago, this bowl of miscellaneous stew soup had been the most delicious. Unfortunately, they had no money then and could only watch others eat; they didn’t dare look too intently and ate cold steamed buns instead. Today they could eat it with full stomachs, and there was more pig offal in the soup.

Lu Gu did not dare to eat too spicy, so Shen Xuanqing added an extra portion of pork offal for him, and the baked white cake was delicious, though a bit expensive.

After taking a break, Shen Xuanqing left with Lu Gu. Shen Yaoqing had to wait to see if there was any other work; the crop field was not busy yet, and it would be good to earn more coins outside.

They walked from the pier to the north of the town, turned the corner, and headed toward the brothel. There were few people going in during the day, and most ordinary people passed by just to look at it. As timid as Lu Gu, after knowing what this building was for, he did not dare to gaze too long, simply keeping his eyes forward.

Shen Xuanqing pulled the cart and did not go to see the brothel. He lowered his eyes slightly, remembering that he still wanted to see Luo Biao, but it was impossible right now. He needed to find an excuse to come to town later.

He was not a whining person; he had already figured out an excuse to find Luo Biao early the next morning, but Lu Gu, who stayed at home, knew nothing about it.
