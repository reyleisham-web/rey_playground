Chapter 36 Part 27


Wei Lanxiang asked them to take the dates up the mountain to eat. If they couldn’t finish them, they could dry some in the sun. The dried dates would also help Lu Gu replenish his energy and blood. Following Langzhong’s advice, the dried dates he had bought had already been eaten. Fortunately, the Shen family didn’t mistreat guests at meals; they often ate meat and fish, and Lu Gu had no other problems. He ate and drank well, slept well, and his body naturally grew stronger than before.

Shen Yan returned with a bundle of grass, but Dahui wasn’t tied after he went down the mountain, so he followed her when she went out and also entered the courtyard gate. Seeing Wei Lanxiang and Lu Gu back, she spoke, “Mother, I just met Caixia. She came down from the mountain and said their family went up to pick chestnuts and hazelnuts, and Caixia alone carried more than half of a basket.”

After autumn, when chestnuts and hazelnuts ripen and fall to the ground, not only people would gather them, but mice and squirrels in the mountains would also collect them for the winter. Several nearby villages would head up the mountain to collect them. When there were more people, everyone gathered; Caixia’s family, in particular, collected a lot, and Shen Yan couldn’t help but be surprised to hear of it.

Chestnuts and hazelnuts could be dried and eaten in winter. There were few vegetables in winter, and Shen Xuanqing wouldn’t go into the mountains after a heavy snow. The only meat was bacon and dried meat. The villagers could stock up more food for the winter.

Wei Lanxiang was plucking onions in the courtyard and preparing to make dinner When she heard this, she looked up and smiled, “What’s so unusual about that? Didn’t your second elder brother always pick it up for you in the deep forest? Or do you want to go pick chestnuts yourself?”

There were many people in Qianshan, but few things; Shen Xuanqing could gather a lot in the deep mountains. Chestnuts and hazelnuts could be collected by everyone, and they were sold cheaply in town. Ji Qiuyue would bring them to town when autumn came two years ago. Shen Yan went to collect them, selling more or less would mean money, not to mention repaying debts. At least it could maintain the family’s livelihood and serve as winter rations.

They not only gathered in front of the mountain but also went into the mountain with Shen Xuanqing, collecting more in the deep forest. Shen Yan rarely went there; she was only eleven this year, and the small mountain road was long to walk. When they came back, they had to carry bamboo baskets, so Wei Lanxiang would go with Ji Qiuyue, and Shen Yaoqing, who had to work in the fields and couldn’t go, would stay at home to cook.

Going to the mountain to pick chestnuts was interesting for Shen Yan. It was more fun than herding ducks or hunting chick grass. After selling them, the money was hers, and the family wouldn’t miss it. It didn’t matter if it was only five copper coins or ten; putting it into her own small money bag made a crackling joy that was very satisfying.

“Mother, do you want to go?” Shen Yan didn’t say that she really wanted to go, but her expression spoke volumes.

Wei Lanxiang, peeling onions, looked up and said with a smile, “If you want to go, you can talk about it. What are you waiting for?”

She thought for a moment and continued, “But it’s really good to pick chestnuts. We’ll keep more this year, and we’ll eat them differently in winter. In past years we steamed them with rice, but this time we’re tired of that, so let’s cook them with chicken. People say it’s more delicious, so let’s try it too.”

She walked toward the kitchen and added, “Your brother Erqing is resting at home these days. Don’t rush away. Let’s first go around the front hill.”

Shen Yan was delighted when she heard this and said to Lu Gu, “Brother Guzi, do you want to go or not? Let’s go together.”

When Lu Gu listened to the two of them talk, he remembered the chestnuts Shen Xuanqing had picked up for him last time, and there were still some unfinished chestnuts. In the mountains, before he went down the mountain, he’d wanted to pick more, but lately he had been busy with other things, so he nodded and agreed.

The three of them talked in the yard, and Shen Xuanqing came out after lying down in the room for a while, eating a few dates.

When Shen Yaoqing and Ji Qiuyue returned, it was already dark. They had done well; watching the sun slip toward the western horizon, there were still some fish left, so they carried the cart to the restaurant and told them what they had caught that day. Yes, even if they were dead, they were fresh; not to mention the half-dead ones in the bucket, which were even fresher, because the fish were big and fat, including crucian carp and river eel, and they sold them to a restaurant at a cheap price; as long as they were sold, they wouldn’t lose money.

When they came back, there were only two small river eels left in the cart, which were set aside and reserved. After autumn, these things would be stewed and nourishing. If you wanted to eat them, you had to make up for it now.

The money from selling the fish was given to Shen Xuanqing, and the copper coins plus the scattered silver added up to two taels of silver. Two taels in a day—worth the hardship of the journey.

There were stewed prawns and fried chili meat slices, both of which could be served with wine. Shen Yaoqing brought out the wine, and not only his brothers drank, but Ji Qiuyue and Wei Lanxiang also tasted a small cup. There was meat and wine, and the whole family chatted and laughed. Even if Lu Gu didn’t drink, the fried chili would make his cheeks blush and his body warm.

He listened and watched, even if he didn’t speak and simply listened to others; he was happy. A kind of hope gradually appeared in his eyes, as he looked forward to living such a happy and harmonious life forever.
