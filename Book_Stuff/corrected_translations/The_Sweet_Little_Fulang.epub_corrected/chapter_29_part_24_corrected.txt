Chapter 29 Part 24


They walked home. Shen Xuanqing thought for a moment, then asked Lu Gu in a quiet voice: “Did you buy wine because you want to drink?”

Lu Gu instinctively turned his head, glanced at him, then quickly lowered his eyes and denied softly: “No, I don’t drink.”

Shen Xuanqing was puzzled and asked: “Then you bought it for me?”

Lu Gu hesitated, and then whispered: “Aunt got up early and came over and said that they ate Honqiao. When I was picking mushrooms last time, I seemed to hear a mountain stream flowing; I should be able to look for green snails, soak them in the wine, you can eat them if you want, and if you can’t finish it, you can sell them.” The more he spoke, the softer his voice became, but Shen Xuanqing heard clearly that he was planning to cook him something delicious.

Lu Gu had seen him go out to drink earlier, and had brought a pheasant as a side dish. He also heard that Shen Xuanqing drank a lot of wine when he got married, so he knew that Shen Xuanqing could drink it. Although eating meat in the mountains wasn’t as bitter, Shen Xuanqing would be happier with a light appetizer.

Even if you didn’t drink, Hongqiao was so expensive in town; it would be good for Shen Xuanqing to try it out, and it wouldn’t cost him to cook it himself.

Shen Xuanqing was happy that his fulang was thinking about him, and he reassured Lu Gu: “Eat, definitely eat. I don’t drink too much wine—three or two glasses is enough, and I won’t get drunk.” Wei Lanxiang often told him to drink less, and didn’t imitate a drunk man to do things that would ruin his reputation. He had educated himself, and his rationality was a bit higher than that of a reckless man who hadn’t been educated. He also knew that alcohol could hurt the body; it was easy to make mistakes if you drank too much.

No matter which man in the village came home drunk, if his body reeked of alcohol, his wife and fulang would feel disgust when they saw him. Even if a man vomited all over, no one wanted to approach him, so he always remembered not to drink too much, and in the past year, when he drank with others he rarely got drunk—first, because he could really drink, and second, because he wouldn’t drink to the point of ruining himself, and mostly he drank until the drink was finished.

Seeing the smile on Shen Xuanqing's face, Lu Gu was relieved; it seemed that he had done nothing wrong, and he nodded in agreement after hearing it, indicating that he understood.

When the two of them went back, Wei Lanxiang was delighted to see her son smiling so much, and she kept looking at Lu Gu’s face, so she knew they were getting along well, and there was nothing to worry about.

Not long after the cock crowed, Qingxi Village gradually began to fill with people’s voices. Lu Gu and Shen Xuanqing woke up, had breakfast, and then pushed the cart into the mountain again. This time they didn’t go at night, and they could see the road under their feet more steadily. When Lu Gu reached the slope and turned his head, he saw Wei Lanxiang still standing at the door. He felt a heaviness in his chest, with an unspeakable sourness, but he also felt warm, though he couldn’t say it. He walked forward with his head down, lost in thought. If his mother were still there, he would surely have sent him away in the same way.
