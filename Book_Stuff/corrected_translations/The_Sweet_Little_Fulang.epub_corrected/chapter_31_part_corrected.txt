Chapter 31
It was just dawn, and there was movement in the house before the sun rose. The first thing Lu Gu did when he came out of his room was to check the chickens and ducks. Because it was cold at night, he kept them in the main room to sleep. He made two shallow flat baskets with bamboo strips and spread hay inside. Before going to bed, he had used two newly made small bamboo baskets to cover the grass nests of the chickens and ducks, poking a wooden stick at the mouth of the upside-down bamboo baskets to leave a gap.

Shen Xuanqing was too tired these days, and he hadn’t woken up yet. When Lu Gu woke up, he knew that sometimes it was like this when one woke early. Lu Gu first boiled water to heat the steamed buns and pancakes, then he dozed for a while. Removing the bamboo baskets, some chickens and ducks stirred, fluttering their wings and chirping twice. Seeing that they were all well, Lu Gu covered the bamboo baskets again and let them out after the sun rose.

He opened the two doors of the main room, and the cold rushed in. The three dogs sleeping in the corner of the room curled up even tighter. Dahui still glanced at him, but he didn’t seem to get up. The flames at the bottom of the stove rose, and some fine firewood was added to it, quickly making the room warm. Lu Gu lifted the lid and saw there were still about ten steamed buns. Shen Xuanqing had brought a few buns to the mountains as dry food.

He placed a rack in the pot and set four steamed buns on it to heat. After covering the pot, he hesitated for a moment, then picked up a bowl and scooped up about a bowl and a half of white flour from the bag. White flour didn’t weigh even thirty catties, so naturally he should eat sparingly. Since entering the mountains, he had only made scallion pancakes with soup twice, and he was reluctant to eat more. The water boiled quickly, and the steamed buns became soft and hot after a while. He used a bowl to cover the hot steamed buns, otherwise the heat would escape. Listening to the movement in the room again, Shen Xuanqing was awake. The sky in the east had just turned white, and it was still early.

Lu Gu scooped boiling water to mix with the flour, so the pancakes would cook faster, and he wouldn’t have to wait for the flour to rise before making them. Having been in the mountains with Shen Xuanqing for so many days, he was more proficient at cooking and frying vegetables than before. He kneaded the dough very neatly. Five small dough cakes came out quickly. The fire at the bottom of the pot hadn’t been extinguished yet. He placed the pancake dough on the pan and turned it over to bake.

Shen Xuanqing finally got up, and the three dogs followed him out. Dahui yawned, and the little dog stretched its four long legs in the yard, then arched its back. Shen Xuanqing was washing in the courtyard, and he wasn’t afraid of the cold, so he washed with cold water. Lu Gu had just washed as well; he picked up the pickles and brought out the four steamed buns on a plate. There were two bowls of water on the table in the main room, warm enough to drink now, and the two of them sat at the table to eat and drink.

Lu Gu ate sparingly in the morning—one steamed bun was enough—and the remaining three belonged to Shen Xuanqing.

“Want to look for the snails today?” Shen Xuanqing asked him, and after Lu Gu nodded, he said, “I will keep Dahui for you. Don’t go too far; it’s okay if you can’t find it.”

“Yes.” Lu Gu nodded in response.

He had been busy with fences and ponds for the past few days, and he hadn’t gone hunting in the mountains. Today he had to go out. Shen Xuanqing packed up after eating. Seeing that the small cloth bag that usually held dry food in the bamboo basket was shriveled, he went to the kitchen by himself and took the food.

Lu Gu was washing the pickle bowl when Shen Xuanqing came in to remove the lid of the pot, took six steamed buns with mixed flour, and stuffed them into the cloth bag. Shen Xuanqing packed them away before he could say a word. He stood up so anxiously that he didn’t even have time to wipe his wet hands. Shen Xuanqing walked out with the cloth bag, just in front of him. In a hurry, Lu Gu reached out to grab the corner of Shen Xuanqing’s clothes, and found that his hands were wet, leaving a damp mark on the corners of his clothes. He was frightened and cowardly, quickly letting go and saying anxiously, “You wait.”

Shen Xuanqing had to stop, and for the first time, Lu Gu held the corner of Shen Xuanqing’s clothes and approached timidly, which made him smile a little meaning. The cloth bag in Shen Xuanqing’s hand was taken away by Lu Gu; he didn’t know what to do, and he saw Lu Gu lift the lid of the pot and take out five white flour pancakes from it.

These pancakes were meant for Shen Xuanqing alone. He had bought chickens and ducklings for him and treated him so well. Embarrassed, he lowered his head and gave a short, shy smile, saying in a low voice, “Eat this for dry food in the mountains.” White flour was better than mixed flour, and since Shen Xuanqing had to work hard to hunt, he should eat better. After receiving the cloth bag, the white pancake inside was still hot, making Shen Xuanqing feel warm in his hands. Seeing Lu Gu smile so shyly, the string in his heart seemed to be gently plucked; his own little fulang thought about him so much that he didn’t know how to express that joy. His handsome face wore a slightly silly smile.

Lu Gu looked at him with such a smile that he grew more and more shy and didn’t dare to raise his head. Finally, he heard the dogs barking twice toward the courtyard gate, and Shen Xuanqing came back to his senses.

Lu Gu escorted him to the door, and he and Dahui watched him and the dog walk into the forest, then turned back to the yard to start a busy day once they could no longer see him.
