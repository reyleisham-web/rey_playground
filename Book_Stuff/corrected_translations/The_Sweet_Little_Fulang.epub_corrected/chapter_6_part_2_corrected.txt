Chapter 6 Part 2


Shen Yan used a long bamboo pole to drive a group of ducks out of the backyard. Seeing that Lu Gu had nothing to do, and there was no one to accompany the ducks, she asked, “Are you going?”

He had work to do at home, so it was natural for him to go along. Lu Gu followed Shen Yan out of the house in a hurry.

“The pond is not far, just behind the house.” Shen Yan said as they walked.

Qingxi Village was nestled against the mountains, and the Shen family was the last family in the village. The Qingxi River, which flowed out from the mountain, was only half a mile behind the house. When they walked behind the house, they could see the river at a glance, and there was a small pond too.

After driving the ducks into the pond, Shen Yan didn’t sit still. She searched for grass nearby that chickens liked to peck at, especially those with seeds. She chopped it up and mixed it with bran to feed the chickens; the birds looked cute as they pecked at it.

Seeing Lu Gu sway a little when he bent down and then stood up, she thought it might be the result of a head injury. She pointed to a white stone on the edge of the pond where she used to sit and said, “Sit here and don’t move. Take this bamboo pole and don’t let the ducks go away. Just let them swim, and I’ll go there to cut the grass.”

Lu Gu sat down. He didn’t dare to be lazy, but as soon as he crouched, his eyes darkened and his head whirled, so he had to wait a while.

In the distance, a few dogs began barking from the hillside, and he immediately remembered the three dogs that had followed Shen Xuanqing out earlier.

Sure enough, it was them.

As they walked toward the hillside, more trees grew thicker; Lu Gu could see Shen Yan’s figure, and two lean dogs rushed down from the slope, as if Shen Xuanqing was nearby.

Lu Gu didn’t dare to look over there any longer and instead watched the ducks swimming in the pond, searching for food.

Soon, Shen Yan came over with a bale of grass. Seeing that the ducks were all present, she said to Lu Gu, “Brother Lu Gu, Brother Erqing said the sun is getting hot, so you can go back.”

It was the first time Shen Yan had called him “brother.” Even Lu Gu’s expression was embarrassed. He didn’t say much, only asked, “What about the ducks?”

Shen Yan pointed to the side of the hill and said, “Just let Da Hui watch it.” Lu Gu followed her finger and, sure enough, the big wolf dog was walking toward the pond.

“Da Hui is so smart. Last time Brother Daqing’s jacket fell on the ground, it brought it home,” Shen Yan mused. She and Lu Gu had grown a bit more comfortable with each other, and their conversations began to drift toward family matters, especially since she had just met Shen Xuanqing a little while ago. Her brother had told her that Lu Gu was the newly wedded man, so his identity was naturally different, and she should talk about family matters as well.

As soon as the two of them entered the house, Ji Qiuyue looked up and said, “Just right—the medicine is ready to drink.”

After Lu Gu finished the medicine, he was pulled into the new room by Shen Yan. He knew this was Shen Xuanqing’s room, and he felt at a loss immediately; he didn’t know why.

“Second brother said to let you drink the medicine and sleep for a while, anyway, to recover from the injury.” Shen Yan saw that he was still wearing the shabby clothes that Du Hehua had given him yesterday, and she opened the box dowry to search for new clothes.

Farmers didn’t have many good things for dowries, and they usually kept Shuang’er’s clothes at their parents’ house, so the dowry looked full.

Back then, the Lu family had agreed to give a new quilt, but they weren’t fooled; otherwise, the box wouldn’t be too light, and the people who moved it from Qingxi Village would shout about it.

Shen Yan only found two pieces of Lu Gu’s clothes in the box, none of which were winter clothes. How she suspected that they had been deducted by Du Hehua.

“Are you going to change?” she asked Lu Gu. He nodded, and she said, “Then you can sleep here, or Brother Erqing will scold me when he comes back.”

Last night, Brother Erqing had come into the room earlier; she had believed that Lu Gu wouldn’t dare to go in, but she and Wei Lanxiang lived in the same room and couldn’t call Lu Gu. If it were cold, she might have given him an excuse, but it was still summer. Besides, she didn’t know what Shen Xuanqing meant yesterday, and her mother was angry again—how could she dare decide on her own?

After Shen Yan finished speaking, she went out and closed the door behind him.

Lu Gu hugged his clothes in a panic, unable to figure out why. If he had been braver, he might have guessed a little, but he wasn’t; he could only do what Shen Yan said, after all, it was what Shen Xuanqing meant.

When he was about to change, he glanced at the bed, which was covered with a red quilt. It looked clean and tidy, far from being soiled by him, so he washed his face and hands, and even his feet and calves, using water. There was no cloth towel here, so he had to sit in the courtyard to dry himself.

Fortunately, Shen Xuanqing and Shen Yaoqing were not there, and the sun was hot enough, so Lu Gu dried quickly.

The night before yesterday, when he was still in the Lu family, according to the pre-marriage rules, Du Hehua had reluctantly boiled a large pot of water for him to bathe in, rubbing his body all over until he was red, but also completely clean and light.

So when Lu Gu changed into clean clothes and lay down on the edge of the bed carefully, he wasn’t afraid of soiling his new quilt. He lay down and found the mattress underneath extremely soft, with a faint scent.

He didn’t know that in order to marry the new fulang, the Shen family had added boiled herb water to the water for washing the bed and the mattress. It smelled cleaner, and there was a lot of thought behind it.

Lu Gu hadn’t slept in such a good bed for a long time. In the Lu family, he and Lu Wen shared a room, and there was a broken wooden bed made of stone and mud. Du Hehua still often drove him to the firewood room to sleep.

Because of the injury on his forehead, he would feel dizzy from time to time, but he recovered quickly and didn’t think much of it. Now, as he lay down, his body and mind seemed to melt into the soft mattress, and he gradually became drowsy.

Lu Gu had originally only intended to lie down as Shen Xuanqing had ordered, so that Shen Yan wouldn’t be scolded. He hadn’t expected that he would fall asleep so deeply that he didn’t even notice Ji Qiuyue coming in to tuck him in with a thin quilt.
