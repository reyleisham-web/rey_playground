Chapter 2


“Auntie, it’s not me who told you this, but if you lie, you’ll be struck by lightning.” Ji Qiuyue, Shen Yaoqing’s wife, saw the old woman go pale and knew she was about to strike. “Who doesn’t know? His face has been arranged by the old woman—so is it possible Aunt doesn’t know?”

Wei Lanxiang continued to scold: “Are you mad? Let him wear Lu Wen’s clothes?” Then she demanded, “Where is Lu Wen? Bring him out so I can see how capable Lu Gu is, and if he’s promoted to be the new fulang, Lu Wen still doesn’t know?”

Ji Qiuyue echoed loudly: “Yes, bring Lu Wen out. Our family is married to Lu Wen, and Lu Gu will return him to you. Lu Wen must come back with us.”

“Let Lu Wen come out.” Two aunts, friends of Wei Lanxiang, pointed to the other side and shouted, “I have never seen Shuang’er get married and hide at home, replaced by someone else. Your Lu family’s trick is really something new.”

Du Hehua could not overpower them, and more voices joined in. Seeing the situation slipping out of control, she changed her expression, sat on the ground, and began to cry.

“You’re a fool; what sins have I committed that you would spread such an unconscionable thing…” She cried and flailed, not knowing who she was shouting at. She refused to say where Lu Wen was.

Most of the unreasonable village women were like this. Ji Qiuyue took it in stride and said, “Auntie, you won’t let Lu Wen come out, but I can go in and fetch him myself. It’s only right and proper for our new fulang to come back with us.”

Du Hehua turned a deaf ear, crying out “oooh, oooh,” ignoring the dust on her clothes, her wails growing louder and more horrible.

When Ji Qiuyue saw her like this, she suspected Lu Wen was probably not at Lu’s house, but she did not give up. She went in and searched in circles, and came out again quickly, not to mention Lu Wen, not even Lu Daxiang’s shadow.

“What happened?” 

Bao Zhiru, head of Anjia Village, arrived with several young, strong men following him. The villagers from Qingxi Village crowded the yard; Du Hehua saw the village chief and cried across the crowd: “They beat people; they beat me. If you don’t come, Third Uncle, I’ll be beaten to death by them.”

Bao Zhiru was already sixty-two, highly respected for his age. Because he was the third oldest, the villagers often called him the Third Uncle, a name that even people from neighboring villages used.

Wei Lanxiang glared at Du Hehua, took the marriage certificate when Bao Zhiru arrived, dried her tears, and cried, “Third Uncle, our family married Lu Wen—it's all written on this—but they’ve married Lu Gu here. Isn’t this bullying? I can’t stand this.”

Bao Zhiru recognized the words, held up the red paper to read it in the sun, then looked at Lu Gu, who wore a wedding dress, and frowned.

This was not easy to resolve; even people in Anjia Village knew the Lu family was at fault.

Bao Zhiru thought it through: the Shen family was in the right, and if this went to the county government, the magistrate would naturally want Lu Wen to go to the Shen family upon seeing the marriage certificate. So he said, “I tell you Hehua, since they have the marriage certificate to prove it, you can’t be mistaken.”

Hearing this, Du Hehua rose from the ground, crying and wiping her tears, “Third Uncle, you don’t understand—our Lu Gu is so big-hearted, but he has no idea. He doesn’t even have a betrothal proposal, and if this drags on, how can I ever meet his mother again? If it weren’t for him, how could I have given him such a good match?”

“I was confused for a moment, seeing him cry pitifully and softening my heart. I just nodded.”

“I thought whoever marries will live behind closed doors. Besides, it’s the same when the lights go out at night.” She said this with a sigh, as if giving in to fate.

Lu Gu stood there at a loss, shaking his head slightly—he hadn’t asked his stepmother for this marriage.

Wei Lanxiang, furious at Du Hehua’s nonsense, blurted out, “Damn you! Your Lu family fooling people and treating others like fools—that’s what’s happening here. I’m going to marry Lu Wen, so I paid a hefty dowry of 20 taels; otherwise, who will take advantage of whom?”

During the quarrel, Lu Gu dared not speak, but Shen Xuanqing—standing a few steps away—gave him a look. The tall man’s brows were furrowed and his face cold, and Lu Gu trembled, understanding that Shen Xuanqing suspected whether Lu Gu had asked his stepmother for this marriage.

Not only Shen Xuanqing, but others too stared at him in a certain way. When Lu Gu’s situation had already been awkward after arriving, now his stepmother’s accusation shattered his dignity in front of so many people, and there was nothing left to salvage.

“Mother, it’s obviously Brother Wen and the people from the town…” Lu Gu whispered, trying to defend himself.

Summer heat notwithstanding, Lu Gu seemed to shiver with cold. He did not raise his voice, but Du Hehua, who had been watching for him to speak nonsense, snapped back: “Oh, you shameless little brawl-raiser! Your wretched mother taught you nothing good? I raised you without a lack of food or clothing, and this is how you repay our brother Wen?”

The word “wild seed” made Lu Gu’s face drain of blood.

Du Hehua’s insults grew harsher, and even Wei Lanxiang could not bear it any longer. Just when she was about to rebuke, Shen Xuanqing spoke again.

“Enough!” He stepped forward, brows still furrowed, and spoke solemnly, “It was your Lu family that regretted the marriage first. Since Lu Wen doesn’t want to marry, I won’t force it. It’s just that the 20 taels of betrothal money cannot be kept by you without consequence.”

Wei Lanxiang pointed at Du Hehua and cried angrily, “Yes, and the betrothal money—every coin was earned by money and clothes given to Lu Wen, and yet the wedding day came and went. If you don’t marry but keep the money, your Lu family will have that to answer for. There’s no such thing in the world without the king’s law.”

“We can’t give the money to your family, and we can’t even see a shadow of a bride—so return the money!” Ji Qiuyue shot back, angrier by the moment.

Du Hehua’s face flushed when she heard “repayment”; twenty taels of silver sat in her hands, and to demand its return would ruin her.

“You were a shameless girl,” she shot back at Ji Qiuyue, pointing in her face and cursing.

Ji Qiuyue did not yield, furious, her breath coming in ragged gasps, but Shen Yaoqing hurried over to steady her and calm her down.

“Du Hehua, you—!” Wei Lanxiang’s hand trembled as she pointed at Du Hehua and berated her.

“Auntie, you can eat and drink as you please, but don’t talk nonsense.”

Shen Yaoqing supported his daughter-in-law and glared at Du Hehua, telling her to shut up.

Ji Qiuyue, never one to back down, snapped back: “This isn’t a prostitute. Aunt’s Shuang’er are the ones who’ve gotten mixed up with people in town. If Aunt is truly ashamed, she should hang herself now, or I’ll be forced to answer for your Lu family’s ancestors!”

The reputations of Shuang’er and the girl were sacred to them. Ji Qiuyue knew this, and she had agreed to marry, but now she regretted it and felt used by them. Du Hehua’s insult to her virtue was the last straw.

Hearing this, Du Hehua blushed furiously, shouting and stomping her feet: “You bastard, making false accusations about my brother Wen—watch me tear your mouth apart!”

She lunged to strike Ji Qiuyue, but Shen Yaoqing grabbed her arm and shoved her aside; she fell to the ground and burst into tears, crying she would die.

A group of young men from Anjia Village glared at the Shen faction and looked ready to fight.

Ji Qiuyue saw they were looking at Shen Yaoqing. If there were a real scuffle on their side’s territory, only the Shen family would suffer, so she sneered and said, “So much for your domination in Anjia Village. You ask for trouble and get beaten? I’ve seen the reputation of your Anjia Village—how many people do you want to stab in the back? I don’t believe there can be no justice in this world.”

Bao Zhiru, the chief, was torn. If the Lu family kept acting this way, it would ruin the reputation of Anjia Village. If the Qingxi Village people got provoked and fought back, the county government would be informed. He was not strong, but he would not tolerate ridicule.

He held out a hand to quiet the crowd and said, “Enough of this. It’s done and dusted. If you have something to say, say it calmly.”

“We can’t give the money back to them in vain,” Wei Lanxiang insisted, wiping her tears. Since it was the Lu family that regretted the marriage first, it made sense for the Shen family to demand the return of the betrothal money. With that, the matter should be settled. Bao Zhiru turned toward Du Hehua.

Before he could speak, she started howling again, sitting on the ground and slapping her thighs: “God damn it, I’ve had eight lifetimes of bad luck, and this is what happens in the morning. You didn’t send it back until now. Your Shen family says you are good people, but behind the scenes I fear someone has been scheming for a long time. How can I live if you take my money?”

Du Hehua cried and cried, not caring what she said, until she blurted out anything.

“Youself!” Wei Lanxiang roared, grabbing the stick Du Hehua had dropped. Never used to cow toward such things, she struck Du Hehua hard.

“It’s not good to beat people!”

Lu Daxiang’s younger brother, Lu Dahe, and his two sons had been watching from the outside. Their own Lu family did nothing for them, but when they saw the fight they came in and pushed a few middle-aged men who had good ties and joined in the melee.

Du Hehua’s loud voice faded as she was beaten, her cries now more pig-like than human. Lu Daxiang, who had been hiding nearby, suddenly appeared, returning from the fields, holding a hoe, and rushed into the courtyard to clash with the Qingxi villagers.

In a moment, the situation flipped. Bao Zhiru grew so angry his beard nearly stood on end. The few young men he had brought drew his anger into action and the two groups clashed.

The yard devolved into a tangle of bodies; Lu Dahe’s wife and daughter-in-law hurried in to witness; otherwise they would have watched Du Hehua being beaten and scolded by Wei Lanxiang, Ji Qiuyue, and the two village women.

Lu Gu lowered his head and kept out of the melee. Even if some knew Du Hehua had spoken nonsense, his own stepmother had said the same. What about his stepmother? Du Hehua had raised him for years, and now it was making him look like a fool.

A few hours later, a shout cut through the commotion: Lu Gu had jumped into the river.
