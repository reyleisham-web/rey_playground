Chapter 80 Part 14


Quanzi’s fulang stepped out from the next door, saw Wei Lanxiang holding a lantern, and said, “Aunt Lanxiang, why don’t you go in and have a look?”

“Your baby not sleeping?” Wei Lanxiang asked as she walked.

“No, he woke up to this big commotion and kept crying. I asked Quanzi to coax him.” Quanzi’s fulang was a chubby shuang’er. He was blunt, but his heart was good, and he had a good relationship with his neighbors, except Lao Zhang.

Quanzi’s parents were good people; because they lived next door to the Zhang family, they had more contact with each other. It was okay when Lao Zhangtou (Lao Zhang’s husband) was around, but since his death, no one could control Lao Zhang’s family.

Quanzi’s fulang couldn’t understand Lao Zhang’s villainous behavior—she beat Li Wanyun badly. She liked to take advantage of her in-laws’ kindness and love to plunder the family; if he hadn’t stopped and scolded her, many things would have been taken away.

Li Wanyun lit the candlestick with the lantern in Quanzi’s mother’s hand; Zhang Zhengzi lay motionless on the bed, breathing very weakly. Seeing the blood on the back of his head, Quanzi’s father was shocked. Lao Zhang was also carried into this bright room, and after a while, she slowly woke up among the crowd.

“Hurry up and invite langzhong, hurry up!” Lao Zhang cried as soon as she woke, but few people moved at her words.

Although the people from the Miao family came in, they stayed far away and watched from the outside. Aunt Miao was so angry with the Zhang family that when her husband wanted to lift someone up, she grabbed his arm and stopped him.

Lao Zhang had always been foul-mouthed, and she swore and cursed all day long. It would be counted as a mercy that these people could bring her and her son in, and there was no obligation to save her.

As for inviting a langzhong, it was still feasible during the day; the langzhong lived in Anjia Village, which was three villages away. How could it be so easy to walk on this snowy night? They couldn’t see clearly. What if they slipped and fell? What if they had bad luck and their legs were broken? If they fell halfway, they might cry out for help, but no one would pass by to rescue them, and they could lose their lives.

If it had happened in someone else’s house, a few young men would help fetch the langzhong, no need for words, but this was Lao Zhang—with a vicious mouth and heart, she could even sell her own daughter. If you helped her, perhaps you would earn a bad name in her mouth later. Such things had happened before.

When Wei Lanxiang came in, she saw Shen Xuanqing, who was the tallest, and she was relieved that he and Shen Yaoqing didn’t interfere, but seeing Lao Zhang and Zhang Zhengzi lying on the bed reminded her of Shen Shunfu, and she felt a little uncomfortable. It would be best to help, but it was Lao Zhang, alas.

In the end, it was Quanzi’s father who stepped forward with good intentions. He felt that he couldn’t leave them alone, so he gritted his teeth and said, “Then I will…”

“Father,” Quanzi’s fulang interrupted him from behind the crowd, and said loudly: “Father, you are old, your legs are bad, and the snow is slippery. Who will save you when you fall?”

“Quanzi is coaxing the baby at home. I usually cook and do the laundry. If you break a leg, who will take care of you? My mother is also getting old. In such a cold day, do you still want mother to take care of you?”

“It costs money to buy medicine, our family wants to raise a baby, how can we spend so much money, or are you willing to let your grandson eat bran and vegetables only in the future? If you have money, you can go, and I will never stop you.”

Quanzi’s fulang’s mouth was very sharp; he wasn’t afraid that his words would be unpleasant, and sometimes if he didn’t speak harshly, his parents-in-law would be confused. What he said made Quanzi’s father patted his head in embarrassment, and he didn’t dare to speak again.

The son didn’t move at all, and Lao Zhang was so resentful that she couldn’t be bothered to scold Quanzi’s fulang. She woke up when she heard the word “money”; she took out Zhang Zhengzi’s rough jade pendant in her hand, and said hastily, “This jade pendant is worth at least three hundred copper coins. If anyone goes, I will give it to him. I beg you to help me.”

Shen Xuanqing looked down on the rough jade pendant and kept silent, but he knew that someone would definitely go. Three hundred copper coins was a lot for others in the village, and it was enough to live on for a while. Some young people looked at each other, but they were afraid of walking on the snowy road in winter nights alone. The langzhong was also old, and the snowy road was not easy to walk; they still had to help him so that the langzhong wouldn’t have an accident on the road. It was better for the three young men to help each other; of course, they took the jade pendant in their hands before leaving, they couldn’t let Lao Zhang regret it later.

There were many people in the room. Seeing that there was nothing else to do, Shen Xuanqing and Shen Yuanqing found that Wei Lanxiang came over with a lantern and they thought about going back. No one wanted to get involved in the affairs of Zhang Zhengzi’s house, and none of them were langzhong, and they couldn’t help while staying here, so they went out one after another.

“Father, you can’t help with anything here, and Quanzi can’t coax the baby alone. You also know that your eldest grandson has to be hugged by several people when he cries. In this way, you go to find Musheng’s parents; at least they are Zhengzi’s second uncles, let them help, aunt Zhang also has someone to take care of her.”

Quanzi’s fulang was afraid of being taken advantage of by the Zhang family, and besides, it wasn’t that the Zhang family had no relatives from the same clan in the village. If there was anything, Lao Zhang could discuss it with their relatives, why should they meddle here?

“Sure, I’ll tell them.” Quanzi’s father, knowing this truth, took the lantern from Quanzi’s mother’s hand and went out.

Lao Zhang half-leaned on the small bed, and she snorted lightly through her nose when she heard Quanzi’s fulang’s words; her eyes were slitted. She hated Quanzi’s fulang, but from the beginning to the end she never flared up.

It was indeed time to fetch the second room. She was the eldest sister-in-law, and she had helped him marry his wife. As the saying goes, the eldest sister-in-law was like a mother; she could use that status to suppress the second room. Now she was injured, so the second room had to come and take care of her no matter what.
