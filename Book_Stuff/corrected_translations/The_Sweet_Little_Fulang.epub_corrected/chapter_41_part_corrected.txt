Chapter 41


It was almost time to arrive at Shenshi (3-5 pm). It was still early, and the hillside was not far away, so Lu Gu carried a basket to gather vegetables from the field. Shen Xuanqing walked with him; this time they left the courtyard door unlocked, but the puppy and the big dogs stayed in the yard and were not allowed to follow, or they would trample the ground vegetables (地皮菜) and sniff at them.

The green and black ground vegetables were soft, a bit like black mushrooms soaked in dirt. After the rain, a lot of them grew on the ground. Lu Gu and Shen Xuanqing picked them, turning over a large area on the hillside, and gathered them. They went back with a full basket.

When they returned, neither of them was idle. The ground vegetables lay on the ground, and grass blades and broken branches got tangled in them as they were picked. Lu Gu washed them three times before spreading them on bamboo trays.

Shen Xuanqing stepped on the wood and sawed the wood. This tree he had cut two days ago, and he sawed it into sections so that it could be chopped into firewood.

Lu Gu got up and wanted to pour water, but a dog’s thick paw stepped on his shoe and bit his trousers. The dog was fat and heavy, so he had to pull the dog away first.

It was getting late; Lu Gu boiled a pot of hot water to wash his hands. He was used to cleaning his teeth with green salt and washing his face at night; he still felt uncomfortable without washing for a day. After washing, he poured hot water to soak their feet and sat on the bed. Their feet were hot, and the mountain stream was icy cold. The two of them soaked their feet for a long time, which helped drive away the cold.

Lu Gu originally wanted to wait for Shen Xuanqing to finish washing before soaking his own feet. There was still water in the pot. If the water wasn’t dirty, he would add some hot water to the basin, but Shen Xuanqing glanced at him and said: "The wooden basin is big; why don’t you and I wash together, and rest early after washing.”

Hearing this, Lu Gu felt that it was inappropriate, but when he thought that Shen Xuanqing was not someone else, and he never dared to refuse Shen Xuanqing’s words, he hesitated, and finally, under Shen Xuanqing’s gaze, he lowered his head, moved a chair opposite, took off his shoes and socks, and carefully put his feet in.

The room was silent for a while; neither spoke, only the candle burned.

Speaking of it, Shen Xuanqing was actually impulsive, and didn’t move after Lu Gu put his feet in, for fear that his rough big feet would hurt his white and soft feet.

Lu Gu was more cautious than him; he lowered his head and didn’t dare to look at anyone, but what he saw were the soaked feet of the two. The wooden basin was indeed quite big; Shen Xuanqing put his feet on both sides, and he had to be careful not to move toward the middle, otherwise he would bump into Shen Xuanqing. If it wasn’t for him being confused, he would soak his feet together with Shen Xuanqing; the hot water would actually be quite comfortable to scald his feet.

After a while, Shen Xuanqing, who had slowed down first, made a move. He shifted his foot slightly to make the water sway a little. He raised his foot and gently pressed it on Lu Gu’s foot, his Adam’s apple bobbing, and he pretended to be natural with a slightly hoarse voice, and said: "It’s good to rub your feet."

Most people would rub their feet subconsciously when they were soaking their feet. Even if they didn’t use their hands, they would rub their feet against each other.

However, it was not normal for Lu Gu right now; he sat stiffly and didn’t dare to move, the sound of water was soft, and the touch on his feet was very obvious—this was a strange but not unfamiliar emotion; sometimes at night when Shen Xuanqing pressed him, he could touch his waist or other places, so he wasn’t entirely unfamiliar with touching.

It was the first time Shen Xuanqing had washed his fulang’s feet; his throat was tight, and his eyes darkened. He had never done this kind of thing before, and after rubbing for a while, he felt that it was abrupt and forward, and he didn’t dare to use any strength for fear of stepping on Lu Gu. He let out a fake cough and took a cloth towel and handed it to Lu Gu to wipe his feet first.

After wiping his feet, Lu Gu went to bed first, and Shen Xuanqing took the basin away to pour the water. He turned over on the bed and couldn’t help but want to curl up. Even if he didn’t understand love, he felt ashamed and couldn’t simply wash with a man.

It was cold at night; Shen Xuanqing closed the main room door and the bedroom door, turned off the light, and went to bed in the dark. He couldn’t sleep; Lu Gu’s bare feet dangled in front of his eyes, his feet hot from the scalding, and the little uneasiness in his heart seemed to grow hotter, and when he could bear it no longer, it became urgent.

When the man next to him stretched out his arms, Lu Gu thought he was going to sleep in his arms, but he pressed him down directly, as anxious as in the first few times, and rushed straight in as soon as he was ready.
