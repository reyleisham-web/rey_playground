Chapter 53


The mountain road was rugged, and pulling a cart was no easy task. Mud from the rain made the ground treacherous on both uphill and downhill sections. After two days of sunshine, the ground had dried a little.

Lu Gu led the dwarf deer up front, Shen Xuanqing pulled the cart, the wheels creaked, and the puppy ran along with Dahui. When the dwarf deer refused to move, Shen Xuanqing imitated a big dog’s bark to urge it along.

The dwarf deer had a wounded foreleg, so it walked slowly. Shen Xuanqing was not in a hurry. Like the village mule, this dwarf deer was a creature that could be ordered about; if it went to the millstone and saw that the person holding the whip was a woman or fulang, and the rope was placed on it, it wouldn’t pull the mill. It could only be led by a man. If a woman wanted to grind rice flour, she could blindfold it and flick a whip in the air, and it would walk forward.

Lu Gu posed little threat to the dwarf deer, and sometimes it would strain at the hemp rope, desperately trying to break free and escape. Fortunately, Lu Gu kept a wary eye on it, and the hemp rope was very tight.

Watching from behind, Shen Xuanqing set the cart down, grabbed the hemp rope from the cart as a whip, and gave the dwarf deer a hard crack or two. His strength was greater than Lu Gu’s, so the dwarf deer wouldn’t wriggle much when it hurt.

Although he wasn’t in a hurry, this dwarf deer was dishonest and took too long to walk, so Shen Xuanqing patted Dahui’s neck and told him to go home first.

Dahui, who understood the cue, ran off, and the puppy, instinctively wanting to follow, almost did. Fortunately, Shen Xuanqing stopped him with quick hands and eyes. The mountain road was long, and the puppy wasn’t as fast as Dahui, so it was easy for it to get lost. The forest was so vast that once lost, it would be hard to find.

“Dahei, come back.” Shen Xuanqing whistled, calling to stop the black dog from running.

The gray-and-white dog, Dabai, turned back when he heard the whistle, but Shen Xuanqing told him to go on, and he ran ahead toward home with Dahui. Dahui was smart and capable, but it was more reassuring to head home with multiple companions.

“Let Brother Daqing come up and pull the dwarf deer, so we won’t be delayed by it all the way,” Shen Xuanqing explained, while taking the puppy and setting it down in front of Lu Gu.

The puppy had followed Lu Gu every day and grew natural to him, rubbing its body against Lu Gu’s calf. With the black dog around, the puppy stopped barking and ran ahead, wagging its tail, turning around to bark at Lu Gu from time to time, as if asking him to keep up.

After walking for a while and resting for a bit, Lu Gu drank a few sips of water, handed the bamboo tube to Shen Xuanqing, and whispered, “You drink.”

The dwarf deer was tied to a tree by Shen Xuanqing. The prey that had scattered due to the bumps along the way was gathered up, along with bamboo baskets full of goods on the cart. In addition to the game, there were baskets of dipicai and fresh mushrooms, which were especially brought home. Yesterday the two of them had dug a bunch of slender young bamboo shoots; not as sweet and tender as spring bamboo shoots, but a bit more tender than thick, old bamboo shoots. They’d also picked a lot of wild soapberries, plus what Lu Gu had gathered before, and they packed half of them in sacks. Even at six copper coins per catty, they could sell for more than three hundred copper coins.

There were people in Qianshan who often gathered wild soapberries to sell. Lu Gu could sell so much money at once, which seemed like a lot, but he walked farther and farther on the mountain road to bring it down. How could he make money so easily? Yes, he had picked so many from that old tree, and the rest hadn’t grown yet, so he had to keep looking for more wild soapberry trees.

When he heard Dahui bark again, Lu Gu looked ahead, and after a while Wei Lanxiang and the other three appeared in his line of sight.

“Erqing, Guzi.” Before Wei Lanxiang reached them, she called the two of them with a smile. Lu Gu responded with Shen Xuanqing, and seeing that Shen Yaoqing didn’t come, Shen Xuanqing knew he was away doing part-time work and not at home.

Ji Qiuyue was very perceptive; as soon as she arrived, she took the hemp rope from Lu Gu and said with a smile, “This dwarf deer is quite fat.”

After finishing the farm work, her hands were not light. In her eyes, Lu Gu was timid, and his hands must be light, so she took the hemp rope from the cart, whipped the dwarf a few times, then turned to Lu Gu and said, “You and Shen Yan go push the cart, and I will pull it with Mother.”

Hearing this, Lu Gu nodded in agreement and went behind the cart with Shen Yan to push.

When they reached the steeper hillside, Wei Lanxiang also came to push. The three of them bent their waists hard, and Shen Xuanqing, who was pulling the cart from the front, looked a little lighter.

The road grew livelier with more people around, and the puppy was very excited. After all, he was small, and Lu Gu obviously doted on him.

After walking a bit longer with a rest in between, Shen Xuanqing carried the puppy into the cart, and now he got out of the cart. Jumping down, the puppy wagged its tail, ran and played, barking amid the laughter of a few people, and soon they were home.

After dinner, Lu Gu took four sachets from the room and handed them to Ji Qiuyue, who was carrying a wooden basin to wash clothes, saying, “Sister-in-law, these are for you and Brother Daqing.”

“So exquisite.” Ji Qiuyue quickly set the wooden basin down, took the sachet, sniffed it, and said, “So beautiful—the fragrance of these herbs is quite good; it smells refreshing.”

The sachets contained herbs Lu Gu had collected in the mountains, among which the cold, refreshing herbs were the hardest to find; he had spent a long time in the mountains to locate them, putting in much thought and effort.

“The embroidery is really nice. I’ll learn from you tomorrow.” Ji Qiuyue knew that Lu Gu planned to embroider a sachet for Wei Lanxiang, but she hadn’t expected that Shen Yaoqing and she would each have one as well.

Lu Gu blushed with embarrassment, but his eyes shone with joy.

Ji Qiuyue went to wash clothes by the river, lest she lose the sachet, and she returned to the room to put it away. Wei Lanxiang was in the east room, and Lu Gu still had two sachets in his hand. He ran into Ji Qiuyue as soon as he left the room, so he handed one to her first.

“Mother, this is for you.” As Lu Gu entered the east room, he saw Wei Lanxiang rummaging through a box and handed the sachet to her; Shen Yan came over, and Lu Gu gave the remainder to Shen Yan.

“This flower embroiderer is really beautiful. Where could I find such a skillful fulang?” Wei Lanxiang had a look that stretched from ear to ear, praising it again.

Even Shen Yan was overjoyed, admiring the embroidered flowers on it with a smile, her almond eyes crinkling. She was grown up, yet she had never possessed such a beautiful sachet.

Everyone in the family had them, and Shen Xuanqing naturally had a sachet; Lu Gu had given it to him when he first made one.

Before long, it was time to prepare dinner in the afternoon, and Wei Lanxiang’s face was bright with a smile. She and Shen Yan went to buy tofu and also bought some bacon to fry with bamboo shoots tomorrow. She wore a brand-new embroidered sachet at her waist, bright and eye-catching, and anyone they met on the road would do a double take. She paused to show off to the three aunts and six wives, her face full of charming smiles.

At dawn, Lu Gu woke early and went with Shen Xuanqing to Fenggu Town to the morning market. Lu Gu often sold wild soapberries and medlars, and this time he brought some bamboo mushrooms and mountain persimmons. When he had gone to dig autumn bamboo shoots yesterday, he had picked five fresh ones; the rest were dried with the bamboo mushrooms.

Shen Xuanqing said that medical clinics and restaurants accepted bamboo mushrooms, and the price was good. This was one of the mountain delicacies. If he sold them in a stall, the price would be suppressed, so he did not bring out the bamboo mushrooms.
