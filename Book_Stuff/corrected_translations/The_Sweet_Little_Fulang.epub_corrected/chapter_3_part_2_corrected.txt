Chapter 3 Part 2


The firewood room had no windows, and the door was tattered, yet it could still be closed, with two people standing in the doorway, blocking any gaps.

Ji Qiuyue sat on a bundle of wood near the firewood room and suddenly heard a few exclamations from inside.

“Why are you beaten like this?”

Hearing this, she suspected it could only be the black-hearted Lu family who had beaten him.

His wet clothes had been stripped off, but Du Hehua hadn’t sent anyone to fetch clean clothes. A Shuang’er in the wood shed peeped through the hole in the door and found that there were no men in the courtyard, so they carefully opened the door and asked, “What’s the hurry, he can’t die.”

Du Hehua replied impatiently, “Just hurry up,” and then walked into the house.

“This aunt,” Shuang’er who had spoken muttered, not pleased.

Seeing the gap in the door, Shuang’er and the girl acted without reservation, and Ji Qiuyue followed them inside.

The wet clothes they had taken off lay next to him, and Lu Gu was barely wrapped in Shuang’er’s jacket. Because it was summer, no one wore more clothes, so even if the people in the woodshed wanted to cover him, they couldn’t.

His exposed arms and legs were covered in large bruises, the worst areas turning purple-black. Lu Gu was curled up, and with a slight movement his waist and back became exposed. The bruises on his waist clearly indicated that his back was also covered in wounds. He was full of injuries.

He was already thin, but beaten like this. When he was struck, there wasn’t much flesh left to cushion his bones. Anyone who saw him would feel pity.

Ji Qiuyue didn’t initially feel much for Lu Gu and had thought the Lu family members were bad people who deceived them. Now, it seemed Lu Gu wasn’t so bad after all. It appeared these injuries had happened not long ago. Perhaps he had agreed to marry because he feared another beating from the Lu family.

Even though the people in the firewood room were kind, Lu Gu felt embarrassed to be watched and curled up as much as he could, hugging his legs.

Du Hehua tossed in a tattered dress, and after resting for a while Lu Gu warmed a little, no longer so cold, and his strength returned. He could dress himself, and the others exited from the firewood door.

Ji Qiuyue whispered to Wei Lanxiang what she had seen. Wei Lanxiang simply nodded and said nothing. The Lu family and the others wouldn’t be able to bear it.

Outside the yard, a young man from Anjia Village spoke a few words to Shen Xuanqing. He nodded, understanding, and thanked him. Anjia Village and Qingxi Village were not very close, but he had come to know a few people here. Although their relationship was ordinary, they could exchange news.

It turned out Lu Wen had contacts with the gongzi of the Li family in town, and it was even seen that a Li family maid delivered something to him. The Li family was wealthy, and Li Yuanwai in the county town was their own kin and had a good relationship with the county magistrate.

Du Hehua refused to pay back the money and looked as if she had swallowed it. It was because of this reliance.

There were quarrels and fights. Since the marriage could not be concluded, Shen Xuanqing and Wei Lanxiang discussed it and didn’t want to entangle themselves any longer.

Who would have guessed that even Bao Zhiru would come forward; Du Hehua would still be reluctant to live or die, rolling on the ground, crying that she had no money, and cursing Lu Daxiang and Lu Gu, but Lu Daxiang was a coward. Yes, the dowry money was all in Du Hehua’s hands, and she didn’t know where it was hidden. He kept patting his legs, pacing, constantly shaking his head and sighing, saying that he had done something wrong.

Where would the Shen family be willing to concede? It was 20 taels of silver.

The fulang wasn’t married, and they were cheated out of money. Wei Lanxiang was almost crying, with Bao Zhiru as a witness. Since the Lu family refused to pay back the money, they would repay the debt with goods.

When the men in Qingxi Village heard they were going to move the Lu family’s belongings, they were all young men, all of them rolling up their sleeves and ready for action, unable to hide their excitement.

Du Hehua was dumbfounded. She never thought the Shen family would do this, but to demand twenty taels was more painful than cutting flesh. After such hesitation, everyone from Qingxi Village broke into the house.

Shen Yaoqing strolled around the kitchen, trying to find the place where Du Hehua hid the money, but found nothing. He remembered that Lu Wen was gone, perhaps the 20 taels were with Lu Wen. And if the money was really hidden somewhere in the Lu family, Du Hehua would surely stop him from entering the house to avoid discovery, but she didn’t.

In the end, he had to endure the foul breath and began to carry things. Tableware and chopsticks were not scarce. He simply removed the iron pot from the stove, which was worth a bit of money.

Lu Gu was still hiding in the firewood room, listening to the sounds of rummaging through boxes outside, and a few men smashing doors and bowls, shouting that the Lu family had nothing of value.

Lu Daxiang and Du Hehua tried to stop this, crying.

In the end, it was Bao Zhiru who stepped forward to talk and stop the people from smashing things, saying they were asked to move items to pay off debts, not to loot.

Lu Gu regained his senses at that moment; his thoughts could return, but when he heard the voice of his stepmother, he trembled. When Shen Xuanqing and the others left, Du Hehua would surely vent her anger on him.

He crouched into a haystack, considering leaping into the river the most reliable way to end it all, but once that resolve faded, he no longer had the courage to seek death; he lingered, exhausted, not knowing how long.

If anything was good, it was that Du Hehua cried and shouted in the wood shed; she could not stop the men who carried things, and she was pushed to the brink. The hatred toward Lu Gu grew; every insinuation that Lu Gu was a murderer and would not die himself.

She grabbed a piece of firewood and struck Lu Gu. Lu Gu instinctively tried to shield himself, but when he heard the cruel word “die,” the last light in his eyes flickered and faded. He lowered his head and said nothing, letting her strike.

The stick that had once struck him hit his head, and his ears rang. After the dizziness subsided, he saw blood running down his forehead, soaking his cheeks.

In a daze, he heard Wei Lanxiang’s angry voice say that such things were worthless and would be sold, not even ten taels of silver in total.

 Hearing this, Du Hehua threw the wooden stick aside and walked out of the firewood room, pointing at Wei Lanxiang with her hands on her hips and shouting. After scolding, she pretended to be a rogue. If Shen Xuanqing took advantage of Lu Gu, the Shen family would have to yield to him. She declared that not a single thing would be allowed to be taken away, and she would compensate Lu Gu for the money he had been prostituted for. Otherwise, she would expose this and shame the Shen family, ensuring they would never dream of marrying off a wife or fulang.

Her words were too vulgar; Ji Qiuyue hurriedly pulled her sister-in-law Shen Yan away, to keep her from being insulted.

Wei Lanxiang would not let her son carry such a stigma—he had not married Lu Wen and would find another good match for Shen Xuanqing in the future, and she would not let him be ruined by such rumors.

The onlookers knew that the Shen family would respond to the rumor, but Du Hehua’s spreading of such filth could ruin Lu Gu forever.

The Shen family was anxious to argue, but Lu Gu, who had come out of the wood shed with the door in his hand, spoke first. He glanced at Du Hehua, then Lu Daxiang, then at the others in the yard, and he said, “I didn’t even say a word to Shen Xuanqing, let alone anything else.”

Lu Gu’s voice wasn’t loud; he was still a bit weak after another beating, but his face stained with blood made everyone fall silent and listen to him.

When Du Hehua beat him in the firewood room, no one heard it, and it wasn’t a big deal for a mother to beat her child. Who would have thought Du Hehua could be so ruthless?

Lu Gu looked at Lu Daxiang and continued, “You want to be greedy for those 20 taels. If you won’t go back, you’ll frame the bitter master. How can there be people like you in the world?”

Blood filled his eyes; though his voice grew weak, he tried to blink away the darkness. He wanted to say more, but his eyes dimmed, and he fainted again.

“Quick, save him.” The yard descended into chaos, and the spectators were startled, thinking he would die, but Du Hehua’s face was ashen, showing no fear.

She slandered Shen Xuanqing for occupying Lu Gu, which was nothing short of driving Lu Gu to death’s door. A Shuang’er’s family would tell him he’d lost his innocence, so rumors would spread and Lu Gu would never lift his head again.

Even if Du Hehua said this to provoke, Lu Daxiang did not reply and let Lu Gu see his father’s true colors.

He might not survive, but the Shen family would not bear guilt on their heads.

In the chaos, Shen Xuanqing, who frowned, stood not far from Lu Gu. At the brink of life and death, decorum went out the window. He stepped forward in two or three strides, and with Wei Lanxiang’s help and that of a few others, carried Lu Gu on his back and sprinted toward the doctor’s house in Anjia Village.

In the strong medicinal smell, Lu Gu, with a linen cloth wrapped around his head, opened his eyes to stare at the ceiling. He only registered the sound of “ooh ooh” outside when his sluggish eyes moved.

Someone pushed open the door; it was the doctor’s wife. Lu Gu instinctively recognized her as “Grandma Wang.” He called out, “Grandma Wang,” trying to sit up.

Grandma Wang hurried over and pressed him down. “Don’t move. Rest again; it’s not serious.”

Lu Gu heard Lu Daxiang crying out in pain outside. He suspected but had no strength to ask. Grandma Wang whispered that Shen Xuanqing had broken his leg, and they were in the process of setting his bone.

“Anyway, the boy is fiery. When his eyes turn red, he loses sight of the severity of his injuries.” Grandma Wang babbled as she poured water.

She helped Lu Gu drink a few sips of warm water, then, with a stern look, said, “Lu Gu, it’s not that Grandma is trying to scare you. From now on at the Shen family, you must stay low and be small; don’t dare to contradict others.”

Seeing Lu Gu’s puzzled look, she set the bowl down and added with indignation, “Du Hehua, with a heart as black as ink, sold you to the Shen family!”
