Chapter 128


When Auntie Miao called out, not only her own family but Quanzi’s kin hurried over to see what was happening.

A massive wild boar sat at the entrance of the Shen family’s second branch, drawing more and more onlookers. Da Chen and his wife came over, touching the boar’s tusks and gasping in awe. “These tusks are really something,” they exclaimed.

Many people patted and touched the boar’s body, not bothering with the dirt it had picked up from being dragged along the road.

The wild boar lived in the mountains and hadn’t been castrated, so such a large male carried a strong, gamey scent.

While others marveled at its enormous size and lingered, Ji Qiuyue couldn’t bear the smell and went back inside. Besides, with so many people outside and her heavily pregnant, it was better to stay indoors to avoid being bumped into.

“Oh, why is the flesh on its neck missing?” Eldest Aunt Zhou Yunzhi hurried over to inspect the catch.

In the countryside, meat to eat was already a luxury, so no one cared if it was tough or smelly. Even though her family was well-off, the sight of such a large wild boar made her eyes redden with envy.

Before the Shen family’s second branch could explain, several villagers eagerly offered that the boar had been poisoned with a numbing toxin.

Shen Xuanqing didn’t hide this fact. After all, numbing toxin was still a kind of poison. If the villagers didn’t mind, they could buy the meat; if they did, there was no reason to deceive them.

Lin Jinhu bent down and slapped the boar’s belly, watching it jiggle. He sighed, “It’s quite fat.”

A bold woman circled the bloodied head and was startled by the holes in its neck and forehead. But she didn’t back away; instead, she shook her head as she fixed her gaze on the boar’s unusually long snout. “The wild boars in the mountains are truly ugly.”

Lu Gu heard this and silently agreed. That day, he’d been terrified; in his panic to escape, he remembered the boar’s ugly, terrifying long snout and dark face.

Guaizi whimpered, and Lu Gu knew he was hungry. He took the dog inside to feed him.

Shen Shunde walked around the boar and finally stopped in front of its head, studying the tusks for a long time. With his hands clasped behind his back and an air of authority, he looked up at Shen Xuanqing, who was taller than him, and said, “Er Qing, these tusks are truly fierce.”

He didn’t ask for the meat or the bones, but everyone could tell his eye was on the tusks.

“I’ve heard that polished wild boar tusks bound with silver can ward off evil spirits,” Shen Shunde hinted again.

Shen Xuanqing smiled and nodded along with him, replying, “I’ve heard that too.”

Hearing this, Shen Shunde’s eyes lit up, and he was about to press his claim further.

Shen Shunwang, standing nearby, frowned. He couldn’t stand his elder brother Shen Shunde, who was known for favoring the rich and looking down on the poor, always eager to take advantage of others. With their second brother gone, the two nephews had worked hard to build a decent life. As an elder, how could Shen Shunde have the nerve to ask for things from his nephews?

But before Shen Shunde could finish his request, Shen Xuanqing interrupted him.

“Thanks to Eldest Uncle’s reminder, ever since my sister-in-law became pregnant, there have been several funerals in the village. My unborn nephew hasn’t been doing well, so I was thinking of polishing these tusks and hanging them at home to ward off evil spirits for my sister-in-law and the child. With only a month left until the due date, it’s best to ensure their safety.”

Shen Xuanqing’s expression was sincere, with no trace of mockery or disrespect toward Shen Shunde. He even thanked him.

The Zhang family had lost two people, and an elderly villager had died earlier in the year. His reasoning made perfect sense, and even Shen Shunwang nodded in agreement, saying, “That’s the right thing to do. Adding a new member to the family is a big event. It’s best to be cautious.”

Having failed to get what he wanted, Shen Shunde’s face reddened with embarrassment. Zhou Yunzhi, his wife, was annoyed that he couldn’t salvage any dignity or secure the tusks. She glared at him discreetly.

When Shen Shunde noticed Zhou Yunzhi’s glare, his expression darkened. He always liked to maintain his dignity, but this time he had brought the awkwardness onto himself. With so many people around, he couldn’t make a scene. His lips twitched a few times, and finally he glared back at her with a grim expression.

The tension between them didn’t escape Shen Xuanqing’s notice, but his smile remained unchanged.

“It’s getting late. Tomorrow, we’ll butcher the boar and boil the medicinal herbs. If any of you uncles and aunties are interested, come by. We’re selling it for only ten copper coins per catty,” Shen Yaoqing announced cheerfully.

“Ten copper coins?” someone exclaimed. That was much cheaper than pork from other sellers.

“Yes, ten copper coins,” Shen Yaoqing confirmed, adding, “This boar still needs to be washed with the medicinal herbs, so it isn’t as fresh as a freshly slaughtered pig. But since we’re all neighbors, we’re selling it cheap. However, we can’t go any lower than this price. Er Qing dragged it all the way down from the mountains, and hunting it was no small feat. Please understand.”

Shen Yaoqing was a master with words. He knew many villagers loved to haggle, but ten copper coins per catty was already a steal. They couldn’t go any lower, or it would be a loss for their family.

As the sky darkened, the crowd at the gate dispersed. Shen Xuanqing and Shen Yaoqing dragged the boar into the yard.

“Go wash your hands. I’ve already served your rice,” Lu Gu said, scooping water into a wooden basin and placing it on the rack.

“All right,” Shen Xuanqing replied, standing at the rack to wash his hands. Lu Gu handed him some wild soapberries.

At the dinner table, the whole family was in high spirits. Shen Xuanqing had only eaten two cold buns at noon and was starving after the long journey back. He mixed the rice with stir-fried eggs and green peppers, and shoveled the food into his mouth. Once his initial hunger was satisfied, he said, “Wild boar meat is gamey. You need strong seasonings to cover the flavor and make it taste good.”

“I know,” Wei Lanxiang replied, setting her chopsticks down. “It’s just wild boar meat. I’ve cooked it before. Tomorrow you’ll see what your mother can do.”

Ji Qiuyue scooped a spoonful of steamed egg and spoke with a hint of regret, “It’s a shame I can’t eat it. Once the baby’s born, I’m going to eat as much spicy meat as I want.”

Seeing Shen Xuanqing eat so quickly that he had no time to pick up dishes, Lu Gu had no choice but to add stir-fried eggs and eggplant to his bowl. The meal tasted better with some vegetables mixed in.

“It’s good to avoid certain foods,” Wei Lanxiang said, taking a sip of tea. Feeling sorry for her daughter-in-law who couldn’t eat much, she added, “Tomorrow you can have a few small pieces to satisfy your craving.”

Ji Qiuyue smiled and nodded eagerly. With only a month left until her due date, she’d been eating well lately, and her face had clearly become rounder. Having endured hardships, she didn’t mind gaining a little weight.

The nights at the foot of the mountain were cooler, so leaving the boar outside overnight wasn’t a problem.

After night fell and everyone had washed up, they returned to their rooms to rest. Lu Gu stood behind Shen Xuanqing with a jar of ointment, ready to apply it to his wounds.

By the candlelight, he saw a raw, rubbed mark on Shen Xuanqing’s right shoulder and a red welt on his left from dragging the heavy hemp rope. The boar was too heavy, and the mountain path was rough.

Before applying the ointment, Lu Gu instinctively blew on the wound on Shen Xuanqing’s right shoulder, prompting a soft chuckle from the man.

“Go ahead, it doesn’t hurt.”
