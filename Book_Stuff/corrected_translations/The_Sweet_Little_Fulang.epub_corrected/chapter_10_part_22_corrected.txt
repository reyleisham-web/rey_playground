Chapter 10 Part 22


After the marriage, on the day they returned to the door they wore the red wedding shoes, and those shoes had to be put away afterward. By custom, this pair of wedding shoes should be worn when visiting relatives during the Spring Festival to show the identity of the bride and groom. It wasn’t that Lu Gu didn’t know this rule, but he had no other shoes, so he couldn’t go barefoot.

After she finished speaking, she also remembered that there were only two summer garments in the box Lu Gu brought as his dowry, and there was no pair of shoes, so he couldn’t change clothes at all. She said with annoyance, “Look at me, I forgot this. Wait, I’ll ask my mother.”

Hearing this, Lu Gu was startled and wanted to stop her from asking; how dare he ask the Shen family for something. Shen Yan’s voice wasn’t loud, but Wei Lanxiang, who was sitting under the eaves of the hall doing needlework, also looked at Lu Gu’s feet. She heard Shen Yan mention the dowry box and inwardly complained to Du Hehua.

Outsiders might think they hadn’t worried about eating recently, since they had meat every day. Obviously they weren’t poor. Yet the newlyweds didn’t even have a spare pair of shoes to change into. If word got out, people would laugh. Wei Lanxiang, who controlled the family’s money, knew how much there was, and it would be enough to pay for a pair of shoes.

The wound on Lu Gu’s head hadn’t healed yet. Pay for medicine and ointment cost money; although Shen Xuanqing paid for it with his private money, most of the money his son earned from selling the prey had been turned over to her. How much was left?

Now that she had acknowledged Lu Gu, Wei Lanxiang didn’t want to complain anymore. Although she had never studied, she knew that a person must have a conscience, and she couldn’t drive Lu Gu away just to save money. Thinking about how to live well was the real motivation.

Before Shen Yan could speak, Wei Lanxiang said, “I see, you’re busy with your work.”

Lu Gu was uneasy. Wei Lanxiang shook her head and sighed when she saw him like this. He was too timid, so she had to comfort him: “Don’t worry. You still have your old clothes; it’s just right to cut them to make the uppers of shoes.”

Knowing she was referring to the tattered clothes Du Hehua had given him, Lu Gu didn’t want to throw them away, so he chose to wash them. Just in case you didn’t have any clothes to wear, you could wear them.

“Mother, I’ve cleaned that one,” he whispered.

Wei Lanxiang nodded and said, “That’s perfect. Bring it.”

Even if he was reluctant, Lu Gu went into the room and took it out. Wei Lanxiang asked him again, “How big are your feet? I don’t know if there are any shoes like yours at home.”

After hearing Lu Gu’s size, she pulled out Ji Qiuyue’s shoes and asked Lu Gu to try them on, saying, “You’re the same size as your sister-in-law. Save yourself trouble and don’t cut.”

She looked at Lu Gu’s body and thought of Du Hehua’s cruel heart, but the clothes for Lu Gu were good, so she added, “Your black-hearted stepmother dressed you well.”

After listening to her, he thought about it and truthfully admitted, “This is what Lu Wen doesn’t want.”

So that explained what Lu Wen had worn before, and Wei Lanxiang immediately understood why. Before Shen Xuanqing got married, no matter who asked about Du Hehua, they said she was a good person, and even Lu Gu looked clean. Several clothes that Lu Wen didn’t want had sustained the pretense, and now others had already seen that Du Hehua was not good to Lu Gu.

She turned over the ragged clothes and said to Lu Gu, “I can make two pairs of shoes for you, and the rest can be used by Shen Yan. She has grown a lot recently, and her feet have grown too. These days she keeps saying that her shoes chafe her feet.”

Lu Gu could get two pairs of new shoes that hadn’t been worn by Lu Wen. Lu Gu said he was happy, but his long-standing temperament kept him from showing it; he whispered, “Yes, Mother.”

Wei Lanxiang saw how obedient he was, even calling her mother, and her affection for him grew twofold.

Lu Gu went to boil the medicine and tend the fire. While she did needlework, she realized that the material for the uppers of the shoes was enough, but not long ago, because of the wedding, she had used a lot of coarse cloth to make new shoes and jackets for the family. Now the remaining fabric was simply not enough for the soles of four pairs of shoes, so she had to think of a way.

Lu Gu drank a full bowl of loach soup at noon, and Wei Lanxiang specially gave him a large portion, saying it would help him recover quicker. This thing didn’t need to be bought; it was dug up by the river. It wasn’t something new, and the Shen family didn’t care who drank more or less.

But for Lu Gu, it was precious. The white broth was delicious and fragrant with just a touch of salt. The loach and small fish were stewed until the bones were soft, and even the bones were tender, which was far better than the roasted version he had earlier.

After washing the dishes, Lu Gu originally wanted to help Shen Yan chop grass for the chickens, but Wei Lanxiang put a large basket on his arm and told him to go out, so he followed along.

As they walked outside the village, they met people along the road. Wei Lanxiang joked with the villagers and even introduced Lu Gu to many of them. She had Lu Gu note in his head that this was his third uncle’s house, and if he wanted to visit, he would know the way.

Although Lu Gu’s voice was small, he did not waver when he spoke up along the way, and he kept all of Wei Lanxiang’s words in his heart.

He followed Wei Lanxiang all the way east without stopping, and suddenly realized they were going to the town. Fenggu Town lay about ten miles from Qingxi Village, passing through four or five villages. A young man with quick footwork could reach it in twenty-five to forty-five minutes, and peasant families didn’t ride in horse-drawn carriages. If you didn’t hurry, you could rest along the way.

Lu Gu didn’t know what Wei Lanxiang was planning until they reached the town. She took out the wedding dress that Lu Gu had worn from a cloth-covered basket. She couldn’t wear it anymore, so she might as well sell it for some money.
