Chapter 94 Part 26


Shen Shuyun was older than Shen Shunde. She was the eldest sister of their generation, with a terrible, shrewish temper. Even Shen Shunde, who usually wore the airs of the eldest son, didn’t dare to show off in front of her.

Shen Xuanqing’s grandparents favored their youngest son Shen Shunwang the most, but she favored her fourth brother Shen Shunfu the most. There was no special reason; she simply felt that the luck, knowledge, and experience of her younger brother were different from ordinary people. When Shen Shunfu had an accident, she did her best to send money and medicine, and Wei Lanxiang respected her very much.

On the way here just now, Wei Lanxiang and Shen Xuanqing had told her about Lu Gu. Now that she saw Lu Gu was well-behaved and looked like a good boy, she felt relieved.

“Eldest sister, try this sugar cake. I boiled the syrup with tianwogen,” Wei Lanxiang sat beside her and chatted.

Shen Shuyun, being the eldest, clearly didn’t like the First Household very much. The Second and Third Households were well-behaved, and a smile never left her face since she arrived.

“Erqing, hurry up and fetch the maleizi for the others.” Wei Lanxiang urged again.

Shen Shuyun went back to her natal home once a year, mostly because she had to feed a big family. She came back with her family, with several grandchildren from her sons and daughters, and it was very lively; the grandchildren would crowd around their uncles’ households.

Shen Xuanqing went to fetch the maleizi; as long as the children had something to play with and something to eat, they were very happy, and the courtyard rang with children's laughter.

Wei Lanxiang refilled the tea and said, “I was in a hurry this morning, and I forgot to bring beef. Daqing bought it especially, and I was waiting for you to come to eat.”

Beef was more expensive than other meats, and she hadn’t brought it on purpose. After the relatives arrived, she brought it to the First Household’s house in front of them so that everyone would know that the beef was bought by her family and had nothing to do with the First Household. Otherwise, with Zhou Yunzhi’s shamelessness, she would surely claim it.

Shen Shuyun, hearing that she could afford beef, said: “That’s the way it is; better days are coming.”

“That’s it.” Wei Lanxiang loved hearing such words most of all, and her face lit up with a smile.

The Second and Third Aunts both came on the first day of the New Year, and there were a lot of people. It was Lu Gu’s first time cooking at the First Household’s house. Because he was not familiar with the place and the people, he and Ji Qiuyue acted as supporting roles in the kitchen. That day, Shen Xuanqing stood outside; it was his job to serve food and hot wine, and he sat at the table with the relatives to eat and drink.

With so many people and the weather so warm, they set up four tables in the courtyard.

Lu Gu delivered the food to the men at the tables; he was a little cautious because he didn’t know them well. But fortunately Shen Xuanqing sat near the kitchen, and every time someone came over, Shen Xuanqing would help him place the dishes, so he didn’t have to talk to others.

Shen Xuanqing took the empty plate and handed it to him, asking, “Are you hungry? Let mother save you some food.”

“I’m not hungry; I already ate some dishes in the kitchen,” Lu Gu smiled faintly, afraid of delaying the kitchen’s service, so he left quickly, and he heard the relatives at the table laughing at Shen Xuanqing.

There were many people chatting and shouting in the yard, and the first day of the New Year was full of noise and excitement.

Lu Gu wasn’t familiar with these relatives, but just listening and watching from the sidelines made him happy.

The next day, it was time to visit the relatives’ houses.

When Lu Gu was washing his face early in the morning, he saw the broken palace lantern in the yard. Shen Xuanqing wiped his face and followed his gaze, “It’s all right. When I was young, I went to my eldest aunt’s house and broke a lot of things there. If you want, I’ll take you to town to buy something better than this during the Lantern Festival.”

Lu Gu only felt a twinge of pity because the palace lanterns were expensive, but he didn’t truly blame anyone. Children played like this; he had broken lanterns bought by his mother when he was a child too.

In the middle of the morning, they packed up. They packed candied fruit, pastries, bacon, and dried fish. Ji Qiuyue and Shen Yaoqing went to Ji’s house. Because they had cut off contact with the Lu family, and Lu Gu had no natal family to go back to, Wei Lanxiang took him with her. The two of them went to Wei’s house with Shen Yan.

Chinese New Year was like this: either visiting relatives or receiving visitors. From the first day to the fifteenth day of the New Year, they were busy. Some old relatives and distant relatives didn’t come often, but you must entertain them properly. Unlike Shen Xuanqing’s eldest uncle’s house, if Wei Lanxiang’s relatives came, they would definitely prepare a good meal for them. So if Wei Lanxiang’s family came one day, the other party’s family would prepare a good meal for them.

Their life was better now; when poorer relatives came over, Wei Lanxiang didn’t look down on them. They cooked fish and meat for them. Not to mention the adults, at least let the relatives’ children eat more meat to satisfy their cravings; the children were pitiful after all.

The days passed quickly.

Before New Year’s Eve, Lu Gu was nervous about the New Year’s Eve, but when it came to the occasion, he found it nothing. Shen Xuanqing was always where he could see him, and he felt at ease.

The day before Lantern Festival, Wangli Village set up an opera stage at the village entrance; Landlord Li had invited an opera troupe to sing.

Wangli Village was a little far from their village, but many people there invited their friends and arranged to watch a big show together the next day.
