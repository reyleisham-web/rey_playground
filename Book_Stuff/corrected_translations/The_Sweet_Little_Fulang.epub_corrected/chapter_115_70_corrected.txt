Chapter 115 Part 70


“Cousin.” Lu Gu echoed softly from the side.

Shen Yutao lifted his head at the sound, offering a polite smile as he said, “It’s you two.”

He wasn’t as tall as Shen Xuanqing, and he was a bit plumper and shorter, but his looks were still pretty good. It was clear his face bore some resemblance to Shen Xuanqing and Shen Yaoqing.

“That’s a lot of stuff.” Shen Yutao’s eyes went straight to the cart. He often worked in the town and, while he did enjoy taking advantage of others on occasion, he knew how to restrain himself. At least he kept things in check, unlike his mother Zhou Yunzhi, who was always itching to ask for more or to push for it secretly.

Shen Xuanqing laughed, picked up a medium-sized pheasant, and handed it over. “Cousin, you’re joking. This isn’t a lot. Hunting in early spring is hard. Pregnant animals can’t be hunted, and those that are too young can’t be hunted either. There are many restrictions. I’ve been in the mountains for a month and only brought back these few. I don’t know how many copper coins I can sell them for today.”

“You, this…” Shen Yutao pretended to be embarrassed and didn’t take the pheasant immediately. He said, “It’s not easy for you to hunt, so you should sell them.”

“It’s only one. Cousin, just take it. There will be times in the future when I’ll trouble you, cousin,” Shen Xuanqing said with a smile.

Shen Yutao forced a flattering smile, took the pheasant, and promised, “We are family; there’s no such thing as trouble or not.”

“Cousin, are you going to do something?” Shen Xuanqing asked.

“That’s right. It’s time to collect the bills on East Street. The shopkeeper is urging us to settle them,” Shen Yutao nodded. There were many people on the street, so it wouldn’t be the right place to talk; he added, “You guys go ahead and do your work. Come to the shop another day and talk.”

Shen Xuanqing nodded and said goodbye, then walked forward with Lu Gu.

In this generation, most people in the village with mixed surnames named their children with the character “Yu.” There were few family trees among the illiterate peasants, so if they cared about naming, they would place one character from the eldest son’s name into the following sons’ names. This is why the third branch of the Shen family had Shen Yu and Shen Yuping following the names Shen Yutao and Shen Yuxi.

[玉 yù: jade]

As for Shen Yaoqing’s name, Shen Shunfu had someone calculate it. The reason was that he had a bad relationship with the First Branch and with Shen Yaoqing’s grandparents; they even fought when the family property was divided. After his eldest son was born, Grandpa Shen came to him and said that even though the family had been divided, his son’s name had to use the character “Yu.”

[In China, a person’s name is “calculated” by considering the meaning and stroke count of the Chinese characters used in their family name (one character) and given name (one or two characters), with the family name always coming first; this practice is often tied to numerology and the concept of the “five elements” to choose characters that are considered auspicious for the individual based on their birth date and time.]

But Shen Shunfu refused to do so. When he got stubborn, he was like an old ox that couldn’t be pulled back.

Just now, Shen Yutao’s eyes had been fixed on the fattest rabbit, but Shen Xuanqing pretended not to see it. Rabbits were more expensive than pheasants, and giving a pheasant as a favor was already very good—just to maintain appearances. There would always be a time when Shen Yutao would be useful to him in the future.

“Eggs—chicken eggs, big duck eggs.” Lu Gu, not knowing what was going on in Shen Xuanqing’s mind, shouted with all his might. His face and eyes were bright and innocent, which made people feel happy just looking at him.
