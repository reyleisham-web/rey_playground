Chapter 49


The ember in front of the grave was moved by the wind, and Lu Gu burst into tears, crying silently. Shen Xuanqing didn’t have a handkerchief on him, so he pinched the corner of his sleeve and leaned forward to wipe the tears from Lu Gu’s face. The tall man was usually rough, so he looked a little lost, as if he’d never done such a delicate job of comforting someone before.

The tears kept flowing and he couldn’t wipe them away. Shen Xuanqing grew anxious, but in the end he had no choice but to hold Lu Gu tightly and clumsily.

The face pressed against the broad chest wasn’t unfamiliar to Lu Gu. Shen Xuanqing often held him when he slept at night, but never during the day, after all, it was broad daylight. The tears on his face were rubbed onto Shen Xuanqing’s clothes, and he slowly stopped crying after realizing it, lest it get dirty.

Regaining his senses, Lu Gu felt embarrassed, crying so much that he didn’t know if Shen Xuanqing would dislike him or scold him. As soon as that thought arose, it was rejected in his heart. If Shen Xuanqing despised him, why would he hug him, with no word of reprimand or abuse?

Hugging someone like this was certainly embarrassing for Lu Gu. It could be said that except for Shen Xuanqing, no one had ever hugged him like this. He was familiar with the tall man’s smell—the faint breath of wild soapberries was warm, mingling with the strong and vigorous masculinity; the scent was indescribably reliable and reassuring.

Lu Gu felt ashamed and didn’t dare to move. He wanted to step back, but Shen Xuanqing held him tightly. The helplessness in the depths of his heart found a stable place at this moment. His fingertips trembled slightly, but he still couldn’t hold back. He boldly grabbed a bit of fabric at Shen Xuanqing’s waist and clutched it tightly, as if he were using a lot of strength, though he still didn’t dare to squeeze too hard.

Lu Gu didn’t move much in his arms, but Shen Xuanqing could feel that he was no longer crying, so he sighed very lightly, hugged him, and the stiffness and clumsiness from before disappeared. This was his fulang; now there were no outsiders watching, so what if you hugged him.

Shen Xuanqing slowly let go of him, realizing it had been delayed for a long time.

Lu Gu lowered his head and wiped the remaining traces of water from his face with his sleeve. Shen Xuanqing carried the bamboo basket on his back, and the two walked back.

After the mood settled, Lu Gu’s eyes were still red. They walked about halfway, and he raised his eyes to look at Shen Xuanqing’s face, only to be caught by those starry eyes. He felt a little flustered, but before he could look away, he saw Shen Xuanqing smile helplessly and say: “You don’t need to be afraid of me. Whatever you want to do in the future, just open your mouth. I’m not someone else; I won’t hold you back.” The deep voice was indescribably pleasant. Lu Gu sniffed lightly, his eyes still a little sore, but he didn’t cry; he just nodded heavily and gave a nasal “huh.”

As they walked, Lu Gu thought about everything that had happened after he arrived at Shen’s house, and Shen Xuanqing’s words just now. His heart felt sour, and this sourness made him want to grab Shen Xuanqing’s clothes again, but he didn’t know why.

They hadn’t reached Qingxi Village yet. The autumn harvest was over, and there were few people on the road. Shen Xuanqing was beside him; Lu Gu couldn’t hold back, and quietly raised his hand to hold some fabric from Shen Xuanqing’s clothes. After a moment, he was too flustered and quickly let go when he saw someone approaching from a distance.

Realizing what he had just done, it was outrageous. Lu Gu lowered his head and didn’t dare to lift it, fearing ridicule for being out of order.

When the corner of his clothes was pinched and held by his fulang—even if only for a moment—Shen Xuanqing was stunned for a moment and smiled brighter than when he had been married. Fortunately, he knew Lu Gu’s face was too thin and would be overwhelmed with fright if he looked again, so he didn’t speak, only the smile on his face remained.

Returning to the village, even the familiar men of the same age in Qingxi Village were a little surprised to see him this way; those who didn’t know might think he had encountered something great or had come into money.

After finishing all the errands, Lu Gu and Shen Xuanqing packed the next day and went to the mountains again. It was mid-morning when they left; as soon as they went out to the north hillside, there were red persimmons hanging from the persimmon tree outside the mud wall to the north of Shen’s house.

Wei Lanxiang saw them and said, “The persimmons are ripe; bring some for you two.”

“Mother, there are some on the mountain, no need.” Shen Xuanqing didn’t go to pick them; the wild persimmons in the mountains were beginning to ripen, and the taste wasn’t much different. They were all sweet persimmons, so there was no need to pull the ones from the bottom to the top of the mountain.

Lu Gu had been in the mountains with Shen Xuanqing for so long, knowing where the persimmons were, so he was not greedy for them. Wei Lanxiang didn’t press the issue, stopping outside the house to watch the two head for the mountain.

There were some things on the cart, including Lu Gu’s chickens and ducks in cages. Dahui and the other dogs were leading the way, sniffing at the roots of the trunks, sometimes lifting their hind legs to mark their territory.
