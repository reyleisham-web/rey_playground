Chapter 75 Part 28


Lu Gu washed all the pots and bowls, scooped water out with a large ladle, and Shen Xuanqing opened the lid of the clay pot and looked inside. There really weren’t many left, so he set the clay pot down, picked up the candlelight, and rummaged under the desk.

In the past two years, it snowed and there was no work. He planed and peeled wood, making two shallow bowls. He hadn’t learned from a carpenter, so the workmanship wasn’t good, and he didn’t use them much. There were other bowls at home for serving rice and vegetables; it was better to use this wooden bowl for badger oil, which was also convenient to take.

Lu Gu wiped the pot clean with a cloth, and only then did he add two and a half buckets of water to the pot. Needless to say, Shen Yan had added a lot of firewood to the bottom of the stove, and the fire roared to life.

“Wipe more oil on your hands and feet, and wipe less on your face,” Shen Xuanqing said as he dug out the badger oil.

Lu Gu picked up the wash basin and was about to go out to pour it. Hearing this, he looked over, nodded, and said, “Yes, I understand.”

In winter, when the cold wind blew, the face tended to chap from exposure, and people would rub a little lard on it to moisturize.

After a busy day, Lu Gu finally sat on the bed and soaked his feet in hot water, feeling very relaxed. Now he and Shen Xuanqing weren’t as restrained about soaking their feet together; they could lift their feet and rub them by themselves.

Seeing that he was rubbing his feet by himself, Lu Gu’s pale feet were stacked together, so Shen Xuanqing suddenly lifted his own feet and pressed them against Lu Gu’s. The big feet belonging to the man pressed against his feet. Lu Gu raised his eyes helplessly and saw a pair of smiling star-like eyes. For some reason, seeing Shen Xuanqing smiling like this made his ears burn red, and he subconsciously wanted to pull his feet away.

Unexpectedly, Shen Xuanqing wouldn’t let go, and the two of them “discussed” with their feet in the wooden basin, with a little water splashing out now and then.

It was Shen Yan walking and yelling outside that made the two of them stop their childish behavior.

“Brother Guzi, my mother, sister-in-law, and I have wiped the badger oil; now it’s your turn.” Before she finished speaking, the half-closed door opened, and Shen Yan came in with a wooden bowl. The candle on the table was lit; Shen Yan set the bowl on it, glanced at the two people soaking their feet, and said, “Then I’ll go back to sleep first.”

“Go to bed soon,” Shen Xuanqing agreed, and Shen Yan left.

After washing his feet, Lu Gu wanted to get out of bed to fetch the badger oil, but Shen Xuanqing stopped him: “Go to bed, I’ll get it for you.”

Every time after washing his feet, Shen Xuanqing went out to pour the water, and Lu Gu followed suit after hearing this. He supported his body with his hands and moved inside the bed; his face had been wiped just now, so all that remained was to wipe his hands and feet.

After applying it, it really felt different. When you rubbed it on your hands and feet, it felt warm and moist, and you didn’t need to worry about it being too oily if you rubbed it on more. Tang pozi warmed the bed, and the rough feeling of friction between the skin and the bedding was much less when you were lying in it.

When Shen Xuanqing poured water and came back, Lu Gu looked at him as he closed the door and whispered, “You wipe it too.”

“I don’t need it; I don’t feel dry yet.” He only wiped his hands and feet when he felt them dry and cold. He was a big man and didn’t have the patience to apply it like this. But after getting into bed, he felt Lu Gu’s hands were moist; he often touched his fulang’s hands, and now he suddenly felt different, not as dry as before.

Badger oil was indeed a good thing. It’d keep better this year. Badger oil stored for more than three years was even more effective. He could buy these things and give them to Lu Gu every year in the future.

The sun was out and there was no wind yet. It was warm and bright—another fine day.

Lu Gu boiled the paste, and then he, Wei Lanxiang, and Shen Yan went to the courtyard to dry the rag for shoes while the weather was sunny. There wasn’t much else to do in winter, and it was the customary work for women and fulangs to make the shoe soles.

There were many people in the family, and plenty of clothes to reuse. Not to mention old clothes and cotton-padded clothes, they also bought some old clothes and rags from the villagers. They washed them all in the water the day before yesterday.

Unlike other farmers, Shen Xuanqing often roamed in the mountains, and he wore out a lot of shoes; whether winter or summer, he needed several pairs. He was almost nineteen years old now. He had grown quickly in the past few years, but had stopped growing in the past two years. He had grown so tall that he wouldn’t grow any taller. Wei Lanxiang felt relieved; when she thought about making shoes for him, if he grew taller, the shoes she made in winter would be too small to wear, and she would have to redo them.

Sometimes Shen Yan didn’t like her family talking about her, but she remained obedient and earnestly learned from Wei Lanxiang how to make the soles.

The three of them were busy, so Ji Qiuyue went to the backyard to chop grass for the chickens and ducks, and called Shen Yaoqing to pick up the chickens and ducks’ excrement at the back.

At noon, the sun grew brighter, and the last piece of cloth was finished. Lu Gu straightened up and beat his sore back.

A puppy lay drowsily in front of the courtyard door, belly exposed to the sun, and suddenly barked twice. Lu Gu turned when he heard it; it should be Shen Xuanqing who had returned, and sure enough, he saw someone.

In the past, no matter who came back from outside, the puppy would wag its tail and run out to greet them, but today it was lazy and only barked. Lu Gu was provoked, but his attention quickly shifted to Shen Xuanqing.

“You’re back.” With a smile in his eyes, he helped Shen Xuanqing unload the bamboo basket from his back and saw a small iron pot with a long handle inside. It was the youzhong ladle that Shen Xuanqing had told him about, for cooking.

Not only the youzhong ladle, but there was also a book in the basket, and because of the previous “lesson,” Lu Gu trembled a little when he saw the book.
