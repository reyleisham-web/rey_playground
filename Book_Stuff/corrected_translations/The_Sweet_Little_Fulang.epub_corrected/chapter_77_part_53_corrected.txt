Chapter 77 Part 53


The sky grew darker, the snow fell heavier, flakes as large as goose feathers, and a biting wind swept through. After dinner they could not go outside, and there was nothing else to do, so the whole family sat around the brazier in the main room. The door to the main room was half-closed, leaving a small gap with which they could glimpse the heavy snowfall outside.

Shen Xuanqing owned five acres of land, and winter wheat had already been planted. This was how fields were bought and sold here. Boss Lu wanted to sell the land himself, not have someone else do the buying. No matter what was planted in his field, others didn’t care; they bought by the number of acres. The market price of a fertile acre hovered around five taels; the top price wasn’t higher than five taels, which was already a good price. Some buyers would test whether the seller was in a hurry, hoping to push the price down; if the seller was in a rush, they would still sell.

Because there were wheat seedlings in the field, the wheat could be harvested in May next year. When Shen Shunfu had an accident, he had bought two medicinal plants from Boss Lu, who sold them to him at a low price. The money he earned came from hard-won work gathering herbs in the mountains, which was why Shen Xuanqing didn’t press down a single coin today.

After the beginning of spring, Shen Xuanqing wanted to go to the mountains. It would be fine if he stayed home for spring plowing, weeding, and watering, but if he were not at home, only Shen Yaoqing could go. The family discussed and decided that Shen Xuanqing’s five acres of wheat would be enough to supply the family for a year before selling it. The family’s land was usually tended by Shen Yaoqing, and the money from the sale would be his as well. There was no need to keep the wheat in the field; all of it could be sold. Living together, there was nothing they could not bear.

Now the rice and flour they ate were all grown by Shen Yaoqing, and they saved enough food for six people, selling the rest for money. In the future, Shen Yaoqing would have to look after Shen Xuanqing’s land. It didn’t matter who worked hard; life was better when they supported each other.

Outside, it was snowing and windy, and the firewood in the brazier crackled softly, its glow reflecting on the faces of everyone present. They felt warm after eating, their clothes thick and cozy.

After chatting for a while, a gust blew the main room door open, and snow sprayed in, chilling them. Shen Yaoqing, the nearest to the door, hurried to close it. Wei Lanxiang said, “Sit on the bed since it’s so cold. Once the door is closed, Tang Pozi will warm you up. After sitting here for a while, your legs and feet will grow cold.”

The firewood in the brazier was almost burned down, and Shen Xuanqing wanted to add a few more logs, but he did nothing after hearing those words and returned to the room. The house’s windows used paper, and Shen Yaoqing had thickened the paper before winter began, allowing some light to filter through.

Listening to the wind outside, Lu Gu sat on the bed and tucked his legs under the quilt, with Tang Pozi beside him, and he didn’t feel cold at all. When there was nothing to do, he moved the small kang table closer and laid out a sewing basket for easy access, so he wouldn’t fear tipping anything over.

Shen Xuanqing came in from outside, holding a bowl, and ran to Wei Lanxiang’s room to fetch red dates and dried persimmons, then set the bowl on the kang table. Lu Gu was embroidering at an embroidery frame, watching him and whispering a few words from time to time. Lu Gu’s eyes drooped as he worked the embroidery, and his face shone with a soft glow. Shen Xuanqing couldn’t help himself; he went over to kiss him, stealing a scent. After the kiss, he was still a little reluctant, so he kissed him several times. Lu Gu, wanting to hide, did not dodge, and though he was bold when kissed, he felt embarrassed afterward, yet his smile never faded.

In the afternoon, even though the wind howled and snow blew outside, inside the house they were full of happiness. The whole family sat around the medicine stove. The mouth of the Youzhong ladle was wide, so they filled it with bone sticks and some broth and placed it on the medicine stove to simmer. The bone sticks had been stewing for more than half an hour, over large firewood, and the meat was so soft that one didn’t even need chopsticks. Everyone could hold the meat in their hands, sprinkle it with salt, and eat. The meat was incredibly tender, almost dissolving in the mouth, and the bones could be eaten without a trace of meat left.

The puppy kept barking when he smelled the meat. Shen Xuanqing had always treated his dogs well. He picked up a big bone with meat and threw it to the four of them. The puppy stopped barking as soon as he gnawed on the bone and meat.

Wei Lanxiang saw that even though the big bones were cheap, she hesitated a moment, but the second child’s nonchalant expression told her she couldn’t object. Seeing the bones all gnawed by dogs, she finally relented.

For Lu Gu, it was his first time eating meat and bones like this, and he found it delicious—the meat rich in aroma, the flavor on the bones different from ordinary meat. If you couldn’t pick at it with your teeth, you could poke it with the tip of your chopsticks, because it was so soft and tender that it would come off the bone easily without spoiling. The white, brittle bones felt especially distinct when bitten. At the end of the meal, Shen Xuanqing took the big bone back to the kitchen and cracked it; everyone sucked the marrow, which was delicious.
