Chapter 68 Part 35


After a while, the puppy still hadn't come back. Lu Gu picked up the bamboo pole, about to drive the duck ashore so he could search for the puppy in the woods. Before he could catch up, he heard some movement from the northern forest.

He looked up and saw the puppy running toward him, but in his mouth was a fat, large, gray-furred rabbit.

Lu Gu breathed a sigh of relief and felt his anxiety fade away. When the puppy ran closer, he could still hear a low whimper from its throat—the puppy was distressed, the big fat rabbit in its mouth not dead yet, and the rabbit kept kicking its legs.

How big was a puppy that was less than four months old? Even if it was well cared for, it was taller and stronger than other puppies in the village, about the same height as Lu Gu’s calf. Yet it had returned with such a big fat rabbit in its mouth. Surprised and happy, Lu Gu forgot to scold it for running around. After all, the mountains were not the village. It would be dangerous if they encountered a bigger beast—things would be rough for the puppy.

If Dahui had the fat rabbit in his mouth, that would be one thing, but now it was the puppy. Lu Gu worried that his mouth would be sore; the rabbit wasn’t small and must be heavy, so he grabbed two rabbit ears and pulled it out of the puppy’s mouth.

“Wow!” The puppy released the rabbit, raised its head, and barked at Lu Gu, wagging its tail excitedly as if asking for credit.

The rabbit wasn’t light. There was blood on its neck from the dog’s bite, and Lu Gu also noticed an old injury on one of its hind legs. Obviously it hadn’t been bitten by the puppy; the injured leg had already suffered before.

“Wow!” The puppy wagged its tail and danced with excitement. Lu Gu set the fat rabbit on the ground, knelt down, and stroked its head. The puppy tilted its head and narrowed its eyes in contentment.

The rabbit tried to run after being set down, but it had been bitten by the puppy, and its energy was limited. After a few steps, the puppy leaped forward and bit its neck again, growling in a low, dignified way.

“Let’s go. Put it in the cage first, and I’ll give you a big rabbit leg tonight.” Lu Gu rubbed the puppy’s head, his brows and eyes curved into a smile. He hugged Tang Pozi with one hand and grabbed the rabbit with the other, walking back. He didn’t worry too much about the ducks in the pond.

The puppy followed him, walking differently, head held high, looking very proud and regal.

When Shen Xuanqing came back in the evening, the first thing Lu Gu did was show him the rabbit. “Guaizi caught it.” He couldn’t hide the joy on his face and was all smiles.

The puppy got excited when it saw the rabbit, running around the yard and even coming close to the two of them, barking loudly at Shen Xuanqing a few times.

Shen Xuanqing was happy too; he saw the injury on the rabbit’s leg at a glance and understood what had happened, but it was a notable feat to bring back such a big, fat rabbit, so he bent down and rubbed the puppy’s head as a reward.

“Give Guaizi a rabbit leg at night,” Lu Gu said.

“Okay.” Shen Xuanqing agreed.

The four rabbit legs happened to be divided among the four dogs. The bamboo rats, voles, rabbits, and pheasants that the puppy usually ate were all caught by Dahui. It wouldn’t be good to give the puppy meat every day.

Guazi had caught the prey for the first time, and in addition to rabbit legs, he also gave a few extra pieces of rabbit meat to the puppy.

It rained again, though only for half a day, but it was colder in the mountains.

They dug out the old copper basin used for fire in previous years, and Shen Xuanqing and Lu Gu sat in the main room to warm up by the fire. The burning wood crackled and spit sparks to the side. The wind blew in from time to time, and it grew dark after they closed the door, so Lu Gu took two thin quilts out of the room for them.

Lu Gu put Tang Pozi on his lap and took the embroidery frame to embroider the handkerchief. Tang Pozi sank a little after drinking, but the warmth was inviting, so he set the frame aside.

Shen Xuanqing looked to the side for a while and said, “How about I kill a rabbit and roast it to eat? There is rock salt in the kitchen. When the roasted meat is rubbed with it, it will taste good.” Rock salt is from a special stone that melts in water and has a salty taste, so it’s called rock salt.

When Shen Xuanqing learned the craft from the old hunter, Lao Yang would roast meat like this.

Lu Gu must have never eaten it; thinking of this, and without waiting for an answer, he got up and went to the bamboo cage in the woodshed to catch a big rabbit and slaughter it in the courtyard.

When the rabbit was roasted, the four dogs watched. The aroma of meat filled the air, and the puppy drooled.

Shen Xuanqing tore a few pieces of meat for them, then cut the rest into small pieces with a knife and rubbed the tip of the knife in rock salt while it was hot. It was salty and fresh, completely different from fried or stewed rabbit meat.

With such delicious meat to eat, Lu Gu’s eyes brightened.

“In a few days, I’ll catch a few more rabbits and pheasants, and then we’ll pack up and go down the mountain to head home. It’s almost winter; it’s too cold in the mountains, and we don’t need to go up again,” Shen Xuanqing said as they ate.

“En.” Lu Gu had just eaten a piece of meat and could only nod and vaguely agree. For him, no matter where he was, he would just follow Shen Xuanqing. However, this dependence hadn’t yet been noticed by him.

Lu Gu, in a quiet moment, thought of Shen Xuanqing as his ger—the man who led and protected him.
