Chapter 92


Shen Yan was so happy that she clapped her hands, lit the tip of a thin wooden branch, and said to Lu Gu, “Brother Guzi, I’m not afraid of firecrackers. I’ll light a big one for you later.”

Shen Xuanqing brought out a bundle of ma leizi (firecrackers that explode very loudly), and there were plenty of items in the yard, so he placed them just outside the doorway. Hearing Shen Yan’s words, he smiled and said, “Then you come and light this ma leizi; its sound is loud enough.”

“Just set it off—you light it for me first.” After all, Shen Yan was young, and her family had no money in the past two years, so during the Chinese New Year she could only watch others set them off and was very envious. Now she was eager to try, and her eyes widened as if fearing nothing in heaven or earth.

When she reached the door, Shen Xuanqing set the ma leizi on the ground for her. She moved forward with the wooden branch in her hand, lit the tip carefully, and immediately ran away like a startled rabbit.

The reason the ma leizi was called a ma leizi was that the sound of the explosion was like thunder. Lu Gu stood in front of the door, covered his ears and retreated subconsciously, but his retreat was blocked by Shen Xuanqing standing behind him.

When the first explosion boomed, Shen Xuanqing immediately cupped his hands over Lu Gu’s ears to shield them.

After the sound subsided, Lu Gu opened his eyes. Behind him, Shen Xuanqing was almost hugging him, his big hands still covering his ears. With a slight movement of his hands, as if to release Lu Gu, Shen Xuanqing let go first.

The lantern hanging at the door cast a red glow. When Lu Gu turned around, he saw Shen Xuanqing smiling with starry eyes, and his heart steadied in his chest.

“Why were you running so fast? Didn’t you say you weren’t afraid?” Shen Xuanqing teased Shen Yan.

It wasn’t only their family who had firecrackers on New Year’s Eve. Almost every family in the village was outside their doors, and Miao Chunsheng’s family had also come out to join in.

Glancing over, Miao Chunsheng smiled and said, “This ma leizi is really powerful. I bought some too. Let’s make a racket.”

Aunt Miao, seeing him about to set off another ma leizi, pressed her fingers to her ears and hurried aside for fear of getting too close.

There was a “bang”; snowflakes and black mud splashed everywhere, followed by another explosion of a ma leizi.

Across the village, the sounds of various firecrackers filled the air one after another.

Compared with the ma leizi, which could only be heard, huoqihua (firecrackers that rise into the sky) and erqi jiao (double-sounding firecrackers) were more entertaining.

Shen Yaoqing took the ignition branch from Shen Yan and lit a second ma leizi. The ma leizi bounced on the ground, a second “bang” split the air, and the red paper wrapping on the firecracker exploded and showered the ground.

He lit several in a row—bang, bang, bang. Wei Lanxiang saw how lively it was and lit two more ma leizi; she was so happy she smiled from ear to ear, then she urged Aunt Miao to do the same. It was amusing.

Miao Chunsheng had bought a few more ma leizi. He was brave and thought the sound was powerful. Aunt Miao, however, was afraid of the noise and did not dare light them at all.

Quanzi’s family had a baby, so Quanzi’s fulang (the baby’s father) didn’t come out. He held the baby and coaxed him in the room. When Quanzi was at home in winter, Quanzi’s fulang often asked him to hug and coax the baby. He couldn’t do anything at home, and today he could finally go outside to enjoy himself.

The firecrackers crackled one after another, and lanterns hung at the doors of every house. It was as lively as daytime, with everyone chatting and laughing at the doors, watching the firecrackers in front of each house.

Huoqihua were tied to long reed poles; Lu Gu didn’t dare to speak, so Shen Xuanqing took one huoqihua for him and said, “Hold it tight. Don’t be afraid—after I light it, it will shoot straight up.”

Lu Gu was very nervous, so he could only nod and clutch his hands tightly. Perhaps Shen Xuanqing worried about him, and after lighting the huoqihua, he grasped Lu Gu’s hand and raised it toward the sky.

The huoqihua tied to the reed pole shot into the sky, crackling as it exploded in the air, emitting radiant flames.

Lu Gu only dared to open one eye to look, and closed the other tightly, but after the first sound he seemed to grow bolder. The reed pole was long enough to be safe, and he became a little more interested.

There were big and small huoqihua; Shen Yan took a small one and asked Shen Yaoqing to light it for her. When it was lit, she raised it toward the sky, and the small huoqihua also soared.

When Lu Gu put down the reed pole, he glanced at the pile of huoqihua, and Shen Xuanqing knew that he still wanted to set off another huoqihua, so he smiled and took out another big one.

This time Lu Gu didn’t need his help; he immediately raised the huoqihua and lit it, his eyes only closing for a moment before the rocket exploded overhead.

“I’ll come too.” Seeing that they were having fun, Ji Qiuyue, who had originally shrank back and covered her ears, now eagerly stepped forward.

Wei Lanxiang grew anxious at the sound of his words, fearing she would be frightened, but the New Year had to be lively, so she quickly took a large huoqihua. This one was better—the ma leizi were too loud and, after lighting, you had to scamper off quickly in case your feet slipped.

Shen Yaoqing, who had always followed his wife’s lead, did so this time as well.

“That’s surprisingly enjoyable.” Ji Qiuyue couldn’t get enough of it; she laughed so hard that you could only see her teeth and not her eyes.
