Chapter 10


Shen Xuanqing washed his face with cold water in the yard, completely awake. When he led the dog from the backyard, Lu Gu had already taken the cloth bag Wei Lanxiang prepared from the kitchen, the bag containing the cakes baked yesterday.

“Er Qing, take the cake with you.” Wei Lanxiang, lying on the bed, heard the movement outside and shouted through the window.

“I know, mother,” Shen Xuanqing answered. After shouldering the hatchet and hemp rope, he took the cloth bag and walked to the door with Lu Gu, saying, “I’ll go out, you close the courtyard door and go back to your room.”

Previously it was Shen Yaoqing who came to close the door, but today it would be Lu Gu.

The village was so quiet that everyone in the room could hear them talking. Wei Lanxiang heard Lu Gu sending her son to the door and grew more and more satisfied with the new fulang. This was how a couple should be. Seeing that Shen Yaoqing didn’t need to go out and closed the courtyard door by himself, she rolled over and went back to sleep.

The wooden door creaked; Lu Gu stood at the door. Under the gray sky, the tall, strong man led the three dogs and turned around. Seeing Lu Gu’s stunned look, he said, “Okay, come in quickly.”

Only then did Lu Gu close the courtyard door as instructed; perhaps he had risen too early. Shen Xuanqing’s low, steady voice made him feel strange, though not afraid—just like when he had cracked an egg, and Shen Xuanqing helped him without telling anyone.

The man he feared the most was a good man.

Lu Gu realized this again. He could faintly hear the clucking of chickens in the backyard. The rooster hadn’t crowed yet. It was still early dawn. He went back to the room and lay down.

A wisp of smoke curled up. Lu Gu boiled water in the kitchen. Today there was no need to steam hot cakes. He sat on a small stool in front of the stove and ate the cake while adding firewood.

The Shen family liked to drink a bowl of hot water when they woke up in the morning. The doctors here often said that drinking raw water was harmful to the body, so even if the water cooled after a while, it had to be boiled first, so it wasn’t harmful. Now it was summer; drinking after cooling a bit was more enjoyable.

The water boiled after he had eaten half the thick cake. He pushed the wooden pot lid to the corner of the stove with a long spoon; otherwise the white steam would burn his hands and arms if he wasn’t careful.

Ji Qiuyue swept the yard and came in. Seeing several bowls of hot water on the chopping board, Lu Gu was still scooping the last bowl. Whoever wanted to drink water could just take a bowl and drink.

She sat down and asked Lu Gu, “Will we cook the loach?”

At Lu’s house, Lu Gu didn’t dare touch the food. Du Hehua watched him closely; even when he went to buy tofu, if he brought it back with even a slight break, she would beat and scold him. The tofu residue was still in the basket. Du Hehua also said that he had no skin and face and dared to steal.

When killing fish and chickens, Lu Gu was sent far away, and he couldn’t come back until the Lu family had finished eating.

When he was hungry, he found something to eat. He did eat loach as well, but without a knife or pot, he had to spend half a day striking fire with flint, then skewer the loach with a branch. The whole loach roasted on the fire, not cooked properly at all.

But when he came to Shen’s house, he couldn’t say he didn’t know anything. This was not free food. Lu Gu hesitated, looked at Ji Qiuyue, and whispered, “I’ll try it.”

Ji Qiuyue saw at a glance that he wouldn’t back down. Without forcing him, she said, “I’ll teach you. Loach is easy to kill without scales. It’s enough to dig out the belly and clean the contents.”

She Yan came to join in the fun, and Ji Qiuyue caught her and said, “Wait, you go to dig ginger.”

Lu Gu wasn’t stupid; he was smart, especially when he was doing something. He watched it once, grabbed a loach from the basin, took the knife in Ji Qiuyue’s hand, and soon learned a lot. He was clearly proficient.

“After boiling the medicine, take this pot and put the loach to simmer,” Ji Qiuyue said, pulling out a black pot. “You can just sit outside and watch the fire; you don’t have to do anything else.”

Lu Gu quickly nodded. He had eaten meat every day for the past few days and slept well at night. He looked better than before.

The small fishes were too small. After raising them for two days, they spit out the sand. They didn’t even need to open their bellies; it was enough to scrape off the fine scales on their bodies.

Shen Yan dug out a piece of ginger from the vegetable field so they could temper the loach’s coldness. The farmers planted onion, ginger, and garlic themselves so they wouldn’t have to buy them.

She went to the kitchen to cut a few slices of ginger, and when she came out, she grabbed a piece of burning wood from under the stove for Lu Gu to light the stove.

Lu Gu took the firewood, and at some point a spark fell on his shoe, and he hurriedly brushed it away with his free left hand.

Seeing that his feet were still the red-embroidered shoes from his wedding, Shen Yan asked, “Brother Lu Gu, why are you still wearing these?”
