Chapter 50


Lu Gu added a few more sticks to the bottom of the stove and hurried to check the courtyard gate. Two small dogs came back first, tails wagging around Lu Gu, noses to the ground as they sniffed at the puppy. Lu Gu scanned the forest and soon spotted Shen Xuanqing and Dahui.

Shen Xuanqing seemed to be dragging something; Lu Gu remembered he was still carrying a bamboo basket, and he wasn’t sure how heavy it would be, so he simply went to pick it up himself. “I caught a dwarf deer, and I shot its leg. I’ll nurse it for a few days and bring it down when we head back down the mountain.” Seeing Lu Gu approaching, Shen Xuanqing brightened. The dwarf deer had a cloth wrapped around its injured front leg, oozing green medicine and red blood stains, and it could not run. A hemp rope was looped around its neck, and Shen Xuanqing hauled it along the path.

The dwarf deer had quite a temper. At times it would tilt its neck forward to challenge Shen Xuanqing, pulling the rope backward and refusing to move. But Dahui roared fiercely at the side, making a gesture of attacking and biting it. The deer flinched, and finally moved forward. Shen Xuanqing was not used to it, and when it refused to walk again, he cracked the hemp rope a few times like a whip, so Dahui’s threats were no longer necessary.

Autumn had arrived; the vegetation hadn't withered completely, and wild fruits were ripe and lush. In this season, most of the wild animals in the mountains were fattened, and there were plenty of edible fruits and grasses, so game was naturally fatter.

“I’ll carry it.” Seeing that Shen Xuanqing would have to drag the dwarf deer, Lu Gu knew he couldn’t control it alone. If the deer broke free, all today’s effort would be wasted, so it would be better for him to carry the bamboo basket.

Not far from the yard, the bamboo basket wasn’t heavy, so Shen Xuanqing handed it to Lu Gu and said, “Dahui and the others caught two long-tailed pheasants, and they’re all inside.”

The basket was roomy, yet Lu Gu could lift it easily. Seeing Shen Xuanqing smiling all the time, Lu Gu’s mood lifted too. “I picked some persimmons, they’re all red—you can eat them after you peel them.”

“Okay, I’ll eat one.” Shen Xuanqing agreed, his handsome face breaking into a smile. Compared to the timid, fearful Lu Gu of before, a light smile now curved his brows and lit his eyes; it was clear he was in a good mood just by looking at him.

Back home, Shen Xuanqing hammered a wooden stake into the corner of the yard, driving it deep into the ground, and tied the deer with a short hemp rope. The puppy was very curious about the dwarf deer and followed Shen Xuanqing into the backyard while he worked. The chickens and ducks were fenced in, so the puppy couldn’t get at them; whenever he tried to bite, Dahui’s growl taught him a lesson. Now he had learned a little and only opened his mouth to bite when he was excited to play, but Lu Gu watched and snatched him away each time.

“Woof!” The puppy sprang forward, barking at the dwarf deer as if he wanted to show off his abilities. The dwarf deer, shorter than a sika deer but bigger than the puppy—especially since this one was an adult—stood its ground and faced the puppy.

When Shen Xuanqing finished his work, he washed his hands in the front yard and sat down to eat. The puppy was still playing in the backyard, barking powerfully. Even when the owner wasn’t there, he showed no fear toward creatures bigger than him.

The persimmons picked by Lu Gu were lined up on the kitchen window sill. Lu Gu had already drawn water for washing his hands. After Shen Xuanqing finished washing, he handed Lu Gu a soft red persimmon and said, “I’m still heating steamed buns and rice porridge, you can eat this first, and then have more later.”

“Yeah.” There was a smile in Shen Xuanqing’s eyes. The persimmons he picked himself weren’t as sweet as the persimmons fulang had brought back and handed to him, but he still enjoyed them. Lu Gu was washing the dishes in the kitchen. By now the courtyard door should have been locked, but Shen Xuanqing hesitated and went into the kitchen to help Lu Gu wash the dishes. He could put away the clean bowls.

“I want to hunt foxes these few days. It’s cold, and fox fur can be sold for a good price. Shifu says he has a network in Fucheng. If I can find good furs, the price will be higher than in town.” Lu Gu lifted his head and listened carefully without interrupting.

“It’s just that when I go out tomorrow, I reckon it’ll take a day or two to come back. Foxes are cunning and quick-witted, hard to catch, and they often come out at night, so tracking them is troublesome. I’m going out tomorrow, so I probably won’t be back at night.”

Lu Gu suddenly felt a touch of nervousness. He couldn’t bear to restrain Shen Xuanqing from hunting, but he Also didn’t want to be left alone. After a moment of hesitation, he asked, “Then where will you sleep at night?”

Shen Xuanqing said, “There’s no need to worry about that. Take the dogs and find a place to rest; bring thick clothes. One night is enough.” Lu Gu replied, “I’m not going to stay here at night, you must remember to lock the door, so come and check how I lock it.” With that, he walked out. Shen Xuanqing followed.

The thick tree trunk used to block the courtyard gate was heavy and made of sturdy wood. If a wild animal attacked the gate, it would be too light to resist.

“When I open the door tomorrow, I’ll lean it to the left and only open one door to go out. You can move it at night, and the door bolt can be secured when you shift it.” Shen Xuanqing wasn’t worried about himself, but he was concerned about Lu Gu who would be alone, and he planned to take all three big dogs with him tomorrow, leaving Lu Gu by himself.

“Okay.” Lu Gu nodded and made a note. He could manage by sliding the gate’s trunk to the middle rather than pulling it away from the wall; it would take only a little effort.

With that, they agreed and went to wash up, then fell asleep.
