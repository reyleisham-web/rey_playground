Chapter 116


After paying the market fee, Lu Gu and Shen Xuanqing found an open space and pushed the cart in. They shouted all the way, and Lu Gu opened the bamboo tube to take a few sips of water before passing it to Shen Xuanqing. Today they had brought two wooden chairs, so they could sit down and rest if they got tired after standing for a while.

The busiest time of the morning market had passed, and now there were fewer people doing business. It was already mid-morning, and fortunately the sun wasn’t very strong today. The small vendors who had arrived early were still holding on to their unsold goods, waiting for buyers to come.

"Duck eggs, fresh duck eggs."  
"Wild buds, freshly picked from the mountains."  
"Aunt, come and have a look. My eggs are cheap."

Whenever buyers with baskets passed by, many of them would start shouting and chatting to try to sell their goods. There were lots of people selling chicken and duck eggs. When they hawked along the road, they would meet women with small baskets who sold eggs. The poor people were always careful when selling eggs, afraid of breaking these precious things. The two of them were the same, so they walked slowly.

There weren’t many people like Lu Gu who carried the eggs in a bamboo basket. When he saw a woman coming over, he called out a few times as well.  
"So many." The woman was surprised to see him using a bamboo basket to carry the eggs. She stopped to join in and peered into the basket. She also said, "These eggs are smaller than others."

"Yes, aunt. My chickens and ducks are smaller, so the eggs are smaller too, but they taste the same. There’s no difference." Lu Gu said seriously, then he took one out and held it in his hand for the old woman to see.

Shen Xuanqing wanted to help his fulang, so he stood up to assist with the sales. He was taller than the average man, so when he sat on the back of the cart no one minded. But he suddenly stood up, which startled the short, thin old woman, and she instinctively took a half-step back.

Seeing the customer’s frightened face, Lu Gu worried that she might be scared away and lose the business, so he quickly said to her, "Don’t be afraid, aunt, this is my husband."  
Shen Xuanqing felt a bit powerless, but the other party was an old, frail woman, so he smiled and said, "I rose too quickly—don’t be afraid, aunt."

The fulang in front of her was gentle and spoke softly, and the man also spoke without any aggression, which made the old woman calm down. She came closer to look at the eggs and said, "Oh, your husband is really tall."

Hearing this, Shen Xuanqing smiled and replied, "Don’t underestimate these small eggs. Aunt, you won’t lose out if I weigh them by catty. Besides, small eggs have their advantages. Since they’re small, there will be a few more eggs per catty than the big ones. If there are children in your family who eat eggs, it’s the same even if you give them the small ones—you can even eat a few more."

"Yes, if you want to buy, the price isn’t expensive." Lu Gu nodded in agreement.

Shen Xuanqing’s words left the old woman momentarily stunned, then she seemed to understand. The family’s life wasn’t easy nor extravagant; they could afford to eat chicken and duck eggs, but they had to be frugal. These eggs were small, but the yolk and white were complete, and there wasn’t much difference from a large egg. The old man of the family had many rules and wanted to eat one egg every day. Since the money spent was the same, wouldn’t it be better to buy the small ones so they could eat eggs for a few more days?

The more she thought about it, the more she felt this made sense, so she said, "Okay, give me a catty."  
Lu Gu smiled as he took the eggs out of the bamboo basket. Shen Xuanqing took the scale from the cart. The old woman looked into the bamboo basket and added, "Don’t give me the ones covered with chicken shit. Take the clean ones."

Who would complain about the hassle of doing business? Besides, the old woman was easygoing and didn’t haggle over the price, so Lu Gu picked out some clean ones for her to weigh.   
Thirteen copper coins clinked into Lu Gu’s hand as he weighed them and tucked them into his purse. The clinking sound of the coins delighted him; his smile widened.

If it weren’t for the warm spring weather, the chickens and ducks would be flocking to lay eggs, and a catty of eggs would cost fifteen or sixteen copper coins. Fortunately, even if there were many eggs, the price wouldn’t drop too low. He had set the price at fourteen copper coins, but the old woman lowered it to thirteen copper coins. Anyway, he wouldn’t lose money, so he sold them.

The eggs would break easily if they were jostled, so they stayed at the market and kept selling until the afternoon.

For lunch, Lu Gu had plain noodles, something he hadn’t tasted for a long time. After the meal, Shen Xuanqing went to return the bowl and pay, and he also took out two eggs from the bamboo basket. He was puzzled, so Shen Xuanqing said that Lu Gu wasn’t full after one bowl of noodles, and he went to ask the stall owner to fry two eggs for him, giving the owner an extra two copper coins for their hard-earned money.

After selling things, Lu Gu could also eat the eggs laid by his own chickens. He ate them with great relish, his eyes full of joy.
