Chapter 83 Part 48


She had helped the Zhang family’s second daughter-in-law marry into the clan, and she talked about it all around the village. So even if the Zhangs’ second daughter-in-law believed her family was closest to the eldest household, she did not dare to step forward to grab the family property. Villagers lined the doorways and windows, neighbors listening in; even if Lao Zhang’s status wasn’t grand, if they bullied such an old widow, they would be stabbed in the back.

Just as Wei Lanxiang thought, as soon as Lao Zhang came out, Lin Zhongcai used the word “filial piety” to suppress the Zhang relatives.

Although Zhang Zhengzi and Shen Xuanqing were the same age, Lao Zhang was nearly ten years older than Wei Lanxiang. Her son was gone, and her daughter-in-law would perform filial piety on his behalf. In this world, widows were pushed out and their property divided. How could an old mother who had lost her son be evicted from her home? Wasn’t that a crime?

As for adoption, Lao Zhang didn’t trust anyone at the moment, so naturally she refused. She cried and yelled, and had someone chase away all the relatives in the yard. She even kicked up clods of dirt and threw them at those ugly-looking people, then spat at them.

Her hair was gray and her leg broken. Sitting there, her hands were stained with mud. Anyone who looked at her would think she was a little crazy. The Qingxi villagers watching from the door were soft-hearted and sighed at her pitiful state.

Only Lao Zhang knew where the house deed and the field deed were. She didn’t speak up; it was broad daylight, and no one dared to seize it openly. After a long quarrel, they couldn’t argue it out, so the Zhang family’s relatives left first.

The farce ended for the moment. Wei Lanxiang heard every word clearly at Quanzi’s house and sighed inwardly. Sitting with Quanzi’s mother, she couldn’t help but lament that Li Wanyun’s life was really bitter.

That night, the snow melted and dripped from the eaves, but the cold turned the melt into icicles. Shen Xuanqing, the tallest among them, didn’t even need a bamboo pole; he stood beneath the eaves, reached out, and snapped off the icicles.

The icicles were sharp—if they fell and hit someone, it would be bad enough; if one struck Ji Qiuyue, it would be worse. Since his mother had reminded him, he followed suit, so as not to let her keep pulling the topic back up.

There were quite a few icicles under the eaves, and Lu Gu came to help when he saw this. But he couldn’t reach them, even on tiptoe, so he found a wooden stick and knocked at the tip of the icicles.

“What do you want to eat tonight? I’ll cook it for you,” Lu Gu said, continuing to knock away at the icicles.

These days Shen Xuanqing had been eating at the Zhangs’ while helping them, and when he saw his fulang standing beside him, he smiled; when he asked what she wanted to eat, his smile widened even more. He said, “I’ll go to Meng Dayue’s to buy some meat, and I’ll ask my mother to make meatballs for us to eat this evening.”

In the past few days, he had feasted on vegetarian dishes, with no meat or fish, so he couldn’t help but feel a bit indulgent. The vegetarian soup at the banquet had been well made, and it reminded him of meatball soup.

“Okay, I’ll chop the scallions and ginger later, then stir-fry the white radish and a few steamed buns.” Lu Gu turned his head and saw Shen Xuanqing’s smile; his eyebrows and eyes curved, and his smile was bright and sweet.

When Shen Yan heard that the meatballs would soon be boiled, her weaving of the bamboo basket stopped. She sat in the main room and cried out, “Brother Erqing, is it true?”

Shen Xuanqing was amused by her greedy look and said, “Of course it’s true. When have I ever lied to you?”

“Then you go and buy it. It takes a long time to chop the meat. Brother Guzi and I will knock the icicles.” Shen Yan was very attentive; she found a long wooden stick and trotted over.

What she said was true—the meatballs would only be delicious if the meat was chopped finely. Shen Xuanqing smiled and plucked her by the pigtail, saying she was so greedy he didn’t know who she looked like.

Shen Yan’s pigtails were often pulled by Shen Yaoqing. Now even the second brother teased her, and when she grew anxious, she called for Lu Gu to help: “Brother Guzi, look at him.”

If it had been Ji Qiuyue and Shen Yaoqing, Ji Qiuyue would have slapped him on the back of the head already, but Lu Gu didn’t have the nerve to do so. Besides, Shen Xuanqing was much taller than him; he didn’t dare to reach out and hit him indiscriminately.

Because of Shen Yan’s words, Lu Gu instinctively looked at Shen Xuanqing, and before he could speak, he saw the tall man shyly withdraw his hand, scratch his head, and look a little embarrassed—as if afraid Lu Gu would call him out. He hurried away, saying he was going to buy meat.
