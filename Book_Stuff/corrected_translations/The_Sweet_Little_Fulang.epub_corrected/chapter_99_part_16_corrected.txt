Chapter 99 Part 16


In the end, there were a few not-very-good shepherd’s purses left at the bottom of the bamboo basket, so Lu Gu shouted that the price was only two copper coins. When an old woman heard him, she wanted to buy a catty.

Seeing the old woman tremble as she opened her handkerchief and found only a dozen copper coins inside, Lu Gu handed all the shepherd’s purses at the bottom of the basket to the old woman and said he would charge only two copper coins.

“How can you do that? Take it, take it.” The old woman was poor but reasonable, and she handed Lu Gu four copper coins.

Because not all the wild vegetables were dug by him alone, but also by Wei Lanxiang and Shen Yan, Lu Gu did not dare give everything away for free, so he took only two copper coins from the old woman’s hand and muttered under his breath, “This one isn’t good, it’s at the bottom, so two copper coins are enough.”

After finishing, he picked up the empty bamboo basket and went to look for Shen Xuanqing. The old woman sighed behind them, carefully wrapping the money in her handkerchief. She told herself she had met a good young man, and then she went home with the wild vegetables.

In the depths of the alley, Shen Xuanqing was unloading firewood. This family had bought half of the remaining firewood, a big customer. Naturally, he helped to move it in. If he wanted to sell firewood in the future, he could come to this family again and ask; at least it would be a familiar face.

He was tall and strong, able to carry a lot of firewood. He and the son of this family moved all the firewood in quickly.

Lu Gu came over and saw some women and fulangs chatting in front of several houses, so he placed the empty bamboo basket on the cart. Then he carried the bamboo basket over and lifted the cloth to reveal the contents inside.

“Handkerchiefs are cheap, three copper coins apiece.” He didn’t speak loudly, but because the items inside the bamboo basket were brightly colored, even those who hadn’t intended to buy gathered around to look.

He often came to town to sell things, and whenever he talked to people he would certainly meet impatient looks. Lu Gu had been timid and afraid, but after meeting a lot of people, even if people ignored him when he started a conversation, he wouldn’t feel embarrassed anymore; he could just move on to another customer.

“How do you sell this sachet?” a slim-faced fulang asked him.

“Forty copper coins for the sachet, five copper coins for the laozi, and ten copper coins for the colored big laozi like this.” Lu Gu named the prices one by one.

“So expensive?” The slim-faced fulang pretended to be surprised, as if he’d never seen such a high price.

Lu Gu, having seen many customers like him, frowned slightly and said, “It’s not expensive. It contains medicinal herbs and spices; those with fragrance are indeed expensive.”

“How about thirty copper coins?” the slim-faced fulang pressed, and the woman next to him chimed in.

Lu Gu had never sold fragrant sachets with medicinal herbs before, but his mother had sold them when she was alive, so he knew the price. His embroideries were good and meticulous, and not to mention there were medicinal herbs and a bit of spice in it; forty copper coins was really not expensive.

Lu Gu shook his head. “Thirty copper coins isn’t enough. My stuff is good, there are many embroidery flowers, and the thread to embroider costs money. Forty copper coins is already cheap.”

The slim-faced fulang picked up a sachet and examined it again and again, but he still couldn’t help bargaining.

“Then if you want to buy two, I’ll sell them for thirty-five copper coins each.” Actually, Lu Gu was not reconciled to selling his sachets for thirty-five copper coins, because he had poured care into each one and couldn’t bear to sell them too cheaply.

“Thirty copper coins; we’ll buy two.” The slim-faced fulang held onto the sachet, and while talking he fumbled for the money in his pocket, trying to force a deal.

The three people beside him also chimed in, saying that thirty copper coins was enough. The sachet was only so big, and the scent would fade after a few days; thirty copper coins was considered a good price; otherwise anyone would be cheated.

They talked too much, and Lu Gu couldn’t say a word. His heart filled with grievance as he muttered, “You give me the sachet, I won’t sell it.”

“I’ll just give you thirty copper coins.” The slim-faced fulang pulled out the copper coins while holding the sachet tightly in his hand.

Lu Gu grew anxious; he didn’t want to sell the sachet to this person, so he said anxiously, “It was agreed beforehand that the price is thirty copper coins if you buy two sachets; you’re only buying one, I won’t sell it to you.”

“One and two are the same.” The slim-faced fulang counted out thirty copper coins.

Lu Gu looked single-handedly powerless, and could not quarrel with them. Shen Xuanqing came out from another house, frowned when he saw it, and strode forward. “My fulang doesn’t want to sell it; how can you forcefully buy it at such a low price?”

A man taller than an ordinary man stood there, with a cold face and a fierce look, enough to intimidate people.

These women and fulangs were used to teasing and flattering, but when such a tall, strong man suddenly appeared before them, they couldn’t help muttering in their hearts, and their momentum weakened.

Lu Gu’s heart was full of his sachet, and when he saw the slim-faced fulang freeze in place, he pulled the sachet back from the other’s hand. As for the thirty copper coins, he didn’t even look at them.

In fact, if the other party had negotiated nicely, it would have been cheaper to buy two as they had agreed before, but these people’s words were too harsh; they still said that he was taking them for a fool and squeezing them.

“Let’s go and find Brother Daqing.” Shen Xuanqing said.

“Yeah.” Lu Gu nodded gloomily, and followed closely, feeling a sense of grievance that he found hard to bear.

The cart creaked as they rolled out of the alley. Lu Gu walked with his head down, and his left hand was suddenly grabbed.

“We won’t come here in the future. They’re not good people, and we won’t sell anything to them. There are a lot of customers in the town, and these people aren’t bad.” Shen Xuanqing squeezed Lu Gu’s hand.

It might be a bit much to call it anger forged in the fire of a common enemy, but it felt good to have someone standing with him, someone who wouldn’t let him swallow the grievance, someone who wouldn’t say, “It’s only a trivial matter, don’t take it to heart.”

Lu Gu didn’t understand all of this yet, but his grievance had now turned into dependence on Shen Xuanqing. He held Shen Xuanqing’s hand from time to time; his nose stung a little, but he nodded solemnly: “Well, I won’t sell anything to them.”
