Chapter 52


Some time ago they had been busy with the autumn harvest, too tired to notice anything else every day. He hadn’t shared a bed with Lu Gu for more than twenty days. He was still young and grew restless whenever he hugged his fulang.

Lu Gu was the most patient, saying little, but Shen Xuanqing grew more and more reckless, nearly banging his head against the headboard. Although he kept a lid on it, under the pain he finally couldn’t hold back his soft hissing and the slight panting in his throat, which trembled with pain.

In the dark, Shen Xuanqing’s ears were sharper than ordinary people’s; how could he not hear it?

The night was hazy, and the faint movements on the bed froze all at once. Shen Xuanqing stopped, silent for a long while, so stiff he could hardly speak.

Before they were wed, he had read the picture books, and had also heard some remarks from men his age in the village, saying that after the consummation there would gradually be joy. Shen Xuanqing didn’t utter dirty words, but he had heard others’ talk about the bed. The young men in the nearby villages knew each other and bragged to others. Such gossip circulated in whispers, and even he heard it. Many men felt that they could feel pride if they could have fun together, or even make the person next to them miss them.

He too felt pain when they shared the same bed for the first time, but it got better, and he supposed Lu Gu was the same as him, so he thought he could derive a lot of pleasure from it.

He never told anyone, but he made his fulang happy, so he felt he had power.

Yet the voice he heard just now told him Lu Gu had endured all this time, without any joy, even pain. The young man full of vigor had never imagined such a night could come, and he was struck dumb, frozen in place.

After the pain passed, Lu Gu suddenly noticed Shen Xuanqing’s stiffness. Reflecting on the sound he had made, he immediately thought he had done something wrong. He shouldn’t have made a sound. He was trembling all over.

The night grew quiet and oppressive; Shen Xuanqing didn’t move for a while. Lu Gu almost cried from fright, tears welling in his eyes. He could only bite his lower lip and endure the extreme shame, and then he moved under Shen Xuanqing, leaning into him.

Lu Gu was no longer the ignorant boy who didn’t understand anything; yet it wasn’t true to say he understood everything perfectly. He only knew that, by their past, now was not the time to stop, and he feared that he might offend Shen Xuanqing by doing something wrong. He tried his best to bring things back to the way they had been on those nights before.

The fulang took the initiative to press forward. It was really warm and fragrant, like soft jade. Shen Xuanqing, caught in his own stiffness, felt embarrassed and angry. He grew impatient and probed slowly.

What followed was the inevitable suffering and torment. Shen Xuanqing, who had always been confident, spent the night in a state of despair, almost unable to sleep.

Early the next morning Lu Gu opened his eyes and looked at the ceiling. Shen Xuanqing lay beside him; the two of them were not entwined.

He still remembered what had happened last night, so he was a little afraid to speak. But sunlight spilled through the cracks in the window, and if he laid down again, he wouldn’t be able to do anything today. He sat up carefully and planned to go downstairs to boil water and cook after he dressed.

He decided to cook scrambled eggs; Shen Xuanqing liked them, and perhaps eating them would keep him from getting angry. Lu Gu couldn’t think of another way.

Shen Xuanqing had slept on the outside, tall and blocking his exit from the bed. If he wanted to get out, he would have to turn Shen Xuanqing over, but how dared he do that?

Fortunately, Shen Xuanqing also woke. He sat up, took off his underwear, and stretched to reach the clothes tossed at the foot of the bed with his bare upper body. His slender, strong figure was on view, making Lu Gu drop his gaze and dare not look.

“You rest. I’ll go boil the water,” Shen Xuanqing said as he dressed. He asked again from beside the bed, “What do you want to eat? Hot steamed buns or pancakes?”

Lu Gu, who had shifted to the edge of the bed and was trying to put on his shoes, looked up at him. His expression was gloomy; the usual smile was gone. He panicked, his throat tightening, and whispered, “I’ll go, I’ll go.”

Shen Xuanqing frowned at the center of his brows, lowered his hands, and pressed Lu Gu’s shoulders, letting him sit back on the bed. With a look of helplessness, he pursed his lips and said, “You’re in pain. You should rest and recuperate. I’ll go.” After that, he ruffled Lu Gu’s tangled hair, offered a small, comforting smile, and went to boil the water.

Lu Gu sat on the edge of the bed in a daze for a moment, then frowned slightly, worried and aggrieved, not knowing what to do. He knew Shen Xuanqing had always been good to him. He had stayed up late working many nights before, and Shen Xuanqing wouldn’t let him work the next day, but this time it was different; Shen Xuanqing didn’t smile when he woke.

If he wanted to ask, he felt ashamed to ask such a thing in front of a man. He was anxious and had no choice, so he finally got up and folded the quilt.

After a day of rest, Shen Xuanqing went out again to hunt foxes.

Lu Gu watched him walk away with the three dogs at the door, then called the puppy back to the yard.

Thinking of yesterday’s embarrassment, even though he still worried about Shen Xuanqing not coming back at night, Lu Gu breathed a sigh of relief. He didn’t really know what had happened to Shen Xuanqing, but the look on Shen Xuanqing’s face was gloomy, and he didn’t dare to make a sound.

And two nights ago, he had shamelessly drawn close to Shen Xuanqing, and every time he thought about it, he couldn’t lift his head, unable to bear doing such a thing. So both of them spent a day in embarrassment, and the atmosphere between them wasn’t as harmonious as before.
