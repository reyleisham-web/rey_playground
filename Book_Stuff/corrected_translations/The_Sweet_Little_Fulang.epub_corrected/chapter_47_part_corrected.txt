Chapter 47


Hearing this, Yang Xian spoke to his four-year-old eldest daughter in a gentle, commanding tone: “Ni’er, take your xiaoma Guzi to find grandma first, and tell her that Uncle Erqing is here. Go quickly.” She looked like a New Year’s doll, obediently taking Lu Gu’s hand and stepping into the courtyard. The younger brother, almost two, seeing his sister go in, followed after like a little beetle.

“Grandma, daddy said Uncle Erqing is here.” Ni’er, at four, could speak clearly enough for adults to understand.

An old lady emerged from the kitchen. She seemed older than Wei Lanxiang, yet her clothes were neat and she had a lively energy. She wiped her hands on her apron when she saw Lu Gu, and smiled hesitantly: “This is…?”

Ni’er, having heard her father’s words, jumped ahead of the adults and proudly announced: “Grandma, I know, this is my xiaoma Guzi.” Yan Shi was momentarily stunned; Shen Xuanqing came in with a cart and, seeing her in the yard, called out: “Mother.”

“Hey!” Yan Shi was happy to see Shen Xuanqing, and the old lady laughed and chided: “My son told me about you before; the second boy has no conscience, so he doesn’t come to see mother.” “Am I not bringing my fulang to see you now?” Shen Xuanqing replied with a smile after he stopped.

“Mother.” With this single word, Lu Gu called out timidly. He understood why the old lady reacted as she did and why Shen Xuanqing had mentioned his name several times; no matter how simple he was, he could sense something.

“Okay, okay, I’m happier still that you’ve brought your fulang here.” Yan Shi took Lu Gu’s hand and patted it lovingly. Their second boy grinned, handsome as ever, and looked at fulang in a new light; she didn’t mind why Ahwen (Lu Wen) had become Guzi.

“There’s no shortage of food on the road. Mother steamed pumpkins and dates, so sit down and rest and eat.” Yan Shi said, pushing Lu Gu onto the stone bench in the yard and letting him sit, then hurried to the kitchen to fetch food and drink.

Yang Xian and Shen Xuanqing unloaded the items from the cart and came upon a basket of mixed hazelnuts and chestnuts. Yang Xian smiled: “So many; why don’t you save some for home?” “There are some left at home; bring some for you to try.” Shen Xuanqing picked up the bag of wild tea, opened it for Lao Yangtou, and said, “Shifu, this tea is for you.”

Lao Yang looked inside and saw his favorite wild tea, and his happiness was hard to hide.

“And this one,” Shen Xuanqing continued, showing him a fur coat for winter, the front piece old but sturdy. Seeing his apprentice care for him, Lao Yang’s eyes narrowed with delight, and he nodded in approval.

Yan Shi brought pumpkins and dates for Lu Gu, and made two bowls of honey water for him and Shen Xuanqing to drink. When she heard there was something for Lao Yang, she urged Lu Gu to try it quickly and then hurried back to the cart to see if there was anything for her.

Shen Xuanqing knew what she wanted and said with a smile: “Aniang, medlar is often soaked to drink; it’s good for the body. There are wild soapberries too, which my fulang picked up in abundance. The qiucai at home were dug by my mother—let you try something new.” After saying this, Shen Xuanqing pulled out some cloth and added, “This cloth is for you to sew new clothes. There’s plenty of fur for the master, and there are still some left—make a pair of fur shoes for winter warmth.”

The old lady took the cloth with a smile and teased, “I just said, you remember the old man and don’t think about me.” The cloth was thick and comfortable; Yan Shi turned aside when Lao Yang approached to look at it, stared, and wouldn’t let him touch it, thinking his hands were dirty. She hurried back into the room, tucked the cloth away, and brought Lu Gu some pastries and dried fruits when she came out again.

When Ni’er saw the sweet cake, she clamored to eat it. Lu Gu took a piece for her and for her younger brother. She ate the sweet cake and then went to the cart to look around; there were so many chestnuts and hazelnuts in the tall bamboo basket that her mouth watered. She had eaten both kinds before, so she knew they were edible.

Shen Xuanqing and Yang Xian carried down the rice, along with two live chickens in bamboo baskets, their feet tied. Looking down, they saw the chubby little girl in front of the bamboo basket reaching in and grabbing a handful of chestnuts and hazelnuts. She was four years old, and no matter how much she tried, she could only manage to grab two.

Ni’er clutched a chestnut and looked up at him, hiding her fat hands behind her back. She knew those things belonged to someone else, and felt a twinge of guilt when someone spotted her.

“Eat.” Shen Xuanqing said with a smile, and asked her again, “Will you peel it?” He smiled, and Ni’er smiled back, took a bite of the pastry, and shook her head, saying vaguely with a mouth full, “No, but my grandpa can peel it.” Shen Xuanqing peeled a chestnut from the basket, gave it a few more strokes, and handed it to her. Ni’er still held the pastry in her right hand, so she had to tuck the two chestnuts back into the bamboo basket.

“Chestnuts are raw; you can only eat one, and the hazelnuts will be cracked and eaten by Grandpa.” After Shen Xuanqing finished speaking, he saw her cheeks bulging with food, and she was still eating, though he didn’t know if she’d listened.

“Second boy, wash your hands quickly, sit down and rest.” Yan Shi asked Yang Xian to carry all the things into the grain room, and she fetched water for Shen Xuanqing to wash.

Lu Gu sat in front of the stone table; the younger brother had finished the sweet cake, spoke slowly, and, pointing at the cake on the table, looked at Lu Gu and urged him to take it: “Eat, eat.”

“Oh, you’re still eating—how much have you eaten today.” When Yan Shi heard that her grandson still wanted more, she snapped off a small piece for him.

In the alley next door, Wang’s little grandson had eaten too much and went to the doctor crying about a stomachache. She wasn’t afraid of her grandson overeating; she feared him getting hurt. That was the kind of suffering she dreaded.

Shen Xuanqing washed his hands and sat down beside Lu Gu; Yan Shi dismissed her grandson and sat down to talk with her second son.
