28 Part 22

Hearing this, the pride on Zhou Yunzhi's face suddenly froze. How could anyone take advantage of their family? A trace of contempt flickered in her eyes, and she grew wary in her heart. Bah, this shameless second daughter-in-law—our family didn’t eat such things often, but she wanted Yutao to buy it for her.

But she still maintained a kind face and quickly said: "You don't know, Yutao is always busy, and he only had two days free before letting me go with his father. The two of us didn’t dare stay longer; I came back after dark last night. If I let you go, I’m afraid he won’t be home when you arrive. We’re so far from town."  
She couldn’t stay any longer; she was afraid the Erfang (the second branch) would come and eat and drink at their house, so after a while she stood and said, "It's getting late now, your elder brother is in the field, I’ll bring him some water."  
"Then Sister-in-law, I won’t keep you." Wei Lanxiang put down her things and sent her out. Lu Gu and Ji Qiuyue also rose.

Zhou Yunzhi’s legs moved noticeably faster than when she had come. After she was out of sight, Wei Lanxiang muttered, "This dog looks down on people, and I care nothing for her things."

Shen Shunde was the eldest, Shen Shunfu the second, Shen Shunwang the third, and the three brothers had grown apart in their early years. After Shen Xuanqing’s grandfather passed away, the second and third sons seldom visited the old family.

After Shen Shunfu left, the Shen family’s Erfang was poor, while the Shen Shunde family was rich, but the whole clan was snobbish, despising the poor and worshipping the rich. When they met in the village, even if the Erfang people spoke to them, they would snort and ignore them, borrowing something from Erfang’s house now and then only to show their “face.”

After they paid off their debts, married Shen Xuanqing, and let the villagers know their family was rich, Dafang spoke to them again.

Wei Lanxiang was about to die of embarrassment. If Zhou Yunzhi hadn’t come to help when Shen Xuanqing got married, and if she hadn’t followed the village custom by bringing eggs and white flour—though not as many as others—their face wouldn’t have been passable; otherwise how could she have come today? The words she spoke were, of course, meant to drive Zhou Yunzhi away; otherwise she would have had to show off.

Ji Qiuyue said to her: "Mom, we don’t need to get angry with her, just keep our contacts limited."

Wei Lanxiang nodded; she wasn’t angry either. She had been angry two years ago, but there was no point in making a scene now, so she laughed again: "When the new rice is harvested, we’ll be happy, and we’ll go to a restaurant in town too. What kind of braised beef and duck feet—you can eat whatever you want."

Ji Qiuyue replied quickly: "Mother, you said that, I’ve written it all down."  
"I said it," Wei Lanxiang smiled and poked at her, afraid she wouldn’t remember.

Although she hadn’t tasted it yet, just talking about it was enough to make everyone extremely happy. Ji Qiuyue also told Lu Gu: "Guzi, remember to be polite when the time comes. Mother will pay the bills. You can eat whatever you want."  
Lu Gu also smiled faintly, said nothing, and nodded.

While they were joking, Shen Yan returned with bundles of chicken hay, and seeing them so merry, she asked curiously. Ji Qiuyue didn’t hide it from her, and Shen Yan’s almond eyes widened as she listened, then she kept calling "mother," causing Wei Lanxiang to poke her on the forehead.

For a while, the courtyard was lively, which made Lu Gu, who didn’t talk much, feel happy.

In the afternoon, Shen Xuanqing, who had been out for a long time, returned with a bamboo basket on his back. He had gone out in the morning and hadn’t even come back for lunch. When he left, he only said he would buy something, and Lu Gu didn’t dare ask more questions.

Seeing Shen Xuanqing return, Lu Gu set aside the embroidery he was doing and went to him, helping Shen Xuanqing unload the bamboo basket. The basket was covered with a lid, and through the gap Lu Gu could hear a soft chirping.

He set the bamboo basket on the ground; Shen Xuanqing reached out to lift the lid, a smile lighting his handsome face as he said, "I bought a few autumn chicks, and I’ll bring them to the mountain tomorrow for you to raise."

Lu Gu watched the little chickens and ducklings inside raise their heads and squeak; their calls were delicate and tender.

Unlike rabbits and pheasants, they often raised chickens and ducks here. Chicken eggs and duck eggs were precious and could be sold for money. For example, in the Shen family, the chickens and eggs belonged to Wei Lanxiang; she had the final say on whether the eggs were sold, and the money from the sales was spent by her and Shen Yan, with no one else allowed to see it. The same applied when Lu Gu was in the Lu family: the chickens belonged to Du Hehua, and even Lu Gu, who threshed the grass, didn’t get a turn to tend the eggs.

This was a symbol of status in the family, but Lu Gu didn’t understand that. He only knew that if these chickens and ducks were really under his care, then he truly belonged to this family, just like the rest of the Shen family.

Shen Xuanqing saw that he had been squatting in front of the bamboo basket, silent for a long time, so he said: "After we go up the mountain, I’ll build a fence in the backyard, then dig a small pond by the river, bring the water in, and put the ducks in it."  
"That part of the river is gentle, and the ducks can swim in the river when they grow up," he added, and saw Lu Gu reach into the bamboo basket, carefully touching the chicks and ducklings inside.

"It’s just that autumn chicks aren’t as easy to raise as spring chicks, so be more careful when it’s cold at night," Shen Xuanqing warned.

The sight of the fluffy little chicks and ducklings brought Lu Gu back to his senses; he no longer panicked, lifted his head, and asked cautiously, "For me?"  
Hearing this, Shen Xuanqing nodded subconsciously: "For you."

Only then did he truly feel it, and joy surged up. Shen Xuanqing had said it was for him, and the sight of the fluffy birds grew even more dear to him; he couldn’t put them down.

"When I left early this morning, I heard people say that the next village planned to kill pigs. I went to buy some meat to take to the mountain." Shen Xuanqing asked casually, "Do you want to buy anything? I haven’t left today, so tell me in advance."

Lu Gu shifted his attention from the chickens and ducks, looked up, thought for a moment, and whispered, "I want to buy a small jar of wine."

Shen Xuanqing was surprised; he hadn’t expected such a timid fellow to want wine. After a moment, he pressed a fist to his lips as if coughing to cover it up, nodded, and agreed as if he hadn’t thought about anything, not even asking Lu Gu what he planned to do with the wine.

Shen Xuanqing didn’t hesitate when he wanted to buy something. The bamboo basket Shen Yan used to have was in front of him, and he went out with Lu Gu, the basket on Lu Gu’s back.
