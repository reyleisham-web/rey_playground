Chapter 84


Shen Xuanqing hurried out, and even Lu Gu could sense his guilty conscience, not to mention Shen Yan.

Shen Yan was the youngest in the family, and her two older brothers treated her well, but sometimes even Shen Yaoqing, who was over twenty, teased her, and Shen Xuanqing was no exception. When her braids were pulled, she couldn’t beat her two older brothers, so she had to ask someone for help.

In the past, Ji Qiuyue could restrain Shen Yaoqing, and now, someone finally could restrain Shen Xuanqing; her joy was evident as she turned to Lu Gu and whispered, “Brother Guzi, you can help me more in the future—the second brother is too annoying.”

After Shen Xuanqing slipped out with a guilty conscience, Lu Gu was a little dazed, and he nodded automatically at her words.

Seeing that he agreed, Shen Yan laughed a few times, her happiness obvious, thinking that Brother Erqing wouldn’t bully her again in the future.

Lu Gu didn’t really understand Shen Xuanqing’s reaction, and he didn’t know how to help Shen Yan in the future. He had seen Ji Qiuyue beat Shen Yaoqing before. Could it be that she wanted him to help her like this?

Another chunk of ice broke off and fell hard to the ground, shattering into several pieces. The puppy wanted to come over, but fearing he would be hit by the icicle, Lu Gu hurriedly chased him away, forgetting what he was thinking just now.

When Shen Xuanqing returned from buying meat, Wei Lanxiang, Lu Gu, and Shen Yan went into the kitchen.

One person made a pot of hot meatball soup to be eaten while it was hot and refreshing, but there were many people in the family, so it had to be cooked in a big pot.

Wei Lanxiang’s meatballs were all the same size, and she could shape them very quickly with her hands. Lu Gu had never done this before, so he practiced a few times. Because he also wanted to stir-fry radish and steam buns, he made a few meatballs and went to cook the vegetables.

Shen Yan boiled water and added firewood; she also learned from Wei Lanxiang in the process.

After the delicious meatballs were cooked, Lu Gu’s share and the steamed buns were served.

There were ten meatballs in each person’s bowl. The minced meat was finely chopped, and the meatballs were tender when you bit into them. The broth was fresh and flavorful; even without shallots or chopped greens, it was delicious enough.

Wei Lanxiang drank the hot soup and felt a warm comfort spread through her. Seeing everyone in the family eating well, she smiled and said, “There’s still some in the pot, so you can scoop more. I made a lot today, I’m sure we won’t finish it in one meal. Tomorrow I’ll slice some radishes and add them—that’ll be good.”

Shen Xuanqing had bought a lot of meat, and the two brothers had hearty appetites, so Wei Lanxiang cooked in a big pot, determined to let her family eat well.

The hot soup, hot dishes, and hot steamed buns were warming, making everyone happy on a winter evening.

The sky was clear, and the ground had dried in the sun. Under the wall, sheltered from the wind, Wei Lanxiang and Ji Qiuyue moved chairs to bask in the sun, while they worked on shoe soles and sewed clothes—there was always something to do, and they rarely had a moment free.

After a while, Zhou Xiangjun brought Shen Yu over to visit. Country women did needlework in winter, and they loved sunny days like this. They could bask in the sunshine, and their hands wouldn’t be cold.

Usually, in such good weather, there would be many women and fulangs in the village gathering to gossip and laugh. Wei Lanxiang also enjoyed going to these gatherings in previous years.

This year, because Ji Qiuyue was pregnant, she didn’t go. There were too many villagers and children running about; it was the same to bask at home in peace.

Shen Yuping’s marriage hadn’t been discussed yet, so Zhou Xiangjun sighed a little and, as he spoke, Quanzi’s fulang came over with his baby in his arms.

Lu Gu hadn’t been home often, so he wasn’t very familiar with Quanzi’s fulang, but Wei Lanxiang was different. When she saw the chubby baby, she was overjoyed, hugging and playing with him for a while, and even Ji Qiuyue liked the baby very much.

Shen Yan came over to tease the fat baby. When she saw Lu Gu take the hoe, she jumped up quickly and said, “Brother Guzi, I’m coming too.”

She turned to Zhou Xiangjun and Quanzi’s fulang and said, “Third aunt, Brother Zhen, I’m going out first.”

“Go, be careful.” Wei Lanxiang reminded her. She felt at ease since Shen Xuanqing was going with them, so she didn’t worry too much and returned to talking with Zhou Xiangjun.

Shen Xuanqing put the hoe into Lu Gu’s hands and placed it in the bamboo basket on his back. Today was a good day, and Shen Yaoqing was also going up the mountain to dig winter bamboo shoots with a bamboo basket on his back. He had brought a hatchet and hemp rope to chop firewood. In a few days they would have accumulated a lot of firewood, and they used a cart to sell it in town; it provided some income, and firewood sold well in winter.

The bamboo groves were far away, and all four dogs in the family went with them.

Digging winter bamboo shoots was a laborious job, not easy. Finding underground shoots required experience. Shen Yan was young and rarely came to the bamboo groves. Lu Gu had dug them before; though not as experienced as Shen Yaoqing, he looked for cracks and bulges in the ground to find the bamboo whip (竹鞭) and followed it to locate the winter bamboo shoots. In the end, he dug up three shoots.

The winter bamboo shoots were buried in the ground; you had to search carefully and grope with your hands from time to time, or you might break the bamboo whip or the shoots if you kept digging with a hoe.

Shen Yan followed Shen Yaoqing to dig not far away. Lu Gu put the three winter bamboo shoots into the bamboo basket and pushed the dug soil back with a hoe. Seeing that Shen Xuanqing was digging the shoots, he carried the bamboo basket over to help.

Seeing him coming, Shen Xuanqing wouldn’t let him do it and smiled, “Go home and cook it with fried bacon; stewed chicken with bamboo shoots is also delicious.”
