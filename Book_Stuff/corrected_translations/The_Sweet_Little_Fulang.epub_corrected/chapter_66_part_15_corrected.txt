Chapter 66 Part 15


“I changed the water for Tang Pozi. It’s cold—put it under the quilt to keep warm.” Shen Xuanqing walked to the bed; he was a little guilty, his confidence not as strong as usual. Since that was the situation, Lu Gu’s nervousness subsided a little. He lifted the Tang Pozi and set it on the quilt, then drew the quilt over it and covered it.

Because Shen Xuanqing was there, Lu Gu lowered his head slightly and didn’t dare to speak freely. After bending over to place Tang Pozi on the bed, Shen Xuanqing stood up straight and felt a bit at a loss. He stood beside the bed for a while, not knowing what to say, so he reached out to rub Lu Gu’s sore head. Before his hand touched it, Lu Gu flinched back in fear.

The outstretched hand hovered in midair, and the atmosphere grew stagnant. After a moment, Shen Xuanqing withdrew his hand in embarrassment, scratching his head awkwardly. It was raining outside, and going into the deep mountains was difficult; the two of them couldn’t go hunting. Besides, Lu Gu was in the room. Even if he wasn’t shy, Shen Xuanqing wanted to stay with him to avoid dwelling on the moment and explained, “I only rubbed your head, nothing else.”

Lu Gu instinctively avoided the touch and froze, afraid of angering him, but when he heard this sentence, he lowered his head and let out a muffled nasal “huh.” Shen Xuanqing felt a little happier again, but he knew he shouldn’t be too pleased, so he sat on the bed and gently rubbed Lu Gu’s head again.

Lu Gu’s hair wasn’t tied up; it hung loose down his back. It was black, soft, and smooth, easy to touch.

“I’ll massage your waist and legs for you.” Shen Xuanqing didn’t forget the mischief he had caused. He knew that treating fulang well—buying things was a sign of kindness to fulang, and buying cloth to make clothes was the same—but he hadn’t learned from others how to treat fulang well after their intercourse, so he could only do what he thought was good. It’s rare in the village for a big man to massage his wife’s waist and legs, but when he thought that Lu Gu’s skin was so tender and he’d been tossed about by a rough man like him, he didn’t feel any shame at all.

Lu Gu had never been served like this before. When he was nervous, he found Shen Xuanqing honest and serious. He moved his leg over and let him massage for a while, and he felt much more comfortable.

Seeing Lu Gu gradually relax and the atmosphere ease, Shen Xuanqing was happy, and even raised his head with a bright smile, a warmth that could make anyone feel sunshine even without the sun.

Lu Gu, too, felt confused about this: why did Shen Xuanqing smile so beautifully today after treating him so cruelly yesterday?

It rained for two days, and the clouds didn’t clear until the morning of the third day, when the sun shone into the forest. After lunch Lu Gu rested for a while, feeling a bit bored, so he shouldered the bamboo basket and went out to pick mushrooms.

“You going out?” Shen Xuanqing stood in the sun, chopping firewood, and asked when he saw the bamboo basket.

“Well, the mushrooms should have grown up. I’ll pick some, and I can fry them this evening,” Lu Gu replied, with the basket on his back.

Shen Xuanqing laid down the long axe in his hand and said, “I’ll go with you.”

The ground was dry, but there were many fallen leaves and grasses in the forest, and it was fine for people wearing shoes. The puppy would certainly get its paws and limbs dirty if they went out walking, so they needed to find a cloth to wipe it off when they returned; otherwise it would chafe their calves. Shen Xuanqing told Lu Gu this, and the two of them only brought Dahui and Dabai with them, locking the courtyard door and leaving Dahei behind to keep the puppy from barking or causing trouble in the yard.

Dabai wasn’t as clever as Dahui, but he was smarter and more loyal than Dahei. At least he’d never bared his teeth at Lu Gu. After the rain, the air was a touch cooler, and when they stepped outside for a breath, they felt refreshed. There were signs of a small path they often followed, and the grass along the ground was sparser.

After the rain, more leaves fell, many trees stood bare, and the marks of water on the ground hadn’t fully dried. After walking for a while, the soles of their shoes were splashed with wet mud, and the shoes grew heavier. Soon they had to stop and pick up a branch to scrape the mud from the soles.

It took more than an hour to go around the mountain, and they gathered mushrooms to fill about half a basket, so they stopped to rest after such a long walk. Lu Gu sat on a white stone by the stream; the stone, warmed by the sun, was still a little hot to the touch, so he sat down. Shen Xuanqing crouched by the bank of the mountain stream, washing some wild fruits that were entirely red. The shriveled ones were not picked, but the plump ripe ones were, and there were only five of them. After the rain, many wild fruits rotted and fell to the ground, so the future would be bleaker, leaving them with only a few to eat now.

Lu Gu took the washed fruit, peeled the skin, and tasted it. It was sweet, and his eyebrows and eyes curled into a soft smile. The sun lit his face, making his smile even gentler and more beautiful. Shen Xuanqing, about to sit down beside him, couldn’t hold back and hugged Lu Gu, curling his legs around him. He wasn’t going to let Lu Gu cuddle him to sleep tonight, but for now, at least, Lu Gu could cuddle him.
