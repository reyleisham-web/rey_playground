Chapter 59 Part 10


There was still sawdust for lighting the fire, but there wasn’t much igniter grass left—no longer easy to ignite. The villagers called that kind of grass igniter grass, or velvet grass. After drying, it turned yellow and the blades crumbled, and it could be used to light a fire; the dry stalks could also be burned. The puppy was running ahead; Lu Gu found a clump of igniter grass and stopped, fearing the puppy would go too far, he shouted: “Guaizi, come back.” The puppy kept darting around, and rubbed Lu Gu’s calf when he came close. The scythe was sharpened, afraid of hurting the puppy, Lu Gu pushed him aside with his leg.

The leaves of the wild wolfberry tree were gradually turning yellow. Lu Gu picked all the wolfberries around the courtyard, then went farther off to mow the grass. Today he planned to find some wolfberries and wild soapberries. After it rained, the mushrooms should grow out too. Not long after autumn, the red and yellow leaves on the hillside were very beautiful, and there were mountain flowers in bloom. By now, most of the vegetation had turned yellow and looked a bit bleak.

As Lu Gu walked, he suddenly saw a section of shed snake skin; its color was like withered yellow grass. He was startled, stepping back quickly, and carefully explored the grass with the long branch in his left hand. The puppy followed his legs. The dog was keener than people, just like Dahui. They could tell if there were bamboo rats and voles in the dirt holes on the ground, and whether there were snakes in the grass. The puppy was usually a bit naughty, yet he was also very perceptive. He didn’t bark at the grass, and Lu Gu believed there were no snakes inside.

The snake sloughs looked scary; if straightened they would be quite long—looking like a big snake. Lu Gu was a little scared; the mountains’ most dreaded danger was poisonous snakes. He clenched the sickle in his hand, took the puppy and left this place, preferring to go where the grass was low and could be seen clearly at a glance. Although snake sloughs could be used as medicine, not all of them could be used. He couldn’t recognize what kind of snake it was, so he didn’t dare to pick it up.

After walking farther away, Lu Gu felt a little more at ease. He saw some mushrooms in the woods not far off and picked some edible ones. He pressed the igniter grass down in the bamboo basket to make room for the mushrooms. In the densely wooded forest, the sun didn’t shine much, and a layer of fallen leaves rested on the ground. The rainwater on the leaves hadn’t dried completely, and the dog’s paws were all wet, and the fur on the legs grew darker.

Lu Gu gathered a lot of mushrooms in the forest, and when the bamboo basket was full, he shouldered it and walked out of the woods. The things on his back were quite heavy. He looked for wild soapberry trees on the way back. He and Shen Xuanqing still had quite a few, and there was no need to pick more recently, but now he really found one; he also found some meddlars. He could bring them when they went down the mountain.

Carrying goods along the mountain road wasn’t easy; he had come out far, and it was already past noon. After they went back to have a meal, he would trim goji berry branches. Autumn was almost over, and the two, diligent as they were, were busy in their own tasks. Shen Xuanqing went out for two days to hunt foxes and came back only on the third day. Feeding live prey meant they couldn’t take care of other things while busy; at night, when they hugged and slept, they could kiss for a while at most.

This time they stayed on the mountain quite a while, and they didn’t come down until more than twenty days later. First, Shen Xuanqing wanted to hunt more foxes; second, Lu Gu worried that so many fish wouldn’t dry properly. If it rained during the days they were away, the undried fish would pile up damp and moldy, so they needed to dry them in the sun before storing.

The two usually went down the mountain once every half a month. The mountain road was so far away that they couldn’t go down often, or they would waste a lot of time on the road.

Lu Gu and Shen Xuanqing walked down the gentle slope, and at a glance they saw the wall of their own courtyard and the persimmon tree outside the house. The red persimmons were all picked, with some left for the birds to eat. The three big dogs had long legs and ran home first, followed by the puppy. Wei Lanxiang, who was in the yard, heard the dogs barking earlier, put down what she was doing and came out in a hurry, not waiting for the two of them to arrive. Stepping forward, Ji Qiuyue greeted them directly.

“Give it to me quickly.” Seeing Lu Gu carrying the bamboo basket, Ji Qiuyue took it with a smile. The bamboo basket contained dried igniter grass, which Lu Gu had specially brought back. A basket of igniter grass wasn’t too heavy, so he carried it all the way instead of putting it on the cart.

“If the two of you don’t come back, mother will let Daqing go to the mountain to see you two.” Ji Qiuyue said while walking with the back basket on her back.

This time they hadn’t come back for so long. Wei Lanxiang looked at the gate of the courtyard many times a day, and asked Shen Yaoqing last night if he wanted to go up the mountain to look for them, otherwise she would be worried if she didn’t hear anything from them.

Hearing this, Shen Xuanqing pulled the cart forward and said, “Mother, there’s no need to worry. It’s autumn and not very cold yet. Before winter comes, I want to hunt for a few more skins, so we’ve stayed longer.”

“Yes.” Wei Lanxiang replied again and again; her second child was the most down-to-earth—if there was anything, there was a dog to report the letter. She took a look at the goji berries and half a sack of wild soapberries in the cart, and knew that it was Lu Gu who had picked them. She turned her head, laughed, and said, “Guzi is really capable. Did you also dry this dried fish?”

Before Lu Gu could respond, Shen Xuanqing answered with a smile, “It’s him.”

Lu Gu thought to himself that Shen Xuanqing had put in a lot of effort catching and killing the fish; how could Lu Gu take all the credit? He spoke up, embarrassed, “Erqing was also busy; we did it together.”

Wei Lanxiang looked at the two of them. The more harmonious they were, the happier her heart.
