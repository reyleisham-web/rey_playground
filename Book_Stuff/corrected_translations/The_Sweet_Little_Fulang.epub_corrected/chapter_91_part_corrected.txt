Chapter 91


“It’s really beautiful.” Quanzi’s mother held the ruyi knot and touched the persimmon tree with her hands, unable to stop praising it. She liked it very much and asked Ji Qiuyue, as if puzzled, “How did you grow your knots? How do you know it looks good when it’s hung like this?” (Ruyi: as one wishes; a symbol of power and good fortune.)

Not to mention the women, even the men in the village found it beautiful to see these knots hanging on the branches.

Seeing that she liked it, Ji Qiuyue drew two ruyi knots from the basket and said, “Since Auntie thinks it looks good, Auntie should hang them up too.”

Quanzi’s mother and Aunt Miao took the ruyi knots, their faces lighting up with happiness as they laughed, then they went home to get busy.

The persimmon tree was outside the yard, and even Lu Gu knew that after tonight there would likely be fewer laozi and ruyi knots left on the branches, so they hung only a few of them, all small. It would be fine if someone took them. Ji Qiuyue also told him that it would be a blessing for everyone during the Chinese New Year.

After hanging the laozis and ruyi knots, they weren’t idle. The couplets, door gods, and Zhong Kui’s portraits had to be pasted, and peach talismans had to be hung in front of the gate. Wei Lanxiang even bought Ji Qiuyue a New Year picture-doll; it was white and plump, holding a red carp. She had Haosheng paste it up.

“A little to the left.” Lu Gu stepped back a few paces and directed Shen Xuanqing on the ladder to paste the horizontal scroll, while Shen Yan helped hold the ladder. It was so busy that noon arrived in the blink of an eye.

After lunch, Lu Gu looked at Shen Xuanqing eagerly a few times, and Shen Xuanqing understood what he meant, saying, “Mother, we will go to Anjia Village to visit mother-in-law’s grave first, and then we will go to Nanpo to visit my father with my elder brother when we come back.”

There were many things going on today. Lu Gu was afraid of delaying the family affairs, so he pulled Shen Xuanqing into bed with him last night, even grabbing the corner of his clothes, saying that they wanted to go to his mother’s grave at noon, and they could come back sooner.

“Alright, go.” Wei Lanxiang had always been considerate of Lu Gu, and she didn’t care about it.

If the girl’s parents or Shuang’er’s had passed away after they got married, they didn’t have to go back to visit the grave by themselves during the Chinese New Year. The brothers in the family would go by themselves. After all, they were married and went to someone else’s house.

But Lu Gu’s mother only had one child. After Lu Gu got married, the Lu family might not necessarily visit her grave every year. Besides, Anjia Village was so close, and even Shen Xuanqing listened to his fulang, so Wei Lanxiang could hardly say anything.

The paper money, joss sticks, and offerings in the basket were all bought by Lu Gu himself at the fair, and now he and Shen Xuanqing hurried to Anjia Village after mentioning it.

As soon as they arrived at the grave, sure enough, there was nothing in front of the grave, and no one from the Lu family had come.

The two had come here before to burn some paper money and offer winter clothes during the festival, but now Lu Gu was no longer the same as before. When he came to the grave, his nose pricked and he wanted to cry. Now he could eat well and wear warm clothes, and no one would beat him anymore, so the grievances in his heart were fewer. At most he missed his mother, but he could still hold back his tears.

After talking for a while and burning the paper money, he wiped his tears and returned with Shen Xuanqing.

Back home, Wei Lanxiang prepared the New Year’s gifts and New Year’s wine, and asked the two brothers to bring their wife and fulang, and Shen Yan to the relatives of the same clan. There were several houses, and it took a while to sit in each one.

The New Year gifts in the gift boxes included salted duck eggs, dried fish, walnuts, chestnuts, and peanuts. The third-room family could have a little more, for Shen Yu was Lu Gu’s embroidered purse, Zhou Xiangjun was an embroidered handkerchief, bacon and New Year’s wine were for Shen Shunwang and Shen Yuping to eat and drink.

The New Year’s wine this year was served in small red lacquered wooden barrels with lids. It was more face-saving than serving it in bowls. The red color was also very festive. When they came out, they were seen by the villagers, and some people asked how much the wooden barrels cost.

After they delivered the gifts for the New Year, other people also came to their home, and the lively afternoon passed like this.

As soon as dusk fell, Lu Gu and the others were busy in the kitchen, steaming the meat and cooking the dumplings. They happily cooked a tableful of delicious dishes: meat, chicken, duck, and fish; vegetarian dishes, tofu, peanuts; dried mushrooms and machicai picked by Lu Gu, soaked and fried into a tasty dish. The New Year’s Eve dinner was very rich.

After the food was served, Shen Xuanqing lit the brazier and placed it in the middle of the yard. The charcoal used inside was good charcoal, more burn-resistant than firewood, so it could burn until dawn without needing to be replenished.

The New Year’s wine filled the cups. Shen Xuanqing spoke a few auspicious words, and the whole family laughed and toasted to welcome the New Year, all very happy and lively.

Eating meat and drinking wine made life feel warmer.

When they were full and merry, they didn’t wash the dishes. The whole family went to the yard and threw the bamboo joints into the brazier. The bamboo crackled and crackled, just like firecrackers.

After the bamboo joints exploded, it was time for the real fireworks.
