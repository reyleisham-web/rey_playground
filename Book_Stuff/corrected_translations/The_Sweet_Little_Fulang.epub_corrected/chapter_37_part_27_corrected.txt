Chapter 37 Part 27


After eating, Shen Xuanqing had nothing to do, so he sat down with a small ax and split firewood. The sun was hot, so he moved to the shade under the eaves.

Lu Gu gathered the eggs from the backyard and stowed them in the kitchen. Watching Shen Xuanqing chop, he paused at the doorway, hesitated, and then squatted to collect the chopped firewood. But before he could pick them up, he looked up at Shen Xuanqing.

“What?” Shen Xuanqing asked instinctively.

Lu Gu spoke anxiously, “Could you go up to the mountain tomorrow? The chickens and ducks are still up there.” His voice was barely above a whisper, and it trembled a little, showing how nervous he was.

When they returned yesterday, they had driven the chickens and ducks back to the yard and laid out a lot of grass and chaff for them to peck at. Lu Gu had been careful, placing the hay nest in a sheltered spot and covering it with a bamboo basket, with a slanted opening beneath the mouth of the basket. The chickens and ducks were used to sleeping in hay nests at night, and they would squeeze in on their own when it grew cold. Now that they had grown up, they were fluffy and huddled together, and they wouldn’t freeze to death. But this time he had to hurry down the mountain, which left Lu Gu uneasy. It would be fine for a day or two, but if he was away longer, he would worry. He thought for a moment before daring to discuss it with Shen Xuanqing, though he hadn’t forgotten the conversation with Wei Lanxiang. Afraid that Shen Xuanqing hadn’t rested well, he swallowed hard and said carefully, “Then we’ll leave the day after tomorrow. You can rest when we go up the mountain, and I’ll cook and work.”

Counting yesterday, today the chickens and ducks had only been up on the mountain for a day, and they had brought back plenty of grass, so going back the day after tomorrow wasn’t impossible. Shen Xuanqing had always been unusually fond of those chickens and ducks, and seeing that Lu Gu wanted to return but was timid, he nodded and said, “Then go up tomorrow. Rest at home is also rest.”

Hearing this, Lu Gu was deeply grateful and thanked him repeatedly, not knowing what else to say. He picked up the bundle of fine firewood and carried it into the firewood room. Shen Xuanqing kept working, but Lu Gu didn’t dare to rest. He sat by the side, waiting for Shen Xuanqing to finish chopping so he could haul the wood into the woodshed.

When Wei Lanxiang came out of the room, she saw the two under the eaves—one chopping firewood, the other hauling it—and she thought they looked like a young couple accustomed to their daily life, so she didn’t bother them and went out.

Shen Xuanqing had originally frowned a little because Lu Gu’s fear of him bothered him, and he didn’t know how to dispel it. But Lu Gu didn’t leave; he sat beside him, obediently. Suddenly this made Shen Xuanqing drop his other worries, and his eyes brightened with a rare smile. Feeling pleased, he thought that although Lu Gu was still afraid of him, he no longer only nodded and agreed in silence, nor did he dare to object. He had spoken up today, telling him that he wanted to go to the mountain early, showing that he was not as afraid as before.

“I’ll go for three to five days at most. You don’t need to cook; go to Uncle’s house for a meal, and I’ll cook for you when I get back.”

Ji Qiuyue summoned Shen Yaoqing into the kitchen, lifted the bamboo steamer lid, and explained that the steamed buns and pancakes were all inside and could be warmed if he wanted to eat. There was still some rice wine at home, and if he couldn’t make rice porridge, he could make rice wine to drink. With these, he wouldn’t go hungry.

“Sure, I’ll see,” Shen Yaoqing replied. “I’ll take the remaining river eel over there, buy a few more pieces of tofu, and bring them to my third aunt to cook. I’ll eat them for the next few days.” Shen Yaoqing didn’t obsess over meals the way some people did and noted that sometimes things could be eaten for two or three days without having to do it himself.

When Ji Qiuyue heard this, she said, “It’s fine. You go eat, and I’ll bring a basket of chestnuts for my third aunt when I come back.”

Before the dew dried, the sun rose in the east like a salted egg yolk. Shen Xuanqing pulled the empty cart and headed into the mountain with Lu Gu, Wei Lanxiang, and the others. There were many people entering the mountain this time, making the trip even livelier. Even the dogs were a little excited, barking and wagging their tails.

Ji Qiuyue spoke briskly, and Wei Lanxiang was easygoing and not strict. After three years, she had grown familiar with the eldest daughter-in-law, and they chatted and laughed along the way, with Shen Yan occasionally interrupting. Lu Gu listened to the commotion.

They rested for a while, and Lu Gu went to pick mountain flowers with Shen Yan, feeling that the journey would pass more quickly this way. When they reached the mountain, Lu Gu hurried to the backyard as soon as he opened the door. The chickens and ducks were fine, but after two days the backyard fence was a bit dirty. He took a shovel and began tidying up, digging the excrement and dirt from the ground into a heap. He planned to later find some green medicinal leaves to spread around, at least to mask the odor and make it cleaner.

Wei Lanxiang saw that the courtyard and the kitchen were neatly tidied, much more orderly than when Shen Xuanqing was alone, and she realized Lu Gu’s diligence, which brought a few more smiles to her face.

She was hungry after the long walk, so she and Ji Qiuyue cooked in the kitchen first. When Lu Gu came out from the backyard and saw that there was nothing he could help with in the kitchen, he went to the east room to sweep and make the beds.

Shen Yan followed him in to help. The bed in the east room was quite large, and the three of them could squeeze onto it. It was cold on the mountain, but it would be warm enough to sleep close together at night.
