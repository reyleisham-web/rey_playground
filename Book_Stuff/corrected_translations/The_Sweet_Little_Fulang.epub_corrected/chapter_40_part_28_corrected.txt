Chapter 40 Part 28


Lu Gu didn’t dare refuse or speak; the young man helping him rubbed his legs and bowed his head in silence.

As they rubbed, Shen Xuanqing’s Adam’s apple bobbed, his downturned, starry eyes growing dim. Lu Gu’s pallor stood in sharp contrast to the other man’s sturdy legs; Lu Gu’s own legs felt soft and smooth, even his toes were round and cute.

So the hands slowed, the rubbing growing gentler, loaded with a meaning Lu Gu could not name. Even Lu Gu, who understood little of emotion, could sense the charm, but he didn’t dare breathe, and his heart trembled slightly. He lifted his eyes, nervous and confused, and looked carefully at Shen Xuanqing’s face.

Shen Xuanqing, gradually losing himself, brushed the fair skin lightly with his warm, rough palms as if blasphemously tasting it, until he inadvertently raised his eyes and the dark color in them frightened Lu Gu.

Seeing Lu Gu’s terrified expression, Shen Xuanqing woke up with a start. He froze, and it took him a long time to recover. He pretended to set Lu Gu’s legs down casually and said, “It’s getting late, let’s go back.”

Lu Gu hurriedly put on his shoes and socks; the trousers snugly covered his legs, and the texture of the fabric gave him a sense of ease he hadn’t felt before. He didn’t quite understand what was happening, but he knew he was embarrassed, so he didn’t dare to look at Shen Xuanqing again.

There were plenty of watercress here, so Lu Gu squatted by the stream and picked about half a basket of the tender tops. When he finished, Shen Xuanqing appeared, today carrying his big bamboo basket as usual, with half a basket of snails in it. It showed how much he had found today, enough to eat for a long time. He tied the sack containing the small crabs to prevent them from climbing out and put it in a bamboo basket to carry along.

Lu Gu followed him back with a smaller bamboo basket on his back, and the two small dogs trailed after them unhurriedly. Compared with their previous journey, the two were a little quieter and more uncomfortable. On ordinary days, even after having intercourse, Shen Xuanqing wouldn’t feel anything; this was his fulang, intercourse was a matter of course, but now he couldn’t figure out why he cared so much.

As they walked, Lu Gu stopped to pick wild tea. He’d seen them on the way back before and told Shen Xuanqing to pick some when he returned. The wild tea he’d picked last time had already half-dried in a basket. He picked it often, since he could carry it down the mountain to Wei Lanxiang and the others, and sometimes to trade with the villagers.

Shen Xuanqing came to his senses and began picking tea leaves as well. It wasn’t as embarrassing as before. While picking the tea leaves, he said, “I saw a lot of them on the other side earlier, and I picked them another day. When Master was here, I would pick them for him; he liked mountain wild tea.”

“Yeah,” Lu Gu nodded and agreed in a low voice, and when the bamboo basket on his back filled up, the two set out again.

In the yard, three bamboo plaques lay on a horizontal wooden frame to dry goji berries, and two large bamboo plaques lay on a pile of firewood to dry wild tea. Chestnuts and hazelnuts were air-dried in a cool, ventilated main room; otherwise the sun would dry out the kernels and they wouldn’t taste sweet.

There was also plenty of slender grass in the front yard. After drying, it could be used for fire or as nests for chickens and ducks. In late autumn, when rain was frequent, the grass in the nests could be replaced often. There was straw in the house at the foot of the mountain, but not much had been brought up. The mountain road was far away, and it took effort to carry everything, so it was better to dry your own hay here.

The mushrooms he picked were sliced and shredded to dry, and he put half of them in a clean cloth bag. It rained yesterday, and he would go out again in the next two days. There would surely be plenty in the forest and on the mountain paths.

Autumn colors were deepening, and the weather was colder than before. In previous years, he was anxious at this time. When winter came and there was no food outside, he would be cold and hungry. This year was different. He had food and clothing, but he still cared about winter, so he wanted to save every bit of winter rations he could. He even dried some wild vegetables, packed them in sacks, stored the mushrooms in a cool, dry low room, and placed two baskets of dried goji berries there as well.

Lu Gu moved the goji berries on the bamboo plaque with his hands, turning them evenly for better drying. He was still thinking about when he went out and saw a lot of horse-tooth vegetables (马齿菜) along the road; they were edible. The tender ones could be fried or steamed, and once dried in the sun, they could still be eaten in winter.

As long as there was enough water, horse-tooth vegetables grew very vigorously, and there were few other wild vegetables; it should be possible to dry these, and there were underground vegetables as well. When he went uphill with Shen Xuanqing, they weren’t far away; you could pick them up for eating, and dry them to eat in winter when you carried them back.

Compared to mushrooms, wild vegetables were not worth much, so Lu Gu always put them in sacks. He also prepared a sack for dried mushrooms. After two days, sweet-nest roots (甜窝根) could be dug up. They were also wild vegetables, but they were eaten in chunks, and the larger ones could be as big as his fist. When dug up, they were piled in the corner of the wall, covered with soil, and could be eaten until the next spring.

There was a lot of work to do, and Lu Gu hurried into the kitchen to cook while thinking.
