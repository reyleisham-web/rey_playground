Chapter 98 Part 19


Zhang Zhengzi was a bastard, but he hadn’t sunk to idleness back then, and he still tended the plow field—even if he wasn’t very proficient—so he didn’t waste their family’s fertile lands.

Because they were in the same village, the Shen family’s second block of paddy fields happened to be right next to the Zhangs’, so Shen Xuanqing knew well the state of the Zhang family’s paddy fields.

It was decided that the land deed would be handed to Lin Zhongcai, the village head, and once the deed was stamped with the official seal and passed along the proper channels, even the Zhangs’ kin would be unable to cause trouble. Shen Xuanqing agreed.

Li Wanyun was a woman, so Shen Xuanqing would go to Fucheng by himself to obtain the official seal. In Fucheng, with just a few more words, as long as the land tax was paid, it would be settled smoothly. The folks who could go pay the tax were honest farmers. How could he not do it? He had asked Boss Lu to go with him before, to avoid government inquiries and save trouble.

Eleven acres in total. From Li Wanyun’s point of view, there would be no hatred with Lao Zhang’s family. Shen Xuanqing bought all the land at a price of five taels of silver per acre. The five acres of paddy fields had nothing to grow in winter, but winter wheat had already been planted on the six acres of dry land. With water and fertilizer, the winter wheat would be harvested in May.

When handing over fifty-five taels of silver to Li Wanyun, Shen Xuanqing glanced at her. Since his hunting skills had become more proficient, his intuition had grown sharper too. When he followed Wei Lanxiang to see Lao Zhang, he felt the other party’s condition was no longer good.

Unlike his close relatives, Lao Zhang had beaten and cursed Li Wanyun in the past two years. He thought that buying medicine for her was a waste of money, and Li Wanyun should try to break free from the Zhang family’s quagmire. But when he thought it over again, if Li Wanyun wanted to rescue someone, that would be her own business; how could it be his place to meddle, or risk the bad reputation of not rescuing and treating the injured and ill? Besides, when he had wanted to cure his father back then, he never let go of any opportunity. Now, if he discouraged others from providing medical treatment, wouldn’t that be unconscionable? So he said nothing in the end.

The field was a farmer’s foundation. Now he had eleven acres of dry land and five acres of paddy fields in his hands, plus the original few acres at home. As long as the land was well cared for, even farming alone would be enough to cover food and clothing for the year.

Even though half the money in his hand had already gone, he had spent a total of eighty taels to buy the land, and he had also spent a lot preparing New Year’s goods before the festival. Now he had just a little over one hundred taels of silver left.

Thinking of money, Shen Xuanqing split another piece of firewood and said, “The ice on the river has melted; tomorrow I’ll see if I can catch winter-spring fish (冬春鱼). After I chop a bit more firewood, I’ll go to catch the fish and take them to the town to sell together.”

Winter-spring fish were as long as a grown man’s finger, about two fingers wide, with tender meat and few small thorns. They could be stewed or fried, and eating them helped children grow taller. But the river was still cold as ice in early spring, so it wasn’t easy to wade in and cast a dense net.

There was another method, though: weave bamboo fish cages and place them in the water. If the bamboo cages were too light, they could be weighted down with stones so they wouldn’t float away down the river.

The Shen family had a fish cage they had made before; Shen Xuanqing retrieved it from the utility room after chopping the firewood.

Lu Gu hadn’t gone to town to sell things for a long time. When he thought of the colorful laozi he had made in his spare time after the Chinese New Year, he stopped the stone pestle in his hand, lifted his head, and said, “Then I’ll go with you and sell some spice bags and laozi.” He also embroidered some handkerchiefs—the brand-new handkerchiefs were embroidered with flowers, and even when sold cheaply they were three copper coins apiece, which was still a gain.

“Okay.” Shen Xuanqing patted the fish cage, and a layer of dust immediately rose, so he didn’t pat it any more. This thing doesn’t need washing—the dirt would be washed away in the river tomorrow.

With spring’s arrival, ice and snow melted and everything came back to life. To earn more money than in winter, everyone in the Shen family’s second room was busy.

The river remained cold, so Shen Yaoqing rowed a wooden boat along the river to place a few more fish cages. Ji Qiuyue and Shen Yan were releasing ducks in the pond behind the house; she kept glancing up now and then to see her husband setting the fish cages.

The task of catching fish was left to Shen Yaoqing, while Lu Gu and Shen Xuanqing went up the mountain to collect firewood. Lu Gu carried a small bamboo basket on his back and planned to dig wild vegetables on the mountain. Wild greens were abundant after mid-spring, but he had only gnawed cabbages and radishes all winter. Now townsfolk were willing to pay good money for these fresh greens.
