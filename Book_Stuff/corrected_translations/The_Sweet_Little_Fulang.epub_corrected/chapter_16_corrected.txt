Chapter 16


Not only Lu Gu, but even Shen Yan stepped back a few paces when he saw the clue, not daring to join in. But because they couldn’t see what was in the cloth bag, their curiosity gnawed at them, and they didn’t dare step forward, choosing to stand aside to take a look.

Seeing that Shen Yaoqing wanted to open the cloth bag to peek, Shen Xuanqing stopped him and said: “That is the five-step snake—the snake gall is the most valuable. Whether you take it out or not depends on whether it’s raw-soaked or cooked.”

Some time ago, Shen Xuanqing had passed the medical center in town and heard that poisonous snake gall was valuable, but this time he had only caught one. If he took the gall and sold it separately from the snake meat, it would have to be soaked in strong wine to preserve its freshness, and not everything was worth it.

Although he used to get snake meat to eat, he didn’t like it, and he hadn’t killed snakes for their gall many times—only twice, and none of them were venomous snakes, so the gall wasn’t that expensive. This time it would be different; if the gall was crushed with too much force, it would be ruined.

Besides, long snakes were slippery and cold to the touch, and he didn’t like to touch them, and Wei Lanxiang was especially afraid of snakes. If he kept it at home, she might not dare to leave the house for days, so it was better to sell the whole thing.

Shen Yaoqing was excited when he heard this, and he said: “Snake gall is a good thing, and it’s very valuable.”

After he finished speaking, he asked Shen Xuanqing: “It didn’t bite you, did it?”

Five-step snakes are venomous; catching venomous snakes is always dangerous—one wrong bite could cost a life.

Hearing this, Shen Xuanqing stopped rubbing the wild soapberries, turned his head, and saw his husband crouched nearby, thin and small, looking at him timidly.

He hadn’t seen Lu Gu for five days. Today Lu Gu wore indigo-blue clothes; he looked paler than before, and perhaps more beautiful. From Lu Gu’s tense expression, Shen Xuanqing understood that Lu Gu had been waiting for a long time, so he lowered his eyes and whispered, “Scramble another egg.”

“Okay.” Lu Gu nodded hurriedly and went into the kitchen.

Ji Qiuyue came out from the backyard to feed the chickens, and seeing Shen Xuanqing return and call him, she smiled, thinking the young couple were finally getting along a little.

Lu Gu hesitated about how to scramble a few eggs for Shen Xuanqing, and Ji Qiuyue came in while he hesitated, telling him to take four from the basket. “Erqing loves eggs, fried or boiled. For him alone, four should be enough, and there are other dishes.” Ji Qiuyue couldn’t stay idle as soon as she came in, and habitually took them from Lu Gu. After chopping vegetables, she didn’t even realize it.

When the water in the pot boiled, Lu Gu put six steamed buns on the drawer. Hearing this, he noted Shen Xuanqing’s preferences and nodded: “Well, that’s enough.”

The vegetables were cut and the eggs beaten. Okay, but the steamed buns hadn’t been heated yet, so we had to wait.

Ji Qiuyue said: “I’ve heard there’s a youzhong ladle (有种马勺), smaller than our iron pot and with a long handle on the side, which can sit on the medicine stove for cooking. If you can buy one, it will be much more useful, so you won’t have to wait any longer.”

Lu Gu had also heard that this kind of youzhong ladle might be called a frying spoon, and it was lighter than a cauldron. He thought Ji Qiuyue was right, so he nodded and spoke in a low voice: “It seems quite expensive.”

“Of course—it's iron.” Ji Qiuyue turned around and added, “When we’re better, I’ll have Brother Daqing go to the iron shop in town to have a look.”

Everyone hoped for a better life, and Lu Gu was included in that hope, a quiet light of aspiration appearing in his timid eyes as he restrained his emotions.

The kitchen was busy, and the steamed buns would be warm as soon as the water boiled. It wasn’t necessary to eat very hot food in winter, so the wait wasn’t long.

Knowing that the eggs weren’t fried by Lu Gu, but seeing him serving the dishes and steamed buns to Shen Xuanqing, Shen Xuanqing felt something different.

“Do you want to eat?” he asked Lu Gu.

Lu Gu, who had set down the steamed bun basket, shook his head: “I’ve eaten. You eat.”

After days of plain rice in the mountains, Shen Xuanqing was satisfied with a bite of scrambled eggs when he came back. He knew how to stir-fry, but his cooking skills were poor; if the fire was too high, the pot would burn. He found it unpalatable. Sometimes, busy and hungry, he didn’t care about frying and simply boiled the vegetables with water, salted them, and mixed them up. In the past two years, his family had fallen on hard times and had no money to buy oil. He often ate boiled dishes and occasionally boiled eggs. Now that life was better, he craved eggs fried in oil.

Lu Gu felt it wasn’t right to watch others eat, so he slipped away to Wei Lanxiang’s room to do needlework. Shen Yan’s shoes hadn’t been finished yet, so he stayed to help.

—

When Shen Xuanqing returned, he often kept a note of the date, so he could remind Lu Gu to accompany him to check his injuries.

At the laozhong’s house, Lu Gu hadn’t finished his diagnosis. He noticed someone entering the door—Lu Wen.
