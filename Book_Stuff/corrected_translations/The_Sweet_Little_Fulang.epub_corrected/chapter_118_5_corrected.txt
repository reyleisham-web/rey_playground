Chapter 118 Part 5


Shen Xuanqing explained softly, “Would you get fat if you became pregnant?”

He wasn’t an expert on pregnancy, but from the pregnant women and fulangs he’d seen since childhood, he knew that a woman’s belly would grow larger during pregnancy.

Before Lu Gu could answer, Shen Xuanqing rolled over and pressed himself on top of him, lowering his head and kissing him with practiced ardor.

The pheasants were kept in the woodshed. At dawn, Lu Gu went out to pluck tender leaves and fresh grass. Aside from feeding them and cleaning the shed, he kept his distance from the woodshed to avoid startling the birds.

The male pheasant was exceptionally beautiful, with a long tail and plumage mostly blue mingled with other vibrant hues. Shen Xuanqing said that some wealthy families liked to admire such beauty and would pay a good price for it.

Since he had captured such a rare mountain bird, Shen Xuanqing went out today with a bird-catching net, setting it up in the woods and hoping to trap some small, pretty birds.

For a hunter, there was no such thing as too much killing. Catching birds to sell was simply another way to make money, and there was nothing wrong with it. Besides, he didn’t hunt birds often. After all, many people came to the mountains to trap birds to earn money from rich patrons, so one more hunter wouldn’t make a difference.

After days of chasing and exhausting work, what Shen Xuanqing looked forward to most was coming home to a hot meal cooked by his fulang.

The young chicken soup was rich and fragrant, the meat tender and soft. Lu Gu had also picked wild bud tips from outside. After blanching them, he tossed them with salt for a simple, refreshing side dish. The slightly bitter wild bud tips carried a sweet aftertaste, making them a perfect complement to the rich meat, both refreshing and palate-cleansing.

Despite regularly eating meat and oily dishes, neither of them had gained much weight, but their complexions had improved compared to before. Shen Xuanqing, in particular, was full of energy, and his smile had become a more common sight.

Almost a month had passed without them realizing it. They had accumulated quite a lot of prey, so they packed up and prepared to return to the foot of the mountain. The day before, Lu Gu had gone to pick wild soapberries, a reliable source of income that they couldn’t forget.

The family was busy yet joyful. When they came back, they saw that the mud walls enclosing the five-acre plot had been completed, and the foundation for the three sturdy tile-roofed houses had been laid, taking shape. The open area was filled with stacks of wood.

The timber cut during the winter was dry and solid, making it highly resistant to rot. Some of it had been purchased from other people, while the rest was cut by Shen Yaoqing from the mountains. They needed to hire a carpenter to strip the bark, smooth the wood, and treat it with medicine to prevent termites. Later, there would be processes like painting and varnishing.

At this time of year, there was no farming work to be done, but the weeds in both the paddy fields and dry fields had to be pulled, or else the grain yield would decrease. Every day, they had to spare half a day to work in the fields. Therefore, building the house properly would take some time, but fortunately, there was no rush to complete it.

The mud walls were tall, and as Lu Gu and Shen Xuanqing walked through the spacious courtyard, Shen Xuanqing pointed to the open areas, explaining where they would raise rabbits and where they would build the chicken coop. The rough plan began to take shape, allowing Lu Gu to imagine the lively future ahead, filling his heart with joy for his ger.
