Chapter 4

After learning from Grandma Wang that he had been betrayed, Lu Gu stood there in a daze for a long moment. Only when a deep voice sounded from outside did he snap to himself; it was Shen Xuanqing asking if he was awake.

The doctor’s house wasn’t a hospital where people could live, so upon waking Lu Gu would have to return to recuperate. The man from Qingxi Village and the rest of the Shen family turned and left first, leaving only Shen Xuanqing.

Lu Gu followed Grandma Wang out and saw the tall man carrying a few medicine bags. Shen Xuanqing glanced at him with little expression and only bade farewell to Grandma Wang and the doctor before walking out ahead.

Lu Gu barely dared to look at Grandma Wang beside him. He was pale and drained of energy. Grandma Wang could scarcely bear the helpless look in his lonely eyes, but Lu Gu had already been sold to the Shen family, and there was nothing he could do but go back with them.

Outside, Shen Xuanqing did not hear Lu Gu’s footsteps at first. He turned around and saw Lu Gu’s gaze trembling; Lu Gu hurried to follow.

The two walked in a single line, detouring from the back of the doctor’s house along a path that was closer than the main route. The Lu family lived at the village’s front, so this way avoided ever having to see the Lu family again.

Lu Gu wandered in a trance, and as they walked farther, he suddenly tilted his head toward Anjia Village, as if in a dream escaping from his stepmother, Du Hehua. Yet there was no joy in his heart. Shen Xuanqing, so tall that it seemed to frighten him, didn’t dare come any closer, and how could he hope to survive in the future?

The sun sank westward and began to descend behind the mountains. Lu Gu kept tracing Shen Xuanqing’s shadow. After he fully understood his situation, he realized that his trance was not feigned but caused by dizziness. Still, he did not dare to stop, did not dare to say anything, and grit his teeth as he kept following.

Just before Lu Daxiang stepped out of the hospital doors, he turned his head and glared at Du Hehua. His face was black with anger, while Du Hehua showed no fear at all.

Du Hehua had only brought along the old patched clothes for Lu Gu. The wedding shoes still on his feet were damp, making walking a little uncomfortable, but patience was something he had long ago learned to endure. He kept following without a word.

Shen Xuanqing, still in front and carrying the medicine bag, had been looking forward to his own wedding day, but now his day was ruined. No one would look kindly on him for this turn of events. And the fact that he had bought Lu Gu was something that had happened only about half an hour earlier.

— After Lu Gu was carried away by Shen Xuanqing, Du Hehua’s face had darkened with anger. She was used to beating and scolding Lu Gu, and today she could hardly believe that Lu Gu had slapped her in return. She whispered in Lu Daxiang’s ear, telling him how Lu Gu had embarrassed his father in front of the villagers and that they were after Shen family money, that it was unfilial, and that Lu Daxiang did not take Lu Gu seriously.

Lu Daxiang had always been unhappy because Du Hehua’s beating had drawn blood and made others laugh at them. Hearing her words, his expression worsened, but he did not say a word. His face remained calm as he planned to go to the doctor’s house. After all, Lu Gu was his shuang’er son, and he would not even look at him, which would only fuel more gossip behind their backs.

Seeing his kindness unappreciated and his silence useless, Du Hehua went out to confront that wild species.

She sneered and strode over, squinting at Lu Daxiang as she spoke in a quiet voice, “You have a good heart, you’ve raised a good shuang’er, and today, along with others, you’ve poured dirty water on Brother Wen. Tomorrow you’ll be able to tell him. The ancestors of the old Lu family were mocked, and the ancestors will be offended to see him. I don’t know which wild man’s seed produced you a green-headed bastard!”

The people from Qingxi Village were still in the yard. Wei Lanxiang watched the two at the door, unsure of what they were muttering about.

Even Lu Daxiang, who had been about to head out, paused, removed the dry tobacco rod from his waist, and sat on the wall to take a few puffs. The smoke veiled his furrowed, aging face, making him seem older than before. The thought of Du Hehua’s words made him angrier and deepened his resentment toward Lu Gu, the “wild species.”

Wei Lanxiang hadn’t left either—she stayed to keep watch, to prevent the Lu family from cheating them out of money again. They would take the things but not the money.

When Shen Xuanqing returned, he learned that Lu Gu’s life was not in danger and that the people from Qingxi Village would go back after moving the belongings.

Wei Lanxiang looked at the pile of trifles—worth less than twenty taels, she thought—yet Shen Xuanqing told her to go home. She sighed and conceded. The Lu family was shameless, but they would have to live with it.

Before the items were moved, Du Hehua blocked the doorway, refused to let them pass, and pointed a disapproving finger at Shen Xuanqing, cursing him as shameless. Then she left, carrying a disheveled shuang’er on her back, and she shouted insults at Wei Lanxiang and the rest of the Shen family.

Lu Daxiang stopped smoking, spat on the ground, then stood and looked at Shen Xuanqing with a very bad look. He did not scold him outright, but he asked reproachfully whether Shen Xuanqing had taken advantage of Lu Gu; otherwise, how could he have carried Lu Gu away on his back?

He no longer trusted Lu Gu, and so he wore his affection for Du Hehua in a different way.
