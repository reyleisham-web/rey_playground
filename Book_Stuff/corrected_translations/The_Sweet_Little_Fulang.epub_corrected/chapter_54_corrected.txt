Chapter 54


There were few people in the brothel during the day, especially in the early morning; most of them had already come and gone from the building.

Shen Xuanqing didn’t go to the brothel. He turned down an alley to find Luo Biao. He happened to be there today; last night Luo Biao had been on duty and slept through the night. As Shen Xuanqing entered the alley, he saw Luo Biao yawning and pushing the door open.

“Second Brother Shen, why are you here so late?”

Luo Biao saw him approach and quickly beckoned him inside.

“Are you going out?” Shen Xuanqing asked.

“I’m hungry. I’m going to buy a steamed stuffed bun. Second Brother Shen, just wait at home; I’ll be back soon.”

Since that was the case, Shen Xuanqing sat down inside. The steamed bun shop wasn’t far away. Luo Biao returned quickly with a large bowl containing six meat buns, and in his other hand a bowl of boiled rice wine. Behind him walked a seven-year-old boy who carried a bowl of rice wine as well.

“Okay, remember to give the money to your father when you go back.” Luo Biao handed money for the two bowls of rice wine to the boy. He lived here and knew the people who sold things nearby. After they finished eating, there would still be time to talk.

Shen Xuanqing had gotten up early and eaten two plain steamed buns at home, but Luo Biao generously treated him, even buying rice wine, so he managed to eat one meat bun as well.

Luo Biao thought Shen Xuanqing had come to ask about Lu Wen and Li Mingshan, so he spoke while they ate.

Li Mingshan hadn’t come to the brothel for a while; he lived in the house he bought outside. From the outside it looked as though he was living there alone. But no wall is truly impenetrable, and he had spent a little money to hire beggars and ruffians to keep an eye on what happened there. Li Mingshan was certainly not the only one who knew what was going on; the maid who tended Lu Wen came and went several times, so Lu Wen must have been living there as well.

Once it was known that Li Mingshan was staying the night, how could Luo Biao not notice that the two of them had been up to something shady behind Li Mingshan’s back? Perhaps the wind had blown whispers toward Lu Wen’s pillow, and Li Mingshan even rushed home to say he would go to Anjia Village to propose marriage, but how could his mother, Mrs. Zhang, agree? The mother and son had quarrelled, Li Mingshan hadn’t gotten his way, and so he probably didn’t dare to go back to see Lu Wen. So in the past few days he’d gone to the brothel to drink again, but while drinking he found a sister to sleep with him last night.

After Shen Xuanqing listened, he nodded twice. Why did this matter to him? He hesitated, cleared his throat, and asked in a low voice, “Have you read the Spring Palace Painting? If there is such a good one, can I borrow it for a few days?”

[Spring Palace Painting (Chūngōngtú): traditional paintings that describe human sexuality, often used as erotic educational aids.]

He spoke bluntly, without beating around the bush. His voice was quiet, but his expression was remarkably natural.

Luo Biao was taken aback. He wasn’t the only thug in the brothel; it was common for men to see this kind thing in private. Shen Xuanqing was no ordinary man; he immediately patted his chest and said with feigned loyalty, “Second Brother Shen, don’t worry—it's just this. If you want it now, brother, I’ll bring it to you. Just tell me and I’ll fetch it for you, no need to ask twice.”

Hearing this, Shen Xuanqing knew he’d found the right person. First, Luo Biao could indeed get it; second, Luo Biao was used to such things, and would speak of it with a certain righteous air, as if it were a serious matter that had to be protected.

But Shen Xuanqing didn’t speak further. He coughed softly, trying to appear calm.

Without delay, Luo Biao finished the last bite of the meat bun and asked, “Second Brother Shen, what do you want? If you tell me, brother will find it for you.”

“Simple and straightforward is best,” Shen Xuanqing replied. He wasn’t picky; he just wanted to know how to do it well. He wanted to ask someone else, but the truth was hard to put into words, and Lu Gu was involved—he didn’t want others to know.

“There’s nothing you can’t understand. It’s mostly pictures. You can understand it at a glance. Since it’s simple, you won’t need those tricks that are too annoying.” Luo Biao stood up after eating and added, “Second Brother Shen, wait a moment. I’ll fetch it right away.”

Luo Biao showed his sincerity and deliberately chose the best volume. When Shen Xuanqing left the place, there was an extra book tucked in his arm.

Meanwhile, Lu Gu went out with Shen Yan, who was letting the ducks out. He was washing clothes by the river, pounding wild soapberries into pieces with a mallet until the white foam and the berries dissolved in the water, leaving the clothes clean. Shen Xuanqing’s shoes also needed washing, though they wore out quickly from traveling all day; he didn’t bother mending them himself. When he wasn’t doing farm work, Wei Lanxiang and Ji Qiuyue often made shoes for the family, and nearly every time they returned, the two of them would make new shoes for them.

Shen Yan beat the grass for the chickens and talked with Lu Gu from time to time, with Dahui and the puppy following them to the river.

As soon as Shen Xuanqing returned, he heard a dog barking behind the house. Seeing that Lu Gu and Shen Yan were not there, he knew they must be at the river. Shen Yaoqing went to work in town. When he returned, Wei Lanxiang told him to watch the house, and she and Ji Qiuyue went to visit the village, carrying the soles of shoes and chatting with villagers for a while—women in the countryside often did that.

Shen Xuanqing was left alone in the house for a while. He went inside the room, pulling the book from his sleeve. He had intended to go up the mountain and read behind Lu Gu’s back, but when he sat at the table, he could not resist. He held his breath and opened it.

The booklet exceeded his expectations. It was completely different from the rough drawings he’d seen in the village before. The pictures were exquisite and detailed, almost lifelike, with text accompanying them. The front pages described the consummation of a marriage, and the annotations were meticulous. Shen Xuanqing only read the first two pages before his face flushed, and he understood what he would have to do in the future.

The more he turned the pages toward the back, the redder his face grew. The book was a step-by-step guide. The front was tolerable, but the later sections were things he’d never seen. It was a pornographic book, a forbidden one, and he didn’t dare to finish flipping through it, quickly shoving it away after a cursory glance.

Lu Gu, washing clothes by the river, remained unaware of anything amiss. He still planned to buy some cloth and embroidery thread later, so that the sachets he made could be sold. After winter arrives and he comes down the mountain, there would be no time to pick wild soapberries and goji berries to sell, so he would have to make sachets and handkerchiefs, which were fairly profitable. There was still a lot of work to do.

When Lu Gu returned from washing the clothes, the heat on Shen Xuanqing’s face had faded, but looking at Lu Gu, his Adam’s apple bobbed as he swallowed hard, though he wasn’t sure why.

Seeing Lu Gu drying clothes in the yard, he went over to help. After getting closer to his fulang, the impatience in his heart finally subsided.

In the mountains there were chickens and ducks, and Lu Gu thought about them as he sat on the bed and scrubbed his feet at night, speaking softly to Shen Xuanqing, “Can we go up the mountain tomorrow?”

Shen Xuanqing’s throat tightened, but he pretended to be calm and nodded. “Okay, then we’ll leave tomorrow.”

Having climbed the mountain many times, Lu Gu already knew the way. As soon as they arrived, he fed the chickens and ducks and cooked as usual, and he felt no guilt at all for being busy. Even when Shen Xuanqing hugged him while they slept at night, Lu Gu didn’t feel any vigilance.

Realizing something was different from before, Lu Gu lay flat and did not dare move. After the strange happiness welled up inside him, he suddenly opened his eyes wide in the darkness.
