Chapter 118 Part 26


“Ah, you’re too heavy now, no, no.” Lu Gu laughed and pushed away the half of Guaizi’s body that had already climbed up, amused and a little exasperated. He knew he’d grown big; how could he compare to when he was small?

Guaizi seemed to sense he couldn’t climb up, but he didn’t want to be pushed away either. He grabbled at Lu Gu’s sleeve with his mouth and kept flirting with him.

After a short back-and-forth, Lu Gu’s heart softened when he heard Guaizi’s pitiful whimpers. He hugged the upper body and rubbed his head for a while, chuckling softly. “Why are you like a baby, crying like this?”

Guaizi didn’t understand the words, but he knew how to press close to people. Lu Gu’s cheek was licked twice, becoming wet. He quickly turned his head to avoid it, and after some more playful tussling, he finally managed to coax Guaizi to release him.

——

Lu Gu grabbed a handful of chives and chopped them on the cutting board. The sound of the knife slicing through the chives was more satisfying than cutting other vegetables. Busy alone in the kitchen, Lu Gu chopped vegetables, scrambled eggs, and mixed the filling. Once the filling was ready, he went to knead and roll out the dough.

In their region, dumpling wrappers were square. Instead of rolling out each wrapper individually, they rolled out one large sheet of dough and cut it into squares with a knife.

Not long after wrapping the dumplings, Shen Xuanqing returned. He immediately boiled water and cooked the dumplings, just in time to eat before dark.

“I was lucky today. I came across a beehive on the way and got two combs of honey. I also caught a colorful pheasant with a long tail and a gray hen alive. These creatures live in flocks. When I chased them, Dahei pounced too fiercely and scared two chicks to death. I brought them all back.”

Shen Xuanqing washed his dirty hands and came into the kitchen to talk. His starry eyes sparkled with joy as he added, “I checked, and the two chicks were just scared to death. They’re still good to eat. Tomorrow we’ll make chick soup—the meat is more tender than older chickens.”

“Alright,” Lu Gu nodded with a smile as he added firewood to the stove.

Shen Xuanqing walked out and brought in two things from outside, both wrapped in clean large leaves. He walked over, squatted down, and opened the leaves with Lu Gu. They peeled back the leaves and saw bright red fruits. Shen Xuanqing smiled and said, “Berries. I’ll go wash them.”

What Lu Gu held in his hand was the honey. Last year, all the honey they found was brought home. Whether it was sugar or honey, both were precious. For Shen Xuanqing, finding such sweet treats was even more gratifying than hunting game because it brought joy to his fulang. The berries he picked were rare, though a little small, but they had a strong sweet-and-sour flavor, much tastier than ordinary fruits.

After he washed them, he brought the bowl over. The two of them, one sitting and the other squatting, both started eating the berries with smiles on their faces.

After brushing their teeth at night, it was time to go to bed as usual, but Lu Gu craved berries. He sat on the edge of the bed and hesitated for a moment, thinking that he had already used up the green salt for cleaning his teeth, and it would be a waste if he still ate, so he had to forgo it.

But Shen Xuanqing noticed there was something on his mind and asked. When he found out it was about craving the berries, the young man chuckled under the candlelight, teasing him for being greedy. Yet he still went to the kitchen to fetch the small bowl of berries.

The bright red berries were small, and when bitten between the teeth, their sweet-and-sour flavor burst forth. No wonder Lu Gu craved them, and even Shen Xuanqing took several.

He picked up a small berry and rubbed it gently between his fingertips. The bright red little fruit reminded him of something else. He lowered his eyes slightly and concealed his wanton gaze. After putting the fruit into his mouth, he couldn’t tell whether it was the sweet-and-sour taste that made his mouth water or the desire that made his Adam’s apple bob.

The young man, (ger) less than twenty years old, had a strong yang energy—a healthy, vigorous physique, and unexpectedly intense energy. With his soft and obedient husband beside him, within easy reach, it was hard to resist.

The candle flickered out, and in the darkness the kiss carried the sweetness of the berries.

Hugging and cuddling might seem a bit clingy, but Lu Gu never thought so. He didn’t know when it started, but he felt joy in his heart whenever Shen Xuanqing hugged or kissed him.

The night was enchantingly sweet, and Lu Gu’s heart overflowed with sweetness. Then a big, warm, strong hand touched his belly.

Shen Xuanqing thought it felt so soft. He couldn’t bring himself to move his hand away and muttered softly, “Why haven’t you gained weight yet?”
