Chapter 50 Part 3


On the bed, Shen Xuanqing hadn’t fallen asleep for a long time. He rolled over, hugged Lu Gu in his arms, and felt satisfied and content. When he couldn’t reply just now, he thought Lu Gu would be frightened. Lu Gu’s eyes were indeed a little flustered, but he said nothing; even after thinking about it, Lu Gu had never told him he was afraid, and trembling with fear, he couldn’t speak. He pondered, sighed softly, and hugged the man in his arms, gradually drifting off to sleep.

The next day, after Shen Xuanqing left, Lu Gu returned to the courtyard, closed the door, and tried to move the tree trunk. It was really difficult, but he could move it, and relief flooded him. It was still early, the dew outside was heavy. He didn’t let the chickens and ducks out. After sweeping the house and as the sun rose, he moved the bamboo plaques out from the main room. The mushrooms and goji berries he had picked yesterday were dried before they could be put up.

The puppy sleeping on the sack woke up, no longer curled into a fluffy ball. He yawned, stood up and stretched, then shook his round, fat body and walked toward Lu Gu, barking and rubbing against Lu Gu’s legs—it was time to eat. Lu Gu broke a steamed bun for him and some pieces of cooked rabbit meat. Dahui and the others liked the cooked ones. After the meat was cooked, the puppy also ate with them. Lu Gu gave the remaining pieces to him, put them into a basin, and poured water into the nearby dog bowl.

The puppy lowered his head and picked at the meat first. He could eat meat every two or three days, and he was really well raised. Seeing that he was eating fast, Lu Gu couldn’t help touching his head. The puppy had been taught not to be too protective of his food, so he allowed Lu Gu to stroke him.

When the sun was high, it was time to let the chickens and ducks out to graze. Lu Gu went to the backyard. The dwarf deer was still tied in a corner, lying on the ground. When people came, he raised his head vigilantly. Lu Gu saw that he was still alive. There was no need to worry; Shen Xuanqing had said the leg injury didn’t matter, and he would change the medicine when he came back.

After driving the chickens and ducks to the fence outside the yard, Lu Gu pulled a bundle of grass before going back. Both the long-tailed pheasant in the woodshed and the dwarf deer in the backyard had to be fed. The long-tailed pheasant could fly by flapping its wings. Shen Xuanqing was worried, so he locked the pheasant in the woodshed.

The pheasant was all right. Lu Gu thought that if he wanted to raise the dwarf deer, he had to feed it; otherwise it would be a pity to breed such a fat prey to be sold. He still remembered the first time Shen Xuanqing took him to town. Although the dwarf deer was different from a sika deer, the young deer was worth some money.

He asked last night, and Shen Xuanqing said that the dwarf deer ate both grass and leaves. He pulled out different grasses and went back to see which kind the dwarf deer liked to eat, but the dwarf deer was wary and didn’t move its mouth much in front of him, so he had to carry a basket to pick dipicai first.

Lu Gu was picking dipicai around the courtyard. He didn’t walk too far, and the courtyard door was still open. The puppy followed him all the time, running and playing.

After picking a basket of dipicai, Lu Gu sat in the yard and cleaned them, washing them twice with water, and spread them on a bamboo plaque to dry when clean. After finishing all this, he didn’t stop working. He carried a bamboo basket and took a small hoe to dig tianwogen (sweet root).

At the bottom of the hill not far away, there were several clumps of tianwogen. The fibrous roots were broken off, leaving only the bottom large root. Because he wasn’t far away, he didn’t lock the door, and let the puppy follow him. He dug tianwogen, and the puppy helped him dig the soil when he saw it, but the pup was so excited that he kicked up dirt all over Lu Gu.

“Okay, okay, I’ll come, you dig here,” Lu Gu said, guiding him aside to avoid trouble.

The tianwogen here were quite big, almost as big as his fist. Lu Gu squatted on the ground and dug out some tianwogen, putting them into the bamboo basket. More than twenty tianwogen were dug from these clumps; he didn’t want the ones that were too small. He didn’t stop until he had dug up all of them.

He saw a lot of sour red fruits in the grass. People didn’t eat them very much, but Shen Xuanqing said the dwarf deer preferred them. The bamboo baskets were full with tianwogen still covered in soil. The sour red fruits were too small, so he even cut off the branches and brought back a bunch.

After returning, he went to the backyard to have a look. The dwarf deer ate the grass, and the rest probably didn’t like it very much. Now he knew what kind of grass to plant.

Throwing the sour red fruit to the dwarf deer, Lu Gu didn’t care about it. He went to the front yard to wash three tianwogen, put a mixed-flour steamed bun in the pot, and lit the fire to steam it. Tianwogen was said to be sweet, but in truth it didn’t taste very sweet. They tasted delicious when steamed. Some people dipped them in honey to make them sweeter. The golden honey Shen Xuanqing found before was still somewhat sweet.

With Shen Xuanqing not around, Lu Gu didn’t dare to eat too much. He opened the honey jar and looked at it, thinking it would be nice to take some and rub it on the tianwogen.

During the day, he was busy alone, digging things and taking care of the chickens and ducks. When the ducks swam, Lu Gu sat on the edge of the pond with bamboo strips in his arms and wove bamboo plaques. He reminded himself not to be too distracted if he wanted to do more delicate work.

No one talked to him; he simply thought to himself. There weren’t many bamboo strips left, and when Shen Xuanqing was busy hunting foxes, he had to chop some bamboo back. The bamboo plaques were small and convenient for drying wolfberries and mushrooms, and he poured them into a cloth bag directly after drying, but he still had to weave a big bamboo mat to dry a lot of wild vegetables.

When he was busy, he didn’t feel anything, but at night, the mountains and forests seemed extraordinarily quiet.

After fixing the courtyard door, Lu Gu sat in the courtyard and waited for a long time, afraid that if he closed the door Shen Xuanqing wouldn’t be able to enter when he came back. The puppy stayed with him in the yard until the moon was high in the sky. There was no sound of people or dogs barking outside. He looked up at the moon for a while before going back to his room to sleep.
