Chapter 80


Several families at the back of the village heard Lao Zhang’s cry for help in the snowstorm. She usually spoke with a shrill voice, but tonight she was fighting against death, crying out with all her strength, and even the children next door woke up, crying continuously.

The Shen family’s dogs kept barking; all four of them barked at the same time, making a racket. Lu Gu’s hand trembled with fright, and the brush in his hand shook until it left a smear of ink on the page. The woman’s cry for help sounded especially stern in the winter night, and Shen Xuanqing jumped out of bed first and said, “Don’t worry—after you get dressed, don’t go out into the yard. I’ll go and have a look.”

The door of the main room was closed, and the four dogs in the house barked at the door. Shen Yaoqing also got up, and even Wei Lanxiang pulled on some clothes when she heard the commotion. “Whose family has had an accident? It sounds like Lao Zhang’s.”

“Go and take a look.” Shen Yaoqing said, and he went out with Shen Xuanqing, the dogs following.

“Don’t be reckless. Look carefully before you go forward.” Wei Lanxiang told them. It was good for the dogs to follow, in case something from the mountain came down and bit someone, they could help too.

Not only the Shen family’s second room, but many nearby families poured out, and there was a lot of noise outside for a while.

“Mother, it seems to be the Zhang family?” Lu Gu pulled on his clothes and came out, his heart aching from the sound of people crying and the dogs barking.

“Oh, I don’t know,” Wei Lanxiang sighed. Seeing Ji Qiuyue come out, she hurriedly said, “What are you doing? It’s cold outside. Go back inside quickly. Shen Yan, light the lamp to accompany Sister-in-law to sit on the bed for a while.”

“Got it, mother.” Shen Yan had just taken down her pigtails and was about to go to bed, but with the chaos outside, she promised to go to Ji Qiuyue’s room to keep her company first.

It was pitch dark outside, but the voices of people and dogs gradually subsided. It didn’t sound like some beast from the mountains. It was windy and snowing, so Wei Lanxiang lit a lantern and told Lu Gu not to go out by himself, and she would go take a look herself. She was old and unafraid of anything untidy; Lu Gu was a young fulang, not to mention thin-skinned, and he had been married for less than a year, so how could she rest assured to let him show his face?

Lao Zhang had cried for help outside after she had fallen, and Xiao Zhang, who lay curled up in the woodshed, was startled when he heard the sound. It was piercing in the quiet night. She felt so cold that she shivered before she mustered the strength to rise from the haystack.

The dog kept barking, and there were movements from the neighbors. Lao Zhang was afraid to go out alone, but she was also afraid of being scolded by Lao Zhang if she did not go out in time, so she grit her teeth and went out to have a look.

It was completely dark; only a big black shadow could be seen in front of the door. Before Li Wanyun could speak, someone emerged from the house next door, holding a lantern and shouting, “Is that you, Lao Zhang?”

The lantern’s glow washed over the scene, so there was no need to answer; the people on the ground were visible.

“Ouch, come help me, my right leg is pinned down and I can’t move.” Lao Zhang cried out in pain, tears and snot smeared across her face, but she could clearly see the person who came to help her. When she saw Li Wanyun stepping out of the door, her face twisted into a ferocious sneer, and she cursed angrily, “What a thief! Do you think I can kill you if you hide in there?”

When she called for help to Quan Zi’s mother, she had already sobbed and grown weak, but this time she seemed to draw energy from nowhere, and in a sudden fit of anger she scolded her daughter-in-law. The neighbors who had intended to help were stunned.

At that moment, all the neighbors crowded in; seven dogs rushed forward. Li Wanyun didn’t dare say anything; she reached out to grab Zhang Zhengzi, who lay on Lao Zhang’s leg. A weak woman, Zhang Zhengzi was a little heavier, and he had fainted again—how could she move him?

Many people gathered around; three lanterns lit up the front of Zhang’s yard, and they could clearly see what had happened to Lao Zhang and Zhang Zhengzi.

Lao Zhang, worried about her son, stopped scolding and, despite the severe pain in her leg, said, “Come help me carry him in quickly.”

Zhang Zhengzi lay unconscious, and watching him freeze outside wasn’t good now that they had come. Young people wanted to move him, but Quan Zi’s father stopped them. “Wait a minute. I’ll protect Zhengzi’s waist first. It seems his waist is broken, and if you lift him like this, he’ll die.”

Lao Zhang’s legs were under pressure, so there was no time to worry about Zhengzi’s waist. When you wanted to lift him up, it was better to secure the waist first, to avoid breaking any bones.

As soon as these words were spoken, Lao Zhang endured the pain in her leg and said, “Slow down, don’t be too hasty.”

They lifted Zhang Zhengzi’s head and carried him into the courtyard steadily, Li Wanyun leading the way.

At that moment, a light appeared; Lao Zhang suddenly saw blood on her hands and arms, all wet. She had been holding Zhengzi’s head and shoulders just now, but hadn’t noticed it in her flurry. Frightened, she let out a short scream and fainted to the ground.

She could still speak and scold just a moment ago, so it was clear her waist was not the problem. The rest of the family carried her, and the Zhang household descended into chaos for a while.

Wei Lanxiang returned with a lantern, peeking into Zhang’s yard, shouting at Dahui, and waving her hand to drive the dogs away. The dogs were sent back to guard the home, and Shen Yan and Lu Gu—together with Ji Qiuyue, who was pregnant, and her sons—were all outside, so Wei Lanxiang sent the dogs back to look after the house.
