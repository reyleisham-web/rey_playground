Chapter 107 Part 27


Now that Lin Zhongcai had spoken, Wei Lanxiang could breathe a little easier.

“Uncle Wei.” Shen Xuanqing handed the wooden stick back to Wei Mingjiang.

“Ah?” Wei Mingjiang, who had just seen how ruthless he could be, was stunned. He lowered his head, and when he saw there was no blood on the stick, he uttered an, “Oh.” He looked down and saw there was no blood on the stick.

“Mother, I’m back.” Shen Xuanqing didn’t look at Huang Da on the ground. When he saw Lu Gu’s tear-streaked face, he sighed. There were so many people gathered outside that it would be difficult to keep hold of hands, so he gently gripped Lu Gu’s wrist and led him home.

The puppy trotted back with them. Wei Lanxiang, trailing behind, glanced at Huang Da, but ultimately ignored him as Shen Xuanqing urged them on.

In the end, Lin Zhongcai waved his hand and called Huang Da’s uncle, who hadn’t been in contact with him, to carry him back to his shabby thatched house. Otherwise, no one would carry him. He couldn’t let Huang Da lie there on the road like that. What a bad omen if he died here, how would others walk this road in the future?

He Zhi came over to pick up the bamboo basket. When Huang Da had been beaten, He Zhi had stepped in too, avenging Chen Dongdong for grabbing the fish, and there was a smile on his sun-tanned face.

Ji Qiuyue stood at the doorway and watched Shen Xuanqing and Lu Gu. Seeing Lu Gu’s face wet with tears and Shen Xuanqing’s hands and sleeves still streaked with blood, she hurriedly scooped a basin of water for them to wash with.

Shen Xuanqing pushed Lu Gu forward and told him to wash his face first. When he looked at his own hand, he explained: “I’m not injured, it’s Huang Da’s blood.”

“Really?” Lu Gu asked, his nasal voice still honeyed with tears.

“Really. Wash your face quickly so I can wash my hands afterward.” Only then did Shen Xuanqing smile.

Ji Qiuyue moved carefully to fetch a cloth to wipe his face as Lu Gu washed.

Shen Xuanqing crouched beside him and watched as he washed. After a moment, he said, “I saw people washing their faces and hands while standing in the town before. It’s more convenient than squatting. I’ll hire a carpenter to make a wooden basin stand later.”

Lu Gu poured a handful of water and washed his face. Hearing this, he could only grunt in acknowledgment.

“Here.” Ji Qiuyue handed him the cloth.

Shen Xuanqing used wild soapberries to wash his hands in the water, and the bloodstains dispersed into the water.

After the white foam from the wild soapberries rinsed away, Lu Gu felt relief when he saw there truly wasn’t a wound on his hand.

Wei Lanxiang returned and stood to the side, unable to hold back her words. “You, don’t go acting recklessly in the future. It was Da Chen’s house he robbed, but he didn’t beat Huang Da as hard as you did. It isn’t our house he robbed, and Da Chen even advised you not to go to war—what is this?”

You’re already married, and you’re not young anymore. You’re not the boy who ran wild to do mischief in the past. How can you not grow up? What if something happens to you because you beat people for no reason? How do we survive?

She went on, her words tumbling out in a rush. As she grew excited, she tried to reach out and poke Shen Xuanqing in the forehead, but Shen Xuanqing was too tall for her to reach—unlike Shen Yan, who she could poke with a finger. In the end, she could only draw her hand back.

Shen Xuanqing remained silent under the scolding, and Lu Gu did not dare to speak.

“Mother, Huang Da is a rotten person; he’ll get what he deserves when beaten. Besides, the second brother has always known his propriety and won’t go around causing trouble. Mother, don’t worry so much.” Ji Qiuyue stepped up to smooth things over. She was pregnant now, so what she said carried weight. Wei Lanxiang glared at Shen Xuanqing and stopped speaking.

“Mother, I had my own reasons for taking action. I wasn’t acting on impulse.” To reassure Wei Lanxiang, Shen Xuanqing followed Ji Qiuyue’s line of reasoning.

He wanted to say that Huang Da’s words had offended Lu Gu, but after a moment’s thought, he decided not to drag Lu Gu into this trouble again.

Wei Lanxiang was persuaded for a while, but she still felt something off. Shen Xuanqing had indeed grown more mature and steady over the past two years. In the few fights he’d had, it was always others who provoked him first. He’d been cruel to Huang Da today; perhaps there was more to it than met the eye.

As soon as she thought of it, she asked, “Er Qing, tell mother—”

“Mother, it’s nothing serious. I saw Huang Da wandering behind our house yesterday. If there wasn’t a dog at home, he might have robbed us in the night. Today was only to give him a long memory. I didn’t want to do anything serious at first. But he wouldn’t back down after being beaten, and he started slandering Guzi’s reputation. You weren’t around at the time and wouldn’t have heard him.”

Shen Xuanqing lied; he feared Wei Lanxiang would be too soft-hearted, so he spun a tale.

Ruining a person’s reputation is no trifling matter. If a man doesn’t speak up, gossip can ruin his wife or concubine. The family wouldn’t be able to hold its head up in the future. Wei Lanxiang was stunned for a moment, then grew angry. Her chest heaved with anger, she frowned, and cursed: “This shameless bastard! He deserves to be beaten!”

“Why didn’t you tell me earlier? If I’d known sooner, I’d have broken his other leg.” She was so furious that she clutched at her chest. Seeing this, Ji Qiuyue quickly pressed a hand to her mother’s heart to help calm her down.

“Mom, don’t be too angry. I’ve already beaten him. If he dares to talk nonsense again, I’ll deal with him.” Shen Xuanqing tried to soothe her.

Lu Gu ran into the main room and poured a bowl of warm water. He handed it to Wei Lanxiang. “Mom, drink some water.”

Wei Lanxiang drew a breath, took the bowl, and took two sips to calm herself. Earlier she’d worried—if Huang Da died for lack of care and nourishment, his death would be laid at Shen Xuanqing’s door. Ji Qiuyue was pregnant and needed to accumulate some virtue for her unborn child, so she’d hoped to soothe his condition with a few things. But now, let alone seeing him, even if Huang Da were dead, it would be a mercy if she did not spit on his corpse. He is a bad person who has lost his conscience! Death would be retribution!
