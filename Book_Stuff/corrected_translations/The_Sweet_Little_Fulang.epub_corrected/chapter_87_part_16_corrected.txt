Chapter 87 Part 16


"Woof!" A dog barked somewhere nearby. Li Wanyun jolted awake, letting go of the other person's hands. Lao Zhang panted heavily and shoved the quilt aside, breaking free. In the dim light by the bed, she saw the figure standing there clearly: Li Wanyun, and her old, horrified eyes seemed to fear that this girl would smother her to death.

Seeing Li Wanyun standing by the bed, Lao Zhang instinctively moved to the other side, afraid to get too close. The memory of the near-death suffocation still clung to her, and now the sight of Li Wanyun—like a ghost—made her even more afraid. After killing her son, was she about to kill me too?

Fueled by extreme anger and the trauma of Zhang Zhengzi's death, Lao Zhang went a little crazy. She glared at Li Wanyun with hatred, then suddenly grabbed the crutch beside the bed and swung it at Li Wanyun's head, shouting, "Goddamn it! You’re my nemesis!"

Li Wanyun was dazed but not unconscious. She dodged to the side instinctively, and the crutch struck her shoulder. The pain stirred a surge of strength from deep inside her. She snatched the crutch from Lao Zhang's hand and returned a few blows herself. She gripped the crutch with both hands, tears streaming down her cheeks, her body trembling.

"Help! Help!" Lao Zhang cried, trying to call for help.

With a clang, the crutch hit the floor. Li Wanyun grabbed the quilt and stuffed the corner into Lao Zhang's mouth. Tears dropped in strings, wetting the quilt and clothes, but Li Wanyun's hands did not stop; she pinched Lao Zhang's arms and thighs with brutal force, twisting the old flesh. Lao Zhang's whole body twitched.

"Wooooo." Lao Zhang tried to pull the corner of the quilt from her mouth, but her tongue couldn’t push it out; Li Wanyun pressed too hard. She tried to pull it out with her hands, but Li Wanyun pinched and twisted her hands again and again—the pain was so fierce she almost lost her breath, frantically trying to shield her face with her hands.

When Lao Zhang realized she couldn’t stop Li Wanyun, she raised a hand to strike back and even pulled Li Wanyun’s hair. Li Wanyun's scalp burned with pain, but she grit her teeth and said nothing. She lifted her hand and yanked Lao Zhang's hair again, grabbing a fistful. Tears and snot streamed down Lao Zhang's face from the pain, and Li Wanyun immediately let go.

Unable to beat or scold, Lao Zhang tried to feign weakness. She finally tugged the corner of the quilt from her mouth, hoping to cry and beg for mercy.

"Wanyun, ah!" Li Wanyun ground her teeth, tears streaming down her cheeks, as if she wanted to reclaim all the suffering she had endured. Maintaining what was left of her rationality, she beat and pinched Lao Zhang's stomach, arms, and thighs mercilessly, saying nothing.

Lao Zhang's pleas for mercy were cut off; she tried to fight back, but she was an old woman who could still work the fields in the past, and now her leg was broken. How could she hope to compete with Li Wanyun, a young woman? After a while, she was beaten to the point of silence.

"Ouch." She collapsed back onto the bed; moving with a broken leg was agony. She cried bitterly, frightened by Li Wanyun's madness tonight, unable to utter a full sentence.

Li Wanyun gradually regained her senses and stopped beating Lao Zhang. She wiped the tears from her cheeks with her sleeve, drew a deep breath, and whispered, "From now on, if you dare beat or scold me again, don't blame me. We will die together, and everything will be cleaned up!"

Her voice was low, but the fierce, unyielding heart behind it did not waver—fish die, nets break, as she had once said. The idiom hung in the air as she spoke: either the fish dies or the net breaks.

She had thought to die, but Heaven did not permit it. She had repaid the Zhang family with her life, and now, her life was her own.

"If you dare spread rumors that I’m not good, wait until your precious nephews leave..." Lao Zhang understood the implication. She did not groan again; she only trembled, its pains all over her body and the broken leg making every movement a torment. Even if Li Wanyun walked away, she did not dare curse back.

In the west room, Li Wanyun did not cry anymore. She stripped off all her clothes and wiped herself with a cloth towel. She didn’t have thick clothes, so she pulled Zhang Zhengzi’s cotton coat over herself and wore it. It was too late to boil water for a bath, but she still went to the kitchen.

Flint sparked as she struck the flint, and the firewood snapped to life. The firelight flickered at the bottom of the stove. She stood for a moment, then, guided by the fire and her familiarity with the kitchen, she peeled a few cabbage leaves and chopped them without washing. She poured a generous amount of oil into the pan, stirred fry, then added water to the pot and brought it to a boil. She pinched two handfuls of fine white flour, added water, and worked a paste with chopsticks. She spooned the paste into the pot and let it cook for a while, making half a pot of dumpling soup.

As it neared completion, she remembered there were eggs in the basket, so she dropped in two. She stirred the soup with a large spoon and produced a bowl of dumpling soup with a glossy, oily sheen. She scooped a bowl and sat by the stove, eating while she cried.
