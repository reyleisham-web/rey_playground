Chapter 6 Part 1

Part 1

Wei Lanxiang was not feeling well, and today it was Ji Qiuyue who brought her meals to eat in the room.

Lu Gu sat between Shen Yan and Ji Qiuyue, so he didn’t have to be next to Shen Xuanqing, but he was still cautious and didn’t dare to stretch out his chopsticks.

Speaking of which, today’s breakfast was quite lavish. There were fish and meat. They were all leftovers from the wedding banquet, not leftovers from others. It was hard for farmers to eat such large fish and meat easily. You couldn’t squander what you had after feasting, so he hurriedly scooped a portion into his own bowl and carried it away.

Now it was summer again, though nearly the end of summer, the weather was still hot, and things spoiled easily, so they had to eat quickly to avoid spoilage.

Everyone at the table had a bowl of chicken soup, which flattered Lu Gu and left him more and more puzzled.

The Shen family probably bought him to vent their anger, but since last night they neither beat him nor scolded him; they fed him, and today even gave him a bowl of oily, fragrant chicken soup.

When Ji Qiuyue saw him, she didn’t dare to eat a single bite. After a night of rest, she regained some energy. Just now, she had seized Lu Gu’s opportunity to serve food and asked Shen Xuanqing what he meant, and learned that Lu Gu was the new fulang.

Although it was still difficult for her to look directly at the Lu family, thinking about how Lu Gu had been beaten like that, it would be hard to believe if she hadn’t seen the injuries on his body, so she softened a bit and said briskly, “Eat the vegetables quickly; if you eat them later, they’ll go bad in two days, so hurry up and eat now.”

Lu Gu was persuaded, but still didn’t dare to stretch out his chopsticks. Ji Qiuyue didn’t urge him much when she saw this; people’s temperament couldn’t be changed in a moment.

Shen Xuanqing and Shen Yaoqing ate fast and a lot. After all, young men were strong and had good appetites.

“I’ll go to the ground for a walk.” Shen Yaoqing stood up first, said to Ji Qiuyue, and left the table.

As for Shen Xuanqing, he had originally planned to stay at home for the past few days after getting married, and wait for three days to return to the door before trekking into the mountains, but seeing Lu Gu bow his head and not speak, he thought about it and went to the backyard.

With only the three of them left at the table, Lu Gu’s tense body relaxed a little.

“There’s still more; let’s eat it separately, don’t leave leftovers.” Ji Qiuyue moved the vegetable bowls toward the three of them.

Shen Yan was full, with a small appetite; the hot weather made it hard to eat much more. When she saw Lu Gu hadn’t moved his chopsticks, she couldn’t bear to waste either, so, like last night, she put a few pieces of fish into Lu Gu’s bowl, followed by some meat.

Before Lu Gu could eat, three dogs ran out of the backyard, drawn by the scent of meat, and headed straight for the main room.

Two lean dogs with smooth fur, long legs, and slender waists—taller than ordinary dogs—shot out first, their exposed fangs reminding Lu Gu of vicious, biting dogs.

A big wolf-dog followed closely behind them, with strong limbs and a well-proportioned body. It didn’t look like a dog, but a wolf-dog tall enough to rival humans, slipping out from the mountains.

However, compared to the two smaller dogs, this big dog’s gray-blue eyes drooped slightly. It walked in, its eyes roaming the main room. Even if it spotted Lu Gu, a stranger, it merely came closer, sniffed around, and then moved on, memorizing the smell, before lying in a shady spot under the eaves with its belly to the ground. Its movements and bearing were calm, but not frightening.

Lu Gu remembered that when he slept last night, there had been a dog barking in the backyard, but he hadn’t expected the Shen family to have three dogs, and that they were all so tall and so big.

“Go!” Ji Qiuyue raised her hand and waved away the two thin dogs that were circling Lu Gu.

At this moment, Shen Xuanqing came out of the backyard, bringing a drink, and the three dogs followed after him as they walked outside.

When the Shen family saw that he didn’t bring anything else, they knew that he wouldn’t go deep into the mountains today, but would chase rabbits and pheasants on the periphery.

“I’m full,” Shen Yan announced, and took away the empty bowls. She went to the kitchen and added a piece of fine wood under the medicine stove to let them boil slowly.

Half a bowl of mixed rice, a bowl of fragrant and warm chicken soup, and Shen Yan also filled Lu Gu with some meat and vegetables—the first time his stomach had felt full in a long while.

The stomach felt full and warm, and even the heart, liver, spleen, lungs, and kidneys seemed to warm, giving him a bit of energy.

Ji Qiuyue was busy in the kitchen after the meal. She was used to it and didn’t let Lu Gu intervene. It happened that the medicine was ready at this time, so she took a bowl and poured it out. The hot medicine was hard to swallow, so she had to set it aside to cool a bit.
