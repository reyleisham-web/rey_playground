Chapter 94


Growing up this tall, Shen Xuanqing had never been laughed at by anyone, let alone by the youngest Shen Yan.

The little girl was blind. She spoke bluntly as if she were two or three years younger. She still talked like a child, but she was almost twelve. How could she speak so nonchalantly and call her brother a fool?

Shen Xuanqing stood there, ruminating in his mind, but when he looked at Lu Gu bowing his head shyly, he could hardly move, the brazier in his hand weighing him down, and he couldn’t even find words to reprimand Shen Yan.

When Ji Qiuyue heard Shen Yan’s words, she couldn’t help laughing. And when she saw Shen Xuanqing’s gaze lingering on Lu Gu and not leaving him, the fool really became a fool.

Wei Lanxiang, tidying up in the room, heard what they said. She came out to look with curiosity, and she saw her son staring at his fulang, then looked at Lu Gu’s shy appearance. Who could understand any of it? She smiled until the creases on her face deepened.

There were too many eyes watching, and Shen Xuanqing finally came to his senses. His ears reddened, and he hesitated, saying, “I—I, I’ll pour out the ashes first.” He used the charcoal brazier in his hand as an excuse and slipped away. He didn’t dare to look back.

Shen Yan was simple-minded and only thought about eating and playing on weekdays. She didn’t understand Lu Gu and Shen Xuanqing’s reactions, but when she saw her second brother fleeing to the backyard with a slightly embarrassed expression, she found it interesting.

Wei Lanxiang saw her daughter laughing foolishly, so she poked her on the forehead and said, “This little girl, don’t call your second brother stupid in the future. If others hear that, they will beat you, and I can’t protect you then.”

Shen Yan was frightened when she heard she might be beaten, but when she turned her head and saw Lu Gu, she grew more confident. With Brother Guzi around, the second brother wouldn’t dare to beat her.

“Alright, alright, go wash your face; your aunt will come later,” Wei Lanxiang urged with a smile.

There would be guests at home today, so the house had to be cleaned up. Everyone in the family went to work in their own ways, and Lu Gu was quietly relieved.

His cheeks were still hot, and Shen Xuanqing’s reaction just now left him too shy to raise his head.

When Jin Qiuyue was combing his hair earlier, he had seen himself in the bronze mirror. The sister-in-law was deft, and the combed hair looked much better than when he usually tied it with cloth. Perhaps he was used to seeing himself and rarely wore hairpins or silk flowers. He only felt a little awkward as he glanced at his reflection in the bronze mirror, but he never imagined that Shen Xuanqing would...

He took the broom leaning against the wall of the house and was about to sweep the main room when Shen Xuanqing came from the backyard with an empty brazier.

The four eyes met, and the two were speechless.

Lu Gu’s cheeks warmed again; now that he was getting closer, when he saw Shen Xuanqing’s gaze pinned on him and his Adam’s apple moving, his ears reddened.

Taking advantage of a moment when no one was looking, Shen Xuanqing spoke softly: “It looks good.”

Lu Gu clenched the broom in his hand tightly. He felt embarrassed and happy at the same time, wriggling and trying to grab the corner of Shen Xuanqing’s clothes; the smile on his face couldn’t be hidden.

But at that moment Shen Yaoqing came out from the main room, so frightened that he quickly withdrew his hands and hurried back to the room to sweep.

Wearing new clothes on the first day of the Chinese New Year, the members of the second room of the Shen family went out with silk flowers and hairpins in their hair, and bracelets on their wrists. As for Shen Yaoqing and Shen Xuanqing, both had sachets hanging at their waists, which could be described as brand-new and beautiful.

Even when Shen Shunfu was here a few years ago, their family hadn’t had such a feeling of wearing gold and silver.

Because the old hunter had earned a lot of money when he lived in the village before, the villagers knew at a glance that Shen Xuanqing made a lot of money as a hunter. Otherwise, how could he rise in just a few years and live a better life than before?

Zhou Yunzhi saw the silver bracelet on Wei Lanxiang’s wrist, then glanced at the other members of the second room. She felt sour in her heart. Her eyeballs kept wandering to other people’s jewelry. Wei Lanxiang smiled, deliberately fixing her hair so that the silver bracelet on her wrist would stand out more. She said, “Sister-in-law, look at Qiuyue—she forced me to comb my hair even though I’m this old. I told her that combing hair is for young people, but she wouldn’t listen; she insisted on putting silk flowers in my hair. I said just one silk flower, but she wouldn’t agree, and she’s put so many in.”

When Zhou Yunzhi heard this, the sour feeling in her heart swelled. She had always despised the poor and loved the rich. Seeing that the second room family was indeed rich, her eyes no longer looked elsewhere but with a hint of longing to curry favor. She replied, “Today we’re celebrating Chinese New Year, and your daughter-in-law is filial. You shouldn’t spoil your child’s good heart.”

“See this silk flower in my hair? It was bought by my eldest daughter-in-law. At first I didn’t want to wear it, but thinking of my child, I couldn’t make her sad.” She gently touched the large silk flower in her hair, but the silver bracelet on her wrist was thinner than Wei Lanxiang’s and still an old piece.

“Oh, it’s beautiful.” Wei Lanxiang boasted smoothly, and when she saw Zhou Yunzhi’s gaze lingering on her wrist, she knew her sister-in-law must be feeling sour, which improved her mood.

The two sisters-in-law spoke insincerely, and Zhou Xiangjun, who wasn’t talkative, just laughed and said nothing. After a while, Shen Yan and Shen Yu announced that their eldest paternal aunt had arrived, and they rushed out to welcome her.

Because the First Room took care of the elderly, they stayed in the old house. Shen Xuanqing’s second and third aunts would remain here for dinner when they returned, and other family members would also come for a reunion dinner.

Even now, even though their grandpa and grandma were gone, the rules of cooking and eating in the First Room hadn’t changed.

Shen Xuanqing’s eldest aunt had married far away and only returned to her mother’s house during the Chinese New Year, so she naturally went to both the First Room and the Third Room to pay her respects and visit.

Lu Gu went back first. He brought out eight plates of pastries and dried fruits prepared at home, placed them in the main room, and poured boiling water into the teapot to make tea. After Wei Lanxiang and the others came inside, chattering and laughing, he poured the tea.

“Eldest aunt, please drink the tea.” Lu Gu served the tea obediently.
