Chapter 7 Part 2


Shen Yan was so happy that Lu Gu hurriedly fetched the basin when he saw she was about to vomit again, letting her purge the mud and sand she had swallowed for two days.

Shen Yaoqing had returned with a hoe on his back, and seeing the mud on Shen Yan’s body, he understood what had happened. Compared to yesterday’s gloomy mood, the family now wore a few smiles, and that made him feel a little buoyed as well. He grabbed Shen Yan’s small braid and teased, “Have you had enough fun?”

The family had endured hard times in the past two years. Shen Yan had to tend ducks and gather grass every day, and sometimes do some needlework. There was hardly any time to play. Today Shen Xuanqing hadn’t caught a rabbit, so he took Shen Yan to the river to dig for loach—this rare little outing made her naturally happy.

“Sister-in-law, look at him!” Shen Yan huffed, knowing she couldn’t beat him, and her brows knitted in a frown as she hurriedly called Ji Qiuyue for help.

With a soft snap, Shen Yaoqing was struck on the back of the head. After being slapped by his wife, he laughed and stopped teasing Shen Yan.

A few loach fluttered in the basin, and the water splashed outside. Shen Yan swirled a little oil into the basin with chopsticks, so the loaches would spit out the mud.

The evening clouds turned orange, and the breeze swept away the day’s heat. The family gathered to eat together, happy and harmonious, a scene Lu Gu had rarely seen.

In front of him sat the only bowl of egg custard, which Ji Qiuyue said had been steamed just for him.

With the memory of yesterday fading, Shen Yaoqing spoke more freely, and while Shen Xuanqing—who was five years younger and three years younger than Shen Yaoqing—spoke less prudently, Lu Gu sensed the atmosphere loosen.

Lu Gu felt the egg custard was too precious for him to touch, so he quietly handed the bowl to Wei Lanxiang and said, “Mother, you eat.”

Although his “Mother” was softly spoken, his thoughtfulness was clear. Wei Lanxiang, in a better mood, gave him a faint smile and pushed the bowl back toward him.

Because Shen Xuanqing had already recognized Lu Gu as the new fulang, and because she hoped he would live with the second son in the future, Wei Lanxiang spoke casually: “No, I’ve eaten enough these days. If you want to eat, you can eat; the injury will heal if you eat well, and in the future you’ll give it to us…” 

Having said this, Wei Lanxiang suddenly felt it might be improper and hurriedly added, “You’ll cook for Erqing in the future. It will be your job to handle meals and laundry.” 

What she really meant to say was to have a big, strong son for Shen Xuanqing, but she knew the words wouldn’t come out correctly, so she swallowed them. Ji Qiuyue paused her chopsticks, eyebrows lowering.

“Eat, eat,” Shen Yaoqing tried to smooth things over, even placing vegetables into Ji Qiuyue’s bowl as a show of flattery.

Lu Gu didn’t know what was going on and held his breath, afraid that a single word or action might displease someone.

Ji Qiuyue had been married for three years. Life had been good at first, but after Shen Shunfu left, things grew hard. They couldn’t even eat well; everyone had to work hard to earn money. For money, Shen Yaoqing and Ji Qiuyue had discussed whether or not to resort to sex. Otherwise, the child would suffer and adults would be dragged down as well. It wasn’t easy to raise a child, not to mention confinement periods when the milk might not be enough, so she hadn’t made a move in three years.

Besides, she couldn’t tell anyone about their troubles. If she spoke of their hardships, Shen Yaoqing would have to strike himself several times over. The two kept silent on this, but the rest of the Shen family understood a little of the truth, while outsiders did not. For farmers, marriage and children were intertwined realities, and gossip inevitably found its way in.

Fortunately, Ji Qiuyue soon lifted her head and regained her composure. Wei Lanxiang knew she’d spoken out of turn, so she pushed more of Qiuyue’s favorite dishes onto the table, saying, “Qiuyue, you love this rabbit meat—eat more.”

Shen Yaoqing grew bolder, loading food onto Qiuyue’s plate himself, which drew a scolding from her.

The sun sank, and another day slipped by.

When night fell, lamps wouldn’t burn for long if there was no work to do. After dark, the Shen family returned to their rooms to rest.

Lu Gu lay rigid on the bed, a statue of stillness, not daring to move.

Normally, after a marriage, the shuang’er would sleep on the outside of the bed so she could bring tea and water to her husband at night, but Shen Xuanqing was used to sleeping alone and felt uncomfortable if someone slept on the outer side, so he pushed Lu Gu toward the inner side.

Entering the room, only the two of them remained; the door and windows were shut, and Lu Gu trembled at the sight of the tall, strong man standing beside the bed.

To tell the truth, he had never been so close to Shen Xuanqing. The overpowering aura made him afraid to look directly at him, until now there was only a rough silhouette in the dark.

He suspected Shen Xuanqing was cold and fierce, but as long as he did not provoke him, it would be fine. As for his appearance, he was too preoccupied with everyday life—banging out a living and filling his stomach—to care about how he looked, or what others thought.

The two lay like that, Lu Gu scarcely breathing.

Night deepened; a few dogs barked in the yard before quieting again. He had planned to stay awake all night, but with Shen Xuanqing lying beside him and not moving, his own breathing steadied, and Lu Gu slowly closed his eyes.

The forehead wound had not yet healed, and he felt lightheaded; his body had been drained by years of hunger, so even though he had slept most of the day, he finally fell asleep.
