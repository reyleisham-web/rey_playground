Chapter 101


"Sister Wanyun's life is really hard; she is the only one left." Shen Yan took the washed vegetables in both hands and shook the water from the wooden basin.

Lu Gu brought a small bamboo dipper, using it to drain the water from the washed vegetables because they would be fried later. After hearing Shen Yan's remark, he frowned and sighed softly.

In the Zhang family's courtyard, Li Wanyun, wearing a filial piety dress, tilted her head up and said, "My mother just passed away; her body hasn’t cooled yet. I haven’t even brought back a coffin, so you’re asking me for money. Where do you think I’ll get it from?"

"I just want to ask you, did you sell my Zhang family's eleven acres of land?" An old man with his hands behind his back widened his eyes in anger. His words plainly treated Li Wanyun as a thief who had sold the family land.

"Your Zhang family," Li Wanyun snapped, furious, a chill running down her spine. "Did you come to help my mother when she was sick? Did you ever give a grain of rice or a handful of flour when my husband was buried?"

"So what if I sold the land? My mother is dying; no one came to see her, and no decent relative gave me money. What could I do? The little money at home was wiped out by you when my husband was buried! Now you ask me for money—I tell you, the money from selling the land has already been spent on medicine, and I can’t even scrape together enough to hold a banquet."

Li Wanyun’s anger made her whole body tremble. Her eyes widened and tears streamed down her face. While she spoke, she suddenly remembered something. She raised her hand to wipe away her tears, then smiled and said, "Fourth Uncle, uncles and aunts, you’ve come just in time. Your nephew’s wife has no money, and I can’t even bury my mother, so I can only ask you to lend me some money. Besides, this is your Zhang family affair—the uncles and aunts should step in and pay for this."

Fourth Uncle Zhang felt a flush of shame at Li Wanyun’s words about eating and taking money. He was an elder who should support the younger generation, but being publicly accused of greed for the younger generation’s property made the old flesh on his face twitch.

Now Li Wanyun was all that remained of the Zhang line; the money from selling the land was at least dozens of taels. It was their Zhang family’s land, so naturally it was their Zhang family’s money. Why should it fall into the hands of a daughter-in-law who was clearly an outsider? It would be fine if Li Wanyun could remain a widow, but he feared that if she remarried someday, the money would truly fall into outsiders’ hands.

Thinking along these lines, he regained the confidence to press for money again.

Next to him, Zhang Gui whispered in his ear, telling him that the land had fetched more than fifty taels.

Hearing this, Fourth Uncle Zhang narrowed his eyes; a gleam flashed within them—more than fifty taels!

"I want to know, did you hide the money from the land sale?" Fourth Uncle Zhang asked solemnly.

"Bah!" Li Wanyun spat. "Who the hell says I hid the money? You blind, pig-headed fools who pretend to be upright, with rotten tongues and black hearts. What immoral thing won’t you do? You’d even steal money from the dead!"

"It’s not proper for a daughter-in-law to scold her uncles and aunts!" Fourth Uncle Zhang and his kin sprang to their feet and railed at her, grabbing for sticks and firewood to beat her.

Seeing this, Li Wanyun gritted her teeth, risking her reputation, and imitated how Lao Zhang used to look. She sank to the ground, patted her thighs, and cried out, "The Zhang family is cruel; they’ll chase me to death. If you beat me today, I’ll rather die."

"I was beaten for no reason in the past, and I couldn’t even get a bite to eat. Now I can only die; they won’t even let me live."

She cried bitterly, Quanzi’s mother and Wei Lanxiang trying to yank her up but failing to move her. She kept weeping, full of sorrow and grief. The crying grew more intense as she muttered about being beaten and starving.

At first Li Wanyun wanted only to drive these relatives away, but the more she spoke, the sadder she became. Her life had never been easy; after marrying that animal, she never found stability. The more she cried, the more tears she shed.

Shen Xuanqing was also at the Zhang household. Although Zhang Zhengzi was gone and the help of these kin could not be repaid, for Li Wanyun’s loneliness, helping her dig graves and bury people with Quanzi could be regarded as a good deed.

He wrested the long door latch from Fourth Uncle Zhang, frowned, and said, "Fourth Uncle, all the money from the land sale was spent on medicine. In the past few days their family has boiled medicine every day. Didn’t you smell it in the village?"

Fourth Uncle Zhang and the Zhang family belonged to the same clan and lived in the same village, but their relationship wasn’t close. They never expected that as soon as Lao Zhang died, Fourth Uncle Zhang and Zhang Gui would come asking for money. The villagers spoke fairly: seeing a doctor and taking medicine was a bottomless pit; let alone a few dozen taels, even a hundred taels would not be enough.

The women and gossips like Wei Lanxiang also scolded them for heartlessness, and whether in the village or outside, it would be embarrassing to be pointed at. The Zhang relatives soon relented.

Fourth Uncle Zhang didn’t realize this; he was blocked by a few young, strong men at the moment, so he couldn’t say anything for a while.
