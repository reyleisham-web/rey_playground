Chapter 78 Part 20


But Xiao Zhang would get beaten even if she hadn't done anything wrong. She couldn't understand Lao Zhang Shi the witch. She once spoke with the Zhang family, but Lao Zhang Shi scolded her for being nosy. Zhang Zhengzi even boasted about how he beat his wife, and it had nothing to do with her as an outsider.

Since then, Aunt Miao hadn’t kept in touch with the Zhang family. It was hard for an upright official to intervene in a family affair. She said so, and Xiao Zhang ended up being scolded for several days. She felt guilty, but she couldn’t help anymore. In the end, she pretended not to see anything.

Lu Gu was most afraid of scenes of beating someone, and he wanted to slip away with anxiety gnawing at him.

Unexpectedly, under his mother’s scolding, Zhang Zhengzi beat his wife even harder, and he struck Xiao Zhang on the head with his fists. Xiao Zhang tried to free herself and staggered out of the house. There was nowhere to hide, so she ran toward the mountains recklessly.

Zhang Zhengzi chased after her with a stick in his hand, shouting, “Stupid woman! Try running again!”

Xiao Zhang ran past Lu Gu. Seeing Zhang Zhengzi about to catch her, he also saw the wooden stick and a man with a grotesque face, his own face pale, while the puppy stood at his feet and barked at the Zhang family.

Wei Lanxiang and Shen Yaoqing heard the commotion outside. Lu Gu felt a bit relieved when he saw his family and hid behind Wei Lanxiang, whispering, “Guaizi, come back.” His voice was soft, drowned in the puppy’s barking.

Zhang Zhengzi flew over, chased a few steps, and watched Xiao Zhang scramble up the gentle slope, running into the mountain with all her might. He muttered curses, spat on the ground, and snarled, “You son of a bitch—you’ll know how to come back when you freeze to death.”

He turned to leave. More people in the village emerged to watch the scene, and he felt a twinge of shame. The puppy, bold as ever, barked at him again.

Zhang Zhengzi, lacking anything constructive to vent, swung the wooden stick and cursed, “What a dog—even grandpa you dare to bite.”

“Good boy!” Shen Yaoqing shouted. The puppy heard him, barked twice, and then ran back. He called again, “Zhengzi, you got up early today.”

Seeing Shen Yaoqing, Zhang Zhengzi lowered the wooden stick and sneered, “It’s Brother Daqing.”

Zhang Zhengzi was the same age as Shen Xuanqing, but their relationship wasn’t very good. When he was thirteen, he and two other village boys had stolen chickens from the Shen family. After a few days, Shen Xuanqing found out, and he and Da Chen chased him outside the village and brought him to the ground. He was beaten so badly that his nose was bruised and his face swollen; his mouth and nose bled. Since then, he never dared to provoke Shen Xuanqing again.

But that was all in childhood, and nothing more came of it. The Shen family helped him a little when he got married; they all lived in the same village, so they didn’t hold such a grudge.

Xiao Zhang, dressed so thinly, had run to the mountains after being beaten. Even Shen Yaoqing couldn’t help but say, “Aren’t you going to go look for her? It’s snowing—something could happen.”

Zhang Zhengzi didn’t care. “It’s fine. She’ll come back after a while when it gets cold, and anyway she knows the way.”

As the saying goes, marry a chicken and follow the chicken; marry a dog and follow the dog. He believed this was the wife he’d married, so she ought to follow him in everything. Even if today she ran around for a long time, she would still have to cook and wash for him, and besides, where could she actually go if she ran away?

Shen Yaoqing frowned, but he didn’t comment on another family’s wife. Some villagers loved to gossip as if they had nothing better to do. They liked to chatter behind others’ backs, especially Lao Zhang Shi, whose tongue was vicious and whose heart was cruel. Zhang Zhengzi, so incompetent, could even sell his own daughter, so people avoided dealing with his family; otherwise, they didn’t know what would happen.

Zhang Zhengzi went back, but his mother was still stamping and cursing in the yard, telling him not to let his wife back into the house after she’d run away, and she slammed the yard door shut.

Wei Lanxiang shook her head, looked toward the slope, but Xiao Zhang was nowhere to be seen, and she sighed, What a good girl to be out here in this winter.

Lu Gu felt sorry for her, and even after he returned to his room, he was restless. He recalled what Lao Zhang Shi had said when she scolded Xiao Zhang Shi, and what she said about stealing food, which reminded him of the winter five years ago. He was so hungry he couldn’t bear it, so he stole a cold steamed bun to eat. Du Hehua found out and pinched his face hard, even grabbing it.

He couldn’t recall whether Lu Daxiang was there that day. He only remembered that Lu Wen was reading a book in the room. He’d heard Du Hehua scold him, and he stood by the window, saying she was too noisy, before slamming the window shut. Du Hehua slapped him to prevent him from crying or making a sound, then pulled him into the main room and pricked his finger with a needle. She kept talking about stealing and let him have a longer memory.

Even thinking about it now, he still remembered that heart-wrenching pain.

Tang Pozi in his arms was warm, and his fingers no longer hurt. Lu Gu himself didn’t know why, but he couldn’t help but keep thinking about Xiao Zhang Shi. She must have been very hungry when she stole, and he didn’t know if she had eaten enough.

He was so panicked that he could barely sew anymore, and after a while he stood up suddenly.

Outside, the wind blew again. Wei Lanxiang and Shen Yan were sitting on the bed; Ji Qiuyue and Shen Yaoqing were studying in their own room. Only the puppy watched Lu Gu come out of the kitchen, clearly carrying something in his arms. He moved carefully, then hurried out the door and headed toward the mountain.
