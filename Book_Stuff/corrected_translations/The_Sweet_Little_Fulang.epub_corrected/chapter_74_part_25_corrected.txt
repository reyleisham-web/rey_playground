Chapter 74 Part 25


In the past two days he had been running up and down the mountain, and he hadn’t wanted to wear the hairpin. He was afraid he would drop it, so he’d left it in the room for safety. Besides, women could wear hairpins, some were common on their heads, but the Shuang’ers in their country always wrapped their hair with cloth, which was more like men. How could anyone wear such a silver hairpin? If he worried someone would glance at him because of it, he would shrink back a bit.

Ji Qiuyue didn’t know what he was thinking and said with a smile, “It’s cold, and there are still plenty of pears. Tonight, let’s boil goji berries with red dates and brown sugar. I’ve heard others talk about it, so let’s eat that today.”

In town, people called brown sugar “rock sugar.” In the countryside, brown sugar was common; when they saw lumps of brown sugar, they described them by color. The brown sugar at home had been bought by Shen Yaoqing, thinking his wife would want some too. Most of the meat at home was bought by Shen Xuanqing, and this year they’d kept the badger skin and made a fur collar to protect their necks.

Qiuyue usually had a bit of selfishness, but she never expressed it. Wei Lanxiang, the mother-in-law, wasn’t the type to be overtly indulgent in her words or deeds, or she would dwell on it; yet their family had always treated her well, so when there was something good for the family to taste, they would let them have it, and there was nothing to hide.

“Okay.” Lu Gu agreed with a smile and went out to fetch firewood after finishing the honey water.

The whole family had been busy on the mountain for three days. On the afternoon of the third day, Wei Lanxiang learned where the Shen family’s third household was making timber, so she went to call Shen Shunwang and Shen Yuping, and on the way she ran into Shi Dachen’s family. She teased a bit and even dragged Shi Dachen to help as a laborer.

Shen Yuping was one year younger than Shen Xuanqing, seventeen this year. He wasn’t short; he had big, piercing eyes, and with those eyes he could be called a handsome boy.

“Yuping needs to practice more. If his wood-cutting skills improve, his father-in-law and mother-in-law will praise him in the future,” Shen Yaoqing said with a smile when he saw his cousin coming over.

Shen Shunwang and Zhou Xiangjun had been showing Yuping some girls and Shuang’ers in recent days. They’d come to Shen’s second room to chat twice and had Wei Lanxiang help learn more, which was why he said so.

Shen Yuping was younger, and the Shen family’s third room had always lived well, so he had never known hardship. He looked more immature, and when he thought about getting married, he scratched his head and smiled shyly, showing his white teeth. His father, Shen Shunwang, saw his naïveté and laughed out loud, patting him on the head. “How old are you? You’re not mature yet—don’t be silly, go to work!”

Shen Shunwang and Shen Shunfu were somewhat alike: they worked hard, were good at farm work, could drive carts, and even drove carts for others. It wasn’t a problem to earn a little money, and the family could eat plenty of oil and meat every few days, and they could still save some.

Almost everyone in the countryside was handy and hardworking. It was already afternoon, and they didn’t waste time: the men tied up several trunks and carried them home. Lu Gu and Shen Yan couldn’t carry much, so they picked lighter pieces of wood and lifted one each.

“Brother Guzi, why don’t we take two?” Shen Yan felt that the wood wasn’t too heavy after lifting one.

She was only eleven, not even as tall as Lu Gu, thin and small, and before Lu Gu could answer, Wei Lanxiang smiled and said, “You and your brother Guzi can just carry one.”

“Okay, mother, we’ll go back first.” Shen Yan nodded; they didn’t need to tie the wood. They were faster than a few men, with Lu Gu in front and her behind, and the two of them descended the mountain first.

After the firewood was cut, it needed to be sawn into sections so it could be stuffed into the woodshed, so it wouldn’t pile up in the yard and get wet when it rained or snowed.

They saw the wood in the yard; there was no need to run outside. There was only one big saw at home, and Shen Xuanqing and Shen Yaoqing took turns sawing. Sometimes they weren’t there, helping the Chen family and the third uncle carry wood, so Lu Gu and Wei Lanxiang sawed the wood in the yard. If one person couldn’t handle it, two people working together would pull the big saw more easily.

There was sawdust in the courtyard for days, and the sawdust wasn’t thrown away. It was light and dry in the sun and could be used as fire starters with igniter grass.

After lunch, Lu Gu finished washing the dishes and saw Shen Xuanqing sawing wood in the yard, so he went over, carried the wood pieces to the woodshed, set them down, and then raised his hand. The rough bark scratched the back of his hand and fingers, breaking the skin a little; a drop of blood oozed out and stopped soon after.

This was common with rough work. Lu Gu glanced at it and didn’t pay it much mind. After going out, Shen Xuanqing noticed the small wound on the back of his hand, stopped what he was doing, and asked, “Scratched?”

“Yeah, there’s no bleeding, it’s fine,” Lu Gu replied, noticing the sawdust on the ground as he fetched the broom again.

In winter, frostbite was easy to develop in the dry air, and the hands were prone to chapping. In the past two years, Shen Yan had frostbite on her hands, painful and itchy, her fingers red and swollen, with scabs that looked miserable.

So last year, when he hunted badgers, he left some badger oil for his family to rub on their hands and feet. He also bought some cotton and asked Wei Lanxiang to sew a tube sleeve for Shen Yan. You could put your hands inside it, and you could tuck them in when you weren’t working in winter to keep warm.

Frostbite was a yearly hazard in winter, and if not treated promptly, it could leave lasting problems, especially as you grew older. He wasn’t sure whether Lu Gu had frostbite himself.
