Luo Biao, who worked as a thug in a brothel, had seen and heard plenty of ugliness, and he had never learned to keep his words to himself. Shen Xuanqing didn’t seem to notice. He kept his usual expression and said simply, “I’ve come here today to entrust you with this matter. Since he likes to visit brothels, arrange for him to go often. If you can keep him staying in the building, best of all—don’t let him go home for ten days and a half months.”

He spoke, and Shen Xuanqing pushed the money wrapped in red cloth across the table. It contained ten taels of silver, a substantial amount.

“Second Brother Shen, you are—” Luo Biao began to say no, but was cut off.

“A word from you while you’re negotiating a business?” Shen Xuanqing raised his eyes slightly, then spoke quietly. “Do what I ask, and I’ll spend the money willingly. There’s no need to shirk. Just deal with it, and later I’ll come to you again.”

“In that case, don’t worry, Second Brother Shen. I’ll certainly do it.” Luo Biao set the money aside and poured the wine for both of them.

After getting him drunk, Luo Biao asked one more question about why he had to deal with Li Mingshan at this moment. He knew about Lu Wen. He’d gone when Shen Xuanqing got married before. When he heard that Lu Wen had threatened the Shen family with the government, he banged the table and cursed a few words, then patted his chest to assure Shen Xuanqing that Li Mingshan, this grandson, would be so hungry for lust he wouldn’t be able to walk straight.

Shen Xuanqing didn’t underestimate him and explained a few more things, asking Luo Biao to find out how Lu Wen was faring in town, and then he drank again.

Speaking of it, Fenggu Town had quite a few wealthy merchants, and two Li families. Li Mingshan’s family ran a silk-and-satin shop in town; they had money, and power was there as well. It was impossible for a countryman to go head-to-head with a rich man, so he could only find another way. Li Mingshan was good for nothing except the lust of the flesh. If he lingered in the flower house and didn’t come home, who could blame anyone?

After Shen Xuanqing left, Luo Biao appeared to be reliable. After a nap in the afternoon, he went to drink with the regulars who often came to the building for their amusement and who knew Li Mingshan. A man who ate, drank, prostituted himself, and gambled was not a good thing. Luo Biao still talked about Lu Wen, who had hooked up with Li Mingshan, thinking of that handsome appearance; the man surnamed Bu licked his lips, a wicked gleam in his eyes.

As the sun began to set and it grew dark, the lanterns in front of the brothel lit up, and the interior shone even brighter and more lively.

Luo Biao came in from the back yard, looked around the downstairs lobby, and saw Bu Zicheng bringing Li Mingshan in. The food had not been laid to waste. He winked at Hong Yao, a familiar girl, and Hong Yao understood. She walked over and pulled Li Mingshan to sit, pressing him to drink a glass of wine.

Li Mingshan looked good in appearance; his silk clothes were noble and proper, and he carried himself like a gentleman studying at a scholar’s side while fanning himself. But it was a pity that after only a glass of wine, the scent of powder clung to Hong Yao, and Li Mingshan’s animal instinct awakened. He caressed her waist lightly and even pinched her buttocks when others weren’t paying attention, making Hong Yao smile inwardly at his lewdness.

The girl in the brothel used a few tricks to keep Li Mingshan there for two nights. Usually he only came for an hour or two before slipping away to enjoy himself in secret.

When he returned home, his mother Zhang Shi blocked him at the door. Li Mingshan lied, saying he had slept at a classmate’s house. If you were upright, you wouldn’t go astray, so that was all.

Most of Li Mingshan’s cronies and friends who came and went in the daytime had the same outward air of scholars, but inside they knew the dirtier truths and helped each other cover them up. They thought nothing of visiting brothels or drinking heavily; if the family didn’t permit it, they simply hid it from the family’s knowledge.

How could Lu Wen, a Shuang’er from the countryside, know about all this? Zhang Shi didn’t know either. The Shuang’er and the town’s respectable girls, just like Li Mingshan, hadn’t left the brothel and couldn’t easily know the dirty things of those men.

Two days later, just before evening, Li Mingshan was thinking about the afternoon’s feelings and found an excuse to look for a classmate to leave the house. Even Lu Wen, he didn’t bother to glance at, and he went straight to the brothel.

It happened to be a coincidence that he ran into Bu Zicheng again, and the two sat at a table in the lobby to drink. While they drank, Bu Zicheng saw Li Mingshan with a girl in his arms, about to drift away, and the wicked thoughts in his heart could no longer be contained. He smiled lightly and said, “Brother Li, if you don’t mind, this Shuang’er beside me will be happy to be with you tonight. Treat you as a brother.”

As soon as he spoke, the Shuang’er beside him slid onto Li Mingshan’s side, making Li Mingshan’s eyes widen with surprise; such a good thing did exist.

Bu Zicheng didn’t invite him for nothing. When he saw Li Mingshan take the bait, he lowered his voice and said, “I heard Lu Wen has been living in town recently. If you don’t mind, Brother Li, how about we play for a while?”

Li Mingshan’s expression changed after a moment. He sneered and angrily said, “What are they doing, trying to replace us? Brother Bu, you dare speak.”

Li Mingshan also had a temper, not a little temper, and he immediately turned away from Shuang’er and pushed Hong Yao aside. Bu Zicheng struck the wall with his palm, his sensuality failing, and sat there with a sour expression, gripping his wine glass tightly.

Besides, Li Mingshan and Lu Wen had been in love since they met, but due to public virtue and appearances, they only held hands and never slept together. Lu Wen was truly good-looking; otherwise Li Mingshan—used to seeing beauties in town—wouldn’t have fallen for him. He was handsome and beautiful, bright and charming when he smiled; when he frowned he could be sad, and that could soften people’s hearts. He sighed, with his pitiful and charming appearance, he gave Li Mingshan everything he wanted.

After leaving the brothel, Li Mingshan remembered that he hadn’t seen Lu Wen in a few days, so he passed by there. He didn’t notice that Luo Biao was watching him from behind, and soon a short, thin boy trailed after Li Mingshan.

Lu Wen pretended to be studying, as if he were staying with his classmate Shuang’er, but in truth he entered a courtyard in the back street through the back-yard secret door of that family. It was a place Li Mingshan had bought. Usually Lu Wen and the servant girl lived in the back street and came out through the secret door when he needed to go out. His classmate’s family took the money and favored him, and they naturally supported him; they didn’t dare reveal any words, so nothing could be found out on the surface.
