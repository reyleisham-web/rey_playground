Chapter 90

Sweeping and Cleaning All Day

Chapter 90
Sweeping and cleaning all day, they had to clean the house inside and out, and it was tiring. Lu Gu sat on a small stool and shoveled the bottom of the pot; there was a lot of soot on his face from the pot, and he didn’t realize it until he looked in the bronze mirror. When he looked up, the rest of the family saw his face, and they all laughed.

Twenty-four hours passed amid hustle and bustle, and the next morning the snow began to drift again. As the saying goes, auspicious snow is a sign of a prosperous year to come. The wind was light today, and the white snow fell slowly, gradually covering the ground.

In the morning, Wei Lanxiang took Shen Yan to the tofu workshop in the neighboring village to buy tofu. Today was the 25th day of the twelfth lunar month, the time to eat tofu. She carried a big basket covered with cloth and bought half a block of tofu. When she got home, she saw Ji Qiuyue and said, “It’s fortunate to go early. There were so many people buying tofu that it was crowded. The young fulang of the He family is timid and poor. He could only buy a small piece of tofu. He was pushed around and didn’t dare to speak. He was squeezed out and could only stand behind, almost crying. It was Quanzi’s fulang who saw it and scolded those women in the neighboring village, and your third aunt and I also helped to scold them.”

Ji Qiuyue usually spoke briskly and was very familiar with Quanzi’s fulang; if she had been there, she would certainly have helped scold those women.

Wei Lanxiang uncovered the basket and pinched a small piece of white tofu to taste. “It's also because the tofu boss and his wife are kind-hearted. When they saw that the young fulang of the He family was standing behind the crowd, they cut a piece for him and sent him home.”

The owner of the tofu workshop was called the tofu boss, and Ji Qiuyue and Lu Gu called him Uncle Tofu Boss when they met. Poor people were indeed more likely to be bullied. Even when a family had no money, they were looked down upon and bullied by others in Qingxi Village. That sense of vexation at not being able to speak up, and the anger at being treated coldly by even their own kin, made them sad and furious.

Because of this, even when the eldest uncle’s family later showed their favor, Shen Xuanqing wouldn’t easily share meat with their family.

Wei Lanxiang kept guard in front of the door and didn’t pay attention to others. After successfully buying the tofu and listening to Quanzi’s fulang scold the others, she clarified the situation three times and then began to help.

“Those people should be scolded.” Ji Qiuyue and Wei Lanxiang shared the same hatred. She remembered how they had been looked down upon two years ago and grew a little angry. What kind of skill did they have to bully the fulang from the He family? She knew those three women; the family’s life was not good at all, so they could only bully those poorer than them.

“They should be scolded, but don’t be angry. Be happy; mother will cook tofu, and you can have a good meal for lunch.” Wei Lanxiang hurriedly persuaded, fearing that Ji Qiuyue would become angry and hurt the fetus.

“Mother, I know.” Ji Qiuyue smiled.

Lu Gu helped Shen Xuanqing mend the sleeves in the room; there was a small hole, and he sewed it so it wouldn’t look sloppy or tattered.

Shen Xuanqing donned his outer clothes, and both he and Lu Gu heard Wei Lanxiang’s words from outside. He knew that the fulang of the He family wasn’t very brave, just like Lu Gu. Now he felt a little worried; he frowned slightly and said, “In the future, if someone pushes you or bullies you when you go out and you can’t beat them, just come back and call me. If not, let mother and sister-in-law scold them.”

He didn’t beat women, but he could beat the men in the other family; there was no need to be overly kind or polite. In their mountain-village at the foot of the mountain, there were many uneducated people. Sometimes reasoning with them didn’t make sense, and they could only speak with their fists. If they were strong, the opponent would be afraid.

“En.” Lu Gu nodded heavily. He knew he was timid, and he didn’t dare quarrel with others when he went out, and his heart always trembled. What Shen Xuanqing said at this moment felt like reassurance, giving him a solid backing and something to rely on.

Wei Lanxiang was cooking tofu in the kitchen, and Lu Gu went to help. Shen Yaoqing swept the snow in the courtyard, while Shen Yan and Ji Qiuyue were not idle; they brought a bowl of paste and pasted the red “Fu” character papers they bought at the fair on the doors and windows.
“Fu” (福): good fortune; happiness; luck

At lunch there were stewed tofu with Chinese cabbage, stir-fried tofu with minced pork, and deep-fried tofu with dried tofu. Shen Yaoqing was amused when he saw the dishes on the table and exclaimed, “It’s all tofu—the Quanfu Banquet.” He said it with a cheerful tone, and Wei Lanxiang couldn’t stop smiling. Indeed, what they ate was the Quanfu Banquet.

Once the red “Fu” character papers were pasted on the doors and windows, the house immediately felt different; the more you looked at it, the more festive it seemed.

The days passed quickly, and before they knew it, the next day arrived. On the twenty-sixth of the twelfth lunar month, it was time to eat big meat. Those who raised pigs and sheep, and those who wished to eat it, began to sharpen their knives. This year Wei Lanxiang only raised chickens and ducks, so she had to buy pork and mutton from Meng Dayue. Shen Xuanqing took Lu Gu to buy the meat in the morning. They went early and witnessed the sight of pigs and sheep being led out of the pens to be slaughtered; the spectacle drew many onlookers and was quite lively.

There is a local custom here: on the twenty-sixth of the twelfth lunar month, rich or poor, you must eat some meat. Lu Gu bought plenty of meat with Shen Xuanqing; the bamboo basket was heavy. As they were leaving, they spotted Li Wanyun in the crowd; she had also come to buy meat.
