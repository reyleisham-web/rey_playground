Chapter 22


As soon as Lu Gu picked up the golden honeycomb, honey began to flow slowly from a small opening. Lu Gu was a little panicked at first, but when he saw the honey about to spill, he quickly turned around and said, “I’ll go fetch the bowl.”

Shen Xuanqing wrapped the honeycomb in large leaves and followed him. The bamboo basket was left at the gate of the courtyard, not carried inside, and he said, “There is an empty jar next to the stove—use that.”

Lu Gu listened, washed the jar, and wiped it dry with a cloth towel.

Shen Xuanqing picked up the small wooden spoon on the chopping board; it was the spoon Lu Gu used when making the pancakes. It wasn’t stained like the cooking spoon and looked relatively clean.

The honeycomb wasn’t very hard and could be cut or squeezed with the wooden spoon. Lu Gu set the small jar on the chopping board and couldn’t help but stand there watching the honey flow into the jar. The two of them stood together; if Shen Xuanqing hadn’t been so tall, their foreheads might have touched.

The golden honey was bright and smelled mellow and sweet. The honeycombs in the mountains weren’t only on the trees; there were clusters under the cover of weeds and wildflowers, waiting to be found.

Today Shen Xuanqing found a lump of honeycomb in the grass; the honey couldn’t flow completely, even after squeezing, so he picked a better place, broke off a piece, and handed it to Lu Gu.

Seeing that Lu Gu hesitated—he obviously hadn’t eaten it before—he spoke softly: “You can eat it. Just chew to taste the sweetness, don’t swallow it.”

When he was a child, his mother gave him honey water or dipped honey on chopsticks and stuffed it into his mouth to taste, but he had never eaten a honeycomb. When he heard that it was edible, Lu Gu bit it carefully. The mellow sweetness filled his mouth and widened his eyes as soon as he tasted it, and Shen Xuanqing, watching him, smiled unconsciously.

Speaking of which, Shen Xuanqing was only half a year older than Lu Gu, and his face was still a little green with youth. That smile faded from his usual composure and maturity, and there seemed to be warmth in his eyes, like the sun in the sky. Lu Gu felt inexplicably at ease, and he didn’t feel so embarrassed about his clumsy appearance, having not eaten any good food.

He thought again that Shen Xuanqing was a good person, and if he were given such a sweet honeycomb, he wouldn’t really laugh at him for not eating well.

“Don’t throw this away—keep it for you to eat every day.” Shen Xuanqing broke off a small piece for himself and put the squeezed and deformed honeycomb into the bowl.

The sweet taste overflowed in their mouths, and no matter how bad their moods were, they were appeased. Before Lu Gu finished chewing, he asked, “Are you hungry? I stewed fish.”

As soon as he stepped into the kitchen, he could smell the spicy aroma, but because honey was important, Shen Xuanqing didn’t bother to ask about it. He nodded and replied frankly, “I’m hungry.”

After spending a day running in the mountains, he had brought a few steamed buns as dry food. How could he not be hungry?

Lu Gu didn’t eat the honeycomb anymore, and lifted the lid of the pot to bring out the rice porridge, then filled a large bowl with stewed fish. Although it had simmered for a long time, the pot hadn’t burnt after adding water; only the bits of fish sticking to the bottom were a little burnt. Spices and salt had been added, and the taste wasn’t bad.

By the time the food was served, it was dark outside.

Shen Xuanqing brought the bamboo baskets inside and sealed the gate of the courtyard. It grew dark quickly, so they had to light the oil lamps they didn’t usually use.

The dogs wandered around. Shen Xuanqing threw a steamed bun to each of them and let them eat freely so they wouldn’t come over during the meal, then said to Lu Gu, “No need to feed them at night; they caught a few big bamboo rats on the way back. I’ve roasted them, and they ate them.”

Dahui and the others were used to eating leftovers, and the bones were saved for stews, with some meat left on top for the three of them to eat. They were used to cooked food; Shen Xuanqing found a safe place to light a fire and roast it at will, and he also poured himself some water from a bamboo tube to drink.

Farmers’ dogs sometimes couldn’t eat at home, so they would dig outside to find something to eat. The country people had long been used to it, but they could still catch big bamboo rats to eat—quite a feat, Lu Gu thought.

The pancakes baked in the afternoon were still fluffy. The thick pancakes could be eaten cold without reheating.

The taste of Lu Gu’s stewed fish wasn’t as good as Ji Qiuyue’s or Wei Lanxiang’s, but it was the first time he cooked it, and it felt good to have pulled it off like this.

He didn’t know if this was Shen Xuanqing’s first time either. For him, after a hunt, he could have a bite of hot food and hot dishes without needing to cook it himself.

Such a big pancake wouldn’t be eaten in one go—at least two or three meals—but with Shen Xuanqing there, there would be nothing left but the soup.

The soup was so spicy that the dogs lapped it up; as long as the meat was gone, there was nothing to regret.

It grew dark, and Lu Gu was washing the pots and dishes in the kitchen. He could still see by the moonlight, but after a while Shen Xuanqing came in with an oil lamp, and the kitchen became much brighter.

“Boil some water. I’ll wash at night. There’s plenty of firewood in the forest.” Shen Xuanqing put the oil lamp away and went to the woodshed to gather firewood after speaking.

Because it was something like taking a bath, he didn’t look at Lu Gu’s eyes as he spoke. If he came back tired, he would wash his face and go to sleep, but it would be different now. If the body was dirty and smelled of sweat, wouldn’t it be dirty to your ger?
