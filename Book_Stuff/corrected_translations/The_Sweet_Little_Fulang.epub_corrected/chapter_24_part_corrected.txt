Chapter 24


The rain continued unabated, and with the mountain wind rising, it blew under the eaves, making the air even colder. The three dogs also hid in the main room to avoid the rain, finding a dry place to lie down. With more people nearby, Lu Gu’s hands weaving bamboo baskets grew more cautious, and he felt a little uneasy.

Shen Xuanqing was momentarily stunned, realizing that standing still wasn’t good, so he moved a bench and sat next to Lu Gu, picking up the bamboo strips and speaking in a low voice: “I’ll do this with you.”

“Yes.” Lu Gu couldn’t refuse, lowering his brows, he complied.

As soon as the words were spoken, he began to respond. Shen Xuanqing, who had been a little at a loss just now, gave up the hesitation and thought of something to say, and asked about Lu Gu’s livelihood in the past few days.

“I see you dried a lot of goji berries.”

Hearing this, Lu Gu paused his hands, raised his eyes nervously, and said, “I heard the medical center accepts them.”

Shen Xuanqing pondered for a moment and said, “That’s right. Not only doctors would buy them; if you sold them in the market, some buyers would take them as well, but the price isn’t high.”

They could see wild goji berries all over the mountains and plains, and they could be used as medicine, but because people often picked and sold them, they were not scarce, so the price hadn’t changed.

After speaking, he got up to look at the two bamboo plaques of wolfberries on the table. Lu Gu picked them up; they were large. They were of better quality than those from Qianshan. Even if the price didn’t rise, they were easier to sell.

He grabbed a handful and took a closer look, then said to Lu Gu: “I’ll take you to sell them when I go down the mountain.” Wasn’t that what he had been busy with these days? It was calmer than before, and he didn’t feel so cramped when he didn’t speak.

A gust of wind blew, carrying the rain and rushing toward the two people in the main room. The cold rain hit Lu Gu’s face and hands, and he hurriedly moved the pile of bamboo strips on the ground to the back; he didn’t add any extra clothes, and he felt cold at the moment.

After a little effort, there were more and darker clouds in the sky, and the outside looked as if it were already night. Seeing that the wind was getting stronger, Shen Xuanqing laid down the bamboo strips he had just woven and said to Lu Gu, “It’s cold, let’s go into the house.” He closed the door of the main room to prevent the rain from blowing in.

It was so dark, and with the door shut again there was no way to work, so Lu Gu had to follow him into a warmer room. The doors and windows were closed, and the heavy rain outside could still be heard, even the wind crashing violently. Fortunately, in the deep mountains and forests, they had such a sturdy house to withstand the wind and rain.

Sitting at the table with nothing to do and unable to see clearly, Shen Xuanqing paused and said, “I’m tired these days, and the rain won’t stop for a while, so why not lie down and rest for a while.”

This was indeed the truth. In the past, he had to cook in the mountains by himself in the evening, so he would come back earlier, but now that Lu Gu was with him, it saved him the trouble of cooking, and he came back later. He had gone out early and come back late for six consecutive days, busy in the mountains, so today’s rain was a good opportunity to rest. Not only him, but Lu Gu also had a lot to do every day, so it would be good for his body to have some free time to lie down.

Sleeping on the same bed was a bit customary for Lu Gu; he opened his mouth after hearing this, but finally didn’t dare to refuse. Besides, if Shen Xuanqing went to sleep, he would just sit at the table and do nothing, which would be even more stupid. The new quilt was pulled onto the bed. He had washed the old quilt two days ago, and when it grew too cold at night, he put the old quilt that had been washed on top of the new quilt, covering with two layers.

Right now, he was not any sleepier than before; Lu Gu lay in bed thinking about something. Shen Xuanqing had suggested taking him to sell things, so perhaps he could bring back more—medlar or other medicinal materials—and he would have to look for them. The bed gradually grew warmer, and the other person on the bed was also thinking about something.

But as the clean, soft breath from Lu Gu drifted over again, Shen Xuanqing forgot about business.

Only a few days had passed, but he had grown used to sleeping at night, and now lying down, with nothing in his arms, the emptiness grew bigger and bigger, like blackness swirling in his chest. If it got out of hand, it would swallow reason.

Lu Gu was a little distracted, and suddenly he was seized by the hand that rested on Shen Xuanqing’s side. He was startled and immediately came back to his senses.

Shen Xuanqing’s hands were bigger than his, strong, and the palms were dry and warm, which made him feel a little afraid, not quite sure what this meant.

In the days prior, when he woke up in the morning, he would always be hugged, but that was only after he had fallen asleep at night; how could it be like this in broad daylight now?

Shame made Lu Gu move his hand slightly, and the big hand holding him tightened even more, gripping firmly, letting him know that Shen Xuanqing hadn’t touched him by mistake.

He had to lie still and let his hands be held.

Who knew that this wasn’t the end yet? After a while of silence, the tall man beside him turned over and pulled him into his arms.

Lu Gu’s whole body went stiff; he didn’t dare to move, flustered. He smelled the wild soapberries on Shen Xuanqing’s body, as if he were warmed by the man’s hot chest, and a restlessness stirred in him, recalling that day—the fear he had felt when he helped Shen Xuanqing wipe up the medicinal wine.

He seemed to hear a soft, satisfied sigh, but he was too nervous to tell whether he had really heard it.
