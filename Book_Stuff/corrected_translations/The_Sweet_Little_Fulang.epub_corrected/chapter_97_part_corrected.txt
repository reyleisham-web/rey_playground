Chapter 97


Because they could not find an excuse for a long time, Lu Gu had no choice but to remove the dirty bed mattress first, and only after that did the two of them put on clean clothes. When Lu Gu looked at the pile of bed sheets and quilts on the chair, neither of them could help but feel embarrassed.

After a long moment, Shen Xuanqing spoke. “How about I find an excuse at noon to let my mother and the others go out first? Then we’ll wash them and hang them in the room. We’ll close the doors and windows for days, and not open them.”

“Is that okay?” Lu Gu asked weakly.

“This is the only way. We can’t take them out for washing right now.” Shen Xuanqing knew that if they hung them outside, it would be a disaster if anyone found out. But if they hung them in the yard today, they had no children to use the excuse that a child wet the bed. That was the truth, so Lu Gu could only nod in agreement.

After lunch, Shen Xuanqing went out, and when he returned, he only said that the third grandma was doing needlework at home, and Wei Lanxiang, who was free and with nothing to do, didn’t need to be urged, and she went over with her unfinished soles. He pretended not to care and then asked Shen Yan if she wanted to practice calligraphy. He said Lu Gu was sick, lying down and unable to come. If Shen Yan wanted to practice calligraphy, he would watch from the side.

When Shen Yan heard this, she quickly replied that it would be better to wait for brother Guzi to recover from his illness; otherwise it would be difficult for the two of them to learn together. After that, she took her embroidery basket and went out to follow Wei Lanxiang’s footsteps.

Ji Qiuyue could stay at home, but when she heard that Quanzi and his fulang were sitting in the yard, basking in the sun and teasing the baby, she also went out. Shen Yaoqing, who had nothing to do, naturally followed his wife.

With no one at home, Shen Xuanqing immediately fetched the laundry wooden basin and poured warm water into it. Just after they had eaten, he gave an excuse that he wanted to wash his hair, so he boiled half a pot of hot water.

Lu Gu saw Shen Xuanqing come in with a wooden basin, and he hurriedly brought over the bed sheets and quilts; both of them had red ears from the shame and excitement.

Shen Xuanqing rubbed wild soapberries to make foam and said, “The sheets are too heavy to wring dry. Just wash the dirty parts.”

“Yeah,” Lu Gu replied in a muffled voice.

Fearing that family members might return suddenly, the two of them acted very quickly. Shen Xuanqing used a wooden stick and a fork to set up a wooden frame and placed it in the gap between the end of the bed and the wall. He laid the bed sheet and quilt on the wooden frame and said, “For these few days, you’ve had to pretend to be sick, because we need an excuse to close the windows and doors. Even if we can’t finish today, maybe tomorrow will be fine.”

Lu Gu’s hands, still stained with water, nodded. As long as they weren’t discovered, there was nothing wrong with it.

It was Lu Gu’s first time doing something so sneaky, and his heart pounded as he washed. After finishing, he felt a wave of relief.

The bed sheet and quilt on the wooden frame were one dark and one light color. Shen Xuanqing looked at them, then glanced at Lu Gu. The nervousness and excitement from the sneaky act had faded, and a smile tugged at his lips.

“I said I need to wash my hair, but it’s sunny outside and there’s no wind, so I’ll wash my hair first; otherwise we’ll be exposed.” When he saw Lu Gu’s eyes curve with a smile, he could not hold back any longer and brushed his fulang’s hair. His heart swelled with joy.

For the next two days, the Shen family members were unusually considerate toward Lu Gu, who “caught a cold.” They did not suspect anything, even though the door and windows of their room stayed closed all the time.

Of course, Lu Gu didn’t stay in the room the whole time; he would come out for meals, and sometimes he sat in the courtyard to bask in the sun to show that his illness wasn’t serious. Otherwise Wei Lanxiang would press Shen Xuanqing to fetch medicine.

Most country folk thought along the same lines: if the illness isn’t serious, it will pass with a little rest.

For the rest of the time, he stayed in the room with the door and windows closed. With Shen Xuanqing dealing with the others outside, he really wasn’t found.

Winter was cold, and they couldn’t dry the bed sheets and quilts in the sun; they could only hang them in the room. The place where they hung the damp sheets and quilts was cold, and they hadn’t dried for two days. Lu Gu couldn’t help but feel anxious.

At night, Shen Xuanqing brought in a charcoal brazier, claiming it was to warm the room for Lu Gu. When it grew dark and everyone else had fallen asleep, the two of them secretly roasted the bed sheets and quilts in the room.

“If I’d known this, I should’ve roasted them last night,” Lu Gu whispered while touching the hot, dry sheets.

His whisper sounded like a thief’s, and Shen Xuanqing laughed.

The firelight reflected in Lu Gu’s eyes, as if it danced there, a warm, bright glow. The orange light from the flame cast a soft glow on Lu Gu’s face; his silhouette looked tender and innocent, his expressions light and curved with happiness, so that anyone who looked at him would be carried away by his happiness.

As Shen Xuanqing thought about it, there was really something about Lu Gu—white jade skin, smooth and flawless. He remembered the day of their wedding when he lifted Lu Gu’s red veil and saw Shuang’er with the red mark between his eyebrows and tears streaming down his cheeks. He knew at a glance that Lu Gu was beautiful.

Seeing Lu Gu’s smooth skin, Shen Xuanqing imagined other things. The white jade skin must be well cared for. The windswept and battered countryside life certainly took a harsher toll than life in town. He couldn’t pamper his fulang as he wished, but he could afford ointment to protect his hands, feet, and face. When he went to town, he would buy it along with a facial cream.
