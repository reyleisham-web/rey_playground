Chapter 58 Part 9


Shen Xuanqing gazed up at the sky while sipping water. A light rain drifted down, but the clouds were lighter than yesterday, and they should clear by tonight at the latest. If the mountain road turned difficult for hunting tomorrow, he would head to the river to catch fish and loaches first.

This year he had paid off his debts and earned a lot of money. He could afford meat to make bacon and sausages. He no longer needed to sell dried fish and loach.

After drinking the warm water from the bowl, his thoughts returned to the present. He glanced at Lu Gu beside him and saw that the little Fulang’s cheeks were brushed pink, his eyelashes fluttering slightly as if nervous.

Realizing that Lu Gu was quietly shifting his legs away, reluctant to lie next to him, Shen Xuanqing suddenly felt lost and confused. He couldn’t figure out why Lu Gu didn’t want to be beside him, and the discontent in his heart rose. He pursed his lips and frowned, utterly puzzled.

If Lu Gu knew what he was thinking, he would certainly never plot to keep his distance from him. But Lu Gu didn’t know. Their legs had shifted a little farther apart.

A cold wind blew in from outside. Lu Gu raised a hand to touch his own cheek, and the heat faded a little with the wind.

Shen Xuanqing treated him well. It wasn’t that he didn’t want to be near Shen Xuanqing. If this had happened yesterday, it would have been fine, but yesterday Shen Xuanqing had actually forced him into such a posture, which shocked him. He didn’t dare resist or refuse. The pain wasn’t unbearable, but the embarrassment of the situation made him a little afraid to face Shen Xuanqing.

One of them was timid, the other sulking, and they remained silent.

Suddenly, the puppy lying on Lu Gu’s shoe toppled to the ground and rolled away, yelping in fright. Shen Xuanqing picked Lu Gu up from the chair. Fortunately, the water in Lu Gu’s bowl had been finished; otherwise it would have splashed everywhere.

The door of the room was slammed shut with a bang, and Lu Gu’s heart gave a jolt. Before he could react, he found himself laid on the bed.

Shen Xuanqing set the bowl on the table beside him, then, discouraged and stubborn, said nothing and simply pressed his body down onto Lu Gu’s.

Lu Gu didn’t understand what was happening. He thought Shen Xuanqing was going to do the same thing as before, and nervousness surged. But Shen Xuanqing pressed down and did not move for a long time, as if he didn’t intend to continue.

After a while Shen Xuanqing finally made a move. The young man, with a good face, lost his temper with Fulang for no reason and kissed the side of his neck several times.

Shen Xuanqing sat up and stopped pressing Lu Gu.

Although they were married, they still hadn’t learned how to say “I love you.” He rubbed his head, unable to think of any affectionate words, and finally said dryly, “You, why don’t you rest a while?”

Lu Gu, being simple at heart, saw him stand and leave, then gently closed the door. After thinking it over, he believed the truth as he understood it: Shen Xuanqing wanted him to rest before he held him again.

Shen Xuanqing was a good man, he reminded himself of that fact once more. After thinking it over, a faint smile crept onto his face, one Lu Gu didn’t notice.

The river’s water had risen a little. After Shen Xuanqing cast his net, he saw Lu Gu squatting by the river, digging wild vegetables. He said something to him, then went to the northern forest to pick hickory leaves.

The rain had cooled the air; the river’s water felt even colder after rain, and the chill seeped into the bones. Lu Gu’s body was slender, and he couldn’t bear it. So he planned to catch more drunken fish today, and wouldn’t need to go into the water; his legs would be numb with cold otherwise. If he wanted to eat fresh fish, they could haul in the net later.

Lu Gu saw that Shen Xuanqing had gone empty-handed, so after digging up the wild vegetables he followed with a bamboo basket. It was convenient to use a bamboo basket to hold the leaves.

The puppy had never seen the sight of drunken fish. The fishing net was hauled in and yielded a bountiful catch. The fish that were still alive flopped and slapped their tails on the bank. There were fresh, fat river prawns in the net. The puppy ran about, barking with excitement.

After the net was pulled up, there were still many fish in the river, so Shen Xuanqing ran back to the yard and brought a large, deep bamboo basket, and scooped up the fish from the river with the basket.

Lu Gu paid little attention to the small wild vegetables in the bamboo basket or to the ones in the fishing net. He put all the fish that Shen Xuanqing threw onto the bank into the bamboo basket, and he didn’t keep any fish that were too small; he threw them downstream with all his might, otherwise they would still be alive and would be picked up by Shen Xuanqing’s basket.

The rain had stopped last night, and today was still a bit overcast. The sun came out briefly, and the river remained icy-cold. They dragged the nets and carried bamboo baskets on their backs, running back and forth two or three times to bring the fish to the yard. Lu Gu boiled hot water; otherwise, with so many fish, their hands would freeze when they killed them. So they soaked their hands in warm water to thaw them out in the yard.

 Fewer people fished in the deep mountains, and there were more big fish than in the village. Hundreds of fish filled the barrels and tubs, and there were many river shrimps as well.

As soon as Lu Gu came back, he went to the kitchen to boil more water. Even if he had seen this kind of harvest once, it still filled people with joy. Almost every fish was longer than his hand. How could anyone be unhappy with so much fish? There were so many fish spread out to dry in the sun that there would be enough to last six of them through the winter.

Shen Xuanqing went into the kitchen to fetch a knife. Lu Gu’s face could hardly hide his joy; he sat on the small stool in front of the stove and looked up at Shen Xuanqing, his eyes bright, like a galaxy at night.

Shen Xuanqing had studied, but at this moment he was still speechless, and could only think of the most common stars.
