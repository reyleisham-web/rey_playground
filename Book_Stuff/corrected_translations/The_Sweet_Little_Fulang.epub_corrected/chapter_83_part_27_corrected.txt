Chapter 83 Part 27


Three days was not long, and soon it would be time to lift the coffin and bury the dead in the soil. The snow had melted, dripping from the eaves like rain. The snow in the yard and on the road outside was also melting, and the ground was muddy.

There was much crying and shouting outside; it was a mess. Lu Gu sat in the room with Ji Qiuyue and did not go out, the door kept shut. They paid particular attention to eating lunch before lifting the coffin, for those who had eaten would have strength. So today Shen Xuanqing and Shen Yaoqing ate at Zhang’s house. The meal was a vegetarian feast; winter offered few dishes, and the most expensive thing on the table was tofu. The others were nothing but cabbage, radish, and a wild vegetable—four bowls in total.

"Sister-in-law, it’s done; now you can use it." Lu Gu handed over the embroidered tiger-pattern purse. A lion and a tiger could ward off evil spirits. Wei Lanxiang felt that a red belt was not enough, so she asked him to sew a purse with a tiger pattern on it. She had gone to the temple yesterday to ask for an amulet, and she hung it around Ji Qiuyue’s neck as soon as she returned.

"It’s so beautiful." Ji Qiuyue took it. Her own purse had grown old; it had been made of rags and bore no embroidery. Now that she had a new purse, her face was filled with joy.

They were talking in the room, and after a while, they heard Shen Xuanqing and Shen Yaoqing coming back. Unlike the death of an elder, there was no need to wail, blow horns, or beat drums at the grave today—only burial was required.

[哭丧 kū sāng: formal wailing while offering sacrifice to the departed]

Wei Lanxiang had prepared the brazier early in the morning, and when she saw the two of them returning, she hurriedly brought the brazier to the gate of the courtyard, so that they could only enter after stepping over it. Their family had a pregnant woman, which was different from other families. People in the village knew what was going on when they passed by, and they did not know how they would talk behind their backs.

After the two of them finished stepping over the brazier, Wei Lanxiang asked her two sons to wash their hands thoroughly and gave each of them a new red belt to change into. Only after changing their belts could they see Ji Qiuyue.

In the past few days, Shen Yaoqing hadn’t slept with Ji Qiuyue at night because he had been digging a new grave, so he had set up a wooden bed for himself in the main room for a few nights.

Everyone in the village had returned, and Zhang’s relatives were no exception. After tidying his two sons, Wei Lanxiang went to Quan Zi’s house. Not long after, there was a noise from the next door, and when she heard the sound, her heart rose and fell. Sure enough, these people had no good intentions.

Fortunately, Lin Zhongcai was at Zhang’s house; with him, even people from other villages had to show him some respect and did not dare to loot Zhang’s house at will. But even if he was here, Zhang Zhengzi had no heirs, and there was nothing he could do if the Zhang family’s relatives came to divide the family property—this was the way of the world.

In the past few days, Lao Zhang had cried so much that she had fainted several times. Her only son was gone, and her heart seemed to be torn out. In addition, her right leg was broken, and she had grown much older in just a few days.

At this moment, Li Wanyun was surrounded by many people who said they wanted to discuss how to divide the property and the land. She, a weak woman, had never faced such a battle before.

There was a lot of noise in the yard, and everything escalated. The Li family scolded Zhang’s relatives for not being humane, and they bullied the widow. Lao Zhang’s natal family also came.

As soon as they started talking, the Zhang family’s relatives brought up the issue of there being no heir, and the two sides argued. Some people said that if the family property was not divided, Li Wanyun would have to adopt one of Zhang Zhengzi’s nephews, so as not to let Zhang family property fall into the hands of outsiders like the Li family; otherwise, it would be said that the Li family had stolen the other family’s property, and their family would no longer be considered humane.

When it was time to bury Zhang Zhengzi, Lao Zhang fainted again and was carried into the room and slept. After the burial, no one thought of her, and she only woke up when she heard the noise. Although her leg was broken and her expression was dull, when she learned that those people cared nothing for her life and wanted to divide her property and fields, anger ignited in her heart. Everyone treated the old woman as a dead person, and she immediately yelled and cursed from the room.

Lin Zhongcai grew tired of this quarrel, and when he heard Lao Zhang shouting, he quickly had her carried out.

Lao Zhang’s son was gone, and relatives who had not seen each other for many years threatened her. She was in great pain and cried; she did not forgive anyone.
