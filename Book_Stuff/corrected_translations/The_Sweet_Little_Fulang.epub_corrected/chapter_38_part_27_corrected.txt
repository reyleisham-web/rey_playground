Chapter 38 Part 27


It was late in the day, and they had only brought out a single basket. After filling more than half of it, they turned back; as long as they had enough for their own use, they would harvest again later to sell.

At dawn the next day, they returned to the hazelnut grove. This time they loaded the cart, and on the way back they had a good haul. In addition to the hazelnuts, they carried a basket of pine nuts and lots of mushrooms.

This harvest wasn’t every year; the trees were tall, and pine cones were often knotted at the treetops. It was dangerous to climb up, the treetops were high, and the mountain winds could be treacherous—a life could be lost in an instant.

Shen Xuanqing did not usually pick pine cones. In previous years, most had been gathered beneath the trees. The winds in the mountains could whip ripe cones from the branches, but as with chestnuts and hazelnuts, squirrels and other small animals could grab many too, so there weren’t many left.

There were quite a lot of mushrooms, and they couldn’t finish them all. They would sell what they could on the way down from the mountain.

With Lu Gu and the others pushing from behind the cart, it was easy for Shen Xuanqing to pull the cart. Normally, he and Lu Gu went up and down the mountain together, without Ji Qiuyue and Shen Yan. The large number of people this time made it easier to haul so much back home.

Wei Lanxiang and the others spent four days on the mountain, rushing to gather everything they could. On the fourth day, they packed the cart early in the morning and prepared to descend. They left some chestnuts and hazelnuts for Lu Gu and Shen Xuanqing, so they wouldn’t have to rush back for them—the harvest was abundant.

Before setting off, Shen Xuanqing fed the three dogs meat, patted Dahui’s neck, and sent them to head down the mountain first.

Ji Qiuyue also came over with further instructions, telling them to go to Shen Yaoqing. Dahui, clever as can be, ran off first with two small dogs, while Ji Qiuyue muttered in the yard, saying Dahui seemed to really understand him.

Shen Xuanqing normally didn’t rely on Shen Yaoqing to fetch him; after all, there was fieldwork, and sometimes Shen Yaoqing had to go to town to take on a part-time job to earn money. But this time, with his mother and Ji Qiuyue along, Shen Yaoqing remembered his father, who had been gravely ill when he was young, saying that the things he wanted were too heavy to carry, and he would help carry them back halfway. The dogs knew the mountain road and the house, and as long as the dogs ran back, he would know what it meant.

During the day, when the sun came out, there were few wild animals along the descent, so it didn’t matter if the dogs weren’t with them. Besides, there were so many of them that they didn’t need to fear the road.

Halfway down the mountain, they finally saw Shen Yaoqing, with three dogs following him. These three dogs were hunting dogs—fast runners who were used to going with Shen Xuanqing in the mountains. When they saw people heading up the mountain, they would tail them again, but judging from how they stuck out their tongues, Shen Xuanqing knew that they would lie on their stomachs when they turned back.

“So many,” Shen Yaoqing remarked as he came over to see the cart, delighted.

Since they paused here, they decided to rest a while. This spot happened to be not far from the river, and the three dogs all rushed to drink. There was still water in the bamboo tube on the cart, so they shared it.

“I met Da Chen yesterday,” Shen Yaoqing said, taking a bite of two sweet buns Shen Yan had brought and sitting down with him. “He told me the Niujia Village dog pup has come into heat and can be brought back in the next few days.” Shen Yaoqing ate two sweet buns and sat down to eat with him.

Shen Xuanqing replied, “I’ll be there tomorrow.”

Before that, he had asked someone to inquire about a suitable wolf pup in Niujia Village; the price had already been agreed, and he calculated that it would take two months. He had seen the bitch—pretty and smart—and the family knew how to raise dogs. After the puppies were weaned, the bitch would take care of them for a few days and teach them, making it more humane and easier to raise.

“Do you still want a dog?” Wei Lanxiang asked, sitting beside him.

When Shen Xuanqing first started raising dogs, Wei Lanxiang had seen her son feeding the dogs meat and bones, which distressed her. People in the countryside usually fed dogs with leftovers or boiled wheat bran, or they went out to scrounge for food themselves, and it was rare to feed them so well; even if it was wild rabbit meat, it would still sting a mother’s heart.

Shen Xuanqing explained that keeping a dog was for hunting—if you didn’t feed them well, how could they run? Moreover, the mountains and groves were dangerous, and a dog was full of energy, giving him a sense of security. After discussing it once or twice, Wei Lanxiang relented, but when she heard that he still wanted to raise another pup, she couldn’t help but ask one more question.

“Now that the two of us are on the mountain, I take the dog out, and I worry about Lu Gu walking outside alone.” Shen Xuanqing explained that he had already decided to adopt the pup, and no matter what the rest of the family said, he wouldn’t change his mind.

Wei Lanxiang, reading his temperament in his expression, knew that this pup would have to be brought back tomorrow. But she was also worried about Lu Gu being alone on the mountain, so she lifted her head, nodded, and said, “Yes, that’s right.”

Lu Gu didn’t realize they were talking about puppies at first, and he was stunned for a moment when he heard his name. Then he realized he had never raised puppies, so he didn’t feel much about it yet. Dahui and the others returned from drinking water, wondering what kind of puppies they were.

There were many people here, and Lu Gu was timid, so he didn’t ask Shen Xuanqing anything. After a rest, Shen Yaoqing put a rope over his shoulders; it was his turn to pull the cart, while Shen Xuanqing and the other five pushed from behind.

“Brother Daqing, it’s different now—much lighter than before,” Shen Yan said as he pushed the cart, and Lu Gu nodded; indeed it was easier to push.

Men who had spent the year farming had the strength to handle it. Shen Yaoqing also went to town to carry large sacks for work from time to time, so he naturally had the strength to pull a cart.
