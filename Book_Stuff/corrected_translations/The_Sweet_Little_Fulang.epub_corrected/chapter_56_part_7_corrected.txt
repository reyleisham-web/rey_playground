Chapter 56 Part 7


Two days ago they had eaten loach at home, and Shen Xuanqing didn’t know whether Lu Gu would still want to eat it. Loaches were easy to keep, but they also required pots or wooden barrels. How could so many pots always be taken up to raise this thing? Sometimes in winter you could dig loaches from the fields and rivers, but if it was too cold and frostbite wouldn’t be worth it, so it was better to eat the dried ones.

Hearing what he said, Lu Gu hurriedly agreed. Previously, they only cared about drying fish and wild vegetables. Here they only paid attention to eating fresh loach, which was very nourishing. They never thought that loach could also be dried in the sun. Earthworms were wrapped in large leaves, and the fish basket held many loaches. Shen Xuanqing washed the mud from his legs in the river before coming ashore.

Anyway, he planned to soak his legs again, so he didn’t wipe them dry, just put on his shoes, filled a bucket of water at a clear spot, and walked back with Lu Gu carrying the fish basket. After returning home, Lu Gu went to the backyard to chop the earthworms first, and Shen Xuanqing ran back and forth to fill the water tank, then poured a bucket of water into the pot so that he could boil the water and soak his legs after killing the fish.

He dumped the loaches from the fish basket into a wooden basin. They would soak for two days to spit out the sand, then they would be stewed, and it wouldn’t be too late to cut the rest and dry them in the sun. There was no need for Lu Gu to kill the fish today; he only needed to throw the guts he took out for the chickens and ducks. Seeing that he didn’t need help, Lu Gu went to the kitchen to boil water, and when Shen Xuanqing finished killing the fish, the water had also boiled.

He also put the sun-dried mugwort leaves into the tub and soaked them in the boiling water for a while; the water grew darker. Soaking his legs in the hot mugwort water could dispel the cold and dredge the meridians. How could Shen Xuanqing be so meticulous when he was alone on the mountain? His handsome face was full of smiles as his legs were soaking in the mugwort bath.

There was still some time before lunch. Lu Gu went to the kitchen to wash the new rice and planned to steam it. It would take a little longer to steam the rice. After lighting the fire under the pot, he had nothing to do, so he gathered a pile of sun-dried rushes from the woodshed to strip their grass cores. The grass cores found could not only be used as wicks for oil lamps, but also for making soup and tea, and the grass cores could also be sun-dried. When dry, they could be sold to the medical clinic. They were used as pillow stuffing too, and were comfortable to sleep on.

[dēng xīn cǎo: rush (botany); Juncaceae]

It was always a good idea to bring back more plants before they dried, yellowed, and wilted, whether they were sold or eaten at home.

Shen Xuanqing changed his clothes and sat with his legs in the main room tub. Lu Gu sat next to him and stripped the grass cores. He reached out to take a straw of the rush and pull out the core grass when he had nothing to do. Although the two talked little, they were very harmonious.

Lu Gu lowered his head slightly; he was always focused and serious when he worked. Besides, when his hands were busy, it could distract him from thinking about other things. It would be a pity and distress if the grass cores broke; his mind was occupied, and even Shen Xuanqing didn’t notice him.

Shen Xuanqing’s Adam’s apple moved slightly, and he looked at Lu Gu’s side profile in a daze. His skin was fair and smooth, his eyes long and narrow, blinking lightly from time to time, trembling timidly, as if passing through his heart, a ripple of emotion rising. He suddenly wanted to stretch out his hand to cover Lu Gu’s eyes; if Lu Gu blinked, his eyelashes would brush gently on his palm. The strange thought caused his Adam’s apple to bob again, until Lu Gu turned his head and looked at the half-dried rush in his hand.

“Let me do it,” Lu Gu said. He hadn’t noticed Shen Xuanqing placing the pulled rush cores at his feet for a long time, turned his head to look, and thought Shen Xuanqing was tired and didn’t want to do it, so he asked him carefully.

Don’t look at such a pile of rushes; there aren’t that many rush cores pulled out, and they will be lighter after drying, so Lu Gu cherished them.

Shen Xuanqing recovered from his stupor and handed the rush to Lu Gu, who, expecting his ger to notice his absent-minded state, felt a little embarrassed and avoided meeting Lu Gu’s eyes.

At noon, as Shen Xuanqing had suggested, Lu Gu fried rabbit meat with spicy peppers; it smelled spicy, and he planned to stir-fry qiucai, so he had all the meat and vegetables ready.

Shen Xuanqing was still young; although at eighteen he could handle the family’s money-making work, he was still like a child in other things, for example, he wanted to rely on Lu Gu when he had nothing to do right now. Usually he hunted outside and only slept together at night. After going down the mountain, there were so many people at home that he was too embarrassed to sit next to Lu Gu. It was embarrassing to follow him in and out openly; how could he be so dependent on his ger on the man’s side?

Scratching his heart and lungs, Shen Xuanqing—who always wanted to follow Lu Gu—took a look at the puppy. The puppy wagged its tail happily, sticking to Lu Gu’s legs. He wouldn’t be angry if he stumbled; instead, he lowered his head and offered a very shallow smile, calling Guaizi’s name in a low voice and letting him play by himself first. The puppy was still young and couldn’t understand human words, so he continued to “tangle” with Lu Gu.

How could a person compare with a puppy? Shen Xuanqing looked away, and when Lu Gu entered the kitchen to cook, he followed him in, seeking a reason to light the fire in a serious manner, which was truly helpful. Lu Gu didn’t know the thoughts he hid; he was busy cooking, but occasionally turned his head to meet Shen Xuanqing’s eyes, feeling a little embarrassed. Lu Gu quickly looked away, not knowing that Shen Xuanqing’s eyes were also a little embarrassed.

The tall, sturdy man sat on the small bench in front of the stove to light the fire, and even he couldn’t explain the mix of anxiety and joy in his heart.
