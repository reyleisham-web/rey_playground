Chapter 86

The village at the foot of the mountain lay quiet. The morning sky was gray, as it often is in cold winters. Time passed quickly, and soon the twelfth lunar month would arrive. Smoke rose from the kitchen; even in winter there was little work, yet the Shen family’s second household still ate three meals a day. If they didn’t fill their stomachs and grow used to eating three meals a day, they would become agitated.

Breakfast was simple. Lu Gu cut a few salted duck eggs today, and when he saw a heap of winter bamboo shoots in the bamboo basket, he said to Shen Xuanqing, “Why don’t you kill an old hen and stew it with the bamboo shoots? The old hen is past laying, and we haven’t made soup for sister-in-law in the past few days.”

“Okay, I’ll do it.” Shen Yaoqing heard it from the side and didn’t let Shen Xuanqing go to the backhouse to catch the chicken; he went by himself. Taking advantage of the fire at the bottom of the stove, Lu Gu added a few more pieces of firewood to scorch the feathers later. He brought the bamboo basket over, sat in front of the stove, and peeled the shells and trimmed the roots of the winter bamboo shoots—the silver bracelet on his wrist glittering.

Shen Xuanqing crouched down to help him cut the roots of the winter bamboo shoots when there was nothing to do. When he saw the bracelet on Lu Gu’s wrist, he felt the purchase had been the right choice. Compared with the hairpin, the bracelet was better.

“Didn't I go to Fucheng the day before yesterday? I passed Fenggu Town on the way back.” Shen Xuanqing’s voice was low, as if there were more to tell Lu Gu.

Lu Gu didn’t answer; he looked up, waiting for him to continue.

“Lu Wen entered the Li family’s gate in town. The Li family’s young master is called Li Mingshan, and he has become Lu Wen’s master.” Shen Xuanqing leaned on a piece of firewood and sat down; he couldn’t squat for long. Then he said, “I’ve heard people say that Li Mingshan’s mother married him as the main wife just three days after he took a concubine.”

After he received the official seal and returned, he first went to the town to find Luo Biao and get a general understanding of the situation. Because of Lu Wen’s matter, Li Mingshan and his mother had quarrelled. His mother, Mrs. Zhang, doted on her son, but she was powerful. Li Mingshan bought a house for Lu Wen to live in and ordered the Li family’s maids and servants to serve Lu Wen. Li Mingshan was rather foolish; he didn’t bother to hire maids and servants, so he pulled two good people from his own household. He felt the maids at home had learned their rules, so there was no need to worry about how to serve others.

Lu Wen thought it was inappropriate at first, but after he took the maid out once, someone recognized that the maid came from the Li family, a family that often bought things on the street. That person thought Lu Wen was a Li family relative, and the flattery that followed gave him face. Besides, Li Mingshan was the Li family’s only son, and everything in the Li family would belong to him in the future. Since Li Mingshan had said he didn’t need to be begged or asked to care, Lu Wen didn’t refuse.

And the money in Li Mingshan’s hands came from his family. Mrs. Zhang didn’t treat her son badly; she never asked how much money he spent, but if she really counted it, Li Mingshan’s plans wouldn’t be hidden from her, and she would soon discover the house.

The maid who had been beaten with the board and slapped in the mouth was sent into Lu Wen’s house. Only then did Mrs. Zhang learn about the affair between him and Li Mingshan. Mrs. Zhang got down from the sedan chair and was helped by the great maid to the gate of the Li family’s house. Mrs. Zhang’s sneer and other people’s gazes made Lu Wen nearly faint. Li Mingshan rushed over after receiving the news. After a confrontation, Mrs. Zhang was so angry that Lu Wen turned pale with embarrassment. The unmarried adulterous arrangement had been revealed, and he was ashamed, but he also knew that only Li Mingshan could save him, otherwise he wouldn’t be able to live.

Fortunately, Li Mingshan had a bit of conscience, so he declared on the spot that he intended to marry him. After the quarrel, Mrs. Zhang was not cruel in front of her son, and reluctantly let go, but Lu Wen could only be a concubine; otherwise, she would go to Anjia Village and let the villagers take a good look at what kind of shuang’er they had raised.

No one else knew what Du Hehua and Lu Daxiang were thinking. Luo Biao watched the town’s spectacle for a few days. Lu Wen was carried into Li’s house in a small sedan chair. There were no “three books and six ceremonies,” let alone an official wedding. After the adulterous affair was revealed, he couldn’t lift his head before Mrs. Zhang for the rest of his life, so even if the Li family didn’t host a banquet, he wouldn’t dare complain.

Mrs. Zhang had originally thought her son would inherit the family business, but she never expected him to be so credulous, easily manipulated by a shuang’er from the countryside. After investigation, she even learned about Li Mingshan’s visits to brothels. She was so angry that she didn’t eat well for several days; in the end she forced Li Mingshan to marry a main wife. In her view, even if Lu Wen was not pleasing to the eye, it was better to marry a wife and take a concubine than to go to a brothel; otherwise the money would be drained and those dirty things would pollute her son.

Li Mingshan’s main wife was Luo Hongsi, the daughter of Ranfang Cloth Shop. She wasn’t bad-looking, but compared with Lu Wen, she was clearly not as good, and Lu Wen, as a concubine, had entered Li’s family before her. “Marry a wife and then take a concubine,” she thought, but when it came to her turn, it was the other way around; how could she feel at ease.
