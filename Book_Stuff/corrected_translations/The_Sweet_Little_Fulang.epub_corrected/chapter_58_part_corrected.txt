Chapter 58


The puppy outside barked and whined until it was beaten into silence. Lu Gu was terrified, yet some inner strength suddenly surged up, and he shoved Shen Xuanqing away. Realizing what he had done, he drew back his hand and froze.

Even Shen Xuanqing was startled for a moment. The puppy’s barking faded to a whimper and then quieted, perhaps retreating to a corner. Dogs play rough and can forget the limits; the puppy was still young, but with Dahui around, there was little need to worry about it being bitten.

The warmth of the room dissipated, as if the temperature had dropped along with the autumn rain. The room fell quiet. The cold rain continued to fall outside, running along the eaves. The sky was dark, with distant thunder rolling and strong winds howling, like the moaning of an invisible giant.

Shen Xuanqing got out of bed first to see what was happening with the puppy outside. He was nudged aside by his fulang on the bed, which made him a little embarrassed.

The puppy had grown quite a bit, though still fat. The main room was dark, and there was no need to light a lamp since they wouldn’t be doing anything soon. Shen Xuanqing found him in a corner with the help of the dim light, and he opened the door to admit a little more light. It was windy outside; the door only opened a crack, and the rain drifted in. When Shen Xuanqing closed the door, his hands and face were covered with cold rain.

He picked up the towel from the rack and wiped his hands and face. Seeing the puppy leaning toward his legs, he couldn’t help but gently kick the puppy’s fat backside.

Lu Gu liked the puppy very much. He usually came out to see what was wrong with it. Shen Xuanqing couldn’t bear to beat it, and besides, such a big puppy wasn’t easy to scold into obedience. This kind of strength wasn’t painful for the puppy, and he still wanted to come into the room with Shen Xuanqing, but the door was locked. The door was closed tightly this time, even the latch fastened.

The puppy scratched the door a few times, but it wouldn’t budge, so it wandered off and left. The rain grew heavier and colder, and Lu Gu knew he should find a warm place, so he lay down next to Dahui on the hay-filled sack.

Shen Xuanqing closed the door, turned around, and walked back toward the bed, pretending to cough: “No injuries. Maybe he was pushed to the ground just now when they were playing.”

In truth, he had gone out to check the puppy just by chance; closing the door had been his intention. Otherwise, what if the puppy had come in and run around?

“En.” Lu Gu answered, still flustered, carefully watching Shen Xuanqing’s face. But the room was so dark that he couldn’t tell whether Shen Xuanqing was angry or not.

Shen Xuanqing lay back in bed, and it became even harder for Lu Gu to study his face. The tall man felt warmer than before, and with more people in the bed, the warmth grew.

The wind blew, the rain pattered, and lying there doing nothing, the sound of rain outside gradually slowed the mind, letting it drift for a while, then snap back as Lu Gu tried to plot a way to keep Shen Xuanqing from angering him again.

Lu Gu’s hand under the quilt moved slightly, his heart trembled, and he felt uneasy. Yet he still wanted to reach out and touch Shen Xuanqing’s hand, to tell him that he wouldn’t push him away again, though the words stuck in his throat and he trembled, not knowing how to speak.

Suddenly, Shen Xuanqing’s hand touched his, and that touch gave Lu Gu the courage to respond. He grasped Shen Xuanqing’s hand, which was warm and strong.

What Lu Gu wanted to say was blocked. Shen Xuanqing, unable to bear it any longer, rolled over and pressed him down. The room filled with heavy, lingering pants, and the two sides’ breathing grew ragged.

The heavy rain continued for half the day and into the night, only easing the next morning. A window opened a crack for ventilation, and the wind subsided, so not much rain blew in.

After breakfast, Lu Gu still felt a little tired. He pushed a chair into the main room and sat, watching the rain outside in a daze. He had slept well last night, but what had happened before sleep was beyond his expectations—completely different from anything in the past and almost shocking. He couldn’t believe it even now.

Shen Xuanqing was busy feeding the chickens and ducks. He also cleaned the woodshed, lit the green medicinal leaves, and piled them in the corner to burn them, since there was no wood. The smell of burning herbs carried away other scents. After tidying up, he stepped out of the woodshed and closed the door to keep the poultry from wandering in.

He crouched at the kitchen door to wash his hands, rubbing the wild soapberries between his hands until white foam appeared. He turned his head and saw Lu Gu still in a daze in the main room. He couldn’t help but laugh, remembering what had happened yesterday, and he felt a touch of embarrassment, though his eyes still shone with brightness. The picture-book memory seemed to work; Shen Xuanqing washed off the foam on his hands, lowered his eyes, and kept his thoughts to himself.

After washing his hands, he went to the kitchen. There were two bowls of warm water on the chopping board. He added honey to one bowl and stirred it with chopsticks before offering it to Lu Gu to drink.

The sweet honey water brought Lu Gu back to his senses. Shen Xuanqing sat next to him, their legs very close together. Shen Xuanqing was tall, and when he sat, his calves were longer than Lu Gu’s. For some reason, the touch of their legs together reminded Lu Gu of what happened yesterday, and his ears and cheeks warmed, causing him to lower his gaze and hold the bowl nervously and shyly.
