Chapter 111 Part 28


Lu Gu ignored the two of them and headed into the kitchen to light the stove. He scooped up two handfuls of the tender buds, washed them clean, and once the pot came to a boil, he dumped the boiling water over the buds to blanch them. This would be enough for him to eat alone.

There was half a bowl of chicken meat left over from yesterday. The fresh buds tasted lighter when stir-fried, so he fried them together with the chicken. It would be good to finish the leftover meat so Shen Xuanqing would have fresh food when he came back in the evening.

After blanching, the buds lost their bitterness and grew tender and crisp. Lu Gu ate them all by himself.

Stir-fried young buds with chicken and rice was enough for him alone; after eating, his stomach felt warm and content. For people who worked hard every day to make a living, a good meal was the happiest thing in the world.

After cleaning up the kitchen, Lu Gu rested in his room for a while. The puppy and Dabai were playing outside. With them around, there was no need to worry about wild animals slinking into the yard.

After a short nap, he carried a wooden bucket to the river to fetch water. He carried only half a bucket back, but hadn’t even stepped into the yard yet when he saw the Sichuan pepper tree outside the house. He realized he hadn’t watered it since he arrived a few days ago, and he would have to rely on the tree to produce peppers in the future. Those peppers would be enough for their family to eat for a year, so there would be no need to exchange or buy more. So he set the wooden bucket down, took out a hoe, loosened the soil, and poured half a bucket of water onto the tree roots.

Weeds could sprout easily. There were weeds sprouting sparsely in the area where he and Shen Xuanqing had weeded before. Snakes and insects would most likely hide in the grass. The two of them had weeded around the yard before. However, because he had already run into a snake today, Lu Gu didn’t tuck the hoe away after watering the pepper tree.

First he ran back five times to fill the water tank, then he picked up the hoe leaning against the mud wall to dig up the weeds and hoe the open space around the courtyard. If the ground was flat and free of weeds, there would be nowhere for snakes, insects, rats, and ants to hide. Thinking of this, Lu Gu grew more enthusiastic about weeding and busied himself, single-handedly.

The puppy was darting about nearby. The trees around the yard had been cut down, leaving a wide open space for the sun to shine through. Now there were only a few tree stumps left on the ground. The puppy sniffed and peed on the stumps to mark its territory and warn other living things away.

Life in the mountains was never idle. After Lu Gu finished preparing dinner, he kept it warming in the pot for a while, waiting for Shen Xuanqing to come back.

“ Sheep.”

When he and the puppy ran outside, they saw Shen Xuanqing dragging a black-hoofed sheep back. He was instantly delighted. The sheep was alive, so they could keep it in the yard and there would be no need to go down the mountain so quickly.

The black-hoofed sheep had an injured front leg, the left one, just like Dabai’s. However, the injury was less serious and it could limp along.

The puppy had always been smart and clever, and sometimes quite naughty. When he saw the black-hoofed sheep walking with a limp, he tilted his head as if thinking, then raised his left front leg and limped along, causing Shen Xuanqing to chuckle and kick him in the rear.

Lu Gu smiled with curved eyes. After the puppy was kicked, it lowered its left front leg, shook its fur, ran over, and rubbed Lu Gu’s leg.

“It’s a ram; we didn’t catch a female. Many females have cubs now. They hide well and are smart,” Shen Xuanqing told Lu Gu of his day’s hunting outside.

Before he married, he lived alone in the mountains. Now there was someone waiting for him to come home every day. Facing his fulang, even though he was a man of few words, he still wanted to tell Lu Gu about what he saw and what happened while he hunted.

Spring hunting was fairly restricted; most likely the cubs wouldn’t survive without their mothers, so hunters like him wouldn’t hunt female animals with cubs or pregnant animals during this period. For animals like sheep and deer, they would only hunt the males.

As for rabbits and pheasants, it wasn’t a big deal, because there were plenty of rabbits and pheasants on the mountain, so it wouldn’t be a big deal even if they killed female rabbits or pheasants.

“If we can catch a ewe, we won’t sell this ram. We’ll raise them at home, and they can give birth to lambs in the future.” Shen Xuanqing spoke with a smile.

Since the idea of raising rabbits had taken root, he had been wondering whether he could raise more animals as well. He earned money from hunting in the mountains, and if he could catch these animals alive and raise them at home, letting them have cubs, he could earn another source of income by raising more.
