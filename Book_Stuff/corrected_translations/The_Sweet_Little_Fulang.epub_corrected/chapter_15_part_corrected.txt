Chapter 15


Not to mention the 600 copper coins, Lu Gu, while under Du Hehua’s control, had to endure beatings that cost six copper coins each time.

Once he fell ill with a cold, and it didn’t improve after several days. Du Hehua discovered it and beat and scolded him again after he recovered from his illness. Du Hehua was ruthless: he didn’t strike him in the face, but pinched his thighs and arms, and would point the stick at his back and stomach. Lu Gu didn’t dare to speak up, and he didn’t dare to show his injuries to anyone, for otherwise he would be even more ruined; if someone saw his thighs and arms, his innocence would be gone.

Now that he had spent more than 600 copper coins, Lu Gu trembled as he looked up at Shen Xuanqing’s face. Shen Xuanqing didn’t have that many copper coins, so he pulled a few pieces of silver from his bosom. Seeing this, Wang Langzhong brought out the family’s scales and declared that it was enough for 600 copper coins, and so he paid in full. Lu Gu accepted the medicine handed to him by Grandma Wang; seeing that Shen Xuanqing’s expression didn’t change after the payment, his anxious eyes grew a little steadier.

They hadn’t spoken in depth, and Lu Gu hadn’t heard a deep, steady voice until they left Anjia Village and walked along the trail toward Qingxi Village. “As long as the injury is cured,” Shen Xuanqing thought, and then spoke softly: “The money was spent and then earned back. If it drags on until it can’t heal, it will cost a lot more.”

Although there wasn’t really any relationship between them yet, there was a possibility they might end up living together in the end. Yes, Shen Xuanqing didn’t want him to be so frightened and cowering; he didn’t want him to be afraid to breathe, and if he didn’t even dare to utter a word, then when they went into the mountains, it would be the same as him being in the mountains alone.

Lu Gu was stunned for a moment, thinking that even if Shen Xuanqing didn’t hit him, he would still blame him, but what he heard were these kind words. “I can work,” he muttered, wanting to show his loyalty, but his expression was too weak and timid to convey any real confidence.

Shen Xuanqing saw the effort in him and replied in a low voice: “Yes, I know.”

After receiving that response, Lu Gu resolved to work hard. It cost 80 copper coins for cloth, and the medicine today was 600 copper coins. His mother had taught him to count money while she was alive, but he hadn’t touched anything money-related for a long time, so he thought it through carefully and realized it came to 680 copper coins in total, not including meals at the Shen household.

680 copper coins was an amount he’d never seen before when his mother was alive; the more he thought about it, the more surprised and flustered he felt at such a large sum.

Leaving Anjia Village, they found another village two miles ahead. To their left rose a gentle slope; after climbing it and then descending, they would reach the cemetery of Anjia Village, where Lu Gu’s mother’s grave was located. His pace slowed, and he glanced toward the cemetery. Today was the fifteenth, and he would have to help with the work when he returned, so he might not be able to come out like this again.

Having spent so much money and detoured to the cemetery, Lu Gu worried about being told that it was troublesome, so his confidence sagged, and he even blew up at Shen Xuanqing a bit.

Shen Xuanqing, still walking beside him, noticed his expression growing weaker, frowned slightly, and asked, “What’s wrong?”

When Lu Gu looked up, he saw the frown on Shen Xuanqing’s face, as if he were displeased, and he couldn’t even lie properly. He stammered the truth: “My mother—my mother’s grave is there.”

Shen Xuanqing stopped and asked, “You want to go there?”

Seeing that there was no sign of anger, Lu Gu carefully said, “I want to go. Let’s go have a look; it won’t take long, and I’ll be back right away.”

Shen Xuanqing didn’t press him; Lu Gu was grateful, though he was still a little slow with words. He glanced at Shen Xuanqing, hesitant to speak further, and then hurried toward the cemetery.

When he had walked down the gentle slope and could no longer see Shen Xuanqing, Shen Xuanqing remembered that he hadn’t brought anything to the cemetery. But he thought to himself: what he said was to go and have a look, not to go to the grave itself. They had only been together for a few days, and they were not even familiar with each other; Shen Xuanqing could not say anything more intimate, so he kept the matter to himself.

In front of the grave, Lu Gu knelt down and placed a handful of small wild fruits on the ground, fruits he had picked from the thorn bushes at the bottom of the slope on his way over. Others burned paper money at graves, but he didn’t have any copper coins except the three Lu Daxiang had given him to buy some during the Chinese New Year. Now, at the Shen household, he was still penniless; he couldn’t even scrape together a copper coin, nor buy paper money, so he offered some wild fruits as sacrifices, as he had before.

“Mother, I’m married.” Lu Gu whispered, fighting back tears, telling her that the person he had married was a good man, someone who could now keep him fed and warmly dressed, someone who even bought him a baiyun cake, which he held in his hand.

Having spoken a few words, he didn’t delay any longer, kowtowing three times before leaving.

Shen Xuanqing waited by the roadside. Lu Gu wiped the dirt from his body as he climbed the slope, and wiped away his tears. Other than a pair of reddened eyes, there was nothing obviously wrong. The ger stood beside him, silent and resolute, ready to face whatever came next.
