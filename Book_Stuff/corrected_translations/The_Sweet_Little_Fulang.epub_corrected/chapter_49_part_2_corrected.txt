Chapter 49 Part 2


After the two of them walked up to the mountain and rested there, they cooked and unloaded the cart, then fed the chickens and ducks, no different from before. A rack was added to the pot, and a few steamed buns were placed on it to heat them up. As soon as Lu Gu lit the fire, Shen Xuanqing came in from outside. He squatted in front of the stove and habitually looked up.

After a brief pause, Shen Xuanqing said, “I’ll cook the pot, and you chop the vegetables.” He looked more natural than Lu Gu, and it wasn’t like he hadn’t helped Lu Gu light the fire before, but after yesterday, something between them had changed. Yes, even Lu Gu noticed it. “Yeah.” Lu Gu nodded, stepped aside, and went to the chopping board to chop the vegetables.

The firewood crackled softly, and he buried his head to chop the vegetables, seemingly concentrating, but actually feeling an inexplicable sensation in his heart—joy or anxiety, he couldn’t tell which. His delicate eyebrows frowned slightly, a hint of depression; he didn’t know what was wrong with him.

Autumn leaves turned red and yellow, and the mountains and forests were dyed with beautiful colors, like clouds in the evening sky. In autumn, various fruits were gradually ripening, and the days on the mountain were busy. He didn’t have time to think about too much.

Withered and yellow leaves fell from the branches. Lu Gu walked to the small slope of the pine forest with a wooden stick, stepping on a layer of fallen leaves. A long-tailed tit fell on a tree not far away, its feathers blue in part. He saw it, but his eyes stayed on the ground. He had come out to pick mushrooms today, and found many; he picked and put them into the bamboo basket on his back.

He left the pup in the yard and couldn’t take him everywhere. Now the pup was almost three months old. When he bought him outside, the puppy had to grow up a bit so he wouldn’t keep barking at people. In a few months the pup would be half-grown, and he could take him with him when he went out; otherwise the mountain road was far away, and the tall grass would block the pup.

At the foot of the pine tree, a nest of yellowish mushrooms was fat and big. There were several flowers. Lu Gu squatted and brushed away the broken branches and weeds with his hands. There were many white mushrooms in the basket, white and full, their caps with skirts that looked like a fishing net. These were the bamboo mushrooms he picked in the depths of the bamboo forest. The sticky black mushroom heads had been removed; he would dry and sell them in town. They were valuable and a rare treasure.

When Lu Gu was young, he went to the mountains with his mother to collect mushrooms and remembered a lot of edible kinds. The bamboo mushrooms looked different from other mushrooms, so they were easier to distinguish. He would only pick the white ones and wouldn’t pick ones that couldn’t be eaten.

Before picking, he checked the bamboo basket. There were bamboo mushrooms and other miscellaneous mushrooms. He had already filled half the basket, which was quite a lot. He walked around for a long time and wandered quite far. Today Dahui and Shen Xuanqing had gone to hunt, so he went back alone.

The mountain forest was dyed in autumn colors and more beautiful than before. Lu Gu walked back with the wooden stick; the bamboo basket on his back felt lighter than usual, and he moved a bit more briskly. The autumn wind was refreshing, and with the beautiful scenery in front of him, Lu Gu was in a good mood. On the way, he came across a mountain persimmon tree, its fruits hanging on the branches, some already red. He tiptoed and pulled down the branches, picking the ripe fruits.

The wild persimmons on the mountains were eaten by birds, especially those on the top of the branches, which ripen early. When Lu Gu saw a few pecked ones at the bottom, he didn’t pick them. He wondered how the birds knew the persimmons were ripe. Yes, perhaps like Dahui, they could smell it.

Persimmons could be soaked in wine and vinegar. Before going up the mountain, Wei Lanxiang had told him he would make persimmon vinegar this year. Before he went down the mountain next time, he’d pick more mountain persimmons and bring them back. You only needed to make some so you wouldn’t have too much to eat, and you had to save sun-dried persimmons for winter.

There were already mushrooms and persimmons. Lu Gu noticed a beautiful mountain flower beside him, so he picked two and played with them in his hands as he walked. Thinking of this, his brows and eyes carried a slight smile.

He was busy picking in the mountains every day, and even Lu Gu didn’t realize it. He hadn’t thought of fear when he first came to the mountains for long; he had gradually grown used to such days. In addition, one could find a little fun in one’s busy schedule. And when he saw Shen Xuanqing in the evening, he would feel more at ease. Sometimes Shen Xuanqing looked at him and smiled, and he would be happy in his heart, but he didn’t know how to express it, so he lowered his head in embarrassment and simply told Shen Xuanqing that the meal was ready, or politely handed Shen Xuanqing a pile of the day’s picked fruits to eat.

As he walked, he heard a mountain stream flowing, so Lu Gu went over to wash his hands, sat on a rock by the stream, and took a mountain persimmon from the bamboo basket. The ripe persimmons were soft; he gently peeled off the outer skin to reveal the sweet, soft flesh inside. There was a layer of slightly astringent skin around the flesh, but it was quite delicious and not terribly astringent.

After tasting the soft, sweet persimmon, Lu Gu felt relieved. He picked up the bamboo basket again and walked back, having gathered a lot of fruit. He planned to bring it to Shen Xuanqing when he returned; if he couldn’t finish them, he could mix the persimmons into the noodles he made—persimmon pancakes were sweet and delicious, and otherwise it would be a pity if the persimmons rotted.
