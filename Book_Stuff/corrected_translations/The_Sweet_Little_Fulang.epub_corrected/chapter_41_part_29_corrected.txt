Chapter 41 Part 29


At noon the sun was blazing, and almost no one in the village with paddy fields returned home to eat. It was enough for the whole family to go back to cook, while the others kept cutting rice, resting on the ridge between fields, eating and drinking as food and water were delivered.

Shen Xuanqing’s family originally owned five acres of paddy and five acres of dry land, earned by Shen Shunfu in his youth. In the village there were many fields, and there was no barren land. They had sold two acres of paddy and three acres of dry land.

After paying off the debt this summer, Shen Yaoqing contemplated buying a few more acres, but since Wei Lanxiang hoped to marry Shen Xuanqing off, she didn’t have much money on hand, so she put it aside to save later.

The three acres of paddy had a bumper harvest, and the two acres of dry land had already harvested autumn beans and then planted winter wheat. Recently there was not much to worry about; harvesting autumn rice was the big thing.

Lu Gu stepped into the mud and bent over to cut the rice. His body was splashed with muddy water, and the sun was hot. After a while, he straightened up slowly, wiped the sweat from his brow with his sleeve, and looked ahead. The figures of people coming out of the village were Shen Yan and Ji Qiuyue.

“Mother, take a break.” From a distance, Ji Qiuyue cried out; she and Shen Yan both carried baskets filled with water and food.

Wei Lanxiang straightened up upon hearing the voice and said, “Breakfast is here; rest a moment.”

Shen Xuanqing and Shen Yaoqing cut quickly and were already ahead. Lu Gu wanted to catch up with them, but Wei Lanxiang told him to stop and work again when he was full and strong. It was early in the morning, so he should take a break for now.

Ji Qiuyue warmed up several steamed buns, fried loach and eel in oil, brought pickled vegetables, and fried another dish. During the busy farming season, people had to eat well and drink enough oil and water, or they would be hungry quickly after work.

The loach and eel had been caught by Shen Yaoqing before the water in the paddy field was cut off, and some autumn rice fish had been kept in pots at home, ready to be eaten immediately after catching, so there was no need to buy meat.

She and Shen Yan sat on the ground to eat together, filled a bamboo tube with water, and made a bowl of brown sugar water for the whole family to share.

“This year’s harvest is good. After the harvest is over, let’s go to town to eat,” Wei Lanxiang said, looking at the golden rice with joy in her eyes. After so long under the sun, her face was ruddy, with a few wrinkles, but it looked kind, and the smile on her weathered face showed warmth.

“That’s for sure,” Ji Qiuyue replied, swallowing the steamed buns. “I don’t want to talk about what comes after. I’ll just wash the rice, steam it, and eat dry rice at night, and we’ll have more strength tomorrow.”

Dry rice, naturally, filled the stomach better than diluted rice porridge, and their family wasn’t badly off; many countryside families could only eat dry rice during the busy farming season.

“Hey,” Wei Lanxiang nodded in agreement; they must eat well after work.

In the joy of the recent rice harvest, the three acres of paddy had been cut and hauled to the grain field by a cart to dry.

Since it was still early, Wei Lanxiang resolved to go back to wash and rest her feet, and then head to town for lunch in the afternoon; they would come back in the evening after finishing.

Meat could be eaten at home, but restaurants were a different matter. Everyone in the Shen family had smiles on their faces, even Lu Gu; he looked forward to it, having never eaten anything in restaurants or taverns.
