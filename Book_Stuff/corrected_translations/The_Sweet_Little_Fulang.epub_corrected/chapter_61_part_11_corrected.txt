Chapter 61 Part 11


There were many coolies at the wharf who couldn’t afford to buy food from the stalls or shops, so they found a place to squat or sit and eat cold steamed buns and pancakes. It was very common for country people to eat on the street.

There were still three quarters of an hour to go back, and the roasted chicken would be cold on the way. It wasn’t inedible when cold, but it was different after all when it came out of the oven. It was hot and fresh.

Lu Gu took a bite of the chicken leg. The roast chicken carried a strong aroma. The meat was crisp and tender, not dry, and it came off the bone easily. It was clean to eat; even the bones were crisp and delicious. In the end, you could still smell the meat on the bones. They usually had stewed chicken at home. If you wanted something spicy, you could add some chili peppers. There weren’t many ingredients available, but roasted chicken made with brined spices had a special taste, no wonder it sold for 30 copper coins.

Lu Gu ate two bites, and it was delicious, but he couldn’t eat alone. How could he let Shen Xuanqing stand beside him? “You eat.” He handed over the chicken leg.

Shen Xuanqing originally wanted to refuse, but Lu Gu raised his eyes and looked at him so seriously that he couldn’t say no even if he wanted to, and he didn’t answer. He lowered his head and took a bite, then pushed Lu Gu’s hand back.

“You eat. There’s still more in the oiled paper bag; go back and eat some with mother.” Shen Xuanqing said with a smile. He had wanted to buy one just now, but he thought the thick chicken thighs were the most delicious. If he bought only one, the youngest in the family, Shen Yan, would definitely want to eat chicken thighs. The rest, his mother would eat, and Lu Gu wouldn’t be able to. If he bought two, he and his elder brother wouldn’t have to eat, and the four women in the family could each have a chicken leg—wouldn’t that be wonderful?

Besides, there were six people in the family; one roast chicken wasn’t enough to go around, and he and Shen Yaoqing alone could share one meal.

After eating the hot and fragrant chicken leg, Lu Gu wiped the oil from his hands with a handkerchief, though not very cleanly, and it would be fine when he returned and washed with the wild soapberries.

The wheels of the cart turned again. Compared with the rush to the town early in the morning, Shen Xuanqing’s steps were much more leisurely. Beside him was his fulang, who smiled so beautifully that it made him feel better. The sky was blue and broad, the sun bright, and when the wind blew, it wasn’t cold at all. It felt refreshing and at ease in his heart.

The two of them had bought these good things on their way back from the early market. Wei Lanxiang touched the soft, warm cotton in the sack and smiled until the lines around her eyes deepened. Even Ji Qiuyue was a little excited, because Shen Xuanqing had said that he would make thick winter clothes for the family, including clothes and trousers, and even a pair of cotton shoes, which meant that she would also have them.

They also bought a Tang Pozi, so they wouldn’t freeze at night in winter. Shen Yan and Wei Lanxiang lived together, and that was enough, not to mention Ji Qiuyue and Shen Yaoqing, who would also sleep in the same bed.

“It’s cold in the mountains, and the cold water in the river seeps into the bones. Give this to Guzi to warm his hands during the day.” Shen Xuanqing saw that Shen Yan was curious to touch the pattern on the small Tang Pozi, and casually said that it was bought for Lu Gu.

The cotton and the roasted chicken—both at his expense, with no copper coins asked from anyone else. The rest of the Shen family naturally wouldn’t say anything. Wei Lanxiang echoed his words and smiled: “You should buy it. It’s time to buy it. What will happen if our Guzi is frozen?”

The two of them had just come back for lunch; Shen Yaoqing didn’t go to work in the town today, so he warmed up the two roast chickens for the whole family to eat, and the two brothers also brought out wine and had a few drinks while eating the meat.

Lu Gu had eaten chicken leg before, and now he didn’t even touch the remaining three, which had been reserved for his mother and others; he knew about it.

After the meal, the two went back to the room to rest. Lu Gu opened the box and took out a big money bag from the bottom. It was the money he had earned from selling at the market before. After counting today’s money, he put it in and kept saving it. Shen Xuanqing’s big money bag was next to it. Today, Shen Xuanqing had only sold eight wild animals, earning a little, and spent more than two taels.

Shen Xuanqing, who was sitting at the table drinking water, saw him looking over and said, “Take out mine too.”

Lu Gu took out both money bags, and the copper coins inside clattered. He held them in his hand; they were heavy, and he couldn’t help but smile, thinking it was really nice.

The two of them sat at the table and laid out the money bags and purses in their arms, pouring out the broken silver and copper coins inside. Today Lu Gu’s pile of copper coins was obviously larger.

Not too much, not too little—the wild soapberries were sold for exactly 190 copper coins, and there were still some not sold out, which they brought back. The goji berries were sold for three taels and eighty copper coins.

Lu Gu opened the big money bag; inside was a five-tael silver ingot that Shen Xuanqing had given him before. It hadn’t been used so far. There were sixteen taels of silver and more than five hundred copper coins. There weren’t many cloth and embroidery threads left, and he would have to buy them again when they ran out.

Add today’s copper coins, and the total was almost 800 copper coins; the broken silver totaled 19 taels.

Sitting at the table, Lu Gu counted all the copper coins, murmuring the number to himself, and laughed as he counted—the more he counted, the happier he was.

Sitting across from him, Shen Xuanqing kept laughing, and finally noticed that he was five copper coins short of the total of 800 copper coins, so he took five from his purse and gave them to Lu Gu, to make up the entire amount.
