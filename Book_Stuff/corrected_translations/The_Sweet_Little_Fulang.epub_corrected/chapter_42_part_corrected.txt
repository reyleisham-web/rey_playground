Chapter 42


After they had rested and settled the accounts, Wei Lanxiang took the money, and the family locked the door as they happily headed to town.

 autumn harvest had kept people busy for days, and there were still many folk unloading autumn rice. Carts filled the roads, a scene of hustle and bustle, all of it about harvest. Red-faced and sun-tanned people wore broad smiles of joy.

In prior years, Lu Gu had gone to harvest with the Lu family and grew tired from the hard work. Now, watching other families gather so much rice with such a cheerful air, he too felt the happiness that a good harvest could bring.

When they reached Fenggu Town, the street was crowded, and they could still see people pushing rice carts. Not all townsfolk were artisans; ordinary people lived there too, farmers who had land beyond the town walls. In essence, they were still like country folk.

Wei Lanxiang scanned the row of restaurants and taverns along the street and asked Shen Yaoqing, “Daqing, where do you think we should eat?”

Shen Yaoqing considered a moment and replied, “We’ve eaten at Chen Ji Restaurant before. Why not go there? It’s an old shop that’s been around for years.”

“Okay, let’s go there.” Wei Lanxiang hadn’t eaten in town for years, so she’d been unsure about where to go. Now she remembered Chen Ji’s braised beef, which Shen Shunfu had taken her to sample several times when she was young. People in the countryside often ate chicken, duck, and pork; beef and mutton were expensive and rarely bought. Moreover, the stewed meat in sauce from restaurant kitchens tasted truly different from what you cooked at home.

Chen Ji’s Restaurant was on East Street, so once the decision was made, there was no need to look around. A party of six followed Shen Xuanqing into East Street and headed straight for Chen Ji.

A young boy, Xiaoer, greeted them at the door. On seeing them, he bowed slightly and said politely, “Guests, please.”

After they were seated, Shen Yaoqing and Shen Xuanqing urged Wei Lanxiang to choose her favorite dishes. There were so many options, and two tall men among them stood out; even though they were dressed like country folk, their means were evident in their ability to dine here. Xiaoer poured water for them and, while filling the glasses, quickly recited the names of the dishes.

Lu Gu had never set foot in a restaurant before and knew little about how to order. He studied the layout nervously and curiously. When he heard Xiaoer’s fast, sing-song voice, he listened with rapt attention, finding the cadence novel and captivating.

Wei Lanxiang thought it over for a moment and said, “Duck feet, big elbows, and sauced beef, plus two jars of good wine.”

The food would be expensive, and they weren’t about to eat vegetarian dishes. These big meat dishes would be enough. When people back home asked how they’d fared, they could tell a decent story.

“Okay, sir, one moment. Let us have a cup of tea first.” Xiaoer draped a towel over his shoulder, then relayed the dish names to the kitchen. More guests arrived at the door, and he stepped forward to greet them as well.

Wei Lanxiang, who had lived a good life with Shen Shunfu, had learned not to fear ordering and was not stingy. Most of the meat and drink would end up in the stomachs of Shen Xuanqing and Shen Yaoqing. If they didn’t eat too much, they wouldn’t be full, but today they would.

Even though she preferred her two sons, they were the main breadwinners of the family, and she had earned the money to treat them to a feast today. It was only natural to spoil them a little.

“Is there any green plum wine?” Shen Xuanqing asked when Xiaoer came by with two jars of wine.

“Yes, sir. Would you like a jar?” Xiaoer replied with a smile.

The price of green plum wine was three copper coins higher than outside. Shen Xuanqing asked for a jar, and Wei Lanxiang kept silent—she didn’t object, since Shen Xuanqing had asked for it and his face mattered.

The big elbows, sauced beef, and duck feet arrived soon. It took a while for the elbow to be braised, but when hot and tender, it was delicious.

With the meat and wine laid out, the table felt alive. Lu Gu and Shen Yan each took a small glass and toasted with Shen Yaoqing and Shen Xuanqing, then each offered a toast to Wei Lanxiang.

Wei Lanxiang laughed so hard she could barely keep her mouth closed. After the wine, she urged, “Eat meat, eat meat—today we’ll go home with our bellies full.”

Chen Ji’s Restaurant charged more, but the meat dishes came in generous portions. The sauced beef boasted a bright, fresh flavor—neither tough nor overcooked, just meaty and refreshing. Lu Gu had never eaten beef before; he took a piece, and his eyes widened at the taste. He stuffed the rest of the meat into his mouth and chewed, astonished. Shen Xuanqing, who happened to glance at him, couldn't help but chuckle and scooped another piece of beef for him with his chopsticks.

Shen Yaoqing ate and drank contentedly and remarked, “I’ve heard that eating beef in winter warms the stomach. I’ll buy a big piece of beef to take home and we’ll eat it then.”

“Look at you—so greedy you can’t even wait to cut it up,” Ji Qiuyue teased him with a sideways glance, though the smile on her face showed she meant it affectionately.

People live for better days, and even talking about it can bring joy.

The aroma of the wine filled the air, mingling with the scent of the duck feet, which carried a salty, fragrant tang. The big elbow appeared on the table, a large plate laid in the center.

“Bring bowls or plates,” Shen Xuanqing told Xiaoer. The elbow meat was so tender that the lean parts and the fat were hard to pick up; it was hot to the touch, so holding it with hands would have risked it slipping. They all passed the elbow around and used bowls to prevent anything from slipping.

Xiaoer moved quickly, setting a bowl for each person.

Everyone reached for the elbow, and Lu Gu lagged a beat behind. With five pairs of chopsticks on the table, he was wary of crowding others. Just as the elbow reached his mouth, he could smell the rich meat aroma, even after having already eaten beef and duck feet.

Before Lu Gu could lift his chopsticks, Shen Xuanqing — whose arms were longer than the others — slipped a piece of fat and elbow meat into his bowl.
