Chapter 88 Part 22


Li Wanyun was somewhat aware of the sympathy and pity others showed, but she was no longer embarrassed by it. She returned home with the bowl, and the old Lao Zhang, who used to call her back and forth, huddled in her room and didn’t dare to speak. Li Wanyun had originally wanted to starve Lao Zhang a few times, to let her taste that feeling, but then she reconsidered: Lao Zhang was old and frail and had a broken leg, and if she couldn’t endure the starvation and died, even if she wanted to remarry, she would have to wait until the funeral was over, and with the Zhang family’s relatives watching, she would have a hard time living alone. So she had to serve meals and tea to her with cold eyes.

Now, she no longer knelt down before entering Lao Zhang’s room, and she didn’t even need to call her mother anymore. She would simply set down the meal and leave, not caring about anything else.

In the kitchen, Li Wanyun was busy alone; in the room, Lao Zhang barely leaned on the headboard, her old eyes half-closed, looking depressed and fatigued. Li Wanyun tidied Lao Zhang up, even combed her hair. If some relatives came over, as long as they had eyes, they wouldn’t say anything when they saw that Lao Zhang was clean and tidy.

Lao Zhang’s eyes were half shut, and she suddenly recalled that on the day she and Zhang Zhengzi were married, she had been beautiful and bright in red cotton clothes. Li Wanyun was chopping vegetables, which brought her back to her senses.

When they first got married back then, Lao Zhang had treated Li Wanyun well, but as a mother-in-law she had to set the rules; otherwise the little daughter-in-law would be lawless. Zhang Zhengzi had once stood up for Li Wanyun, and at the time she felt a sting in her heart: if she couldn’t control her daughter-in-law in the future, her son wouldn’t listen to her.

In addition, Li Wanyun’s belly hadn’t moved since she got married. It was okay for six months, and she could wait, but after a year, after a year and a half, she still couldn’t bear a son or a daughter, so she grew anxious, feeling as if she had been cheated and had married her son to a barren wife. All these things made Lao Zhang dislike Li Wanyun more and more; beating and scolding gradually became commonplace.

As for regrets, Lao Zhang herself didn’t know. Sometimes she even forgot Zhang Zhengzi’s death, and still called out her son’s name. She knew she was afraid of Li Wanyun, but she still couldn’t figure out how she had arrived at this point. Sometimes she subconsciously called Li Wanyun to serve her, and when she saw the coldness in the other person’s eyes, she would shrink her head back, obedient and not daring to speak again.

In the past few days, she grew more and more depressed, as if living in a dream, both illusory and muddled. Old age with a weak body was like this: if Li Wanyun didn’t give her food and thick quilts, and decoctions, she would have gone underground to meet Zhang Zhengzi long ago.

The laba porridge was fragrant, and the second room of the Shen family’s house was full of people, like in other families. The Zhang family was cold; Li Wanyun sat alone in the main room eating laba porridge and vegetables. She dressed warmly and ate well enough, and she felt that she could become part of the bustle in this world.
