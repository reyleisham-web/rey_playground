Chapter 98


The winter of the year before last was exceptionally cold, and wild vegetables were scarce in early spring. That year, wild vegetables could fetch four to five copper coins per catty.

Dahui and the puppy followed Lu Gu up the mountain. It was early spring, and the puppy had grown again. The thick coat from winter hadn’t yet faded, and he looked strong and healthy, running around the mountains with boundless energy, his ears quivering.

Digging wild vegetables was very familiar to Lu Gu. He wasn’t far from the place where Shen Xuanqing collected firewood, and in a short while the bamboo basket he carried was half full. In a few days, spring bamboo shoots would appear; even if they didn’t go into the deep mountains, they could still find them and sell them in the market later. He shook mud from the roots of the wild vegetables as he thought about earning money.

Relying on the mountains and eating from the mountains—if they worked hard, they could earn money. Of course, it would be best if the money they earned were in their own hands, so they could feel at ease in their hearts. Unlike before, he couldn’t even scrape together a copper coin from his hard work.

When he heard the dogs barking away, he stood up and called into the mountain, “Guaizi!”

“Wow!” 
The puppy answered and came running after a while.

Lu Gu picked up the bamboo basket and was about to go see Shen Xuanqing, when a thin figure gradually appeared on the hillside not far away: Chen Dongdong, the fulang of the He family.

Because Chen Dongdong was timid and cowardly, he didn’t speak loudly, which made Lu Gu feel easy to get along with him. So Lu Gu smiled and said, “Dong ger, you also come to dig wild vegetables?”

Chen Dongdong wore patched clothes. He was shorter than Lu Gu and thin, and the mole between his eyebrows wasn’t very red. He also got along well with Lu Gu, pursed his lips, and gave an unusual, shy, jerky smile.

Guaizi ran over; he looked strong, and to Chen Dongdong’s eyes he looked vicious, so Chen Dongdong couldn’t help but feel a little scared. Fortunately, Lu Gu called the puppy back, and his emotions settled.

“Are you alone?” Lu Gu patted the puppy on the head. Although this place wasn’t in the deep mountains, it was still far from the foot of the mountain. It was okay for men to go alone to this place, but if it was women or Shuang’er, it would be worrisome.

“He Zhi is behind,” Chen Dongdong said as he came over. When he saw the bamboo basket in Lu Gu’s hand, he said, “You’ve dug quite a lot.”

“Yeah.” Lu Gu smiled; his brows and eyes curved. He no longer worried when he heard that He Zhi was following him.

While they talked, another figure appeared on the hillside—He Zhi himself.

Because Lu Gu hadn’t been familiar with him, He Zhi hesitated when he saw Lu Gu. In the end he only nodded slightly, and Lu Gu nodded back.

“Guzi, let’s go dig wild vegetables first.” Chen Dongdong glanced at his husband, said goodbye, and then went to the other side. Lu Gu had already dug in this place before, so there weren’t many wild vegetables left to find.

Staying at home for the past few months, Lu Gu had already learned about the He family from Wei Lanxiang. Not to mention Chen Dongdong; the clothes on He Zhi’s body were also full of patches. It wasn’t that they were lazy; there was a sick old woman and a lame father in their family. They were old and prone to illness, and when they fell sick they had to buy medicine and spend money.

In the past few years, it seemed their luck hadn’t been good, and there were other money-draining things that hampered them. They always spent as much as they earned, and life was a little stretched—poor.

He Zhi was usually a quiet person who seldom spoke to others even though he was bullied, but he was honest and enthusiastic. If someone in the neighborhood had something to do, they would ask him to help, and he rarely refused. So even if someone wanted to bully him, the neighbors would help him and scold the bully for him.

Also, even though they were poor, he wasn’t like some men who beat their wives or fulangs to vent their anger. Chen Dongdong lived with him in poverty, but he was never beaten.

There were often men in the village who beat their wives and fulangs. Chen Dongdong sometimes thought his rare luck was that he had married the right person. His natal family was also poor, so he didn’t feel life in the He family was so bad.

Chen Dongdong and his husband left, and Lu Gu also took the puppy to find Shen Xuanqing.

“Er Qing?” He had walked a long way, and now he couldn’t hear the sound of chopping firewood, so he called out to the front.

“Here.” Shen Xuanqing leaned out from behind the shaded trees, bending over to bundle the firewood.

When Lu Gu walked over, Shen Xuanqing had already tied up a large bundle of firewood.

“Do you still want to continue?” Lu Gu asked.

“Yeah.” Shen Xuanqing nodded: “Two more bundles, then pull them to town with a cart and we can sell more.”

“Then I’ll go dig wild vegetables.” Lu Gu thought for a moment and said, “Isn’t there still a shorter rope? I can carry it back if you chop some more later.”

“Okay.” Shen Xuanqing knew he was diligent, so he could just tie a few fewer bundles of firewood and let him carry them back. He thought Lu Gu could carry them for fun.

Lu Gu spotted shepherd’s purse under his feet, so he squatted to dig it up, and the puppy took advantage of the moment to rub his legs and his back. He was really a clingy dog.

Feeling the weight on his back, Lu Gu laughed and said, “Why are you so heavy?”

He picked the puppy up with his own hands; the puppy was smart and childlike, so his tone sounded like coaxing a child.

The puppy couldn’t understand human words, but he knew Lu Gu was talking to him, so he whined and pressed himself closer.

Shen Xuanqing, standing beside them, heard it and suddenly remembered that Lu Gu had just called him Er Qing. Usually Lu Gu stayed with him all the time and rarely called his name directly. That voice sounded unusually warm and soft. Obviously others might call him Er Qing as well, but those two syllables shifted in Lu Gu’s mouth, becoming intimate and lingering.
