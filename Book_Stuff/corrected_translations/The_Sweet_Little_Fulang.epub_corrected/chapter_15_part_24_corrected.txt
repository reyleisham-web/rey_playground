Chapter 15 Part 24


Shen Yan was very happy, crooked smile on her face as she held Lu Gu’s hand. “Brother Lu Gu, let’s buy this next time when we earn money.”

“Yeah.” Lu Gu nodded, but both of them knew they couldn’t decide what to buy, since neither of them had earned any money yet.

After lunch, everyone went back to their tasks. Lu Gu washed the dishes and found nothing else to do for the moment, so he planned to go back to his room to rest. But when he opened the door, he saw half a piece of Baiyun Cake left in the bowl on the table. He finished what he had, and the others rarely entered this room—only Shen Xuanqing’s entered.

At this time, Shen Xuanqing was in Wei Lanxiang’s room, talking about eight taels of silver. Wei Lanxiang’s eyes widened; after taking seven taels of silver, she looked at the scattered coins and whispered like a prayer, “Amitabha, Amitabha, if you lose, you gain. This is true.”

She muttered on, unable to stop: “People always say don’t do wrong; good luck will come. We’ll earn back the money the Lu family swindled from us.” She added, “I told you to treat Lu Gu better; you still should do good deeds, not be as dark-hearted as Du Hehua.” She hurriedly hid the broken silver. A few more taels would be enough to repay the debt. Once the debt was paid, all the money they earned in the future would be theirs.

Shen Xuanqing came out while she hid the money. The ten taels of silver had been borrowed for his marriage to a wife, and he was supposed to repay it, so he had given the money to Wei Lanxiang and handed it over to the public. When he married, they had not only spent the ten taels of silver, but half of the other expenses were from his private money, and the other half was paid by Wei Lanxiang and Shen Yaoqing. Since Shen Yaoqing had paid all the money, he could not ask his elder brother to help pay off the debt. Now that he had earned eight taels, he had nothing to hide and had made his position clear to his family. He kept the remaining silver for himself, because he often went to the town to sell prey, and he bought most of the oil, salt, and other things that were lacking at home, so he had some money in hand.

When he returned to his room, he saw Lu Gu holding the clothes he had changed into last night and about to go out to wash. The two met at the door, and Lu Gu quickly stepped aside.

Shen Xuanqing glanced at the Baiyun Cake on the table, lowered his voice and said, “I tasted it; it’s not good. You can eat it.”

He kept his voice low so that no one else could hear him. Except for his own mother, no one gave him anything good to eat. Lu Gu felt an indescribable emotion, but it quickly faded. In his eyes, Shen Xuanqing was tall and cold. It must be that it wasn’t delicious, or there wouldn’t be leftovers.

Days passed quickly. Lu Gu’s indigo-dyed new clothes were ready, and Wei Lanxiang accompanied him to pick them up and had a new pair of shoes made. Lu Gu, who worked on shoe soles, and Wei Lanxiang each had a pair, and they could finish things quickly.

The weather grew colder, the sun no longer as hot, and after dinner he had some free time. He wore the new shoes and clothes and, led by Wei Lanxiang, visited Aunt Gu’s house in the east of the upper village.

Shen’s house was close to the mountain, in the innermost part; going to the village entrance felt almost like wandering around the village.

“Aunt Lanxiang, the clothes on the second family’s body are really pretty.”

Grandma A Jin and the other mothers were chatting and working under the big tree at the village gate, and they all praised Lu Gu for wearing that indigo dress. “He’s handsome too.” Lu Gu blushed; Wei Lanxiang smiled from ear to ear and went on to Gu’s house after a few words.

Aunt Gu’s son, Gu Sheng, was a street peddler who sold all kinds of groceries. When there wasn’t enough needlework at home, Wei Lanxiang came to buy some.

As they entered, they saw Aunt Gu drying dried fish in the yard, and the smell filled the air. She smiled and asked, “Shengzi’s mother, do you have any cotton thread?” Aunt Gu said quickly, wiped her hands on the apron tied around her waist, and went into the house to fetch it.

Wei Lanxiang also bought some colored thread, saying she would embroider a few flowers on the shoes Lu Gu and Shen Yan made. They didn’t wear gold and silver in the countryside as they did in the county town; a few flowers embroidered on their clothes were considered beautiful.

It wasn’t just a purchase of thread; it was also a chance for Lu Gu to be shown off in front of people.

When Aunt Gu saw Lu Gu’s new clothes and shoes, she naturally wanted to praise him. Besides, Lu Gu’s appearance wasn’t bad; he was pretty and fair, and the indigo cloth made him look even whiter.

Recently, Lu Gu had eaten well and slept well at Shen’s house, his complexion had improved, even the red mark between his eyebrows looked a little pink and healthy—he really was becoming handsome.

Wei Lanxiang led him around the village until she was satisfied.

When the two returned home, Lu Gu looked at the colorful embroidery threads in the sewing basket, his expression a little concentrated, as if lost in thought. A few dogs barking outside interrupted his reverie.

Shen Xuanqing, who had been in the mountains for five days, returned with a catch of hares and pheasants. It wasn’t a bad haul for wild game, but when he pulled out the cloth bag from the bottom of the bamboo basket, Lu Gu saw what lay inside. The strip moved; it looked like a snake, no matter how you looked at it.
