Transmigration Farming Slapping in the Face Chapter 90 Part 2 (End)

Since one table couldn't seat everyone, they split into two tables.

Xu Rong and the others sat at one table, while Lan Qianjue's family and Zhang Tianwen's family sat at the other.

Little Doubao began to cling to Little Doumi. The two little ones didn't sit next to the adults. Instead, they each held a bowl of rice, piled with many dishes, placed them on a wooden stool, and squatted on the ground facing each other, playing and eating. From time to time, they picked food from each other's bowls to eat, just like playing house, often laughing heartily.

After the group ate the New Year's Eve meal boisterously, the dishes on the table were left for Xu Rong and the others to clean up. They didn't all stay together; instead, they paired off or gathered in small groups to find places to play. Little Doumi and Little Doubao were inseparable. They went to ask each elder for lucky money. After Shen Jiyao and Lan Qianjue each gave ten taels to the two children, they paid them no more mind and instead went hand in hand to the river outside the village.

The sky gradually darkened. The two found a large rock to sit on. Shen Jiyao sat in front of Lan Qianjue, leaning against his chest. The two quietly watched the river below, not speaking for a long time.

From the direction of the village came the crackling sound of firecrackers. The noise came wave after wave, lasting for a full quarter of an hour before gradually subsiding. Moments later, brilliant fireworks shot up into the clouds. The river reflected the splendid scene above. Both of them looked up, shared a smile amidst the dazzling fireworks, and lightly touched their lips together in a moment of perfect understanding.

After a long silence, Shen Jiyao said: "I had someone clear a few plots of land on the outskirts of the woods outside the resort. In the future, we can grow some vegetables there, raise some chickens, ducks, and geese, and cultivate some fish and shrimp in the lake. What do you think."

"You say it, I'll do it." Lan Qianjue lowered his head and kissed Shen Jiyao's forehead, saying: "As long as I'm with you all, there's no problem doing anything."

Shen Jiyao smiled and continued planning:"Then we'll hire a private tutor for Doudou, cultivate him properly, so he can take the imperial examinations and become the top scholar someday. Life will be better for Little Doubao if he follows him."

Lan Qianjue said: "Whether he becomes the top scholar or not is unimportant. Look at us, and Brother Zhang and his family - aren't we all living perfectly well? As long as Doudou treats Little Doubao well, they will have a good life together regardless of whether they're poor or wealthy."

"That's true." Shen Jiyao nodded.

"Just like us, whether wealthy or poor, it doesn't affect our feelings." Lan Qianjue said tenderly: "Jiyao, I'm so fortunate to have met you."

Hearing this, Shen Jiyao smiled. He leaned sideways into Lan Qianjue's embrace and said: "I feel the same."

The two held each other quietly. The biting cold wind couldn't reach Shen Jiyao at all, blocked entirely by Lan Qianjue. Shen Jiyao closed his eyes and sat quietly for a long time before softly saying to Lan Qianjue: "Actually, I know you've always thought I've changed too much, that I'm not the same as when we first met. I just pretended not to see your strange looks because I didn't know how to explain it to you back then. But I want to tell you now. Do you want to hear it."

"Tell me."

Shen Jiyao pondered for a long time before saying, "I'm not Shen Jiyao. You knew about his accident back then. But I'm also him, this body is his. It just depends on how you see it."

Shen Jiyao had originally intended to explain in more detail, regardless of whether Lan Qianjue believed him or not, but he suddenly didn't want to go into too much detail. The truth was simply too unbelievable, and since he himself couldn't make sense of it, he didn't want to burden Lan Qianjue with the confusing details.

Sure enough, Lan Qianjue was a bit confused by what he heard, but he didn't overthink it. Instead, he said, "Your behavior is indeed different from before. It just feels a bit strange, that's all. You are still you, and that's what matters. I love who you are now. Whether you are him or not, as long as you stay by my side, I'm happy."

Shen Jiyao had expected Lan Qianjue to say something like this, but hearing it in person still stirred a unique feeling of warmth in his heart. He turned his head, kissed Lan Qianjue, and then held his waist tightly.

Some truths are better left unspoken, for when hearts understand each other, words become unnecessary.

The Spring Festival gradually passed, and the group returned to the town. The restaurant was now completely handed over to Lan Qianjue to manage, while Shen Jiyao went to take care of matters at the mountain resort.

Five months later.

Six ox carts, loaded full of goods, slowly made their way along the main road. Shen Jiyao and Lan Qianjue, along with a group of workers and Little Doumi, sat on the first ox cart. The whole family joyfully headed towards the outskirts.

Heidan ran around happily, but Little Doumi wasn't very pleased. Because they were leaving the town, he wouldn't be able to see Little Doubao every day. Feeling a bit gloomy, he asked Shen Jiyao, "Dad, when can Doubao be with me all the time, like you and Father?"

Lan Qianjue and Shen Jiyao were huddled together, talking quietly. Hearing this, they were both surprised. Shen Jiyao was stunned for a long moment before saying, "In about fifteen years, you can do what you want. Of course, that's only if Little Doubao wants to be with you too. Doudou, you'll have to keep a close watch, don't let your future fulang slip away."

"What are you teaching him that for?" Lan Qianjue said from the side, not knowing whether to laugh or cry.

"Didn't you also teach him to yield to Little Doubao, or else he wouldn't be able to marry him in the future?" Shen Jiyao teased him with a laugh.

Lan Qianjue rubbed his nose awkwardly and fell silent. The topic was successfully diverted once again. Little Doumi looked gloomy, but when they arrived at the mountain resort, he brightened up. Little Doubao, along with his dad and father, were waiting outside the main gate of the resort. Shen Jiyao, however, was stunned when he saw the resort decorated inside and out in bright red.

He said to Lan Qianjue with disbelief, "I haven't been here for just a few days, and it's already been made so festive. It's just a move, there's no need to make it this celebratory."

Lan Qianjue smiled, exchanged a look with Zhang Tianwen who was waiting outside the resort gate, and then said to Shen Jiyao, "I specifically asked Brother Zhang to arrange this. Jiyao, today isn't just about moving, it's also our wedding day. I want to properly hold a joyous occasion with you. What do you think?"

Upon hearing this, Shen Jiyao was so moved that his heart ached. His eyes, which had never shed a tear before, instantly welled up. Before long, his eyelashes were damp with tears. Lan Qianjue felt immense heartache and quickly pulled him into his arms, comforting him softly.

Not speaking of it didn't mean not wanting it. Lan Qianjue had always kept this matter in his heart, which deeply moved Shen Jiyao and made him even more grateful that he had entrusted his life to the right person. He held tightly to Lan Qianjue's lean and strong waist, reluctant to let go for a long time.

That day, the vermilion main gate of the mountain resort was adorned with exquisitely cut red double happiness characters. The joyful sound of firecrackers lasted for a long time, and words of blessing never ceased.

In life, it's rare to find a soulmate. Being with someone who understands and loves you brings happiness every day, regardless of life's ups and downs.

The gers, who had struggled to find their place among the crowd, each found simple and sincere relationships. Whether in wealth or poverty, their affection remained unchanged—a truly precious thing.
